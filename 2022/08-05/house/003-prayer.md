# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Rev. Vincent DeRosa, St. Mary Mother of God Catholic Church, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God who order all things well and beautifully, 
grant to this people's House wisdom and prudence that amid the ever-
changing dynamics of a complex world we may secure justice and 
prosperity for our countrymen and peaceful coexistence with our 
neighbors. In a particular way, we ask You to hear our prayers for the 
sick or infirm in our land: grant them healing and bless their 
caregivers. Finally, author of life, we ask You to watch over the 
deceased whom we commend to Your care and those who grieve for them.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
