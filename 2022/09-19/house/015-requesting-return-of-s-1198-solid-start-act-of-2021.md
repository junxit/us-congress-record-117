# REQUESTING RETURN OF S. 1198, SOLID START ACT OF 2021

**Measures:** S. 1198

```
         REQUESTING RETURN OF S. 1198, SOLID START ACT OF 2021

  The Speaker pro tempore laid before the House the following 
privileged message from the Senate:

       Resolved, That the Secretary of the Senate be directed to 
     request the House of Representatives to return to the Senate 
     the bill (S. 1198) entitled ``An Act to amend title 38, 
     United States Code, to improve and expand the Solid Start 
     program of the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other 
     purposes.''.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the request of the Senate 
is agreed to, and S. 1198 will be returned to the Senate.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
