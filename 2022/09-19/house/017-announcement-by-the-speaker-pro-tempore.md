# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Brendan F. Boyle of Pennsylvania). 
Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair will postpone further 
proceedings today on motions to suspend the rules on which a recorded 
vote or the yeas and nays are ordered, or votes objected to under 
clause 6 of rule XX.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  September 19, 2022, on page H7886, in the third column, the 
following appeared: today on motions to suspend the rules on which 
or votes objected to under clause 6 of rule XX.
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: today on motions 
to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the yeas and nays 
are ordered, or votes objected to under clause 6 of rule XX.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


  The House will resume proceedings on postponed questions at a later 
time.

                          ____________________
```
