# OUR UNITED STATES CONSTITUTION

**Speaking:** Boyle, Brendan F. (B001296) [D-PA]

```
                     OUR UNITED STATES CONSTITUTION

  (Mr. BRENDAN F. BOYLE of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission 
to address the House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. BRENDAN F. BOYLE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, as the Chaplain 
just previously mentioned, we recently celebrated the 235th anniversary 
of the signing of the United States Constitution in Philadelphia.
  As the Member of Congress who represents the Independence Hall where 
it was drafted and adopted, it is a great honor for all of us in my 
district to know that our city played such an important role in the 
history and founding of our country.
  It is now up to all of us to again read our Constitution and bring it 
close to not only our minds but our hearts. Every single day we must 
work to uphold it and uphold our democracy.

                          ____________________
```
