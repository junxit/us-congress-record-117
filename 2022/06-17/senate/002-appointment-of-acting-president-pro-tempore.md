# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Parliamentarian will please read a 
communication to the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Leahy)
  The Parliamentarian read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                    Washington, DC, June 17, 2022.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule 1, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Elizabeth Warren, a Senator from the Commonwealth of 
     Massachusetts, to perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                 Patrick J. Leahy,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Ms. WARREN thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________
```
