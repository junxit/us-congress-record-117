# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we pause to thank You for providing us with strength for 
each day. As You continue to be our help in times of trouble, may our 
hearts be lifted to You in gratitude.
  Lord, guide our lawmakers to show their gratitude for Your mercies by 
obeying Your precepts as You help them navigate through these 
challenging times. May their reverence for You provide them with a 
wisdom that will glorify Your Name. Keep our Senators from deviating 
from integrity so that their thoughts, words, and actions will please 
You.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
