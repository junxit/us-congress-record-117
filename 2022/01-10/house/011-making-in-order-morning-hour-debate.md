# MAKING IN ORDER MORNING-HOUR DEBATE

**Speaking:** Clyburn, James E. (C000537) [D-SC]

**Measures:** H.Res. 11, H.Res. 863

```
                  MAKING IN ORDER MORNING-HOUR DEBATE

  Mr. CLYBURN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the order of 
the House of January 4, 2021, providing for morning-hour debate be 
extended for the remainder of the 117th Congress, except that House 
Resolution 863 shall supplant House Resolution 11.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from South Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
