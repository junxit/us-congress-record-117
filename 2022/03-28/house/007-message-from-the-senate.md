# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 253, S. 2102

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 253. An act to expand research on the cannabidiol and 
     marihuana.
       S. 2102. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     direct the Under Secretary for Health of the Department of 
     Veterans Affairs to provide mammography screening for 
     veterans who served in locations associated with toxic 
     exposure.
```
