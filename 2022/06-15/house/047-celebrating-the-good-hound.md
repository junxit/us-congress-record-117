# CELEBRATING THE GOOD HOUND

**Speaking:** Auchincloss, Jake (A000148) [D-MA]

```
                       CELEBRATING THE GOOD HOUND

  (Mr. AUCHINCLOSS asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. AUCHINCLOSS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Pride Month 
by recognizing an LGBTQ-owned local business, The Good Hound.
  The Good Hound offers off-leash hikes, pet sitting, training, and 
socialization in my hometown of Newton. I first met owner Shannon Wood 
when my dog, Donut, joined the group of dogs he helps take care of 
every day. Every June, we look forward to seeing Donut and all her 
friends sport pride bandanas as they join Haley for jaunts around our 
neighborhood.
  Pride Month is a time to celebrate the LGBTQ individuals we all know 
and love who are crucial parts of our communities, economy, and 
families. Congress must stand up and support small, LGBTQ-owned 
businesses across the country that serve our constituents every day. My 
family and Newton as a whole are better for the dedication, cheer, and 
openness Shannon brings to The Good Hound.

                          ____________________
```
