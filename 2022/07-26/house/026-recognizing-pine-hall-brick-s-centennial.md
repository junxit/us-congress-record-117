# RECOGNIZING PINE HALL BRICK'S CENTENNIAL

**Speaking:** Foxx, Virginia (F000450) [R-NC]

```
                RECOGNIZING PINE HALL BRICK'S CENTENNIAL

  (Ms. FOXX asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Ms. FOXX. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize an impeccable family-
owned company in North Carolina that is celebrating its 100th 
anniversary this year: Pine Hall Brick.
  In 1922, a man by the name of Flake Steele, Sr., founded Pine Hall 
Brick, and today, the Steele family's fourth generation owns and 
operates the business.
  In the construction industry, Pine Hall Brick is a force to be 
reckoned with. From building schools to major production facilities, 
this company has a reputation that spans the entire country and one 
that is highly regarded within the Tar Heel State.
  Congratulations to the Steele family and all the employees at Pine 
Hall Brick on 100 years of excellence. Here is to many more.

                          ____________________
```
