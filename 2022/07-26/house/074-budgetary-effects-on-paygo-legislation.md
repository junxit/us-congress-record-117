# BUDGETARY EFFECTS ON PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 310, H.R. 4590, H.R. 7132, H.R. 8454

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS ON PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 310, to posthumously award the 
Congressional Gold Medal, collectively, to Glen Doherty, Tyrone Woods, 
J. Christopher Stevens, and Sean Smith, in recognition of their 
contributions to the Nation, as amended, would have no significant 
effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such 
bill are estimated as zero.
```
