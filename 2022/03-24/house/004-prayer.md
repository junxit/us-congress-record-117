# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, we come to You with a fervent prayer for 
peace. You are a God of peace. May Your peace reign in our hearts and 
in our world.
  We make a special petition for peace in the Ukraine. May You watch 
over all Your children, especially those in harm's way.
  May Your love and peace triumph over hatred and violence. May we love 
our neighbor just as You do.
  We pray this in Thy sacred name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
