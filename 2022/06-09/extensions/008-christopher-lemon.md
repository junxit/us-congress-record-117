# CHRISTOPHER LEMON

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                           CHRISTOPHER LEMON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, June 9, 2022

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, last month, I had the pleasure of 
attending Christopher Lemon's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. Christopher 
belongs to Troop 1079 of Seaville, South Jersey. For his Eagle Scout 
project, he created and gifted Shore Medical Center over 200 wooden 
hearts for their memorial recognizing COVID-19 patients who recovered 
in their hospital. Christopher painted the hearts with Shore Medical 
Center's colors, purple and white, and put them on posts. The posts 
were placed in the front lawn of the center and resided there for 
several weeks during the summer of 2021. This young man should be proud 
of his contributions to the memorial and I wish him the best of luck in 
his future endeavors. God Bless Christopher, and God Bless our America.

                          ____________________
```
