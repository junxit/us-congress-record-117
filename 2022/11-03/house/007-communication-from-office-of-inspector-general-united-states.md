# COMMUNICATION FROM OFFICE OF INSPECTOR GENERAL, UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, JOSEPH C. PICOLLA

```
COMMUNICATION FROM OFFICE OF INSPECTOR GENERAL, UNITED STATES HOUSE OF 
                   REPRESENTATIVES, JOSEPH C. PICOLLA

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Joseph C. Picolla, Inspector General, United States 
House of Representatives:

                                      Office of Inspector General,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, October 30, 2022.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker of the House,
     House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of 
     the House of Representatives, this letter is to notify you 
     that I, Joseph C. Picolla, Inspector General for the U.S. 
     House of Representatives, have been served with a subpoena 
     for documents issued by the Superior Court for the District 
     of Columbia.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is not 
     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                Joseph C. Picolla,
     Inspector General.

                          ____________________
```
