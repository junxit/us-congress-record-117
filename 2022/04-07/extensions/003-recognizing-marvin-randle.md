# RECOGNIZING MARVIN RANDLE

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MARVIN RANDLE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 7, 2022

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Marvin 
Randle, former mayor of Irving. Randle is a native and lifelong 
resident of Irving and served two consecutive two-year terms from May 
1977 to April 1981.
  Marvin has also had a great impact on our community as a small 
business owner, founding Irving Counter Top in 1962. Over the years, 
the family-owned-and-operated business has continued to grow along with 
the DFW Metroplex.
  Marvin is a longtime friend and mentor. It is an honor to help 
celebrate such a full and impactful career and legacy, as Marvin 
retires this month. I thank Marvin for his great contributions to our 
local government and the impact he has made on our community.

                          ____________________
```
