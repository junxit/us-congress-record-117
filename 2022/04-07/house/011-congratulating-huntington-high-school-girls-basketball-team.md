# CONGRATULATING HUNTINGTON HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS BASKETBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Miller, Carol D. (M001205) [R-WV]

```
      CONGRATULATING HUNTINGTON HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS BASKETBALL TEAM

  (Mrs. MILLER of West Virginia asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute.)
  Mrs. MILLER of West Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate the Huntington High School girls basketball team. The team 
won the championship title, and they finished their season with a 24-1 
record. They are the only team to win in the newly developed AAAA 
division.
  While there is no ``I'' in team, they were led by Dionna Gray and 
coached by the legendary Lonnie Lucas, who has devoted 54 years of his 
life to coaching.
  Congratulations for this outstanding accomplishment.

                          ____________________
```
