# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, APRIL 11, 2022, AT 11:30 A.M.

**Measures:** S.Res. 593

```
        ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, APRIL 11, 2022, AT 11:30 A.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order and pursuant to S. 
Res. 593, the Senate stands adjourned until Monday, April 11, 2022, at 
11:30 a.m., and does so as a further mark of respect to the late 
Kaneaster Hodges, Jr., former Senator from Arkansas.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 5:10 p.m., adjourned until Monday, April 
11, 2022, at 11:30 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
