# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 865, Julia Ruth Gordon, of 
     Maryland, to be an Assistant Secretary of Housing and Urban 
     Development.
         Charles E. Schumer, Jacky Rosen, Cory A. Booker, 
           Elizabeth Warren, Benjamin L. Cardin, Patty Murray, 
           Brian Schatz, Robert P. Casey, Jr., Margaret Wood 
           Hassan, Alex Padilla, Amy Klobuchar, Tina Smith, Jeff 
           Merkley, Jack Reed, Angus S. King, Jr., Chris Van 
           Hollen, John W. Hickenlooper, Richard J. Durbin.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Finally, I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory 
quorum calls for the cloture motions filed today, May 9, be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
