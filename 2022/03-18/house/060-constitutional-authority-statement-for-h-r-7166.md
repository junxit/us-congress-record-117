# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7166

**Measures:** H.R. 7166

```
            By Mr. MOONEY:
        H.R. 7166.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 1 provides that ``All legislative Powers 
     herein granted shall be vested in a Congress of the United 
     States, which shall consist of a Senate and a House of 
     Representatives.''
       Article I, Section 5, Clause 2 provides that each house of 
     Congress ``may determine the Rules of its Proceedings.''
```
