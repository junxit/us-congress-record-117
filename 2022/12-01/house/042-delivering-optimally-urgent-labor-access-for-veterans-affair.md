  Mr. BISHOP of North Carolina changed his vote from ``yea'' to 
``nay.''
  So (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the rules were suspended and 
the bill, as amended, was passed.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title of the bill was amended so as to read: ``A bill to require 
the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to conduct a study on the feasibility 
and advisability of furnishing doula services to certain veterans, and 
for other purposes.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
