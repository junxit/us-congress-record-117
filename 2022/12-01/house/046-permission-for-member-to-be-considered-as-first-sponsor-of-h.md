# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 5296

**Speaking:** Bonamici, Suzanne (B001278) [D-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 5296

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 5296

  Ms. BONAMICI. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 5296, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Charlie Crist of Florida, for 
the purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Oregon?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
