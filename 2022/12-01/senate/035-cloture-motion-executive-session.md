# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The cloture motion having been 
presented under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the 
motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 1186, Mia Roberts Perez, of 
     Pennsylvania, to be United States District Judge for the 
     Eastern District of Pennsylvania.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Chris Van Hollen, 
           Richard Blumenthal, Tim Kaine, Michael F. Bennet, Gary 
           C. Peters, Benjamin L. Cardin, Margaret Wood Hassan, 
           Jack Reed, Alex Padilla, Robert P. Casey, Jr., 
           Christopher A. Coons, Debbie Stabenow, Tammy Baldwin, 
           Elizabeth Warren, Cory A. Booker, Mark R. Warner.

                          ____________________
```
