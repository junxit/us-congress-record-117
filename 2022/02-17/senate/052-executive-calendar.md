consider the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 114, 744 to 759, and 
that all nominations on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army,

motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with 
no intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order to 
any of the nominations; that the President be immediately notified of 
the Senate's action.
