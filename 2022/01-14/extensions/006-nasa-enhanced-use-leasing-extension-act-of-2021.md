# NASA ENHANCED USE LEASING EXTENSION ACT OF 2021

**Speaking:** Raskin, Jamie (R000606) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.R. 5746

```
            NASA ENHANCED USE LEASING EXTENSION ACT OF 2021

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                           HON. JAMIE RASKIN

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 13, 2022

  Mr. RASKIN. Madam Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
letters of support for the Senate Amendment to H.R. 5746.


                                                 Democracy 21,

                                 Washington, DC, January 13, 2022.
       Dear Representative: Democracy 21 strongly supports and 
     urges you to vote for the House Amendment to the Senate 
     Amendment to H.R. 5746, the Freedom to Vote: John R. Lewis 
     Act and for the rule to bring it to the House floor.
       The right of every eligible American to vote and have their 
     vote properly counted is the cornerstone of our democracy. 
     This fundamental right and our democracy are at grave risk 
     today.
       In 2021, Republican-controlled state legislatures across 
     the country enacted a wave of unprecedented voter suppression 
     and election sabotage laws in the name of ``election 
     integrity.'' More are expected to be enacted in 2022.
       The state laws stemmed from the Trump Big Lie that the 2020 
     presidential election was stolen from him, even though there 
     was not a shred of evidence to support his demagogic claim. 
     Trump's own top government security officials found that the 
     2020 election ``was the most secure in American history.''
       The Freedom to Vote: John R. Lewis Act would override the 
     state voter suppression laws for federal elections and combat 
     the state election sabotage laws that empower partisan 
     election officials to rig the results of federal races. The 
     Act would also prevent partisan gerrymandering and provide 
     essential campaign finance reforms.
       The Act would protect the right of every eligible American 
     to vote and preserve our democracy.
       Democracy 21 strongly urges you to vote for the Freedom to 
     Vote: John R. Lewis Act.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Fred Wertheimer,
     President.

     participate in safe, accessible, and transparent elections.
       Demos is a think tank that powers the movement for a just, 
     inclusive, multiracial democracy. Demos means ``the people'' 
     and is the root word of democracy. Our name reminds us that 
     America's power is derived from the diversity of its people, 
     and that we do not have democracy unless all of our people 
     can participate meaningfully in the political process.
       The Freedom to Vote: John R. Lewis Act is a major step in 
     the direction of ensuring all Americans can participate in 
     our democracy. Our organization fully endorses this bill and 
     urges you to support this legislation.
           Sincerely,
                                               Taifa Smith Butler,
```
