# U.S. Supreme Court (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                           U.S. Supreme Court

  Madam President, speaking of public safety, as the Supreme Court 
prepares to issue its final opinions of the term, far-left activists 
are openly calling for riots and violence in the streets.
  For months, many of the country's most prominent Democrats have 
indulged in reckless talk and irresponsible rhetoric, fanning the 
flames of fear and rage among their supporters. This poisonous climate 
has illegal mobs assembling outside Justices' private family homes and 
has prompted one unhinged person to travel across the country for the 
purpose of committing an assassination.

[[Page S3103]]

  The far left is promising that all of this will only be the prelude--
the prelude--to the main event if they don't like the rulings coming 
down the pike. One activist group is promising that our cities will be 
submerged in--listen to this--``a night of rage.''
  Well, yesterday, the Senate unanimously passed some supplemental 
funding for law enforcement to protect the rule of law and to keep the 
Court, its staff, and the Justices safe from all this. This 
noncontroversial funding passed here unanimously last night--zero 
objections. The House of Representatives needs to pass this urgent bill 
without delay.
```
