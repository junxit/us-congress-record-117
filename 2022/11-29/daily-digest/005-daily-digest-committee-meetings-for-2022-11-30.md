                   COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR WEDNESDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Strategic Forces, to

  Committee on the Judiciary: to hold hearings to examine the

United States District Judge for the Eastern District of New York, 
James Edward Simmons, Jr., to be United States District Judge for

the District of Columbia, to be an Assistant Attorney General, 
Department of Justice, 10:30 a.m., SD-226.
