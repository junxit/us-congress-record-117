# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 1230, H.Res. 1496

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 1 of House Resolution 
1230 and House Resolution 1496, the House stands adjourned until 9 a.m. 
tomorrow as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late A. 
Donald McEachin.
  Thereupon (at 7 o'clock and 50 minutes p.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until tomorrow, Wednesday, November 30, 
2022, at 9 a.m., as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late 
A. Donald McEachin.
```
