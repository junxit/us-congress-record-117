# SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 4524

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  The Speaker announced her signature to an enrolled bill of the Senate 
of the following title:

       S. 4524--An act to limit the judicial enforceability of 
     predispute nondisclosure and nondisparagement contract 
     clauses relating to disputes involving sexual assault and 
     sexual harassment.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Virginia?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
