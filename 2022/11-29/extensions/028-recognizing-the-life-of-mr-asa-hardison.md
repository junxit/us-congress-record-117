# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF MR. ASA HARDISON

**Speaking:** Guest, Michael (G000591) [R-MS]

```
                RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF MR. ASA HARDISON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL GUEST

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 29, 2022

  Mr. GUEST. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life of Mr. 
Asa Hardison. Mr. Hardison served his community, state, and Nation and 
set an example for those who work to make the world around them a 
better place.
  Mr. Hardison served the United States of America in the 101st 
Airborne Division during the Vietnam War. Following his service, he 
returned to the United States of America where he and his wife, Ruth 
Hardison, raised two daughters. As a civilian, Mr. Hardison served as 
Senior Vice President for Georgia Pacific and, in his free time, could 
often be found playing golf or tennis. Later in life, he enjoyed 
spending time with his grandchildren.
  It is my honor to recognize the life of Mr. Asa Hardison for his 
service to our Nation.
```
