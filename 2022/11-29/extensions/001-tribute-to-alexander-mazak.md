# TRIBUTE TO ALEXANDER MAZAK

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO ALEXANDER MAZAK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 29, 2022

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, last month I had the pleasure of 
attending Alexander Mazak's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. Alex started 
his scouting career 10 years ago as a Tiger Cub Scout and worked his 
way all the way up to the rank of Eagle Scout. For his Eagle Scout 
project, which he named the ``Remembrance Tree Project,'' he planted 40 
trees at Reed's Organic Farm and Animal Rescue in Egg Harbor Township, 
South Jersey. Alex also built benches for the farm. In his free time, 
Alex plays the violin, has a black belt in karate, participates in 
CrossFit, and is working towards getting Scuba Diving certified. Alex 
should be proud of his many achievements, and it was my pleasure to 
attend his Eagle Scout ceremony. God Bless Alex, and God Bless our 
America.

                          ____________________
```
