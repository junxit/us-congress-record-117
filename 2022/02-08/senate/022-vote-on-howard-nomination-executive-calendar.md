# Vote on Howard Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       Vote on Howard Nomination

  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Howard 
nomination?
  Mr. BROWN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from California (Mrs. 
Feinstein) and the Senator from New Mexico (Mr. Lujan) are necessarily 
absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Wyoming (Mr. Barrasso) and the Senator from South Dakota (Mr. 
Rounds).
  The result was announced--yeas 62, nays 34, as follows:
```
