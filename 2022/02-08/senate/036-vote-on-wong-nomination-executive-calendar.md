# Vote on Wong Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Baldwin, Tammy (B001230) [D-WI], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        Vote on Wong Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Wong nomination?
  Ms. BALDWIN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
```
