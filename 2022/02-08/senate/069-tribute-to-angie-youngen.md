# TRIBUTE TO ANGIE YOUNGEN

**Speaking:** Portman, Rob (P000449) [R-OH]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ANGIE YOUNGEN

  Mr. PORTMAN. Madam President, finally, I want to acknowledge someone 
who is in the Chamber tonight. I want to acknowledge her dedicated 
service. This is one of my senior staff members, who is moving to 
Richmond, VA, to join the new Virginia Governor's administration.
  Angie Youngen is an Ohio native and an Ashland University graduate--
who is very proud of that--and who was the scheduler for my 
predecessor, Senator George Voinovich. I have had the good fortune of 
having her on my team. Her loyalty, hard work, and judgment over the 
past 8 years is deeply appreciated.
  So, Angie Youngen, we will miss you here on Capitol Hill, but best of 
luck in your new adventure.
  I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
