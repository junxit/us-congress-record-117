# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, dressed in a robe of light, this is the day that You 
have made. We will rejoice and be glad in it.
  Lord, we place our trust in You. You have guided our life's journey 
through many dangers, toils, and snares. Your amazing grace continues 
to sustain us.
  Today, show our lawmakers the right path. Lead them by Your truth and 
save them with Your matchless love. You continue to be the God of our 
salvation, and without Your mercies, we would be consumed.
  And, Lord, we thank You for our incoming summer pages, and, as 
always, we ask You to bless Ukraine.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
