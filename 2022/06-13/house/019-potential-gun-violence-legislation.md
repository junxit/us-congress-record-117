# POTENTIAL GUN VIOLENCE LEGISLATION

**Speaking:** Good, Bob (G000595) [R-VA]

```
                   POTENTIAL GUN VIOLENCE LEGISLATION

  (Mr. GOOD of Virginia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. GOOD of Virginia. Madam Speaker, it is disappointing to see 
Republicans in the Senate ready to make a deal that will do nothing 
except slide us down the slippery slope toward gun registration and 
confiscation.
  The gun deal being contemplated in the Senate will do nothing to stop 
criminals intent on doing harm. This is just another surrender by 
uniparty RINOs. This will do nothing to satisfy the radical left that 
wants, again, registration and confiscation.
  Why not do something that would truly reduce violent crime in blue 
cities where there are hundreds of shootings every weekend? Why not 
support our police, prosecute criminals, end no-cash bail, harden our 
schools, and do something that will actually make a difference in 
reducing the deaths by violent crime in this country?

                          ____________________
```
