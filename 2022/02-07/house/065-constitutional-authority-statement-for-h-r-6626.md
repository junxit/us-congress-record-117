# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6626

**Measures:** H.R. 6626

```
            By Mr. ELLZEY:
        H.R. 6626.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United State Constitution, 
     which states ``[t]he Congress shall have power to lay and 
     colect taxes, duties, imposts, and excises, to pay the debts 
     and provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States; but all duties, imposts and excises shall be 
     uniorm throughout the United States''.
```
