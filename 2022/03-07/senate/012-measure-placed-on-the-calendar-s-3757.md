# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3757

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 3757

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3757

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3757) to prohibit the importation of crude oil, 
     petroleum, petroleum products, liquefied natural gas, and 
     coal from the Russian Federation.

  Mr. SCHUMER. In order to place the bill on the calendar, under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceeding.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.
  Mr. SCHUMER. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
