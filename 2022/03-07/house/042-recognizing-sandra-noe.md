# RECOGNIZING SANDRA NOE

**Speaking:** Mrvan, Frank J. (M001214) [D-IN]

```
                         RECOGNIZING SANDRA NOE

  (Mr. MRVAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. MRVAN. Madam Speaker, it is with great respect and admiration 
that I rise to celebrate Women's History Month and recognize Sandra 
Noe, Executive Director of Meals on Wheels in northwest Indiana.
  Sandra has worked with Meals on Wheels for 36 years to combat senior 
isolation and hunger.
  As the demand for services increased over the past year during the 
COVID-19 pandemic, Meals on Wheels rose to the challenge and delivered 
over 1,700 meals daily, provided over 750,000 wellness checks, and 
celebrated its nine-millionth delivery.
  Madam Speaker, as we recognize Women's History Month, please join me 
in thanking Sandra and everyone at Meals on Wheels for the 
extraordinary services they provide to northwest Indiana.
  Let us recommit to continuing to support their invaluable work and 
recognize this month and every day the invaluable ways that women 
continue to provide healing and promote hope, not just in caregiving, 
but in all aspects of our society.

                          ____________________
```
