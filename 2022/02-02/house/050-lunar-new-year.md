# LUNAR NEW YEAR

**Speaking:** Kim, Young (K000397) [R-CA]

```
                             LUNAR NEW YEAR

  (Mrs. KIM of California asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. KIM of California. Madam Speaker, I rise today to join my 
community in celebrating Lunar New Year.
  Madam Speaker, 2022 is the year of the Tiger, which represents, 
strength, passion, bravery, and courage. These are qualities we have 
seen in our communities every day.
  While this past year brought new challenges, I know through the 
strength and drive of the Tiger, we will face these challenges head-on. 
And as someone who was born in the year of the Tiger and as a proud 
Tiger mom, my hope is to embody this courage and persistence every day 
as I fight for communities to receive the proper services and support 
that we need to come back stronger than ever.
  In Congress, I will continue to promote policies to help Americans 
succeed and achieve their dream.
  Happy New Year.

                          ____________________
```
