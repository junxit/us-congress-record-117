# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF MIKE DOSS

**Speaking:** Kelly, Trent (K000388) [R-MS]

```
                   RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF MIKE DOSS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TRENT KELLY

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 2, 2022

  Mr. KELLY of Mississippi. Madam Speaker, I rise today to celebrate 
the life and service of Mr. Mike Doss who recently passed away after 
serving the great state of Mississippi as a member of the Pontotoc 
Police Department. Since 1999 Mr. Doss has been a great servant to the 
Police Force and community spending most of his time in the narcotics 
division of the department.
  Mr. Doss hails from Houston, Mississippi. He is predeceased by; 
Arthuer Lee his father, and survived by Eva Criddle Doss his mother, 
Elizabeth Ann Walters Doss his wife, Jonathan Doss his son, and Leann 
Nabors his sister. He also had a grandson Conner Doss.
  I am so thankful for Mr. Doss' years of service to the great state of 
Mississippi. I send my deepest condolences to the extended Doss family.

                          ____________________
```
