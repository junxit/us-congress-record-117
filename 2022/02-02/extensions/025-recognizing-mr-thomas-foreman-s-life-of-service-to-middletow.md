# RECOGNIZING MR. THOMAS FOREMAN'S LIFE OF SERVICE TO MIDDLETOWN BOROUGH

**Speaking:** Perry, Scott (P000605) [R-PA]

```
 RECOGNIZING MR. THOMAS FOREMAN'S LIFE OF SERVICE TO MIDDLETOWN BOROUGH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 2, 2022

  Mr. PERRY. Madam Speaker, I am honored to recognize Mr. Thomas 
Foreman for his life of service to Middletown Borough. Mr. Foreman has 
dedicated 25 years of service as the director of the Emergency 
Management Agency (EMA), 55 years to the Middletown Volunteer Fire 
Department, and over 30 years with the Pennsylvania Lottery. Mr. 
Foreman has become a pillar of the community through more than five 
decades of service.
  Mr. Foreman's work with the Liberty Fire Company in Middletown began 
in November of 1966, where he's served as secretary, vice president, 
and president. While executing countless hours as a volunteer 
firefighter, he also maintained his involvement with the Emergency 
Management Agency, helping prepare the Borough for its next emergency. 
In the performance of his duties, he assists residents during natural 
disasters--to include floods, snow, and TMI events, and myriad other 
situations that require his oversight and assistance. He works 
regularly in the EMA office, attends monthly preparedness and borough 
meetings, and his dedication to the residents of Middletown is tireless 
and unmatched.
  Mr. Foreman is a lifelong resident of the Borough of Middletown. Upon 
his retirement from the Pennsylvania Lottery in 2004, he maintained his 
tireless commitment to the residents and Middletown community at large. 
Tom has two beautiful children--Michael, a fireman, and Kelly--and four 
grandchildren.
  I am privileged to honor Mr. Thomas Foreman and recognize his 
significant contributions to our community throughout more than five 
decades of selfless and honorable service to Middletown Borough, 
Dauphin County, and the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. On behalf of the 
citizens of the 10th Congressional District of Pennsylvania, I wish him 
Godspeed as he embarks upon his next adventure.

                          ____________________
```
