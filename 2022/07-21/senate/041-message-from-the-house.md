# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 8351, H.R. 8373

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:48 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:


[[Page S3609]]


  

       H.R. 8373. An act to protect a person's ability to access 
     contraceptives and to engage in contraception, and to protect 
     a health care provider's ability to provide contraceptives, 
     contraception, and information related to contraception.


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  The message further announced that the Speaker pro tempore (Mr. 
RASKIN) has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 8351. An act to amend the Harmonized Tariff Schedule 
     of the United States to suspend temporarily rates of duty on 
     imports of certain infant formula products, and for other 
     purposes.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the Acting President pro 
tempore (Ms. Cortez Masto).

                          ____________________
```
