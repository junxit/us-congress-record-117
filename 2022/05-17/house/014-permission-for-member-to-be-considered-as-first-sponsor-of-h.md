# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2271

**Speaking:** Cole, Tom (C001053) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 2271

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2271

  Mr. COLE. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 2271, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Young of Alaska, for the purpose of adding 
cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Oklahoma?
  There was no objection.
```
