# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-140. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Board of 
     Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco, CA, 
     relative to Resolution No. 344-22, urging public service 
     employees to apply for student loan forgiveness through the 
     Public Service Loan Forgiveness program and further urging 
     the Federal Government to cancel all student debt; to the 
     Committee on Education and Labor.
       PT-141. Also, a petition of the Board of Supervisors of the 
     City and County of San Francisco, CA, relative to Resolution 
     No. 319-22, urging the Honorable Gavin Newsom, Governor of 
     California, to grant Salesh Prasad, a full pardon to allow 
     him to remain in the United States with his family and to 
     continue contributing to his community; to the Committee on 
     the Judiciary.
```
