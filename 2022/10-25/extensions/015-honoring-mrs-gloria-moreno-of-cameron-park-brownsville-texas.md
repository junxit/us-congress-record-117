# HONORING MRS. GLORIA MORENO OF CAMERON PARK, BROWNSVILLE, TEXAS

**Speaking:** Flores, Mayra (F000473) [R-TX]

```
    HONORING MRS. GLORIA MORENO OF CAMERON PARK, BROWNSVILLE, TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MAYRA FLORES

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 25, 2022

  Mrs. FLORES. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Mrs. Gloria Moreno 
of Cameron Park, Brownsville, Texas on her amazing work as the 
community leader of Cameron Park.
  Mrs. Moreno currently serves as the community leader of Cameron Park, 
Brownsville. For the past 45 years, Mrs. Moreno has served her colonia 
by lobbying her local and state officials for the needs of her 
community. Her work as a community leader has brought many needed 
utilities to her neighborhood such as the paving of the streets and the 
installment of running water and sewer lines. Mrs. Moreno is the prime 
example of a community leader who is focused on helping her community 
grow.
  Mrs. Moreno has served Cameron Park faithfully by being a voice for 
her fellow residents and advocating for the needs of Cameron Park and 
beyond. Her commitment to her home and her community deserves honor, 
praise, and gratitude. It is only fitting that Mrs. Moreno celebrates 
amongst her friends and family as her impact will be felt by 
generations to come.
  Madam Speaker, it is my honor to recognize Mrs. Moreno for her 
accomplishments.

                          ____________________
```
