# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 6376, H.R. 6604

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 6604, the Veterans Eligible to 
Transfer School (VETS) Credit Act, as amended, would have no 
significant effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects 
of such bill are estimated as zero.
                                  ____

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, the attached 
estimate of the costs of H.R. 6376, the Student Veteran Work Study 
Modernization Act, as amended, for printing in the Congressional 
Record.

                          ____________________
```
