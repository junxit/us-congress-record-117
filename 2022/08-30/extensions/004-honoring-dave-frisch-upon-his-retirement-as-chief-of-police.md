# HONORING DAVE FRISCH UPON HIS RETIREMENT AS CHIEF OF POLICE FOR LAPEER MICHIGAN

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
HONORING DAVE FRISCH UPON HIS RETIREMENT AS CHIEF OF POLICE FOR LAPEER 
                                MICHIGAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 30, 2022

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate 
Lapeer Chief of Police Dave Frisch, who will be retiring on September 
23, 2022, after more than 30 years of serving our community in law 
enforcement.
  Chief Frisch has lived the mantra of ``to protect and to serve''. His 
leadership has enabled Lapeer to be a peaceful community for the 
families and individuals who make it their home. It may not have always 
been easy, but Chief Frisch has served with honor, dignity, and grace.
  The community of Lapeer and the State of Michigan thank Chief Frisch 
for his decades of service. I wish him and his wife, Arlene, the best 
as they embark upon their next chapter together. He has left big shoes 
to fill, but I am

[[Page E886]]

confident the leadership he has displayed in his decades of service 
will be followed in the Lapeer community for years to come.

                          ____________________
```
