# HONORING JOEL SHERBURNE'S 65 YEARS OF SERVICE

**Speaking:** Pappas, Chris (P000614) [D-NH]

```
             HONORING JOEL SHERBURNE'S 65 YEARS OF SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHRIS PAPPAS

                            of new hampshire

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 30, 2022

  Mr. PAPPAS. Madam Speaker, I rise today in recognition of Joel 
Sherburne and his 65 years of service to Calef's Famous Country Store. 
Since age 17, Joel has served the Town of Barrington as Calef's 
``Cheese Man,'' sharing advice on how to care for your cheese and 
secret recipes using Calef's famous ``Snappy Cheddar.'' He has devoted 
himself to the safety, community and connectedness of his neighbors. 
Joel has been instrumental over the last six decades in bringing the 
people of Barrington together.
  Joel's dedication to New Hampshire spans beyond his cheese expertise. 
He has taken on

[[Page E889]]

an active role in serving his community, designing the town sign and 
serving as Fair Ambassador to the Rochester Fair. Joel founded the 
Barrington Ambulance Service and taught EMT classes, which has saved 
countless lives and ensured that his neighbors have help should they 
need it. Joel even helped restore the nearby Rochester Opera House, 
inviting music and art into the lives of his neighbors. Joel's 
commitment to Granite Staters was commemorated in 2017 with a United 
States Postal Service postmark.
  On behalf of my constituents in New Hampshire's First Congressional 
District, I congratulate Joel for 65 years of service and thank him for 
his outstanding contributions to the community of Barrington.
```
