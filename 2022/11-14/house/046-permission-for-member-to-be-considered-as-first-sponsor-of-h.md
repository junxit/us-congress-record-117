# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 7229

**Speaking:** Hern, Kevin (H001082) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 7229

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 7229

  Mr. HERN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 7229, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Walorski of Indiana, for the purpose of 
adding cosponsors and requesting re-printings pursuant to clause 7 of 
rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Oklahoma?
  There was no objection.
```
