# CELEBRATING SIGMA GAMMA RHO SORORITY'S CENTENNIAL

**Speaking:** Kelly, Robin L. (K000385) [D-IL]

```
           CELEBRATING SIGMA GAMMA RHO SORORITY'S CENTENNIAL

  (Ms. KELLY of Illinois asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Ms. KELLY of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I want to take a moment to salute 
my sorority, Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Incorporated, for its centennial 
celebration on November 12, 2022. We were founded in Indianapolis, 
Indiana, at Butler University November 12, 1922, by seven illustrious 
schoolteachers. Now we are led by the wonderful Grand Basileus Rasheeda 
Liberty, and we will embark on our next 100 with greater service, 
greater progress.

                          ____________________
```
