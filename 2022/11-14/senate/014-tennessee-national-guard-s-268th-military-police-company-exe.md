# Tennessee National Guard's 268th Military Police Company (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

```
        Tennessee National Guard's 268th Military Police Company

  Mrs. BLACKBURN. Madam President, I want to begin by welcoming the 
Tennessee National Guard's 268th Military Police Company home after a 
305-day deployment to Africa.
  We are so incredibly grateful for their service and for the service 
and sacrifice that their families make during these times of 
deployment, and I know I speak for every Tennessean when I say to them: 
Welcome home. Job well done.
```
