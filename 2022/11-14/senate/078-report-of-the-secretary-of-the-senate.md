# REPORT OF THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

```
                 REPORT OF THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

                                                November 14, 2022.
     Hon. Kamala Harris,
     President of the United States Senate,
     Washington, DC.
       Madam: I have the honor to submit a full and complete 
     statement of the receipts and expenditures of the Senate,
```
