# CONGRATULATING THE BULLDOGS ON THEIR STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
        CONGRATULATING THE BULLDOGS ON THEIR STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, November 14, 2022

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Ft. Zumwalt South Bulldogs on their 
victory in the Class 3 Girls Soccer State Championship.
  The Bulldogs took home their second consecutive Class 3 title. The 
Bulldogs should be commended for all their hard work throughout the 
past year and for bringing home the state championship to their school 
and community.
  Please join me in recognizing the Bulldogs for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
