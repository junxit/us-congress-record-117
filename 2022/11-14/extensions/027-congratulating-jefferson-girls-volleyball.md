# CONGRATULATING JEFFERSON GIRLS VOLLEYBALL

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
               CONGRATULATING JEFFERSON GIRLS VOLLEYBALL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, November 14, 2022

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Jefferson Girls Volleyball team on their 
victory in the Class 2 Girls Volleyball State Championship.
  Jefferson Girls Volleyball made history by claiming the schools first 
state title in any sport. Jefferson Girls Volleyball should be 
commended for all their hard work throughout the past year and for 
bringing home the state championship to their school and community.
  Please join me in recognizing Jefferson Girls Volleyball for a job 
well done.

                          ____________________
```
