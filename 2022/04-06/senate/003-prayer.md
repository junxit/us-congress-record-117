# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, listen to our cries for help. Guide our lawmakers, 
empowering them to act with integrity. Lord, give them wisdom to test 
their motives as they become more aware of Your mercy. Keep them from 
drowning in shallow water. Inspire them to resolve to cultivate an 
unwavering trust in the unfolding of Your prevailing providence.
  Lord, we thank You that Your mercies are new each day. Great is Your 
faithfulness.
  And, Lord, we continue to pray for Ukraine.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
