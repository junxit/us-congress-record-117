            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Ms. STABENOW (for herself and Mr. Thune):
       S. Res. 577. A resolution designating April 2022 as

     Judiciary.

     of Texas at Austin; considered and agreed to.

       S. Res. 581. A resolution supporting the designation of the 
     week of April 24 through April 30, 2022, as ``National Crime 
     Victims' Rights Week''; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. KING (for himself, Mr. Daines, Mr. Reed, Mr.

       S. Res. 582. A resolution designating the week of April 16
