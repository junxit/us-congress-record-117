# CASSIE TANNER

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                             CASSIE TANNER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 26, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Cassie Tanner for her service to the West Metro Fire Protection 
District.
  Cassie has served on the Board of Directors of the West Metro Fire 
Protection District for eight years. During her tenure, she has 
provided strong leadership through a number of significant changes and 
is currently serving as Board President.
  During her tenure on the Board, Cassie oversaw the budget and policy 
decisions for the West Metro Fire District. In this role, she supported 
local firefighters who dedicate their lives to serving and protecting 
people, property and the and community at-large. Her background in 
banking, finance, employee relations as well as her common sense, 
problem-solving skills helped make her a valuable member of the Board.
  I have known Cassie for many years and know her to be a hardworking, 
dedicated member of our community. I extend my deepest thanks and 
appreciation to Cassie Tanner for her service to the West Metro Fire 
District and to our local community. I know she will continue on to do 
great things in our community and I wish her well in all of her future 
endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
