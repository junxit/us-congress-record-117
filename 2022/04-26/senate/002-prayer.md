# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, who has promised to hear our prayers, incline Your ear 
to us, providing us with those things we have faithfully requested 
according to Your will.
  Lord, we have asked for Your presence in Ukraine. We have desired for 
You to continue to be the refuge and strength for those experiencing 
the horrors of war, providing for their needs with Your mighty power.
  We have requested that You inspire and empower our lawmakers to walk 
by faith and not by sight.
  Lord, You are a faithful God, and we place our trust in You. Save us 
from the traps set by the forces of evil.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
