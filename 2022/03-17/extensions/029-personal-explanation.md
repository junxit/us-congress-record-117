# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Reed, Tom (R000585) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 5001

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. TOM REED

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 17, 2022

  Mr. REED. Madam Speaker, on Tuesday, March 15, 2022, I was unable to 
vote on Roll Call vote No. 72: Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass 
H.R. 5001, to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to continue to 
implement endangered fish recovery programs for the Upper Colorado and 
San Juan River Basins, and for the other purposes. Had I been present, 
I would have voted ``yes.''
```
