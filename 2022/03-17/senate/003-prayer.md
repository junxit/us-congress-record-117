# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, our shelter from the storms, protect the Ukrainian 
people as they trust You for safety.
  Lord, all the good we will ever have comes from You. You have been 
faithful to Your people for millennia. Do not disappoint us now in this 
season of desperation. We see no other God but You, as this conflict 
continues to maim, kill, and destroy.
  Lord, provide our lawmakers with the wisdom to cooperate with Your 
divine omnipotence in accomplishing Your purposes on Earth. May 
generations not yet born be told that You saved your people.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
