```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:18 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

     to convey to the City of Eunice, Louisiana, certain Federal 
     land in Louisiana, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4380. An act to designate the El Paso Community 
     Healing Garden National Memorial, and for other purposes.

     to establish, within the National Park Service, the Japanese 
     American World War II History Network, and for other 
     purposes.

Senate:
```
