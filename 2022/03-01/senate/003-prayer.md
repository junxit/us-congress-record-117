# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Merciful God, guide our lawmakers and empower them to meet today's 
challenges.
  Lord, our world seems under attack, but we continue to place our 
trust in You. When we feel fear, remind us that all power belongs to 
You. We thank You that mere human beings cannot prevail against Your 
might and majesty.
  Lord, keep a record of the tears of those who cry out to You from 
around the world. Rescue them from their anguish and keep them from 
defeat.
  We pray in Your matchless Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
