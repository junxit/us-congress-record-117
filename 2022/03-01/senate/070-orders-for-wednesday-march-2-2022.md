# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 2, 2022

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 69, H.R. 3076

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 2, 2022

  Mr. SCHUMER. Now, Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate recess until 8:30 p.m. today and proceed as a body to the Hall 
of the House of Representatives for the joint session of Congress 
provided under the provisions of H. Con. Res. 69; and that upon 
dissolution of the joint session, the Senate adjourn until 11 a.m. on 
Wednesday, March 2, 2022; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and morning business be closed; that upon conclusion of 
morning business, the Senate resume consideration of Calendar No. 273, 
H.R. 3076, the Postal Service Reform Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
