# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Pocan, Mark (P000607) [D-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 55, S. 321, H.R. 2116

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MARK POCAN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 1, 2022

  Mr. POCAN. Madam Speaker, I was not present for yesterday's roll call 
votes in the House, and I wish to state how I would have voted on all 
three bills had I been present. As a cosponsor of all three measures, I 
would have proudly voted in the following manner: YEA on Roll Call No. 
48: H.R. 2116, the ``Creating a Respectful and Open World for Natural 
Hair Act''; YEA on Roll Call No. 47: H.R. 55, the ``Emmett Till 
Antilynching Act''; and YEA on Roll Call No. 46: S. 321, the ``Six 
Triple Eight Congressional Gold Medal Act''.

                          ____________________
```
