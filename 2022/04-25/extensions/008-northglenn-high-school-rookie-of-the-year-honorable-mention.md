# NORTHGLENN HIGH SCHOOL ROOKIE OF THE YEAR--HONORABLE MENTION AWARD RECIPIENTS

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
  NORTHGLENN HIGH SCHOOL ROOKIE OF THE YEAR--HONORABLE MENTION AWARD 
                               RECIPIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 25, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Aiden Boushie, Victor Ibarra, Makayla Clemons and Alex Guzman for 
earning the Rookie of the Year--Honorable Mention Award in the 2022 
Congressional App Challenge.
  As first-time participants in the challenge, this team from 
Northglenn High School built an app entitled ``School Club Finder'' to 
help students discover the extracurricular activities at their school.
  The students spent countless hours and used numerous tools to learn 
about coding and app development while developing new skills and 
further advancing their STEM education. Their perseverance and 
dedication to this challenge exemplifies the character and 
determination the faculty instills in the students at the school and 
the students' commitment to being lifelong learners.
  I extend my deepest congratulations to Aiden Boushie, Victor Ibarra, 
Makayla Clemons and Alex Guzman for their hard work on this project. I 
wish each student the best in all their future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
