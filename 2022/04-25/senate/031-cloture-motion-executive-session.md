# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 670, Lisa M. Gomez, of New Jersey, 
     to be an Assistant Secretary of Labor.
         Charles E. Schumer, Ron Wyden, Sheldon Whitehouse, 
           Sherrod Brown, Richard J. Durbin, Maria Cantwell, 
           Debbie Stabenow, Jacky Rosen, Raphael G. Warnock, Chris 
           Van Hollen, Christopher A. Coons, Richard Blumenthal, 
           Robert Menendez, Jeff Merkley, Tina Smith, Martin 
           Heinrich, Alex Padilla.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, finally, I ask unanimous consent that

[[Page S2121]]

the mandatory quorum calls for the cloture motions filed today, April 
25, be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
