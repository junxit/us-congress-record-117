# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, upon the recommendation of the majority leader, pursuant to 
Public Law 105-292, as amended by Public Law 106-55, Public Law 107-
228, and Public Law 112-75, re-appoints the following individual to the 
United States Commission on International Religious Freedom: Reverend 
Frederick A. Davie of New York.
  The Chair announces, on behalf of the majority leader, pursuant to 
the provisions of Public Law 68-541, as amended by the appropriate 
provisions of Public Law 102-246, and in consultation with the 
Republican leader, the appointment of the following individuals to 
serve as members of the Library of Congress Trust Fund Board for a 5-
year term: J. Richard Fredericks of California; and Kevin Young of New 
York.
```
