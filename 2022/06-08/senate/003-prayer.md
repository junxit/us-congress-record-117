# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Eternal God, robed with honor and majesty, we praise You for the 
marvelous things You have done throughout our Nation's history. Lord, 
thoughts about Your mighty deeds continue to fill us with joy. You are 
Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending.
  Lord, in these tumultuous times, we continue to trust You to compel 
evil forces to retreat. Surround this Nation with the shield of Your 
divine favor as You execute judgment from Your throne. Lord, continue 
to remind our lawmakers of their accountability to You.
  And Lord, bless Ukraine.
  We pray in the Name of our Savior. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
