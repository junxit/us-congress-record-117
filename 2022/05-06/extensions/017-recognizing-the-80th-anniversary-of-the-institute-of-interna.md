# RECOGNIZING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE INSTITUTE OF INTERNAL AUDITORS

**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE INSTITUTE OF INTERNAL AUDITORS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 6, 2022

  Mrs. CAROLYN B. MALONEY of New York. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
celebrate Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA) on the occasion of its 
80th anniversary and to recognize Internal Audit Awareness Month.
  The IIA was founded in 1941 by a small group of auditors based in New 
York City who understood the importance of the profession as well as 
its critical role in governance, risk, and control. From these humble 
beginnings the IIA has grown tremendously with branches in 170 
countries and over 210,000 members. By relentlessly advocating for its 
members, providing valuable training to students, hosting conferences 
and so much more the IIA works to uplift this critically important 
profession.
  Madam Speaker, the IIA is a real New York success story. I ask that 
my colleagues join me in celebrating the Institute for all that they do 
to promote the critical field of auditing and wish them continued 
success.
```
