# LET TEACHERS TEACH

**Speaking:** Green, Al (G000553) [D-TX]

```
                           LET TEACHERS TEACH

  (Mr. GREEN of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GREEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, and still I rise with a message for 
those it may concern.
  At Robb Elementary School, more than 15 good guys with guns, 
ballistic shields, and body armor confronted a bad guy with a gun and 
didn't do what many expect teachers to do with a pistol.
  Expecting teachers to take out bad guys with guns is unreasonable, 
outrageous, and dumb. Let teachers teach.

                          ____________________
```
