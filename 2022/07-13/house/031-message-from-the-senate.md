# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 42

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has agreed to a concurrent resolution of the following 
title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. Con. Res. 42. Concurrent Resolution authorizing the use 
     of the rotunda of the Capitol on Thursday, July 14, 2022, for 
     the lying in honor of the remains of Hershel Woodrow 
     ``Woody'' Williams, the last surviving Medal of Honor 
     recipient for acts performed during World War II.

                          ____________________
```
