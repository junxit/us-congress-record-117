# Order Of Procedure (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                           Order Of Procedure

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, for the information of the Senate, there 
will be two rollcall votes starting at 2:30 p.m. today. They will be on 
the confirmation of the Barr nomination to be Vice Chair of the Federal 
Reserve and the motion to invoke cloture on the Herrnstadt nomination 
to be a member of the Export-Import Bank.
  Senators can expect two additional votes in the 5:30 p.m. range 
today. Those votes will be on confirmation of the Herrnstadt nomination 
and cloture on the Heinzelman nomination to be general counsel of the 
CIA.
```
