# Vote on Herrnstadt Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, all postcloture time 
has expired.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Herrnstadt 
nomination?
```
