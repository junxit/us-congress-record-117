# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Mighty God, help us. Protect us and preserve lives. Be a shield for 
the nations of our world. Do not abandon those who put their trust in 
You. Lord, provide our Senators with a durable faith that will cling to 
You even during a raging tempest. Thank You for being the God of our 
salvation who refuses to permit evil to triumph. Be merciful to us, 
dear God, and grant us Your peace, for we find joy when we take refuge 
in You.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
