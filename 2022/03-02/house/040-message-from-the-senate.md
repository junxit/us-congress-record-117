# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 3600

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 3600. An act to improve the cybersecurity of the Federal 
     Government, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 117-81, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Ranking Member of the Senate Committee on 
Foreign Relations, appoints the following individual to serve as a 
member of the Afghanistan War Commission:

       Daniel P. Fata of Massachusetts.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 106-398, as 
amended by Public Law 108-7, the Chair, on behalf of the Majority 
Leader, and in consultation with the Chairmen of the Senate Committee 
on Armed Services and the Senate Committee on Finance, announces the 
appointment of the following individuals to serve as members of the 
United States-China Economic and Security Review Commission:

       Carte P. Goodwin, of West Virginia for a term beginning 
     January 1, 2022 and expiring December 31, 2023 
     (reappointment).
       James Mann of New York for a term beginning January 1, 2022 
     and expiring December 31, 2023.

                          ____________________
```
