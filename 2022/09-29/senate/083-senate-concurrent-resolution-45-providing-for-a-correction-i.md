# SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 45--PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 6833

**Measures:** S. 45, S.Con.Res. 45, H.R. 6833

```
  SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 45--PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE 
                        ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 6833

  Mr. LEAHY submitted the following concurrent resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                            S. Con. Res. 45

       Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives 
     concurring), That, in the enrollment of H.R. 6833, the Clerk 
     of the House of Representatives shall amend the title so as 
     to read: ``Making continuing appropriations for fiscal year 
     2023, and for other purposes.''.

                          ____________________
```
