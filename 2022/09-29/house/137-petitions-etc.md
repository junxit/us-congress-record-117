# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-149. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Board of 
     Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco, relative 
     to Resolution No. 363-22, urging the United States Department 
     of Health and Human Services Secretary Xavier Becerra to 
     suspend a requirement by the Centers for Medicare and 
     Medicaid Services on relocating and transferring vulnerable 
     patents at Laguna Honda Hospital and Rehabilitation Center; 
     and to extend coverage of Medicare and Medicaid payments 
     until the determination on recertification; which was 
     referred jointly to the Committees on Energy and Commerce and 
     Ways and Means.
```
