# HONORING WILLIAM N. ECKERSON

**Speaking:** Good, Bob (G000595) [R-VA]

```
                      HONORING WILLIAM N. ECKERSON

  (Mr. GOOD of Virginia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. GOOD of Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of my 
constituent, Mr. William Eckerson of Crozet, Virginia, who celebrated 
his 100th birthday on August 20, 2022.
  Mr. Eckerson currently resides in Virginia's Fifth District but, as a 
young man, he faithfully served the United States marines during World 
War II, even serving in Iwo Jima.
  Following his time in the Marines, he worked in the private sector 
but maintained his commitment to the Marines by participating in the 
Marine Band, various parades, and many World War II marine reunions.
  Mr. Eckerson has further demonstrated his commitment to the Marines 
and this country by spending years of his life cleaning up veteran 
cemeteries.
  When he is not serving others, Mr. Eckerson is also an avid golfer 
and cheers for his beloved Boston Red Sox and New England Patriots.
  It is a great privilege to honor my constituent following his 100th 
birthday and thank him for his many years of service to the United 
States of America and his local community.
  Mr. Eckerson, I certainly hope that you and your loved ones enjoyed a 
great day and celebration. May God bless you, and happy 100th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
