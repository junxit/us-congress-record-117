# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9073

**Measures:** H.R. 9073

```
            By Mrs. RODGERS of Washington:
        H.R. 9073.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. I, Sect. 8, Clause I: to provide for the common 
     defense.
       Art. I, Sect. 8, Clause 12: to raise and support Armies.
       Art. Sect. 8, Clause 13: to provide and maintain a Navy.
       Art I., Sect. 8, Clause 14: to make Rules for the 
     Government and Regulation of the land and naval Forces.
```
