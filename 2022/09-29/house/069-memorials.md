# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 11

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       ML-235. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of 
     the State of California, relative to Senate Joint Resolution 
     No. 11, supporting expanding Social Security and calls on our 
     state's Representatives in Congress to bring to a vote, 
     during this Congress, and vote in favor of the Social 
     Security 2100 Act: A Sacred Trust; which was referred jointly 
     to the Committees on Ways and Means, Education and Labor, and 
     Energy and Commerce.
```
