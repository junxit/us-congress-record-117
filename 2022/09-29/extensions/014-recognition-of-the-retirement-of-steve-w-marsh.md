# RECOGNITION OF THE RETIREMENT OF STEVE W. MARSH

**Speaking:** Lofgren, Zoe (L000397) [D-CA]

```
            RECOGNITION OF THE RETIREMENT OF STEVE W. MARSH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 29, 2022

  Ms. LOFGREN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to offer congratulations to 
Steve W. Marsh on the occasion of his Congressional retirement from the 
U.S. House of Representatives, CAO Office of Finance--Accounting 
Department as of Thursday, May 5, 2022, after over 24 years of service. 
Steve started as a Staff Accountant with the Chief Administrative 
Office on March 16, 1998, tasked with the Property, Plant, and 
Equipment (PP&E) reconciliation process where he quickly became the 
subject matter expert. Steve was promoted on October 1, 2000, to Senior 
Accountant, and July 1, 2012, to Accounting Manager. Steve played a 
significant role in the implementation of the House's financial system 
of record, PeopleSoft, and was an integral part of the CAO Office of 
Finance team that has received a clean audit opinion for twenty-three 
consecutive years for the U.S. House of Representatives. Best wishes to 
Steve W. Marsh on a long, happy, fulfilling, and well-deserved 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
