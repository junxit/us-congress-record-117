# HONORING RORY LOGAN

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                          HONORING RORY LOGAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 29, 2022

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Rory 
Logan, a Criminal Detective with the Benicia Police Department. I have 
named Ms. Logan as a Public Safety Hero of the Year for Solano County 
in California's 5th District as a result of her extraordinary 
selflessness, dedication, and service to others.
  Ms. Logan was born in Los Angeles, California and graduated from 
Eagle Rock High School. She served for over 12 years in both the United 
States Marine Corps and United States Navy Reserves where she qualified 
as a field radio operator, parachutist and Stinger/Avenger gunner. Ms. 
Logan's overseas deployments include Korea, Thailand, Japan, Panama, 
and Kuwait. She is a recipient of two Navy Achievement Medals, a Navy 
Unit Commendation, and a Global War on Terrorism

[[Page E997]]

Expeditionary Medal, among other awards and decorations.
  In 2020, following nearly two decades of experience with other law 
enforcement agencies, Ms. Logan joined the Benicia Police Department. 
As a Criminal Detective, Ms. Logan played a key role in the 
investigation of illegal gun sales, burglaries, and child predator 
cases. In each of these instances, Ms. Logan displayed remarkable skill 
and determination, often resulting in the arrest of wanted suspects and 
criminals. In March 2022, Bahadur Singh, the owner and clerk of Rose 
Market, was murdered during a robbery. Ms. Logan worked tirelessly to 
investigate the incident, cooperating with multiple other agencies, and 
led to the swift arrest of the suspect before he was able to cause 
further harm.
  Outside of her service with the Benicia Police Department, Ms. Logan 
is a licensed vocational nurse and proud mother of two sons, Anthony 
and Scott.
  Madam Speaker, I thank Ms. Logan for her persistence and 
investigative skill. Therefore, it is fitting and proper that we honor 
her here today.

                          ____________________
```
