# COMMUNICATION FROM CHIEF OF STAFF, THE HONORABLE JARED GOLDEN, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
 COMMUNICATION FROM CHIEF OF STAFF, THE HONORABLE JARED GOLDEN, MEMBER 
                              OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Aisha Woodward, Chief of Staff, the Honorable Jared 
Golden, Member of Congress:

                                                     Jared Golden,


                                Congress of the United States,

                           2nd District of Maine, August 15, 2022.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I, Aisha Woodward, have been served 
     with a subpoena to testify as a witness in a criminal trial 
     in the United States District Court for the District of 
     Columbia.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                   Aisha Woodward,
     Chief of Staff.

                          ____________________
```
