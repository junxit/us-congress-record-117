# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 3921

**Speaking:** Murphy, Gregory F. (M001210) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 3921

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 3921

  Mr. MURPHY of North Carolina. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that I may hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 
3921, a bill originally introduced by Representative Walorski of 
Indiana, for the purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting 
reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
