# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty and everlasting God, the source of power and might, 
strengthen us. Lord, strengthen our faith so that we will trust You in 
our seasons of joy and sadness. Strengthen our will so that we will 
choose the difficult right and resist the easy wrong. Strengthen our 
decisions so that we will not vacillate when commitment is needed. 
Strengthen our affection so that we will learn to love You as You have 
first loved us. Lord, strengthen our Senators to believe that Your 
purposes will ultimately prevail.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
