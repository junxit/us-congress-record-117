# Unanimous Consent Agreement--S.J. Res. 63 (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 63

```
               Unanimous Consent Agreement--S.J. Res. 63

  Mr. CARDIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to execute the 
order with respect to Calendar No. 523, S.J. Res. 63, at 3:30 p.m. 
today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. CARDIN. Mr. President, for the information of the Senate, there 
will be a rollcall vote at approximately 4 p.m. today on passage of 
S.J. Res. 63, related to the national emergency declared by the 
President on March 13, 2020.
```
