# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S. 23

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       ML-246. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to Senate Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 23, requesting the Joint Committee on the 
     Library of Congress approve the replacement of Michigan's 
     statue of Lewis Cass with a statue of Coleman A. Young as 
     part of the National Statuary Hall Collection and to take 
     other actions related to this request; which was referred to 
     the Committee on House Administration.
```
