# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 20, 2022

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 20, 2022

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 11 a.m. on 
Thursday, January 20; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, for the information of Senators, we 
expect a rollcall vote on confirmation of the Thomas nomination at 12 
noon.
```
