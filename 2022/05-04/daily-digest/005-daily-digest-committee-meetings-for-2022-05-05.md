# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 2022-05-05

                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR THURSDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Armed Services: to hold hearings to examine the 
posture of the Department of the Army in review of the Defense 
Authorization Request for fiscal year 2023 and the Future Years

examine the President's proposed budget request for fiscal year 2023 
for the Department of Energy, 10 a.m., SD-366.

Justice, 9 a.m., SH-216.


                                 House

  No hearings are scheduled.
