# BIDEN BUDGET DELIBERATELY WORSENS CRISES

**Speaking:** Smith, Jason (S001195) [R-MO]

```
                BIDEN BUDGET DELIBERATELY WORSENS CRISES

  (Mr. SMITH of Missouri asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. SMITH of Missouri. Mr. Speaker, Democrat policies have created 
multiple crises: the highest spike in prices in 40 years, gas prices 
over $4 a gallon, a violent crime crisis, school closures that have 
drastically harmed our children, and so many other items.
  President Biden's budget, which was released this week, deliberately 
makes every crisis American families are facing that much worse.
  With $73 trillion in spending, Biden doubles down on his delusion 
that spending more will cause inflation to go down when Americans are 
facing a $5,200 inflation tax this year.
  Biden's budget charges Americans another $2.5 trillion in taxes, 
upward of $4 trillion if you count the build back broke agenda, which 
he tries to put in the budget as being free.
  American families can't afford what this budget is costing.

                          ____________________
```
