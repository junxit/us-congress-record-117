# HAPPY 100TH BIRTHDAY JOSEPHINE BIZZARRO

**Speaking:** Obernolte, Jay (O000019) [R-CA]

```
                HAPPY 100TH BIRTHDAY JOSEPHINE BIZZARRO

  (Mr. OBERNOLTE asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. OBERNOLTE. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to rise this afternoon in 
celebration of the 100th birthday of a member of my district. Josephine 
Bizzarro, a resident of the High Desert, turns 100. She is preceded in 
death by her husband, Mr. Domenick Bizzarro. They are both members of 
the Greatest Generation.
  The Nazis held Domenick Bizzarro as a prisoner of war in Germany, 
during World War II where he earned a Bronze Star and three Purple 
Hearts.
  His wife, Josephine, was the consummate military spouse whose 
unwavering commitment to her husband is what saw him through those dark 
days in World War II.
  Mr. Speaker, everyone that knows her celebrates her life as the 
epitome of what a life well lived should be, and Heather and I join 
them in wishing Josephine Bizzarro a very happy 100th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
