Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) as well as calling on all members 
of NATO to ratify the protocols of accession swiftly, as amended, on 
which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.

gentleman from California (Mr. Costa) that the House suspend the rules
