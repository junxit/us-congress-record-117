# EXECUTIVE REPORTS OF COMMITTEE

```
                     EXECUTIVE REPORTS OF COMMITTEE

  The following executive reports of nominations were submitted:

       By Mr. REED for the Committee on Armed Services.
       *Musetta Tia Johnson, of Virginia, to be a Judge of the 
     United States Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces for a 
     term of fifteen years to expire on the date prescribed by 
     law.
       *Marvin L. Adams, of Texas, to be Deputy Administrator for 
     Defense Programs, National Nuclear Security Administration.
       *Erik Kristopher Raven, of the District of Columbia, to be 
     Under Secretary of the Navy.
       *William A. LaPlante, Jr., of Massachusetts, to be Under 
     Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment.

  *Nomination was reported with recommendation that it be confirmed 
subject to the nominee's commitment to respond to requests to appear 
and testify before any duly constituted committee of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
