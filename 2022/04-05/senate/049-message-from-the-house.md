# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1916, H.R. 5657

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:15 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1916. An act to provide health insurance benefits for 
     outpatient and inpatient items and services related to the 
     diagnosis and treatment of a congenital anomaly or birth 
     defect.
       H.R. 5657. An act to amend the Controlled Substances Act to 
     make marijuana accessible for use by qualified marijuana 
     researchers for medical purposes, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
