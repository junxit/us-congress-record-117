# IN RECOGNITION OF JACKSONVILLE HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL 4A STATE TITLE

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
 IN RECOGNITION OF JACKSONVILLE HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL 4A STATE 
                                 TITLE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 16, 2022

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Jacksonville High School boys' basketball team for winning the AHSAA 
Class 4A state title.
  On March 4, 2022, The Jacksonville Golden Eagles played fast and left 
no doubt as to who deserves to be state champion, beating Escambia 
County 85 to 49 to secure their title win. The game was played at 
Legacy Arena in Birmingham, Alabama.
  Cade Phillips was named tournament MVP. This was head coach Shane 
Morrow's first year and the Golden Eagles' first basketball state 
title.
  Madam Speaker, please join me in congratulating Coach Morrow, the 
basketball team, students, faculty and all the fans. Go Golden Eagles.
```
