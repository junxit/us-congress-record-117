# HONORING BURLINGTON HIGH SCHOOL BOYS BASKETBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Miller-Meeks, Mariannette (M001215) [R-IA]

```
          HONORING BURLINGTON HIGH SCHOOL BOYS BASKETBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. MARIANNETTE MILLER-MEEKS

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 16, 2022

  Mrs. MILLER-MEEKS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
Burlington High School Boys Basketball Team for their first appearance 
in the Iowa High School State Tournament since 1987.
  These extraordinary young men have worked tirelessly to hone their 
skills and overcome obstacles during this season. I am extremely 
honored to represent these young men in Congress and proud that their 
hard work was rewarded by an appearance in the state tournament. I look 
forward to seeing this program continue to grow and develop and cannot 
wait to see more tournament appearances soon.

                          ____________________
```
