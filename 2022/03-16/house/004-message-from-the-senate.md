# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 623

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 623. An act to make daylight saving time permanent, and 
     for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 117-81, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Ranking Member of the Senate Committee on Armed 
Services, appoints the following individuals to serve as members of the 
Congressional Commission on the Strategic Posture of the United States:

       The Honorable Jon Kyl of Arizona.
       The Honorable Lisa Gordon-Hagerty of Florida.

                          ____________________
```
