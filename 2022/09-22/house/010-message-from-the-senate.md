# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 516, H.R. 6899

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 6899. An act to prohibit the Secretary of the Treasury 
     from engaging in transactions involving the exchange of 
     Special Drawing Rights issued by the International Monetary 
     Fund that are held by the Russian Federation of Belarus.

  The message also announced that the Senate agrees to the amendment of 
the House to the bill (S. 516) ``An Act to plan for and coordinate 
efforts to integrate advanced air mobility aircraft into the national 
airspace system, and for other purposes.''.
```
