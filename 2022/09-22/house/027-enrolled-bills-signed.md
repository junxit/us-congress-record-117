# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 5577, H.R. 6899

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Lisa P. Grant, Deputy Clerk of the House, reported and found truly an 
enrolled bill of the House of the following title, which was thereupon 
signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 5577. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 3900 Crown Road Southwest in 
     Atlanta, Georgia, as the `` John R. Lewis Post Office 
     Building''.

  Kevin F. McCumber, Deputy Clerk of the House, further reported and 
found truly an enrolled bill of the House of the following title, which 
was thereupon signed by the Speaker

       H.R. 6899. An act to prohibit the Secretary of the Treasury 
     from engaging in transactions involving the exchange of 
     Special Drawing issued by the International Monetary Fund 
     that are held by the Russian Federation or Belarus.

                          ____________________
```
