# Vote on Prabhakar Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Vote on Prabhakar Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Prabhakar nomination?
  Mr. BROWN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.

the Senator from New Mexico (Mr. Heinrich), and the Senator from 
Washington (Mrs. Murray) are necessarily absent.

from Idaho (Mr. Crapo).
```
