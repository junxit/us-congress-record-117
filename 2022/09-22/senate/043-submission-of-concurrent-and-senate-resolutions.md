            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were

       S. Res. 794. A resolution proclaiming the week of September 
     26 through September 30, 2022, to be ``National Clean Energy 
     Week''; considered and agreed to.
           By Mrs. FISCHER (for herself, Mr. Peters, and Mr. 
             Warnock):
       S. Res. 795. A resolution designating September 2022 as 
     ``School Bus Safety Month''; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. CORNYN (for himself and Mr. Cruz):
       S. Res. 796. A resolution honoring the life and legacy of 
     the late Senator Robert ``Bob'' Charles Krueger; considered 
     and agreed to.
