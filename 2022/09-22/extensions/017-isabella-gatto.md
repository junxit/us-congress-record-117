# ISABELLA GATTO

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                             ISABELLA GATTO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 22, 2022

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, In July, I had the pleasure of attending 
Isabella Gatto's Eagle Scout Court of Honor ceremony. Throughout her 
scouting career, Isabella participated in the merit badge program, 
practiced leadership tactics, worked with other scouts on outdoor 
projects, and completed her Eagle Scout project. For her Eagle Scout 
project, she led a team to construct a GaGa ball pit. Isabella built 
this ball pit for the youth group at the Greentree Church in Egg Harbor 
Township, South Jersey. In addition to her Eagle Scout project, she 
also completed lifeguard certification training and the National 
Advanced Youth Leadership Experience, the highest level of leadership 
training available to the Scouts of America. Isabella should be proud 
of her scouting achievements, and it was a pleasure to attend her Eagle 
Scout ceremony. God Bless Isabella, and God Bless our America.

                          ____________________
```
