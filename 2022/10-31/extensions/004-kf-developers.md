# KF DEVELOPERS

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                             KF DEVELOPERS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize KF 
Developers of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the City of Northglenn's 
Business Investment Award.
  The Business Investment award recognizes one established business or 
Northglenn property owner that has made major capital investments in 
equipment and/or facility improvements. KF Developers is passionate 
about transforming Karls Farm into a mixed-use development that 
commemorates the legacy of the dairy farm. With a focus on meeting the 
needs of the community, KF Developers is a valued developer in 
Northglenn.
  Congratulations to KF Developers for receiving this award and I wish 
them all the best in the future. I thank them for their contribution to 
our community.

                          ____________________
```
