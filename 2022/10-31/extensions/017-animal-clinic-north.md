# ANIMAL CLINIC NORTH

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                          ANIMAL CLINIC NORTH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Animal 
Clinic North of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the City of 
Northglenn's Business Longevity Award.
  The Business Longevity Award recognizes a local business that has 
existed in Northglenn as a business for over 20 years and provides 
exceptional customer service. Animal Clinic North, owned by Dr. Kerry 
Madole, is committed to providing comprehensive care for everyone who 
is seeking care for their pet. With a focus on small animal care, 
Animal Clinic North is a valued business in Northglenn.
  Congratulations to Animal Clinic North for receiving this award and I 
wish them all the best in the future. I thank them for their 
contribution to our community.
```
