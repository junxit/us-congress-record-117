# YVETTE KLINGER

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                             YVETTE KLINGER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Yvette 
Klinger, The Anubis Emporium of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the 
City of Northglenn's Young Entrepreneur of the Year Award.
  The Young Entrepreneur of the Year Award recognizes one student with 
an established business who either lives in Northglenn or attends a 
Northglenn (K-12) school and exemplifies an entrepreneurial spirit. The 
Anubis Emporium, founded by Yvette Klinger, is passionate about sharing 
art that customers will resonate with. With a focus on providing 
unique, creative products that display her digital art, Yvette 
Klinger--The Anubis Emporium is a valued business in Northglenn.
  Congratulations to Yvette Klinger--The Anubis Emporium for receiving 
this award and I wish her all the best in the future. I thank her for 
her contribution to our community.

                          ____________________
```
