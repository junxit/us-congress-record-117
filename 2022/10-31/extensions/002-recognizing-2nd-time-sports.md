# RECOGNIZING 2ND TIME SPORTS

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING 2ND TIME SPORTS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize 2nd Time 
Sports of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the City of Northglenn's 
People's Choice of the Year Award.
  The People's Choice of the Year Award recognizes one business that 
has been chosen through an online popular vote for having the best 
product or service and overall customer satisfaction. 2nd Time Sports 
is passionate about providing new and used sports equipment to 
consumers in the north metro area. With a focus on excellent customer 
service, 2nd Time Sports is a valued business in Northglenn.
  Congratulations to 2nd Time Sports for receiving this award and I 
wish them all the best in the future. I thank them for their 
contribution to our community.

                          ____________________
```
