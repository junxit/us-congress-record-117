# RUBY JEAN PATISSERIE

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                          RUBY JEAN PATISSERIE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ruby Jean 
Patisserie of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the City of Northglenn's 
Home-Based Business of the Year Award.
  The Home-Based Business of the Year Award recognizes a business 
operating from a Northglenn residence and exemplifies exceptional 
customer service and products. Ruby Jean Patisserie, owned and operated 
by Jennifer Essex, is dedicated to creating sweet delicacies with 
European techniques. With an emphasis on exceptional quality and giving 
back to the community, Ruby Jean Patisserie is a valued home-based 
business in Northglenn.
  Congratulations to Ruby Jean Patisserie for receiving this award and 
I wish them all the best in the future. I thank them for their 
contribution to our community.

                          ____________________
```
