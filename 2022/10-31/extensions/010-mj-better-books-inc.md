# MJ BETTER BOOKS, INC.

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                         MJ BETTER BOOKS, INC.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize MJ Better 
Books, Inc. of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the City of 
Northglenn's Rookie Business of the Year Award.
  The Rookie Business of the Year award recognizes a newly formed 
business that has been operating for less than two years and 
exemplifies innovation. MJ Better Books, Inc., founded by Miriam Nunez 
and Jamie Nunez, is passionate about helping small businesses and 
entrepreneurs be successful. With an emphasis on helping Hispanic and 
Spanish speaking businesses, MJ Better Books, Inc. is a valued newly 
established business in Northglenn.
  Congratulations to MJ Better Books, Inc. for receiving this award and 
I wish them all the best in the future. I thank them for their 
contribution to our community.
```
