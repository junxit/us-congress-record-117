# MURRAY-BROWN LABORATORIES

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                       MURRAY-BROWN LABORATORIES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Murray-Brown 
Laboratories of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the City of 
Northglenn's Mid-Size Business of the Year Award.
  The Mid-Size Business of the Year Award recognizes a local business 
with up to 50 employees that exemplifies a high level of business 
ethics and professionalism. Murray-Brown Laboratories, co-owned by John 
Murray and Brenda Brown, exists to provide testing to food and beverage 
companies that depend on the results to keep the products for consumers 
safe for consumption. With a passion for providing excellent customer 
service, Murray-Brown Laboratories is a valued mid-size business in 
Northglenn.
  Congratulations to Murray-Brown Laboratories for receiving this award 
and I wish them all the best in the future. I thank them for their 
contribution to our community.

                          ____________________
```
