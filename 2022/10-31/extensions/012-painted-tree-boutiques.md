# PAINTED TREE BOUTIQUES

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                         PAINTED TREE BOUTIQUES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Painted Tree 
Boutiques--Northglenn of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the City of 
Northglenn's Small Business of the Year Award.
  The Small Business of the Year Award recognizes a local business with 
up to 20 employees that impacted the community with its entrepreneurial 
spirit. Painted Tree Boutiques--Northglenn exists to provide space for 
hundreds of other small businesses in its store. With its ability to 
create space for others to pursue their entrepreneurial dreams, Painted 
Tree Boutiques--Northglenn is a valued small business in Northglenn.
  Congratulations to Painted Tree Boutiques--Northglenn for receiving 
this award and I wish them all the best in the future. I thank them for 
their contribution to our community.

                          ____________________
```
