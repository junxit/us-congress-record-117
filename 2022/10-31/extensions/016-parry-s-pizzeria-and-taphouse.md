# PARRY'S PIZZERIA AND TAPHOUSE

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                     PARRY'S PIZZERIA AND TAPHOUSE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Parry's 
Pizzeria & Taphouse of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the City of 
Northglenn's Large Business of the Year Award.
  The Large Business of the Year Award recognizes a local business with 
over 50 employees and shows business growth and stability. Parry's 
Pizzeria & Taphouse is dedicated to providing craveable food and beer 
served with uncommon hospitality. With its dedication to community 
commitment and involvement, Parry's Pizzeria is a valued large business 
in Northglenn.
  Congratulations to Parry's Pizzeria & Taphouse for receiving this 
award and I wish them all the best in the future. I thank them for 
their contribution to our community.

                          ____________________
```
