# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Spirit of Life, who soars upon the wings of the wind, breathe Your 
Grace in us. Remind us to be thankful for the people who bless our 
lives.
  Thank You for the people who live exemplary lives and show us what 
productive living should be. Thank You for the people who inspire us to 
strive for noble things. Thank You for the people who comfort us, who 
help ease life's hurts. Thank You for the people who strengthen us, who 
give us courage to tackle the difficult. Thank You for the people who 
enlighten us through the written word. Lord, thank You for our 
Senators, who strive to protect and defend our freedom.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
