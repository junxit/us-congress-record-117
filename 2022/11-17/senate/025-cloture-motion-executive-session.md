# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 1147, Anne M. Nardacci, of New 
     York, to be United States District Judge for the Northern 
     District of New York.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Jeff Merkley, Tina 
           Smith, Sheldon Whitehouse, Benjamin L. Cardin, Maria 
           Cantwell, Amy Klobuchar, Jon Ossoff, Mark Kelly, Jacky 
           Rosen, Brian Schatz, Mazie K. Hirono, Angus S. King, 
           Jr., Thomas R. Carper, Sherrod Brown, Tim Kaine.

  Mr. SCHUMER. I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory quorum calls 
for the cloture motions filed today, Thursday, November 17, be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
