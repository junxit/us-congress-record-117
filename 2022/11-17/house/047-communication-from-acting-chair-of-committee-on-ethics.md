# COMMUNICATION FROM ACTING CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON ETHICS

```
         COMMUNICATION FROM ACTING CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON ETHICS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the acting chair of the Committee on Ethics:

                                         House of Representatives,


                                          Committee on Ethics,

                                Washington, DC, November 16, 2022.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that the Committee on Ethics has been served 
     with a third-party subpoena to produce documents in the 
     Superior Court of the District of Columbia.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, the 
     Committee has determined that only partial compliance with 
     the subpoena is consistent with the privileges and rights of 
     the House.
           Sincerely,
     Susan Wild,
       Acting Chairwoman.
     Michael Guest,
       Acting Ranking Member.

                          ____________________
```
