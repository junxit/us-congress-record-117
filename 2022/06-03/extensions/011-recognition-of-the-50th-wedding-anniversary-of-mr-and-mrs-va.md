# RECOGNITION OF THE 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY OF MR. AND MRS. VASTOLA

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
  RECOGNITION OF THE 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY OF MR. AND MRS. VASTOLA

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, June 3, 2022

  Mr. WITTMAN. Madam Speaker, on Friday, May 27th, Alfonso and Rose 
Marie Vastola of King George, Virginia celebrated their 50th wedding 
anniversary. They were joined by family and close friends to celebrate 
the occasion.
  Alfonso and Rose Marie met in Newark, New Jersey at an Italian-
American club dance in the neighborhood where Rose Marie grew up 
shortly after Alfonso immigrated to the United States from Italy. They 
were married at Sacred Heart Catholic Church in Vailsburg, New Jersey 
in 1972 and were blessed with one daughter, Maria.
  Madam Speaker, please join me in congratulating Alfonso and Rose 
Marie on this exciting occasion. Happy 50th wedding anniversary.

                          ____________________
```
