# CONGRATULATING PHILLIPS DINER ON ITS DESIGNATION AS A HISTORIC BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Stefanik, Elise M. (S001196) [R-NY]

```
CONGRATULATING PHILLIPS DINER ON ITS DESIGNATION AS A HISTORIC BUSINESS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, June 3, 2022

  Ms. STEFANIK. Madam Speaker, I rise today to celebrate an incredible 
North Country establishment that has been recognized as a historic 
business, Phillips Diner in Ogdensburg. Phillips Diner has been named 
to the New York State Historic Preservation Registry for operating for 
over fifty years and contributes greatly to the Ogdensburg community.
  Phillips Diner has been owned and operated by members of the Phillips 
family since 1948. In 1930, the Diner got its start as the Theater 
Dining Car and was owned by M&F Dining Corp. The restaurant quickly 
became a favorite dining spot for those seeing a movie at the Strand 
Theater located next door. In 1948, Russell and Ollie Phillips 
purchased the Theater Dining Car with their son, Bud, and renamed it 
Phillips Diner. Phillips Diner was extremely successful for several 
decades, until a devastating fire destroyed the building in 1970. Not 
discouraged by this setback, the Phillips family rebuilt and opened 
once again in 1973, proudly serving their community ever since.
  The Diner is now owned and operated by Judi Ashley, the granddaughter 
of the original owners, and her husband. Over seventy years later, the 
diner staff still use Russell and Ollie's original recipes to prepare 
their famous dishes. Phillips Diner is a true community staple priding 
themselves on serving great meals at a great value.
  On behalf of New York's 21st District, I am proud to celebrate the 
rich history of Phillips Diner. This establishment has been an 
important asset to the Ogdensburg community for several decades and 
will undoubtedly continue to provide service for many more to come. 
Congratulations to Phillips Diner for their recognition on the New York 
State Historic Preservation Registry.
```
