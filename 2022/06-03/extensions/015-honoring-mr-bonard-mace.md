# HONORING MR. BONARD MACE

**Speaking:** Crawford, Eric A. "Rick" (C001087) [R-AR]

```
                        HONORING MR. BONARD MACE

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ERIC A. ``RICK'' CRAWFORD

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, June 3, 2022

  Mr. CRAWFORD. Madam Speaker, today I rise to recognize Mr. Bonard 
Mace, a distinguished Superintendent from my district who is retiring 
this year after a career of service.
  Mr. Bonard Mace is a dedicated educator of 39 years, who served as a 
teacher, coach, and administrator in the Hoxie, Westside, Nettleton, 
and Gosnell school districts. He is retiring as the Superintendent of 
Gosnell after also serving as an Assistant Principal, High School 
Principal, and Assistant Superintendent.
  Mr. Mace devoted his careers to his students and played an essential 
role in teaching and preparing the next generation to be leaders and 
public servants. His hard work and dedication is invaluable in our 
communities, and I am certain the First District is better because of 
his service.

                          ____________________
```
