# SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF AIDAN SWARTZ ON HIS OFFER OF APPOINTMENT TO ATTEND THE UNITED STATES AIR FORCE ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
  SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF AIDAN SWARTZ ON HIS OFFER OF APPOINTMENT TO 
               ATTEND THE UNITED STATES AIR FORCE ACADEMY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, June 3, 2022

  Mr. LATTA. Madam Speaker, it is my great pleasure to pay special 
tribute to an outstanding student from Ohio's Fifth Congressional 
District. I am pleased to announce that Aidan Swartz of Sylvania, Ohio, 
has been offered an appointment to the United States Air Force Academy 
in Colorado Springs, Colorado.
  Aidan's offer of appointment permits him to attend the United States 
Air Force Academy this fall with the incoming Class of 2026. Attending 
one of our Nation's military academies offers the opportunity to serve 
our country and receive a world-class education. At the same time, 
these young men and women undertake one of the most challenging and 
rewarding experiences of their lives.
  Aidan brings a tremendous amount of leadership, service, and 
dedication to the incoming Class of 2026. While attending Northview 
High School in Sylvania, Ohio, Aidan participated in Leadership Academy 
and Football Leadership. Throughout high school, Aidan played football 
earning his varsity letter and becoming team captain. After high 
school, he attended the United States Air Force Academy Preparatory 
School in Colorado.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Aidan 
Swartz on his offer of appointment to the United States Air Force 
Academy. Our service academies offer the finest military training and 
education available. I am positive that Aidan will excel during his 
career at the Air Force Academy, and I ask my colleagues to join me in 
extending their best wishes to him as he begins his service to our 
Nation.

                          ____________________
```
