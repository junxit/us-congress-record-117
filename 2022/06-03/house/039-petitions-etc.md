# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.R. 69

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-118. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislature 
     of Rockland County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 221 
     of 2022, urging the U.S. House of Representatives to pass the 
     Sunshine Protection Act of 2021 (H.R. 69); which was referred 
     to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       PT-119. Also, a petition of the Council of the District of 
     Columbia, District of Columbia, relative to Resolution 24-
     419, the ``Sense of the Council to Denounce the Russian 
     Attack on Ukraine Resolution of 2022''; which was referred to 
     the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
```
