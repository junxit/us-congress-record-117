# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, our light and salvation, You have invited us in Psalm 
50:15 to call You in the day of trouble, and so we are here. We thank 
You that prayer is a key to unlock the arsenal of Your power, mercy, 
and grace. Because of prayer, we remain confident that You are still in 
control of our troubled world.
  As we see a nightmare unfolding in Ukraine, we ask You to deliver the 
Ukrainian people from this season of despotism, desperation, 
destruction, and death.
  Lord, continue to use our Senators to accomplish Your purposes on 
Earth.
  We pray in Your majestic Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
