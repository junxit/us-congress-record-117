# CELEBRATING APRIL AS FAITH MONTH

**Speaking:** Miller, Mary E. (M001211) [R-IL]

```
                    CELEBRATING APRIL AS FAITH MONTH

  (Mrs. MILLER of Illinois asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. MILLER of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, this month, Americans all over 
the country are celebrating April as Faith Month. I rise in support of 
this effort and call on all people of faith to join in prayer and 
celebration.
  As a Member of Congress, I am happy to reaffirm my commitment to our 
Judeo-Christian values and freedom of religion for all that this 
country was founded on.
  Religious organizations in America have a rich history of charitable 
engagement, helping the sick, poor, and afflicted. They should be 
celebrated for their contributions to our way of life.

                          ____________________
```
