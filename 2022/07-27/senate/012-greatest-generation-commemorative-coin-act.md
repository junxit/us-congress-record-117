# GREATEST GENERATION COMMEMORATIVE COIN ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 1057, S. 1569

```
               GREATEST GENERATION COMMEMORATIVE COIN ACT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate having 
received H.R. 1057 the text of which is identical to S. 1569, the bill 
is considered read a third time and the questions occur on passage of 
the bill.
  The bill was ordered to a third reading and was read the third time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill having been read the third time, the 
question is, Shall the bill pass?
  The bill (H.R. 1057) was passed.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the motion to 
reconsider is considered made and laid upon the table.
  The Senator from Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
