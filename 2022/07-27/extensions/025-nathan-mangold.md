# NATHAN MANGOLD

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                             NATHAN MANGOLD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 27, 2022

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, last week, I had the pleasure of 
attending Nathan Mangold's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. Nathan is a 
recent graduate of Cedar Creek High School Class of 2022 and has plans 
to continue his education at a University in the fall. During his time 
as a scout, he earned 21 Merit

[[Page E791]]

Badges and held numerous leadership positions. For his Eagle Scout 
project, Nathan constructed osprey nests at the Atlantic City Yacht 
Club In Pleasantville, South Jersey. These nests will provide ospreys 
with a place to nest upon and help to boost the endangered species' 
population. Nathan should be proud of his efforts to conserve the 
wildlife of South Jersey. God Bless Nathan, and God Bless our United 
States of America.

                          ____________________
```
