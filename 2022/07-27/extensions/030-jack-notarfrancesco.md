# JACK NOTARFRANCESCO

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                          JACK NOTARFRANCESCO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 27, 2022

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, last month, I had the pleasure of 
attending Jack Notarfrancesco's (No-tar-fran-sis-co) Eagle Scout Court 
of Honor. Jack is a scout in Troop 55 in Somers Point, South Jersey. 
For his Eagle Scout project, he built and constructed a chicken coop 
for the Funny Farm Animal Rescue in Mays Landing, South Jersey. This 
chicken coop will create a home for unwanted chickens, who may have 
otherwise been euthanized. Jack should be proud of his project and 
donation to the Funny Farm Animal Rescue, and it was my pleasure to 
attend his Eagle Scout Court of Honor ceremony. God Bless Jack, and God 
Bless our America.

                          ____________________
```
