# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 783

**Speaking:** Larsen, Rick (L000560) [D-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 783

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 783

  Mr. LARSEN of Washington. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
I may hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 783, a 
bill originally introduced by Representative Young of Alaska for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Washington?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
