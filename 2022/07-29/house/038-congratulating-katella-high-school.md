# CONGRATULATING KATELLA HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                   CONGRATULATING KATELLA HIGH SCHOOL

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to congratulate Katella High 
School in my district for winning the National Green Ribbon Award.
  Katella High School is one of 36 high schools nationwide to win this 
honor. Katella High School has also been recognized as a California 
Green Ribbon School not once but twice; in 2020 earning the bronze 
award, and in 2021 earning the silver award.
  From a Colonist to the Knights, congratulations. Keep up the good 
work.
```
