# CONGRATULATING THE WARWICK WARRIORS

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
                  CONGRATULATING THE WARWICK WARRIORS

  (Mr. SMUCKER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)

[[Page H7411]]

  

  Mr. SMUCKER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the young 
men of Warwick School District's baseball team.
  The Warwick Warriors baseball beat Liberty High to close out a 
successful 23-2 season by winning the district's first PIAA 6A State 
Championship.
  The game, held last month, was impacted by a prolonged weather delay, 
but the warriors held firm to the early lead to capture the 
championship.
  Throughout their dominating season, the Warriors also clinched the 
District Three title, as well.
  Madam Speaker, it is no surprise that the community is very proud of 
these students for their achievement on the baseball diamond. It was 
great to see the local law enforcement and volunteer firefighters join 
with the community by participating in a parade to honor their 
achievement.
  Congratulations to the members and coaches of the Warwick Warrior 
State championship baseball team. The whole community is wishing them 
continued success. May they carry the lessons they learned playing as 
part of a successful team toward their future goals.

                          ____________________
```
