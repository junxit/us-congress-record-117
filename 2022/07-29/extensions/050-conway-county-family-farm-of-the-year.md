# CONWAY COUNTY FAMILY FARM OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                 CONWAY COUNTY FAMILY FARM OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 29, 2022

  Mr. HILL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 2022 Conway 
County Family Farm of the Year, the Stobaugh Brother Family Farm.
  Right off Highway 64, the Stobaugh family has been operating their 
family farm since the 1950s, producing soybeans, rice, and corn over 
6,000 acres of land.
  Known for their first-class quality of crops, the Stobaugh's continue 
to utilize traditional farming methods while welcoming advancements in 
agricultural technology. These advancements include weather stations 
throughout the farm, and innovative irrigation system, and computerized 
calculations to ensure efficiency.
  Despite labor shortages and the rising inflation currently plaguing 
our agricultural industry, the Stobaughs continue to produce staple 
goods for many to enjoy at their dinner table.
  Congratulations to the Stobaughs and I thank them for being part of 
the family farms that feed 86 percent of what feeds American families 
every night.
```
