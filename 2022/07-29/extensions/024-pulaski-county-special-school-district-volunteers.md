# PULASKI COUNTY SPECIAL SCHOOL DISTRICT VOLUNTEERS

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
           PULASKI COUNTY SPECIAL SCHOOL DISTRICT VOLUNTEERS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 29, 2022

  Mr. HILL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the volunteers of 
the Pulaski County Special School District who have devoted nearly 
18,000 hours to Pulaski County students during the 2021-2022 school 
year.
  Several individual volunteers earned awards for extraordinary 
commitment of their time.
  Recognized volunteers include Ms. Charnelle Joslin of Cato 
Elementary; Mr. Demone Gillette of Joe T. Robinson Middle School; and 
Ms. Amber Henson of Joe T.

[[Page E806]]

Robinson High School. Together, these 3 volunteers dedicated over 850 
hours to the needs of students within the district.
  Additionally, Joe T. Robinson Elementary, Joe T. Robinson Middle 
School, and Maumelle High School were recognized for having the highest 
average volunteer hours donated per student.
  These volunteers are vital to the educational success of our students 
and promotes better citizenship by encouraging our kids to volunteer 
and make an impact in their communities.
  I commend the Pulaski County Special School District for their 
exemplary volunteer programs.

                          ____________________
```
