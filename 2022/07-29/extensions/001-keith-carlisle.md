# KEITH CARLISLE

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                             KEITH CARLISLE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 29, 2022

  Mr. HILL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Searcy's 
Battalion chief, Keith Carlisle, on his retirement from the fire 
service.
  For 34 years, Chief Carlisle has worked to protect our community. 
Born and raised in Cabot, he went on to the fire academy and later 
returned on down the road to Searcy.
  His greatest moments in service included comforting victims of car 
accidents or fires.
  Every member of the fire service deserves admiration for their 
exemplary work for their communities. Public servants, like the chief, 
play a vital role in the infrastructure that keeps out citizens safe 
and healthy. I commend Keith Carlisle for his leadership and give best 
wishes as he transitions into retirement.

                          ____________________
```
