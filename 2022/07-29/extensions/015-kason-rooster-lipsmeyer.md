# KASON ``ROOSTER'' LIPSMEYER

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                      KASON ``ROOSTER'' LIPSMEYER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 29, 2022

  Mr. HILL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate seventh grader 
Kason ``Rooster'' Lipsmeyer, of Vilonia, who has earned a position on 
the Arkansas National Junior High Rodeo team.
  Rooster and his teammates will be competing in the 17th annual 
National Junior High Finals Rodeo in the light rifle competition in 
Perry, Georgia.
  While it is only Rooster's first year competing, he won both the 
state championship and was the average champion in light rifle for the 
Arkansas Junior High School Rodeo Association.
  The art of competitive shooting is a long-standing tradition. Gun 
safety and accuracy are vital skills for gun-owners and all who will be 
around firearms. I commend Rooster for his determination, precision, 
and good sportsmanship and look forward to rooting him on as he 
competes against over a thousand others in the national competition.
  Congratulations and good luck to Rooster and the entire Arkansas 
National Junior High Rodeo team. We look forward to cheering them all 
on.

                          ____________________
```
