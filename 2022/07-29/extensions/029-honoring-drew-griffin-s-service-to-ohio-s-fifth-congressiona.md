# HONORING DREW GRIFFIN'S SERVICE TO OHIO'S FIFTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
 HONORING DREW GRIFFIN'S SERVICE TO OHIO'S FIFTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 29, 2022

  Mr. LATTA. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize a dedicated 
member of my staff, Drew Griffin, who will be leaving our office at the 
beginning of September.
  Hailing originally from the Pacific Northwest, Drew has dedicated his 
career to public service, including time spent working on behalf of the 
people of Washington and Minnesota. We have been blessed to have Drew 
working on behalf of the Buckeye state for the past six years, three of 
which as my Chief of Staff, and we know it will be hard to replace him.
  A dedicated sports fan, Drew could always be counted on to discuss 
the latest news impacting his Seattle Sounders or Washington Nationals. 
He instilled in everyone he interacted with a sense of comradery and 
purpose, showcasing the positive impact that individuals can have on 
their community if they worked together.
  Drew is also a loving father to daughter, Eve, and son, Jack, and a 
dedicated husband to Ann. As he enters this next chapter in his life, I 
know that he will have the love and support of his family and friends 
to help him along the way.
  I am eternally grateful for Drew for all he has done for me, and I 
wish him and his family the best.

                          ____________________
```
