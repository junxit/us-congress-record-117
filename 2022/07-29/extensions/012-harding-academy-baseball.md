# HARDING ACADEMY BASEBALL

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                        HARDING ACADEMY BASEBALL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 29, 2022

  Mr. HILL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Gavin Alveti, a 
2022 graduate from Harding Academy and distinguished member of the 
Wildcat baseball team, who was recently named the Arkansas Democrat 
Gazette All-Arkansas Preps Baseball Player of the Year.
  Gavin suffered a broken orbital bone during a game in April after 
being hit by a fastball. Despite these injuries, Gavin returned to the 
mound within a week and was able to finish out his senior season.
  The Harding Academy baseball team went on to win the AAA Arkansas 
State Championship, under the leadership of Coach Shane Fullerton.
  Despite obstacles throughout the season, I am pleased to recognize 
Gavin, Coach Fullerton, and the Harding Academy baseball team on a 
strong, hard-fought season.
  I congratulate them on their achievements.

                          ____________________
```
