# NORTH CENTRAL ARKANSAS ARTIST LEAGUE

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                  NORTH CENTRAL ARKANSAS ARTIST LEAGUE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 29, 2022

  Mr. HILL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
work of the artists who make up the North Central Arkansas Artist 
League.
  The league recently debuted a new art exhibit, currently on display 
at the Ozark Health Medical Center in Clinton through August 17th.
  The exhibit is made up of 43 original pieces of art, created by local 
artists inspired by ``Summer Fun''. Subjects include landscapes, 
florals, wildlife, and portraits.
  All artwork is made from oils, acrylics, watercolors, and charcoal.
  I commend the North Central Arkansas Artist League on another 
successful exhibit, and I encourage all to spend an evening in Clinton, 
admiring the work of our local artists.

                          ____________________
```
