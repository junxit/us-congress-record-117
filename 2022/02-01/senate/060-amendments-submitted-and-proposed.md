# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

**Measures:** S.Res. 35, S.Res. 345, S.Res. 496

```
                   AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 4918. Mr. MENENDEZ (for Mr. Cardin (for himself and Mr. 
     Young)) proposed an amendment to the resolution S. Res. 35, 
     condemning the military coup that took place on February 1, 
     2021, in Burma and the Tatmadaw's detention of civilian 
     leaders, calling for an immediate and unconditional release 
     of all those detained, promoting accountability and justice 
     for those killed by the Tatmadaw, and calling for those 
     elected to serve in parliament to resume their duties without 
     impediment, and for other purposes.
```
