# Vote on Ruiz Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        Vote on Ruiz Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Markey). Under the previous order, all 
postcloture time has expired.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Ruiz 
nomination?
  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from New Mexico (Mr. Lujan), 
is necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from North Dakota (Mr. Hoeven) and the Senator from Utah (Mr. Romney).
  Further, if present and voting, the Senator from North Dakota (Mr. 
Hoeven) would have voted ``nay.''
  The result was announced--yeas 62, nays 35, as follows:
```
