# Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
            Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that, 
notwithstanding rule XXII, at 5 p.m. today all postcloture time on the 
Ruiz nomination expire; and that following the confirmation vote, the 
Senate vote on the motions to invoke cloture on the Puttagunta, Lopez, 
and Staples nominations in the order listed, without intervening action 
of debate; further, that if cloture is invoked on any of the 
nominations, the confirmation votes be at a time to be determined by 
the majority leader in consultation with the Republican leader.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DURBIN. I want to notify all Senators that they should expect 
four rollcall votes beginning at 5 p.m.
```
