# CONGRATULATING AUSTIN HUFF FOR WINNING THE SPECIAL OLYMPICS STATE BOWLING CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Bustos, Cheri (B001286) [D-IL]

```
   CONGRATULATING AUSTIN HUFF FOR WINNING THE SPECIAL OLYMPICS STATE 
                          BOWLING CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHERI BUSTOS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 11, 2022

  Mrs. BUSTOS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Austin Huff 
on winning the Special Olympics State Bowling Championship.
  In the state championship, Austin bowled 89, 71, and 75 to win the 
gold. I am pleased to see Austin representing the Rock Island community 
in the Special Olympics. I congratulate Austin on his win at the state 
championship, and I commend him for his good sportsmanship and exciting 
win at the state championship.
  It is because of inspirational people such as Austin Huff that I am 
especially proud to serve Illinois' 17th Congressional District. Madam 
Speaker, I would like to again formally congratulate Austin Huff on his 
win at the Special Olympics State Bowling Championship.

                          ____________________
```
