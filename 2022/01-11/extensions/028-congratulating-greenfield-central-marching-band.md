# CONGRATULATING GREENFIELD-CENTRAL MARCHING BAND

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
            CONGRATULATING GREENFIELD-CENTRAL MARCHING BAND

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 11, 2022

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Greenfield-
Central's Marching Band and Guard, Cougar Pride, for winning the Class 
B Championship at the state band competition at Lucas Oil Stadium.
  This is the second straight marching band state title Greenfield-
Central High School has taken home. These students have been perfecting 
their performance, titled ``Ramped Up'', since early summer.
  Congratulations to Band Director Chris Wing and all 150 members of 
Cougar Pride on this achievement.
```
