# HONORING REVEREND MARY McDONALD

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                    HONORING REVEREND MARY McDONALD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 24, 2022

  Mr. GRAVES of Missouri. Madam Speaker, I proudly pause to recognize 
Reverend Mary McDonald. Mary McDonald is an exceptional woman and 
winner of the Woman in the Workplace Leadership Award from the YWCA St. 
Joseph Awards Program.
  Reverend McDonald has served for 27 years at Holsey Chapel, working 
faithfully and tirelessly on behalf of her congregants. During her 
years of ministry, Mary also worked for 34 years at Boehringer-
lngelheim and was twice the Outstanding Performance in Sales and 
Marketing Award recipient. Her hard work and dedication have enshrined 
her as a leader worth looking up to.
  Madam Speaker, I proudly ask you to join me in thanking and 
congratulating Reverend McDonald for winning the Woman in the Workplace 
Leadership award as well as her service to the people of the Sixth 
Congressional District. I am proud to represent Reverend Mary McDonald 
in the United States House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
