# HONORING MAX P. WOLF-JOHNSON AND HIS SERVICE TO THE U.S. HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING MAX P. WOLF-JOHNSON AND HIS SERVICE TO THE U.S. HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 7, 2022

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Madam Speaker, I rise to honor the service and 
accomplishments of Max P. Wolf-Johnson, my senior legislative assistant 
who is transitioning from my office to pursue an advanced degree. Max 
joined my office in 2018 and has blossomed into an extraordinary policy 
expert and leader.
  Max has been instrumental in my efforts to change the landscape of 
American higher education and provide more support to HBCUs. Further, 
he has helped to advance labor-related legislation that ensures 
critical provisions for America's workers and supports Job Corps in the 
next WIOA reauthorization. His actions have led to countless wins in 
education and labor policy, and he has gained a wealth of knowledge in 
both respective areas.
  Additionally, Max has been willing to take on whatever assignment or 
job placed before him. His ability to take on new positions and excel 
is a testament to his work ethic and versatility. Though a proud native 
of the state of California, Max has treated Miami as his second home 
through his tireless work. A son of educators and a graduate of public 
K-12 schools, he understands the importance of the public school 
system.
  After graduating high school, Max earned an associate degree at the 
College of Marin and a bachelor's degree at the University of 
California Berkeley, both in political science and government.
  On behalf of Florida's 24th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in honoring Max Wolf-Johnson for his 
extraordinary accomplishments, commitment to public service, and 
trailblazing leadership.

                          ____________________
```
