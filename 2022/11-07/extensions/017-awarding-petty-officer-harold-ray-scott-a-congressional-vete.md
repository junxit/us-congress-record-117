# AWARDING PETTY OFFICER HAROLD ``RAY'' SCOTT A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Fallon, Pat (F000246) [R-TX]

```
  AWARDING PETTY OFFICER HAROLD ``RAY'' SCOTT A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN 
                              COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PAT FALLON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 7, 2022

  Mr. FALLON. Madam Speaker, I am honored and privileged to award Petty 
Officer Harold Scott with a Congressional Veteran Commendation. His 
service and sacrifice through the United States Navy exemplify his 
national pride and embodiment as a role-model citizen to serve the 
country he loves. Petty Officer Scott is truly an ideal candidate to 
receive the Congressional Veteran Commendation.
  Petty Officer Scott is a true American patriot whose sacrifices truly 
bettered the United States of America. As a volunteer, Petty Officer 
Scott was willing to risk his life to protect the freedoms he takes 
pride in.
  Petty Officer Scott held multiple positions as a versatile 
serviceman. As a Fireman in the engine room, he maintained order and 
created an environment of order to ensure nothing would happen to the 
ship. During a time when the United States of America needed strong 
young men to step up, Petty Officer Scott certainly delivered during 
his time in the Korean War. Serving across various ports on the east 
coast, west coast, and along the Mediterranean sea, Petty Officer Scott 
sacrificed all he could with no complaint.
  I am beyond honored to have Petty Officer Scott in Texas' 4th 
Congressional district and am privileged to award such a distinguished 
citizen for his sacrifices.

                          ____________________
```
