# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 9277, H.R. 9278, H.R. 9279, H.R. 9280

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. KHANNA:
       H.R. 9277. A bill to prohibit the exportation of gasoline 
     during periods of high gasoline prices; to the Committee on 
     Foreign Affairs.
           By Mrs. CAROLYN B. MALONEY of New York:
       H.R. 9278. A bill to amend chapter 22 of title 44, United 
     States Code, to require certifications regarding certain 
     Presidential and Vice Presidential Records, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Oversight and Reform.
           By Mr. PANETTA (for himself, Mr. Bacon, Mr. Larsen of 
             Washington, Mr. Waltz, and Mr. Fallon):
       H.R. 9279. A bill to amend title 10, United States Code, to 
     authorize more enlisted members of the Armed Forces to attend 
     the Naval Postgraduate School; to the Committee on Armed 
     Services.
           By Mr. VAN DREW:
       H.R. 9280. A bill to prohibit the provision of Federal 
     funds to a local educational agency that imposes or enforces 
     a COVID-19 vaccine mandate on students at the schools served 
     by such agency; to the Committee on Education and Labor.

                          ____________________
```
