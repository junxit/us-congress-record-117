# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 9350

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII,

           By Mrs. RADEWAGEN:
       H.R. 9350. A bill to restore ability of the people of 
     American Samoa to approve amendments to the territorial 
     constitution based on majority rule in a democratic act of 
     self-determination, as authorized pursuant to Act of Congress 
     delegating administration of Federal territorial law in the 
     territory to the President, and to the Secretary of the 
     Interior under Executive Order 10264, dated June 29, 1951, 
     under which the Constitution of American Samoa was approved 
     and may be amended without requirement for further 
     Congressional action, subject to the authority of Congress 
     under the Territorial Clause in article IV, section 3, clause 
     2 of the United States Constitution; to the Committee on 
     Natural Resources.

                          ____________________
```
