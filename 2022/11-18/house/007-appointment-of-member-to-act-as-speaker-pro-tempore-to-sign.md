# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS DURING THE 117TH CONGRESS

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED 
         BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS DURING THE 117TH CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                                November 18, 2022.
       I hearby appoint the Honorable James P. McGovern to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through the remainder of the One Hundred and 
     Seventeenth Congress.
           Best Regards,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker of the House.

                          ____________________
```
