         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.

     Security. H.R. 6824. A bill to authorize the Cybersecurity 
     and Infrastructure Security Agency of the Department of 
     Homeland Security to hold an annual cybersecurity competition 
     relating to offensive and defensive cybersecurity 
     disciplines, and for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 
     117-324). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
