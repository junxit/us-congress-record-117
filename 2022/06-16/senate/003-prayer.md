# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, our refuge and strength, give us reverence for Your greatness. 
Guide our Senators around the pitfalls of their work, enabling them to 
have hearts sustained by Your peace. May they surrender their will to 
You, as they trust You to direct their steps.
  Lord, give them the wisdom to receive Your reproof with the 
understanding that You chastise those whom You love for their good.
  Make their lives productive for the glory of Your Name. And, Lord, 
bless Ukraine.
  We pray in the Name of our redeemer. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
