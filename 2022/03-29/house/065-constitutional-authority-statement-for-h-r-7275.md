# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7275

**Measures:** H.R. 7275

```
           By Mrs. LURIA:
       H.R. 7275.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 12 and 13, which grant 
     Congress the power to establish a military. and Clause 18, 
     which grants Congress the necessary and proper powers to 
     carry out its other enumerated powers.
```
