# MOURNING THE LOSS OF SERGEANT DANIEL MARTINEZ

**Speaking:** Garcia, Jesus G. "Chuy" (G000586) [D-IL]

```
             MOURNING THE LOSS OF SERGEANT DANIEL MARTINEZ

  (Mr. GARCIA of Illinois asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GARCIA of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to mourn the 
untimely loss of Sergeant Daniel Martinez.
  I express my deepest condolences to my friends and neighbors, the 
Martinez family. There is no greater pain than that of losing a child, 
and there are no words I can provide to mend your pain. But I want you 
to know that the entire Southwest Side of the region has your back.
  Sergeant Daniel Martinez served his country with pride, forming close 
bonds with his fellow marines during his 4 years of service.
  He will be remembered as a young man with a sense of humor who 
enjoyed watching movies and television with his siblings and loved to 
travel.
  Daniel will be remembered by his family and our community for his 
commitment to his country. Above all, he will be remembered for his 
kindness and devotion to his family and friends.
  Rest in peace, Daniel.

                          ____________________
```
