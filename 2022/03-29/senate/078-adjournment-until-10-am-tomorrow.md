# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 AM TOMORROW

**Measures:** S.Res. 565

```
                    ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 AM TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, and pursuant to S. 
Res. 565, the Senate stands adjourned until 10 a.m., Wednesday, March
```
