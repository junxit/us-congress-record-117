                               of oregon

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 29, 2022

  Yea on Roll Call Vote 83, H.R. 1621--To amend section 3661 of title

conduct at sentencing; and
  Yea on Roll Call Vote 84, S. 3294--To obtain and direct the placement 
in the Capitol or on the Capitol Grounds of a statue to honor Associate 
Justice of the Supreme Court of the United States Sandra Day O'Connor 
and a statue to honor Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the 
United States Ruth Bader Ginsburg.
