# APPOINTMENT OF HON. ROSA L. DeLAURO TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE 117TH CONGRESS

```
 APPOINTMENT OF HON. ROSA L. DeLAURO TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO 
SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE 
                             117TH CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

[[Page H9922]]

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                December 21, 2022.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Rosa L. DeLauro to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through the remainder of the One Hundred and 
     Seventeenth Congress.
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
