# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 21, 2022, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY VOLODYMYR ZELENSKYY, PRESIDENT OF UKRAINE

**Speaking:** Sherrill, Mikie (S001207) [D-NJ]

```
AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 21, 
  2022, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY 
               VOLODYMYR ZELENSKYY, PRESIDENT OF UKRAINE

His Excellency Volodymyr Zelenskyy, President of Ukraine.
```
