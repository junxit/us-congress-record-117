# TRIBUTE TO MANDY CARTER

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

```
                             TRIBUTE TO MANDY CARTER
     
     ● Mr. OSSOFF. Madam President, I rise to honor and commend Mandy 
     Carter, a first-grade teacher at Waynesboro Primary School in Burke 
     County. Ms. Carter has spent the last two decades enriching the lives 
     of young students in Georgia. She has worked as a preschool, 
     kindergarten, first grade,
     
     [[Page S9776]]
     
     and third grade teacher. Since first graduating from Georgia Southern 
     University with her bachelor's degree in early childhood education, Ms. 
     Carter has dedicated her life to teaching and sharing her passion for 
     reading with her students.
       Earlier this year, my team visited Waynesboro Primary School and met 
     Ms. Carter, whose positive attitude, passion for teaching, and 
     motivation to help young people thrive were clear.
       As Georgia's U.S. Senator, it is my honor to recognize Mandy Carter 
     for her unwavering commitment to Georgia's children and her two decades 
     of teaching.●
     
                               ____________________
```
