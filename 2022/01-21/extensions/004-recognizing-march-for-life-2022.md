# RECOGNIZING MARCH FOR LIFE 2022

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

```
                    RECOGNIZING MARCH FOR LIFE 2022

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 21, 2022

  Mr. LaHOOD. Madam Speaker, today, I recognize the thousands of 
faithful and devoted students from central and west-central Illinois 
and around the country who are participating in this year's March for 
Life celebrations. The March for Life began in Washington, DC, as a 
small demonstration and has since become the largest pro-life event in 
the world.
  I am proud to represent Catholic schools in my district, like Peoria 
Notre Dame High School, Quincy Notre Dame High School, Routt Catholic 
High School in Jacksonville, Sacred Heart-Griffin High School in 
Springfield, and Bloomington Central Catholic, as they continue to show 
unending and unwavering support for the pro-life movement. I am honored 
to recognize the efforts of pro-life advocates across the 18th District 
who have

[[Page E62]]

worked tirelessly to protect unborn infants and provide necessary 
support and resources to pregnant mothers.
  On this 49th anniversary of the Roe v. Wade ruling, we must continue 
to pursue a national culture that promotes and protects life for all 
individuals. The right to life is a human right, and it is critical 
that our current and future generations work towards a pro-life future. 
Students, such as the ones that I represent in the 18th District, 
continue to play an integral role in fighting for the lives of the most 
vulnerable.
  As we aspire for a world where every life is celebrated and 
protected, I want to commend the support for pro-life values in my 
district and the annual support of March for Life celebrations across 
the country.

                          ____________________
```
