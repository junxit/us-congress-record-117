# HONORING THE CAREER OF BERTHA HENRY

**Speaking:** Wasserman Schultz, Debbie (W000797) [D-FL]

```
                  HONORING THE CAREER OF BERTHA HENRY

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. DEBBIE WASSERMAN SCHULTZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 21, 2022

  Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ. Madam Speaker, it is with great pleasure that 
I rise to recognize the tremendous career and meaningful work of Bertha 
Henry, Broward County Administrator.
  Broward County is a sprawling and culturally diverse metropolitan 
coastal community in the heart of South Florida, spanning from the 
ocean to the Everglades, with 1.8 million residents in 31 
municipalities. Ms. Henry's commitment to Broward County's families and 
businesses has been extensive, impressive, and inspiring.
  Bertha Henry graduated from Florida State University with a 
bachelor's degree in Government and Business Management, and a master's 
degree in Public Administration. One of her fondest memories was 
watching her parents smile with pride to see their daughter graduate 
with a master's degree, particularly since neither of her parents were 
able to complete their education. Ms. Henry's can-do attitude has 
guided her and served her well, both personally and professionally.
  Her professional career in public service includes her native City of 
Miami, Florida, Dayton (Montgomery County), Ohio, and Broward County, 
where she was named County Administrator in 2008.
  Under Ms. Henry's leadership, Broward County has received numerous 
awards and recognitions. Some highlights include Fort Lauderdale/
Hollywood International Airport being ranked third on the list of best 
large airports in the country; receiving the Smart Growth Partnership 
Excellence Award for a safe, convenient, and sustainable transportation 
system; being awarded the Keep America Beautiful President's Circle 
Award for exemplary performance in building and sustaining vibrant 
communities; and Broward County's Port Everglades receiving the Port of 
the Year and Best Domestic Departure Port Award.
  Bertha Henry is a selfless, compassionate, and devoted public servant 
whose commitment to our community is exemplary, and I am proud to call 
her my dear friend. I wish her a heartfelt congratulations on her 
retirement and am grateful for her invaluable work to improve the lives 
of those in Florida's 23rd Congressional District, Broward County, and 
beyond.
```
