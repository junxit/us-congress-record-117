# Introductory Statement on S. 3569

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 3569

```
      By Mr. THUNE (for himself and Mr. Casey):

[[Page S525]]

  S. 3569. A bill to extend the program to provide liability 
protections for volunteer practitioners at certain health centers; to 
the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the text of 
the bill be printed in the Record.
  There being no objection, the text of the bill was ordered to be 
printed in the Record, as follows:

                                S. 3569

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

       This Act may be cited as the ``Help Expand Liability 
     Protections for Volunteers Act'' or the ``HELP for Volunteers 
     Act''.

     SEC. 2. EXTENSION OF PROGRAM.

       Section 224(q) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 
     233(q)) is amended by striking paragraph (6).
                                 ______
```
