# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Wyden, Ron (W000779) [D-OR]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that all 
postcloture time with respect to all nominations on which cloture was 
invoked on February 2 and 3, 2022, be considered expired and that the 
confirmation votes be at a time to be determined by the majority leader 
in consultation with the Republican leader.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
