# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord of our destiny, whose commands enable us to experience rich and 
satisfying living, we thank You for today's National Prayer Breakfast 
where we were told to please You by doing justice, loving mercy, and 
walking with humility.
  Inspire our lawmakers to strive to meet these celestial expectations. 
Enlighten their hearts with Your wisdom that leads to fairness. Give 
them civility of demeanor and decisiveness of character that brings 
mercy. Clothe them with Your humility and provide them with the gift of 
Your peace as they seek to represent You.
  We pray in Your righteous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
