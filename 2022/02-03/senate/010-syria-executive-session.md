# Syria (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                                 Syria

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, earlier this morning, Americans learned 
that, thanks to the skill and bravery of our Armed Forces, we have 
taken off the battlefield the world leader of ISIS. I want to thank the 
incredible work of our brave military servicemembers who work day and 
night to keep Americans safe. So many have suffered at the brutal hand 
of ISIS.
  Senators will learn more about the operation later this morning 
during our classified, all-Senators briefing with Secretary Austin and 
other senior administration officials.
```
