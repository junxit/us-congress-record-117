# HONORING ROBERT C. WHITE

**Speaking:** Fleischmann, Charles J. "Chuck" (F000459) [R-TN]

```
                        HONORING ROBERT C. WHITE

  (Mr. FLEISCHMANN asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. FLEISCHMANN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor the service of 
Mr. Robert C. ``Bob'' White.
  For 11 years, Bob has served the people of Tennessee's Third 
Congressional District, first as my field representative, then as my 
deputy district director, and finally, as my senior adviser.
  Bob has been instrumental in helping myself and my office do the 
people's work in the House of Representatives.
  Bob retired last month from his career in public service. Throughout 
his tenure as a public servant, he ceaselessly showed what we are all 
here to do: to serve our constituents, those who have elected us to 
represent them in Congress.
  Madam Speaker, I thank Bob for his service, friendship, and devotion 
to our State and Nation. I wish him success in the next chapter of his 
illustrious career.

                          ____________________
```
