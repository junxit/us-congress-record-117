# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. MURPHY. Mr. President, I have two requests for committees to meet 
during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of the 
Majority and Minority Leaders.

[[Page S5112]]

  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:


           COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE, NUTRITION, AND FORESTRY

  The Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry is authorized 
to meet during the session of the Senate on Tuesday, September 27, 
2022, at 5:30 p.m., to conduct a nomination business meeting.


                 COMMITTEE ON RULES AND ADMINISTRATION

  The Committee on Rules and Administration is authorized to meet 
during the session of the Senate on Tuesday, September 27, 2022, at 4 
p.m., to conduct a business meeting.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader.

                          ____________________
```
