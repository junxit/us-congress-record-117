# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 2022-07-12

**Measures:** H.R. 6538, H.R. 7900, H.R. 8296, H.R. 8297

```
                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR TUESDAY,

                             JULY 12, 2022

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: to hold hearings 
to examine public transportation under the Bipartisan Infrastructure 
Law, focusing on an update from the Federal Transit Administration, 
10 a.m., SD-538.
  Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Subcommittee 
on Tourism, Trade, and Export Promotion, to hold hearings to examine 
the 2022 national travel and tourism strategy, 10 a.m., SR-253.
  Committee on Finance: to hold hearings to examine the nominations 
of Jay Curtis Shambaugh, of Maryland, to be an Under Secretary of 
the Treasury, and Rebecca Lee Haffajee, of Massachusetts, to be an 
Assistant Secretary of Health and Human Services, 10 a.m., SD-215.
  Committee on Foreign Relations: to hold hearings to examine 
instability and the state of democracy in the Sahel and the United 
States policy response, 10:30 a.m., SD-419/VTC.
  Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: to hold 
hearings to examine the nominations of Errol Rajesh Arthur, Kendra 
Davis Briggs, and Carl Ezekiel Ross, all of the District of 
Columbia, each to be an Associate Judge of the Superior Court of the 
District of Columbia, 10 a.m., SD-342.
  Committee on the Judiciary: to hold hearings to examine a post-Roe 
America, focusing on the legal consequences of the Dobbs decision, 
10 a.m., SD-106.
  Select Committee on Intelligence: to receive a closed briefing on 
certain intelligence matters, 2:30 p.m., SVC-217.
  United States Senate Caucus on International Narcotics Control: to 
hold hearings to examine the role of the Federal government in 
attacking the financial networks of cartels, 2 p.m., VTC.


                                 House

  Committee on Rules, Full Committee, hearing on H.R. 7900, the 
``National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal

[[Page D728]]

Year 2023''; H.R. 8296, the ``Women's Health Protection Act of 
2022''; H.R. 8297, the ``Ensuring Access to Abortion Act of 2022''; 
and H.R. 6538, the ``Active Shooter Alert Act of 2022'' [Rule Markup 
Only], 12 p.m., H-313 Capitol.
  Select Committee to Investigate the January 6th Attack on the 
United States Capitol, Full Committee, hearing entitled ``January 
6th Investigation'', 1 p.m., 390 Cannon and Webex.
```
