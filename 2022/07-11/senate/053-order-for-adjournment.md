# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Portman, Rob (P000449) [R-OH]

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the previous 
order following the remarks of Senator Portman.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Ohio.
  Mr. PORTMAN. First, I want to thank my colleague and cofounder and 
cochair of the Senate Ukraine Caucus for his comments today.
  What you just heard was that the White House has sent the application 
for membership to NATO for Finland and Sweden to the U.S. Senate. It 
now goes to our committee, the Committee on Foreign Relations, and 
then, I hope, to the floor soon. I suspect there will be an 
overwhelming vote because everybody in this Chamber understands the 
importance not just of NATO expansion but of these two countries in 
particular.
  I thank my colleague for offering those words today, and my hope is 
we can move very quickly on that.

                          ____________________
```
