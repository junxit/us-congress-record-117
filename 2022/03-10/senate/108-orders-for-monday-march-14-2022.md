# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 14, 2022

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 14, 2022

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
March 14, and that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and morning business be closed; that upon the conclusion of morning 
business, the Senate proceed to executive session and resume 
consideration of the Young nomination; further, that the cloture 
motions filed during today's session ripen at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
