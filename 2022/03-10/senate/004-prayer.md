# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, as the war in Ukraine continues, we look to You. Lord, 
You have been our help in ages past. You are our hope for the years to 
come. You continue to be our shelter from the storms of evil that 
buffet our world. You cause the enemies of freedom to retreat.
  Today, execute judgment from Your throne as You use our Senators for 
Your glory. Lord, rule the nations with fairness, and continue to be 
our refuge in times of trouble.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
