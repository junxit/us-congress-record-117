# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 718, Susan Tsui Grundmann, of 
     Virginia, to be a Member of the Federal Labor Relations 
     Authority for a term of five years expiring July 1, 2025.
         Charles E. Schumer, Brian Schatz, Jack Reed, Angus S. 
           King, Jr., Elizabeth Warren, Chris Van Hollen, Raphael 
           G. Warnock, Jacky Rosen, Tim Kaine, Patty Murray, 
           Margaret Wood Hassan, Tammy Duckworth, Alex Padilla, 
           Tammy Baldwin, Christopher A. Coons, Gary C. Peters.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Finally, I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory 
quorum calls for the cloture motions filed today, March 10, be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
