# HONORING PETER STEC FOR ACHIEVING SCOUTING'S HIGHEST AND MOST PRESTIGIOUS RANK, EAGLE SCOUT

**Speaking:** Stefanik, Elise M. (S001196) [R-NY]

```
     HONORING PETER STEC FOR ACHIEVING SCOUTING'S HIGHEST AND MOST 
                     PRESTIGIOUS RANK, EAGLE SCOUT

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 18, 2022

  Ms. STEFANIK. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Peter Stec for his 
exceptional community service that has earned him the rank of Eagle 
Scout.
  Peter has been involved with the Scouts for many years and, through 
perseverance and hard work, has become the Senior Patrol Leader of Boy 
Scouts of America Troop 6016 in Queensbury, New York. Peter has 
dedicated his time in the Scouts to the well-being of the community of 
Queensbury, culminating in his Eagle Scout community service project.
  For his Eagle Scout project, Peter created a COVID-19 memorial garden 
recognizing Warren County residents who passed away during the 
pandemic. Peter built benches and planted trees and shrubs along the 
Warren County bike path in Queensbury. The COVID-19 memorial garden 
creates a space for Warren County residents to sit and reflect on those 
they have loved and lost. Peter's project exemplifies his deep 
commitment to improving his community.
  Peter's dedication to his community is evident not only in his Eagle 
Scout achievement, but also in his engagement in music, arts, and 
school. Peter is an exemplary student who is also an accomplished 
musician in his school's jazz band and has had his artwork displayed in 
the Saratoga National Racing Museum.
  Rising through the ranks to Eagle Scout requires dedication and 
perseverance, and Peter stands as an exemplar of that fact. His work is 
a strong indication of his exceptional leadership skills and commitment 
to service.
  On behalf of New York's 21st Congressional District, I would like to 
thank Peter for his contributions to the community and congratulate him 
on achieving the rank of Eagle Scout. It is so important, now more than 
ever, that our youth learn the values of service to the community and 
nation. I am excited to see where Peter's bright future takes him.

                          ____________________
```
