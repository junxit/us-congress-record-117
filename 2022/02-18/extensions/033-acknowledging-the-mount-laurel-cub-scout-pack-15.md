# ACKNOWLEDGING THE MOUNT LAUREL CUB SCOUT PACK 15

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
            ACKNOWLEDGING THE MOUNT LAUREL CUB SCOUT PACK 15

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY KIM

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 18, 2022

  Mr. KIM of New Jersey. Madam Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge the 
hard work and dedication shown by the Mount Laurel Cub Scout Pack 15.
  During the holiday season, Pack 15 partnered with BookSmiles, a 
501(c)(3) organization that collects and distributes books throughout 
New Jersey and the Greater Philadelphia area and have been serving our 
communities since September 2017. As a result of their efforts, Pack 15 
collected more than 7,000 books--the largest single day book collection 
in BookSmiles's history.
  I commend every member of Cub Scout Pack 15, their leaders Jeffrey A. 
Shaman and Daniel Pelaez, and the entire team at BookSmiles for 
accomplishing something that will benefit children, students, teachers, 
and parents throughout our community.
```
