# IN RECOGNITION OF LETRENT'S PHARMACY

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                  IN RECOGNITION OF LETRENT'S PHARMACY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 18, 2022

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Letrent's Pharmacy as they celebrate their 50th anniversary of serving 
the people of Bedford, Pennsylvania. For 50 years, Letrent's Pharmacy 
has served the people in that community with the utmost care and 
consideration. The pharmacy has been a family owned and operated 
business since Richard Letrent opened the pharmacy in 1972 after 
spending 5 years in another location not too far from his current 
flagship store.
  Richard, his wife Connie and their daughter, Connie are all proud 
alumni of Duquesne University in Pittsburgh, and their tight-knit 
family make up three of the five practicing pharmacists at Letrent's.
  Throughout the time they have operated the pharmacy, the Letrent's 
have dedicated themselves to ensuring the health and wellness of the 
Bedford County community.
  On behalf of the people of Pennsylvania's 13th Congressional District 
it is my honor to wish the Letrent family a happy 50th anniversary, and 
many, many more to come. Congratulations on this incredible milestone.

                          ____________________
```
