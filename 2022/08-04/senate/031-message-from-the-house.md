# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 5093

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 1:58 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 5093. An act to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to 
     transfer certain National Forest System land in the State of 
     Washington to Skamania County, Washington.

  The message further announced that pursuant to section 1094(c)(5)(A) 
of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2022 (Public 
Law 117-81), and the order of the House of January 4, 2021, the Speaker 
and Majority Leader of the Senate jointly appoint the following 
individual to the Afghanistan War Commission as co-chair: Ms. Shamila 
Chaudhary of Washington, DC.

                          ____________________
```
