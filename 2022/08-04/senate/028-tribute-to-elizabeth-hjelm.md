# TRIBUTE TO ELIZABETH HJELM

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO ELIZABETH HJELM

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Elizabeth Hjelm, 
an intern in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard work she 
has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Elizabeth is a graduate of Viborg-Hurley High School in Viborg, SD. 
Currently, she is attending Wayne State College in Wayne, NE, where she 
is pursuing a degree in communications studies. She is a hard worker 
who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Elizabeth for all of 
the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years 
to come.●

                          ____________________
```
