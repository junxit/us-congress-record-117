# Vote on Stark Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        Vote on Stark Nomination

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The question is, Will the Senate advise 
and consent to the Stark nomination?
  Mr. HEINRICH. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there a sufficient question?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.

is necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Wyoming (Mr. Barrasso), the Senator from Missouri (Mr. Blunt), and 
the Senator from South Dakota (Mr. Rounds).
```
