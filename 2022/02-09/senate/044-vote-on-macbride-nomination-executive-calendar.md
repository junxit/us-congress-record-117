# Vote on MacBride Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Warren, Elizabeth (W000817) [D-MA], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Vote on MacBride Nomination

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the MacBride nomination?
  Ms. WARREN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from New Mexico (Mr. 
Heinrich), the Senator from New Mexico (Mr. Lujan), and the Senator 
from Vermont (Mr. Sanders) are necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Wyoming (Mr. Barrasso), the Senator from Missouri (Mr. Blunt), and 
the Senator from South Dakota (Mr. Rounds).
  The result was announced--yeas 61, nays 33, as follows:
```
