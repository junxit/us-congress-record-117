           By Mr. RUBIO (for himself and Mr. Scott of Florida):
       S. Res. 508. A resolution honoring the memories of the 
     victims of the senseless attack at Marjory Stoneman Douglas

     New England Patriots for 20 incredible seasons, and briefly 
     the Tampa Bay Buccaneers for 2 seasons, for a combined 22 
     seasons in the National Football League; to the Committee on 
     Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
