# Vote on Baker Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```

consent to the Baker nomination?
  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. I ask for the yeas and nays.

  There appears to be a sufficient second.

is necessarily absent.
```
