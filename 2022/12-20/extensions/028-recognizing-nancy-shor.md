# RECOGNIZING NANCY SHOR

**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

```
                         RECOGNIZING NANCY SHOR

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 20, 2022

  Mrs. CAROLYN B. MALONEY of New York. Madam Speaker, I rise to pay 
tribute to Nancy Shor in recognition of her receipt of the Silver 
Shingle Award from her alma mater, Boston University School of Law.
  Ms. Shor graduated from Boston University School of Law in 1976 and 
was presented this award by the Dean on December 7, 2022, in honor of 
her 40 years of Public Service on behalf of dis.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Nancy 
Shor's dedicated years of service and in expressing my gratitude for 
her continued commitment to service.

                          ____________________
```
