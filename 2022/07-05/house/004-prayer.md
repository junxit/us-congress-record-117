# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church, Washington Parish, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  O, God, the fountain of wisdom, whose will is good and gracious, and 
whose law is truth: We beseech You so to guide and bless our 
Representatives in Congress, that they may enact such laws as shall 
please You, to the glory of Your name and the welfare of this people.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
