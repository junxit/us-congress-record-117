# HONORING COMMUNITY CHAMPION PAUL NELSON

**Speaking:** Kelly, Mike (K000376) [R-PA]

```
                HONORING COMMUNITY CHAMPION PAUL NELSON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE KELLY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 25, 2022

  Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I would like to recognize 
one of my constituents from back home in Western Pennsylvania, Mr. Paul 
Nelson.
  Mr. Nelson's commitment to the Erie and surrounding communities has 
existed for decades. He began working at Waldameer Park and Water World 
at the age of 11 and took over the park in 1965. His loyalty and 
tireless efforts have positively impacted not only the people of Erie, 
but those who visit. Mr. Nelson is truly a Community Champion and 
should be honored with highest regard for his dedication and 
commitment.

                          ____________________
```
