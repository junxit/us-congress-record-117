# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6504

**Measures:** H.R. 6504

```
           By Mr. SAN NICOLAS:
       H.R. 6504.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause I provides Congress with the 
     power to ``lay and collect Taxes; Duties, Imposts, and 
     Excises'' in order to ``provide for the . . . general Welfare 
     of the United States''. And Article I, Section 8, clause 3 
     provides Congress with the power to ``regulate commerce with 
     foreign nations, and among the several states, and with the 
     Indian tribes.''
```
