# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-92. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Human Rights 
     and Human Relations Commission, relative to a resolution 
     supporting Postal Workers, Postal Unions, customers, 
     including small businesses, and all artists and community 
     organizations that understand the value of and need for a 
     universal affordable public postal service serving the needs 
     of the American people; to the Committee on Oversight and 
     Reform.
       PT-93. Also, a petition of the Iberia Parish Council, 
     relative to Resolution No. 2021-152, in support of resuming 
     Federal off-shore leasing in the Gulf of Mexico; to the 
     Committee on Natural Resources.
```
