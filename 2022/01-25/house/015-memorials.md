# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S. 3, H.Res. 158, H.Res. 198

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:


[[Page H302]]


  

       ML-135. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of Texas, relative to Senate Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 3, respectfully urging the Congress of the 
     United States to pass legislation that would grant licensing 
     authority for public school bus drivers to the states; to the 
     Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
       ML-136. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to House Resolution No. 198, 
     urging Congress to support legislation to strengthen the 
     Workforce Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC); to the Committee on 
     Ways and Means.
       ML-137. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to House Resolution No. 158, 
     urging the Congress of the United States to clarify its 
     position on the legality of marijuana under the Controlled 
     Substances Act of 1970; jointly to the Committees on Energy 
     and Commerce and the Judiciary.
```
