# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 9534

**Measures:** H.R. 9534

```
            By Mr. BEYER:
        H.R. 9534.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land and naval force, as enumerated in 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 15 of the United States 
     Constitution.
```
