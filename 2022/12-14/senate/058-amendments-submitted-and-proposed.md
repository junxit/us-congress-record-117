# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 6520. Mr. RUBIO (for himself and Mr. Scott of Florida) 
     submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by him to the 
     bill H.R. 7776, to provide for improvements to the rivers and 
     harbors of the United States, to provide for the conservation

     purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table.

     ordered to lie on the table.

     to require the Chief Operating Officer of each agency to 
     compile a list of unnecessary programs, and for other 
     purposes.
       SA 6525. Mr. SCHUMER (for Mr. Peters) proposed an amendment
```
