# ORANGE COUNTY DELEGATION HONORS DR. MARSHALL ROWEN

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
           ORANGE COUNTY DELEGATION HONORS DR. MARSHALL ROWEN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 28, 2022

  Mr. CORREA. Madam Speaker, I rise on behalf of the Orange County 
delegation, which includes Representatives Alan Lowenthal, Katie 
Porter, Michelle Steel, Young Kim, Mike Levin, and Linda Sanchez, to 
honor the career and service of Dr. Marshall Rowen.
  Dr. Rowen has recently reached the tremendous milestone of serving on 
the Children's Hospital of Orange County's (CHOC) Board of Directors 
for 50 years.
  For over 60 years, Dr. Rowen has lived in and served Orange County's 
most vulnerable children. He was the first Pediatric Radiologist in 
Orange County, and he was Medical Director of Radiology at both St. 
Joseph and CHOC for many decades.
  Dr. Rowen has also taught generations of doctors in Orange County. He 
was Clinical Professor of Radiology and Pediatrics at U.C. Irvine 
College of Medicine, served on the Executive Committee of the Faculty 
and was President of the Clinical Faculty Association. In addition to 
the Editorial Board of the Western Journal of Medicine, he published 
more than 35 Scientific articles, and lectured coast to coast on 
Imaging Center and Radiology Department Management.
  He is greatly respected as the previous President of Orange County, 
Southern California, and California Radiological Societies as well as 
The Hawaiian Society of Nuclear Medicine and Pacific Coast Pediatric 
Radiology Association.
  His compassion and contribution to our community are extraordinary, 
and we are proud to recognize Dr. Marshall Rowen's service to Orange 
County's children.
```
