# 4-H FOUNDATION

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                             4-H FOUNDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 28, 2022

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, last week, I had the pleasure of 
attending the 2022 Cape May County Fair, which is a major fundraising 
effort for the 4-H Foundation, the largest youth development program in 
America. The 4-H Foundation in South Jersey supports young people from 
elementary school through high school by providing programs that are 
designed to shape leadership and innovation skills. This foundation 
collects donations and contributions, as well as operates and 
supervises various fundraising and business enterprises. The 4-H 
Foundation truly creates wonderful opportunities and community-wide 
benefits for our nation's youth. Everyone involved in this organization 
should be proud of their contributions to South Jersey, and it was my 
honor to attend the Cape May County 4-H Fair. God Bless the 4-H 
Foundation, and God Bless our America.

                          ____________________
```
