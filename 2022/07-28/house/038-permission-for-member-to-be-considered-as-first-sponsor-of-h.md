# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 4720

**Speaking:** Schweikert, David (S001183) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 4720

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 4720

  Mr. SCHWEIKERT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 4720, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Reed of New York, for the 
purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.

[[Page H7402]]

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Arizona?
  There was no objection.
```
