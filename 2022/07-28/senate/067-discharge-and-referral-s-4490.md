# DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL-- S. 4490

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 4490

```
                        DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL--
                                S. 4490

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions Committee be discharged from 
further consideration of S. 4490, a bill to promote digital citizenship 
and media literacy, and the bill be transferred to the Committee on 
Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
