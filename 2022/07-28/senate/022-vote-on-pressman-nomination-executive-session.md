# Vote on Pressman Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Hassan, Margaret Wood (H001076) [D-NH], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Vote on Pressman Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Pressman nomination?
  Ms. HASSAN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. I announce that the Senator from Illinois (Ms. 
Duckworth), the Senator from Illinois (Mr. Durbin), the Senator from 
Vermont (Mr. Leahy), the Senator from West Virginia (Mr. Manchin), and 
the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Sanders) are necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from North Carolina (Mr. Burr), the Senator from Oklahoma (Mr. Inhofe), 
the Senator from Alaska (Ms. Murkowski), and the Senator from Nebraska 
(Mr. Sasse).
  The result was announced--yeas 61, nays 30, as follows:
```
