# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7502

**Measures:** H.R. 7502

```
            By Mr. BOWMAN:
        H.R. 7502.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article 1 Section 8 of the Constitution gives Congress the 
     power to lay and collect taxes to provide for the general 
     welfare of the United States.
```
