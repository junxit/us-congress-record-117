# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 870

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the provisions
```
