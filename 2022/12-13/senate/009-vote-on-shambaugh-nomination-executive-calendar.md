# Vote on Shambaugh Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA], Scott, Tim (S001184) [R-SC]

```
                      Vote on Shambaugh Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Shambaugh nomination?
  Mr. CARDIN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
```
