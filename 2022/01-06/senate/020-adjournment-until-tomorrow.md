# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Cortez Masto, Catherine (C001113) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 485

```
                       ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

  Ms. CORTEZ MASTO. If there is no further business to come before the 
Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the previous order, under 
the provisions of S. Res. 485.
  There being no objection, pursuant to S. Res. 485, as a further mark 
of respect to the late Harry Mason Reid, Jr., former Senator from 
Nevada, the Senate, at 3:10 p.m., adjourned until Friday, January 7, 
2022, at 12 noon.
```
