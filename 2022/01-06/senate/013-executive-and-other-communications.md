                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communications were laid before the Senate, together

indicated:

        EC-2869. A communication from the Associate General 
     Counsel, Department of Agriculture, transmitting, pursuant to

     Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry.

     entitled ``Pyflubumide; Pesticide Tolerances'' (FRL No. 9282-

     Senate on December 15, 2021; to the Committee on Agriculture, 
     Nutrition, and Forestry.
        EC-2871. A communication from the Congressional Review 
     Coordinator, Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service, 
     Department of Agriculture, transmitting, pursuant to law, the 
     report of a rule entitled ``Importation of Sheep, Goats, and 
     Certain Other Ruminants'' ((RIN0579-AD10) (Docket No. APHIS-
     2009-0095)) received in the Office of the President of the 
     Senate on December 15, 2021; to the Committee on Agriculture, 
     Nutrition, and Forestry.

        EC-2874. A communication from the Alternate Federal

        EC-2878. A communication from the Director of Legislative 
     Affairs, Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, transmitting,
