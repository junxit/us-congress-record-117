# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The President pro tempore offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Give us the wisdom, the strength, and the faith to trust in You in 
all things and above all things.
  Let us pray for this great country--a great country, even when its 
democracy is threatened. Let us pray that we keep it together.
  In Your Name we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
