# RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

**Speaking:** Hirono, Mazie K. (H001042) [D-HI], McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                       RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the leadership 
time is reserved.
  The Senator from Hawaii.
  Ms. HIRONO. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Ms. Hirono). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
