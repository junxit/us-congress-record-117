# Vote on the Wang Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** King, Angus S., Jr. (K000383) [I-ME], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Vote on the Wang Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Maine.
  Mr. KING. I ask that the scheduled vote occur immediately.

[[Page S3349]]

consent to the Wang nomination?
```
