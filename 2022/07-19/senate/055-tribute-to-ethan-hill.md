# TRIBUTE TO ETHAN HILL

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ETHAN HILL

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Ethan for his hard work as an 
intern in the Senate Republican conference. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Ethan is a native of North Carolina. He attends Aurora University, 
where he studies political science and communications. He has 
demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made him an invaluable 
asset to our office. The quality of his work is reflected in his great 
efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Ethan for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
