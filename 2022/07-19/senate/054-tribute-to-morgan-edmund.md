# TRIBUTE TO MORGAN EDMUND

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MORGAN EDMUND

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Morgan for her hard work as 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office. I recognize her efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Morgan is a native of Nebraska. She attends the University of 
Wyoming, where she studies history. She has demonstrated a strong work 
ethic, which has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of her work is reflected in her great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I want to thank Morgan for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.●

                          ____________________
```
