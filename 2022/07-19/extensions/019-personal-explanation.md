# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 7900

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 19, 2022

  Ms. CRAIG. Madam Speaker, I intended to vote Yea on Roll Call No. 
320, Mr. Foster's amendment (No. 18) to H.R. 7900, the National Defense 
Authorization Act, which repeals the restriction on funding for the 
Preparatory Commission for the Comprehensive Nuclear--Test-Ban Treaty 
Organization. I inadvertently voted Nay when it was my sincere 
intention to vote Yea. Mr. Foster's amendment is a welcome addition to 
this bill and it has my support.
```
