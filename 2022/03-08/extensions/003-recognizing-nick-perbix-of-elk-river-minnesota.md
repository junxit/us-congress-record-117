# RECOGNIZING NICK PERBIX OF ELK RIVER, MINNESOTA

**Speaking:** Emmer, Tom (E000294) [R-MN]

```
            RECOGNIZING NICK PERBIX OF ELK RIVER, MINNESOTA

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. TOM EMMER

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 8, 2022

  Mr. EMMER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Nick Perbix of 
Elk River, Minnesota, for his participation in the 2022 Winter 
Olympics. Nick represented the United States as a defenseman on our 
men's ice hockey team. He carries on a legacy of talented Minnesotans 
who share this sport with the world.
  I am proud to count such an amazing athlete among my constituents in 
the Sixth District. I congratulate Nick and thank him for representing 
our great state and country at the 2022 Winter Olympics.

                          ____________________
```
