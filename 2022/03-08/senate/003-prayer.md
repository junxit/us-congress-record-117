# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, Your majestic Name fills the Earth. Nature testifies to 
Your might, wisdom, and love. Lord, Your glory is higher than the 
Heavens.
  As our lawmakers depend upon Your strength to silence the enemies of 
freedom, deliver us all from evil. Defend the righteous who cry to You 
even in the night seasons. Send Your angels to protect those who stand 
up against the fury of their enemies. Arise, O Lord. Wake up, and bring 
justice to our Nation and world.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
