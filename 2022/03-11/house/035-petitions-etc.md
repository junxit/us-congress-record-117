# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.R. 235

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-104. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the New York 
     City Council, relative to Resolution No. 1222, calling upon 
     the United States Congress to pass and the President to sign 
     the Green New Deal for Public Housing Act; which was referred 
     to the Committee on Financial Services.
       PT-105. Also, a petition of the New York City Council, 
     relative to Resolution No 1201-A, calling on Congress to pass 
     and the President to sign, H.R. 235, the Public Housing 
     Emergency Response Act; which was referred to the Committee 
     on Financial Services.
```
