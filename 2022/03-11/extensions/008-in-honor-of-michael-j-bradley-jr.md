# IN HONOR OF MICHAEL J. BRADLEY JR.

**Speaking:** Boyle, Brendan F. (B001296) [D-PA]

```
                   IN HONOR OF MICHAEL J. BRADLEY JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRENDAN F. BOYLE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 11, 2022

  Mr. BRENDAN F. BOYLE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, on behalf of the 
United States House of Representatives, I recognize and celebrate 
Michael J. Bradley Jr., on the occasion of his service as Grand Marshal 
of the Philadelphia St. Patrick's Day Parade.
  The United States Congress is privileged to honor individuals who 
work to enrich Philadelphia's communities, and is proud to honor our 
Irish and Irish-American communities as we celebrate St. Patrick's Day 
at one of the oldest and largest St. Patrick's Day parades in our 
country.
  Now, therefore, Congressman Brendan F. Boyle, on behalf of the United 
States House of Representatives, offers his best wishes and 
appreciation to Michael J. Bradley, Jr., as he leads the Philadelphia 
St. Patrick's Day Parade as Grand Marshal, and issues this proclamation 
on Sunday, March 13, 2022.

                          ____________________
```
