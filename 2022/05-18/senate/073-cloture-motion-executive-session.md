# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 792, Kenneth L. Wainstein, of 
     Virginia, to be Under Secretary for Intelligence and 
     Analysis, Department of Homeland Security.
         Charles E. Schumer, Tina Smith, Christopher Murphy, Tim 
           Kaine, Patrick J. Leahy, Jack Reed, Benjamin L. Cardin, 
           Richard J. Durbin, Brian Schatz, Jacky Rosen, Catherine 
           Cortez Masto, Margaret Wood Hassan, Martin Heinrich, 
           Sheldon Whitehouse, Richard Blumenthal, Christopher A. 
           Coons, Tammy Baldwin.

                          ____________________
```
