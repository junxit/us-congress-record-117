# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 675, Todd M. Harper, of Virginia, 
     to be a Member of the National Credit Union Administration 
     Board for a term expiring April 10, 2027 (Reappointment).
         Charles E. Schumer, Christopher Murphy, Tina Smith, 
           Robert Menendez, Christopher A. Coons, Michael F. 
           Bennet, Robert P. Casey, Jr., Patty Murray, Jack Reed, 
           Sheldon Whitehouse, Benjamin L. Cardin, Elizabeth 
           Warren, Tim Kaine, Tammy Duckworth, Debbie Stabenow, 
           Edward J. Markey.
```
