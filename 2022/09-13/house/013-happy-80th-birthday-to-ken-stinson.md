# HAPPY 80TH BIRTHDAY TO KEN STINSON

**Speaking:** Flood, Mike (F000474) [R-NE]

```
                   HAPPY 80TH BIRTHDAY TO KEN STINSON

  (Mr. FLOOD asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to wish Ken Stinson from Omaha, 
Nebraska, a happy 80th birthday.
  A Notre Dame grad, a degree in civil engineering, two tours in 
Vietnam, a master's degree in civil engineering from Stanford, he had a 
wonderful career with Peter Kiewit Sons in Omaha, one of the Nation's 
largest general contractors.
  He is an accomplished engineer credited with the McHenry Tube Tunnel 
in the Baltimore Harbor, which more than 43 million vehicles pass 
through annually.
  He and his wife, Ann, live in Omaha with six children. He has changed 
lives in the State of Nebraska through his public service and through 
his philanthropy. Nebraska and America are a better place because of 
it.
  On behalf of the people of the First District of Nebraska, all 
Nebraskans, and our Nation, a very happy birthday to Ken Stinson.

                          ____________________
```
