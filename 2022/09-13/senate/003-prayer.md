# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Our Father and our God, in the silence of this moment, we feel 
gratitude for the privilege of approaching Your throne of grace.
  Lord, we come to You aware that You know us better than we know 
ourselves. We are grateful that You know what we need even before we 
ask. Forgive our mistakes and the wrong we have done.
  Lord, You know that, at times, we have been difficult to live with. 
Enliven our lawmakers. Let Your power come among them, as You direct 
them according to Your will. Remind them that their lives, Nation, and 
world are in Your hands.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
