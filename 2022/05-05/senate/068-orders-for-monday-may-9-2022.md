# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 9, 2022

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

```
                     ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 9, 2022

  Mr. OSSOFF. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m. on Monday, 
May 9; that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and morning 
business be closed; that upon the conclusion of morning business, the 
Senate proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the 
Phillips nomination; further, that the cloture motions filed during 
today's session of the Senate ripen at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
