# APPOINTMENT OF SENATOR LEAHY TO READ WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

```
   APPOINTMENT OF SENATOR LEAHY TO READ WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to the order of the Senate of January 24, 1901, as modified 
today, appoints the Senator from Vermont, Mr. Leahy, to read 
Washington's Farewell Address on Monday, February 28, 2022.
```
