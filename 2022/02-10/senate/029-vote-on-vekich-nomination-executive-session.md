# Vote on Vekich Nomination (Executive Session)

```
                       Vote on Vekich Nomination

  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Vekich 
nomination?

[[Page S640]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?

from Wyoming (Mr. Barrasso), the Senator from Missouri (Mr. Blunt), the 
Senator from North Carolina (Mr. Burr), and the Senator from Tennessee
```
