# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, the center of our hope, open our hearts to Your 
movement in our midst.
  As we trust Your prevailing providence, and cling to Your promises, 
provide us wisdom and spiritual discernment to see You at work.
  Lord, save our lawmakers from being intimidated by today's challenges 
as You protect them by guiding their steps.
  Clothe our Senators with the honor of integrity. Shield them with 
Your truth. And guide them with Your power. Give them a hunger for Your 
Word and a desire to apply Your knowledge in their daily walk, pleasing 
You by living with humility, honesty, and joy.
  We pray in Your precious Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
