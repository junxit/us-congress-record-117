# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Pastor Zack Randles, Waterfront Church, Washington, D.C., offered the 
following prayer:
  God of Heaven, we come before You today seeking wisdom and guidance 
in how we should lead. My heart is drawn to the prayer of David in the 
25th Psalm, and I bring it to Your ear once again:
  ``No one whose hope is in You will ever be put to shame, but they 
will be put to shame who are treacherous without excuse. Show me Your 
ways, O Lord, teach me Your paths; guide me in Your truth and teach me, 
for You are God my Savior, and my hope is in You all day long.''
  There is no part of our path that is a surprise to You. There is no 
treachery that is beyond Your sight. Share with our leaders Your 
unmatched wisdom, and let their actions today bless the world.
  I ask all this in the mighty name of Jesus.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
