  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
consider the follow nominations: Calendar Nos. 1078 through 1093 and 
all nominations on the Secretary's Desk in the Air Force, Army, Marine 
Corps, Navy, and Space Force; that the nominations be confirmed en 
bloc; that the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions 
be in order to any of the nominations; and that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
