# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER OF THE HOUSE

```
    COMMUNICATION FROM THE CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Chief Administrative Officer of the House of 
Representatives:
         Office of the Chief Administrative Officer, House of 
           Representatives,
                                 Washington, DC, October 18, 2022.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that the Office of the Chief Administrative 
     Officer has been served with a third-party subpoena to 
     produce documents in the Superior Court of the District of 
     Columbia.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, the 
     CAO has determined that only partial compliance with the 
     subpoena is consistent with the privileges and rights of the 
     House.
           Sincerely,
                                               Catherine Szpindor,
     Chief Administrative Officer.

                          ____________________
```
