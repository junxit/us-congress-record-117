# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 100

```
                    ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly an 
enrolled joint resolution of the House of the following title, which 
was thereupon signed by the Speaker on Thursday, December, 1, 2022:

       H.J. Res. 100. To provide for a resolution with respect to 
     the unresolved disputes between certain railroads represented 
     by the National Carriers' Conference Committee of the 
     National Railway Labor Conference and certain of their 
     employees.
```
