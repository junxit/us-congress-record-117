# COMMUNICATION FROM BOARD MEMBER OF THE OFFICE OF CONGRESSIONAL ETHICS

```
 COMMUNICATION FROM BOARD MEMBER OF THE OFFICE OF CONGRESSIONAL ETHICS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Governing Board of the Office of Congressional 
Ethics:

                                  Flagstaff, AZ, December 2, 2022.
       Dear Speaker Pelosi: It has been an honor and a privilege 
     to serve on the Office of Congressional Ethics Board for the 
     last 14 years. It is time for me to step down. I would like 
     to be appointed to the alternate position at this time and 
     switch positions with Bill Luther who was recently appointed 
     to the Board.
       I have had the opportunity to serve with many extraordinary 
     members who recognized the appointment as an important and 
     non-partisan role to the Congress.
           Thank you,
     Karan L. English.

                          ____________________
```
