# REQUESTING THE SENATE TO RETURN TO THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES H.R. 3076

**Measures:** H.Res. 925, H.R. 3076

```
 REQUESTING THE SENATE TO RETURN TO THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES H.R. 
                                  3076

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair laid before the House a privileged 
resolution requesting return of official papers on H.R. 3076, and ask 
for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 925

       Resolved, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives 
     request the Senate to return to the House the bill (H.R. 
     3076) entitled ``To provide stability to and enhance the 
     services of the United States Postal Service, and for other 
     purposes.''.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the resolution was agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
