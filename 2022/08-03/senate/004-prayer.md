# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, our shield, look with favor upon our Senators today. Guide 
them around the obstacles that hinder their progress, uniting them for 
the common good of this great land. Lord, free them from anxiety and 
fear as they put their trust in You. Enable them to go from strength to 
strength, fulfilling Your purpose for their lives in this generation.
  Guide them to use their abilities to accomplish Your holy will. 
Striving to please You, empower them to stand for right and leave the 
consequences to You.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
