# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business, with Senators permitted 
to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each for debate only.
```
