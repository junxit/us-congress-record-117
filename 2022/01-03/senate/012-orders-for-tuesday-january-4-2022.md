# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JANUARY 4, 2022

**Speaking:** Baldwin, Tammy (B001230) [D-WI]

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JANUARY 4, 2022

  Ms. BALDWIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., Tuesday, 
January 4; that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day and 
morning business be closed; that upon the conclusion of morning 
business, the Senate proceed to executive session and resume 
consideration of the Witkowsky nomination; further, that at noon the 
Senate vote on confirmation of the Sanchez nomination, with all 
provisions remaining in effect; finally, that upon disposition of the 
Sanchez nomination, the Senate recess until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the 
weekly caucus meetings.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
