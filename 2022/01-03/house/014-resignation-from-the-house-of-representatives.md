# RESIGNATION FROM THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

```
             RESIGNATION FROM THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
resignation from the House of Representatives:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, January 1, 2022.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: I write to inform you that I have 
     notified California Governor Gavin Newsom of my resignation 
     from the U.S. House of Representatives, effective today at 
     11:59 PM EST.
       I has been the honor of my life to represent the people of 
     California's San Joaquin Valley

[[Page H7848]]

     for the last 19 years. Please let me know how I can be of 
     help during this transition.
           Sincerely,
                                                      Devin Nunes,
     Member of Congress.
                                  ____



                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, January 1, 2022.
     Hon. Gavin Newsom,
     Governor, State of California,
     Sacramento, CA.
       Dear Governor Newsom: I write to inform you that I am 
     resigning my position as the United States Congressman for 
     California's 22nd Congressional District, effective today at 
     11:59 PM EST.
       It has been the honor of my life to represent the people of 
     California's San Joaquin Valley for the last 19 years. Please 
     let me know how I can be of help during this transition.
           Sincerely,
                                                      Devin Nunes,
                                               Member of Congress.

     

                          ____________________
```
