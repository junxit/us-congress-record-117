# Remembering Madeleine K. Albright (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   Remembering Madeleine K. Albright

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I, along with others, had the 
opportunity to say goodbye to Madeleine Albright this morning--a truly 
remarkable life: first woman Secretary of State, a remarkable diplomat, 
and a friend. We had an opportunity to bond over an issue I am about to 
discuss: the people of Burma and the plight that they have had to 
endure over the years. Regretfully, as her life came to an end, things 
did not go in the right direction in Burma--at least not yet. I know 
she would not have given up hope; nor have I.
```
