# Remembering Madeleine K. Albright (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                   Remembering Madeleine K. Albright

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, earlier today, leaders past and present 
from around the world joined together to celebrate the life of 
Madeleine Albright. While Secretary Albright may have been small in 
stature, she was a titan in American history; she was one of a kind and 
first of a kind; a mentor; a natural leader as well as a mother and 
grandmother. Her brilliance and sharp wit made her a formidable 
presence on the world stage, and her story inspired women and girls all 
across the globe.
  Today was a wonderful celebration of her life. My thoughts are with 
Secretary Albright's family as our Nation remembers her life and her 
great legacy.
```
