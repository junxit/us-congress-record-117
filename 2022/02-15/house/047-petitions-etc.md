# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.R. 5502

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-97. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Council of 
     the County of Maui, relative to Resolution No. 22-5, FD1, 
     urging the United States Navy to permanently remove and 
     relocate the bulk fuel storage facility underground storage 
     tanks located at Kapukaki (Red Hill, O'Ahu); to the Committee 
     on Armed Services.
       PT-98. Also, a petition of the Board of Supervisors of the 
     City and County of San Francisco, relative to Resolution No. 
     554-21, urging Congress to pass H.R. 5502, the INFORM 
     Consumers Act, requiring online marketplaces to collect, 
     verify, and disclose certain information from high-volume, 
     third-party sellers, with no further amendments; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       PT-99. Also, a petition of the Board of Supervisors of the 
     City and County of San Francisco, relative to Resolution No. 
     573-21, urging the United States Senate to ratify the 
     Convention on the Elimination of all forms of Discrimination 
     Against Women (CEDAW); to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
```
