# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2021

```
Committee Meetings
REVIEW OF THE OFFICE OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL REPORT ON ``USDA 
OVERSIGHT OF CIVIL RIGHTS COMPLAINTS''
Committee on Agriculture: Subcommittee on Nutrition, Oversight, and 
Department Operations held a hearing entitled ``Review of the Office of 
the Inspector General Report on `USDA Oversight of Civil Rights 
Complaints' ''. Testimony was heard from Phyllis K. Fong, Inspector 
General, Office of Inspector General, Department of Agriculture.
BACK FROM THE BRINK: RESTORING BROWNFIELD SITES TO ECONOMIC ENGINES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Environment and 
Climate Change held a hearing entitled ``Back from the Brink: Restoring 
Brownfield Sites to Economic Engines''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.
RENEWED AMERICAN ENGAGEMENT WITH INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS: GOALS, 
PRIORITIES, AND SUCCESSES
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Subcommittee on International 
Development, International Organizations and Global Corporate Social 
Impact held a hearing entitled ``Renewed American Engagement with 
International Organizations: Goals, Priorities, and Successes''. 
Testimony was heard from Michele J. Sison, Assistant Secretary, Bureau 
of International Organization Affairs, Department of State.
CONCEALED CARRY-ONS: CONFRONTING THE SURGE IN FIREARMS AT TSA 
CHECKPOINTS
Committee on Homeland Security: Subcommittee on Transportation and 
Maritime Security held a hearing entitled ``Concealed Carry-Ons: 
Confronting the Surge in Firearms at TSA Checkpoints''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
IS THERE A DOCTOR IN THE HOUSE? THE ROLE OF IMMIGRANT PHYSICIANS IN THE 
U.S. HEALTHCARE SYSTEM
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Immigration and Citizenship 
held a hearing entitled ``Is There a Doctor in the House? The Role of 
Immigrant Physicians in the U.S. Healthcare System''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE FOR ALL ACT
Committee on Natural Resources: Full Committee held a hearing on H.R. 
2021, the ``Environmental Justice for All Act''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.

[[Page D176]]


STRENGTHENING THE U.S. MICROELECTRONICS WORKFORCE
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Subcommittee on Research 
and Technology held a hearing entitled ``Strengthening the U.S. 
Microelectronics Workforce''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.
THE INFRASTRUCTURE INVESTMENT AND JOBS ACT'S BENEFITS FOR SMALL 
BUSINESSES
Committee on Small Business: Subcommittee on Underserved, Agricultural, 
and Rural Development held a hearing entitled ``The Infrastructure 
Investment and Jobs Act's Benefits for Small Businesses''. Testimony 
was heard from public witnesses.
EXAMINING THE ECONOMIC IMPACT OF FEDERAL INFRASTRUCTURE INVESTMENT
Committee on Ways and Means: Subcommittee on Select Revenue Measures 
held a hearing entitled ``Examining the Economic Impact of Federal 
Infrastructure Investment''. Testimony was heard from Shawn Wilson, 
Secretary, Department of Transportation and Development, Louisiana; 
Victoria Sheehan, Commissioner, New Hampshire Department of 
Transportation; and public witnesses.
KEEPING THE LIGHTS ON: STRATEGIES FOR GRID RESILIENCE AND RELIABILITY
Select Committee on the Climate Crisis: Full Committee held a hearing 
entitled ``Keeping the Lights On: Strategies for Grid Resilience and 
Reliability''. Testimony was heard from Nancy Sutley, Senior Assistant 
General Manager of External and Regulatory Affairs and Chief 
Sustainability Officer, Los Angeles Department of Water and Power, 
California; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
