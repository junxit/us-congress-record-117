**Measures:** H.R. 3076

```

  Mr. SCHUMER. Now, first, Mr. President, I understand there is a bill 
at the desk that is due for a second reading.

second time.

       A bill (H.R. 3076) to provide stability to and enhance the

provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceeding.
```
