# RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

**Speaking:** Warnock, Raphael G. (W000790) [D-GA], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                       RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the leadership 
time is reserved.
  The Senator from Georgia.
  Mr. WARNOCK. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Warnock). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
