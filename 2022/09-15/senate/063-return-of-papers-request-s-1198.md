# RETURN OF PAPERS REQUEST--S. 1198

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 1198

```
                   RETURN OF PAPERS REQUEST--S. 1198

  Mr. SCHUMER. I ask unanimous consent that the Secretary of the Senate 
be authorized to request the House of Representatives to return the 
papers on S. 1198; further, upon receipt of the bill from the House, 
the Senate's actions on September 8, 2022, with respect to S. 1198, be 
vitiated; that the Senate then proceed to the immediate consideration 
of S. 1198; that the committee-reported substitute be withdrawn, the 
Tester substitute amendment at the desk be considered and agreed to; 
the bill, as amended, be considered read a third time and passed, and 
the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table, 
all without intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
