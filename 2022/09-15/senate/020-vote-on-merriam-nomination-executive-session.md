# Vote on Merriam Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       Vote on Merriam Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, all postcloture time 
is expired.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Merriam 
nomination?
  Mr. DURBIN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk called the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from North Dakota (Mr. Cramer), the Senator from Texas (Mr. Cruz), and 
the Senator from Kansas (Mr. Moran).
```
