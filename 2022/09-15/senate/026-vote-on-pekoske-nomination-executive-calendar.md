# Vote on Pekoske Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Hoeven, John (H001061) [R-ND], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       Vote on Pekoske Nomination

  Mr. HOEVEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the vote 
scheduled for 1:45 p.m. commence.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Pekoske 
nomination?
  Mr. HOEVEN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from North Dakota (Mr. Cramer), the Senator from Texas (Mr. Cruz), the 
Senator from Iowa (Ms. Ernst), the Senator from Kansas (Mr. Moran), and 
the Senator from South Carolina (Mr. Scott).
  The result was announced--yeas 77, nays 18, as follows:
```
