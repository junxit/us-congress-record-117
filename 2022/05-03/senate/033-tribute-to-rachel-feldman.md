# TRIBUTE TO RACHEL FELDMAN

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       TRIBUTE TO RACHEL FELDMAN

  ● Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Rachel for her hard work as 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office. I recognize her efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Rachel is a native of California. She is a student at UC Berkeley, 
where she is studying political science and legal studies. She has 
demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made her an invaluable 
asset to our office. The quality of her work is reflected in her great 
efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Rachel for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.●
```
