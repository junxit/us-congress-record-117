# Vote on Bhargava Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                      Vote on Bhargava Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from South Dakota.
  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, we yield back time, and I would ask for 
the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  All postcloture time is expired.
  Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays are ordered.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Bhargava 
nomination?
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Colorado (Mr. Bennet) 
and the Senator from New Hampshire (Mrs. Shaheen) are necessarily 
absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Alabama (Mr. Shelby) and the Senator from Alabama (Mr. 
Tuberville).
  The result was announced--yeas 62, nays 34, as follows:
```
