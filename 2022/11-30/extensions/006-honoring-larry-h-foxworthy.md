# HONORING LARRY H. FOXWORTHY

**Speaking:** Barr, Andy (B001282) [R-KY]

```
                      HONORING LARRY H. FOXWORTHY

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY BARR

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 30, 2022

  Mr. BARR. Madam Speaker, I rise to honor a special citizen of our 
great nation, Judge Larry H. Foxworthy. Mr. Foxworthy lives in Fleming 
County, where he and his wife are lifelong residents. He serves the 
people of that wonderful county as Judge-Executive.
  Judge Foxworthy has served in this important leadership role since 
2003. Since taking office, he has served on many important boards and 
commissions. These include Buffalo Trace Area Development District 
(Chairman), KCJEA Executive Board Member, Fleming County Board of 
Health, Fleming-Mason Airport Board of Directors, Fleming County 
Extension Service Board of Directors, Fleming County Hospital Board of 
Directors, Fleming County Industrial Authority Board of Directors, and 
the Kentucky Association of Counties Board of Directors.
  After nearly 20 years of service, Judge Foxworthy is retiring. During 
his time in office, there has been significant growth in business 
opportunities in Fleming County. The county has made needed updates to 
their book department and to the county's equipment. Judge Foxworthy 
found that working together as a team was important in the successful 
governance of Fleming County.
  It is my honor to recognize this amazing public servant and thank him 
for his many years of service to the citizens of Fleming County. Judge 
Foxworthy's leadership, his dedication, and his love of community are 
greatly appreciated.

                          ____________________
```
