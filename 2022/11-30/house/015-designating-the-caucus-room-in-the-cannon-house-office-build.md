# DESIGNATING THE CAUCUS ROOM IN THE CANNON HOUSE OFFICE BUILDING AS THE "SPEAKER NANCY PELOSI CAUCUS ROOM"

**Measures:** H.Res. 1495, H.Res. 1499

```
DESIGNATING THE CAUCUS ROOM IN THE CANNON HOUSE OFFICE BUILDING AS THE 
                  ``SPEAKER NANCY PELOSI CAUCUS ROOM''

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to House Resolution 1499, H. Res. 
1495 is considered as adopted.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 1495

       Resolved, That the caucus room in the Cannon House Office 
     Building (room 390) is designated as the ``Speaker Nancy 
     Pelosi Caucus Room''.

```
