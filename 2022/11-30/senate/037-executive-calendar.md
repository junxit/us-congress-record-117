# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to the consideration of the following nomination, which the 
clerk will report.

of the District of Columbia, to be Inspector General, Department of 
Defense.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. There will now be 2 minutes for debate, 
equally divided.
  Mr. CARDIN. I yield back our time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
