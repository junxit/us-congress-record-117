# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.J. RES. 100

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 100

```
               MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.J. RES. 100

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I understand there is a joint resolution 
at the desk, and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the resolution by title 
for the first time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (H.J. Res. 100) to provide for a 
     resolution with respect to the unresolved disputes between 
     certain railroads represented by the National Carriers' 
     Conference Committee of the National Railway Labor Conference 
     and certain of their employees.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I now ask for a second reading, and in 
order to place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule 
XIV, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
