# TRIBUTE TO DEPUTY GREG COUNTRYMAN, JR.

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

```
                 TRIBUTE TO DEPUTY GREG COUNTRYMAN, JR.

● Mr. OSSOFF. Mr. President, I rise to honor Muscogee County 
Sheriff Deputy Greg Countryman, Jr., for his heroic and livesaving 
service to the Chattahoochee River Valley.
  On May 16, 2022, emergency personnel received a harrowing call about 
two children who were unconscious and not breathing after being pulled 
from a swimming pool. Already on the way home from his shift, Deputy 
Countryman quickly sprang into action and was the first to arrive on 
the scene. He swiftly administered CPR and successfully helped 
resuscitate the children until EMS arrived. Deputy Countryman's swift 
response saved their lives.
  As Georgia's U.S. Senator, it is my honor to commend Muscogee County 
Sheriff's Deputy Greg Countryman, Jr., for his heroism and service to 
the Chattahoochee River Valley community.●
```
