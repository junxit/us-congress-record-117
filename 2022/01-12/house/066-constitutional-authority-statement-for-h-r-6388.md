# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6388

**Measures:** H.R. 6388

```
           By Mr. SAN NICOLAS:
       H.R. 6388.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 1 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``impose taxes and spend the money collected to pay 
     debts and provide for the ``common defence'' and ``general 
     welfare.''
```
