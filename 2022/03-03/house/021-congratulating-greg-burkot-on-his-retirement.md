# CONGRATULATING GREG BURKOT ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
              CONGRATULATING GREG BURKOT ON HIS RETIREMENT

  (Mr. GUTHRIE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the service of my 
friend and district office staffer Greg Burkot.
  A proud Bardstown resident, Greg served the Second District as a 
field representative from 2009 to 2021. He represented my office in 
Edmonson, Hart, LaRue, Meade, and Nelson Counties and was a key liaison 
to constituents in those communities.
  Greg and his wife, Paula, have two daughters, the apple of his eyes, 
Rebecca and Allyson, with their husbands, and their three 
grandchildren. They are also active in their community and church.
  I will miss Greg's welcoming personality, and I thank him for his 
service to the Second District and the Commonwealth.
  I wish Greg the best on his retirement, and I hope he and Paula enjoy 
this next chapter of their lives as parents and grandparents and as 
retirees.

                          ____________________
```
