# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-94. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Nye County 
     Board of County Commissioners, relative to Nye County 
     Resolution No. 2021-41, banning the use of Vaccine Passports 
     and Vaccine Mandates; which was referred to the Committee on 
     Energy and Commerce.
       PT-95. Also, a petition of the Nye County Board of County 
     Commissioners, relative to Nye County Resolution No. 2021-39, 
     calling on the State Legislature to immediately end the state 
     of emergency called by the governor for the covid-19 
     pandemic; which was referred to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
```
