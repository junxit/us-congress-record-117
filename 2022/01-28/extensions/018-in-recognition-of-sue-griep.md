# IN RECOGNITION OF SUE GRIEP

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                      IN RECOGNITION OF SUE GRIEP

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 28, 2022

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Sue Griep as the recipient of the Blair County Chamber's prestigious 
Leadership Blair County Fellow Award.
  This award was created in 1997 when the Leadership Blair County 
Steering Committee understood the need to recognize those who uphold 
their mission of leadership and stewardship towards the Blair County 
Community. While no small feat, Sue's efforts to benefit those around 
her have rippled through her neighbors, and her modesty only underlines 
the true selflessness of her dedication.
  On behalf of the people of the 13th Congressional District, I am 
grateful to the Blair County Chamber for their environment that fosters 
community growth and success, and for constituents like Sue for their 
tireless efforts.

                          ____________________
```
