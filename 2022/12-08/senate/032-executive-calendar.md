  Mr. CARDIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
consider the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 1285, 1286, 1287, and 
all nominations on the Secretary's desk in the Coast Guard and Foreign 
Service; that the nominations be confirmed en bloc; that the motions to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with no 
intervening action or debate; and that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
