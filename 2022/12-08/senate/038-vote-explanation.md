# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Rosen, Jacky (R000608) [D-NV]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Ms. ROSEN. Mr. President, on November 30, 2022, I missed rollcall 
vote No. 363 while attending a meeting and event at the Department of 
the Interior announcing the future designation of a new National 
Monument in Nevada. Had I been present, I would have voted yes on vote 
No. 363, cloture on the nomination of Camille L. Velez-Rive to be U.S. 
District Judge for the District of Puerto Rico.
```
