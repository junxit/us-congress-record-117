# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, in a divided nation and world, use our lawmakers to 
bring order from chaos, harmony from discord, and truth from falsehood. 
Remind them that You alone are the way, the truth, and the life in good 
or bad times.
  Lord, give our Senators wisdom to provide our Nation and world with 
exemplary models of excellence, integrity, and faithfulness. Keep them 
from stumbling or slipping as they face the precarious challenges of 
today and tomorrow. Lord, do for our legislators more than they can ask 
or imagine.
  And, Lord, continue to strengthen Ukraine.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
