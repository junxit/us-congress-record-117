# Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
            Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that all 
postcloture time on the Gordon nomination expire at 6 p.m. today and 
that upon disposition of that nomination, the Senate immediately vote 
on confirmation of Executive Calendar No. 809, the nomination of Philip 
Nathan Jefferson to be a Member of the Board of Governors of the 
Federal Reserve, with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Oregon.
```
