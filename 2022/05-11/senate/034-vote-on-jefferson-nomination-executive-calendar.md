# Vote on Jefferson Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Vote on Jefferson Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Jefferson nomination?
  Mr. CARDIN. Madam President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Delaware (Mr. Carper) is 
necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Missouri (Mr. Blunt).
  The result was announced--yeas 91, nays 7, as follows:
```
