# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 7691, H.R. 7791

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly an 
enrolled bill of the House of the following title, which was thereupon 
signed by the Speaker on Thursday, May 19, 2022:

       H.R. 7691. An act making emergency supplemental 
     appropriations for assistance for the situation in Ukraine 
     for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2022, and for other 
     purposes.

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, further reported and found 
truly an enrolled bill of the House of the following title, which was 
thereupon signed by the Speaker pro tempore, Mr. Connolly, on Thursday, 
May 19, 2022:

       H.R. 7791. An act to amend the Child Nutrition Act of 1966 
     to establish waiver authority to address certain emergencies, 
     disasters, and supply chain disruptions, and for other 
     purposes.

                          ____________________
```
