# CONGRATULATING THE WILLINGBORO HIGH SCHOOL BOYS TRACK TEAM

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
       CONGRATULATING THE WILLINGBORO HIGH SCHOOL BOYS TRACK TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY KIM

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 20, 2022

  Mr. KIM of New Jersey. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
the Willingboro High School Boys Track Team as they commemorate nearly 
50 years of athletic accomplishment with a celebratory parade.
  Since the 1970s, the high school's boys track team has been a shining 
example of athleticism, leadership, and teamwork for the community of 
Willingboro, New Jersey. The school's track program has produced some 
of the greatest runners in New Jersey Track and Field history, 
including Olympians Carol Lewis, Carl Lewis, and Lamont Smith.
  Today, the team is coached by Martin Booker, Aaydah Copprue-Worthy, 
Chuck James, and Robert Cornelius, whose leadership and guidance have 
upheld the team's impressive legacy and positively impacted the lives 
of so many student-athletes. The Willingboro Boys Track team continues 
its reputation of excellence as record holders for the most State 
Indoor Titles in New Jersey Public School history.
  I know I speak on behalf of the entire 3rd Congressional District 
when I extend my congratulations to the current and former Willingboro 
players and coaches for their impressive achievements and extraordinary 
legacy.

                          ____________________
```
