# HONORING ARMY VETERAN EDWARD A. FONTAINE

**Speaking:** Rosendale Sr., Matthew M. (R000103) [R-MT]

```
                HONORING ARMY VETERAN EDWARD A. FONTAINE

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. MATTHEW M. ROSENDALE, SR.

                               of montana

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 20, 2022

  Mr. ROSENDALE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Army veteran, 
Mr. Edward A. Fontaine, from Montana for being awarded the Bronze Star 
Medal with `V' device.
  The Bronze Star Medal with `V' device is awarded for distinguished 
acts of valor and heroism in active Federal military service. While 
serving in Company A, 2D Battalion, 502D Airborne Infantry Regiment, 
1st Brigade, 101st Airborne Division in the Republic of Vietnam, 
Specialist Four Fontaine demonstrated exceptional bravery and valorous 
action in combat as a machine gunner.
  On October 7, 1967, when a portion of the Company defensive perimeter 
was attacked at night, he responded immediately. Courageously running 
into enemy fire aimed at the crew, Specialist Four Fontaine opened fire 
with a M-60 machine gun at only 20 meters away from the enemy, which 
helped turn the momentum of the battle.
  Not only did Mr. Fontaine's conduct play a major part in securing 
victory against enemy forces, but it also contributed to saving 
countless lives of his comrades. Mr. Fontaine's valorous actions are a 
credit to him, Army Command, and the United States Armed Forces.
  I congratulate Edward on receiving this noble achievement, and thank 
him for his service to our country.

                          ____________________
```
