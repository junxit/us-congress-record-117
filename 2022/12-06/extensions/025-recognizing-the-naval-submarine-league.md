# RECOGNIZING THE NAVAL SUBMARINE LEAGUE

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                 RECOGNIZING THE NAVAL SUBMARINE LEAGUE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 6, 2022

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I am proud to congratulate 
the Naval Submarine League on their upcoming 40th Annual Symposium, and 
to thank them for their diligent work to support the men and women of 
the Silent Service.
  The Naval Submarine League was founded in 1982 as a non-profit 
organization for civilians, as well as active-duty, reserve, and 
retired military members who remain passionate about the importance of 
submarines for national security. Members work to promote education, 
awareness, and research to increase

[[Page E1225]]

the practical usage of submarines in today's world.
  As we mark the 40th year of the League's existence. we are reminded 
of the incredible devotion and commitment of our sailors who ply the 
waters of the globe.
  On behalf of the people of Pennsylvania's 13th District, I 
congratulate the Naval Submarine League on their accomplishments and 
efforts and join in celebration of their 40th symposium.

                          ____________________
```
