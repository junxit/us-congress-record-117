# RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES MARINE CORPS SECOND LIEUTENANT JOHN GRAHAM

**Speaking:** Simpson, Michael K. (S001148) [R-ID]

```
  RECOGNIZING UNITED STATES MARINE CORPS SECOND LIEUTENANT JOHN GRAHAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MICHAEL K. SIMPSON

                                of idaho

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 6, 2022

  Mr. SIMPSON. Madam Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate United 
States Marine Corps Second Lieutenant John Graham, who recently 
completed USMC Officer Candidate School at Quantico, Virginia. John was 
an exceptional member of my staff for the past two years before leaving 
for Quantico. While we were sad when he departed, we were extremely 
proud he was going to become a Marine Corps officer. We always want to 
support our United States Marine Corps and we know that John will be 
upholding their finest traditions.

                          ____________________
```
