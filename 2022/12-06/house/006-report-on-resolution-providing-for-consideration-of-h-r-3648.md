# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3648, EQUAL ACCESS TO GREEN CARDS FOR LEGAL EMPLOYMENT ACT OF 2022; PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 7946, VETERAN SERVICE RECOGNITION...

**Measures:** H.Res. 1508, H.R. 3648, H.R. 7946

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3648, EQUAL 
 ACCESS TO GREEN CARDS FOR LEGAL EMPLOYMENT ACT OF 2022; PROVIDING FOR 
 CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 7946, VETERAN SERVICE RECOGNITION ACT OF 2022; 
                         AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

  Mr. McGOVERN, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 117-590) on the resolution (H. Res. 1508) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3648) to amend the Immigration and 
Nationality Act to eliminate the per-country numerical limitation for 
employment-based immigrants, to increase the per-country numerical 
limitation for family-sponsored immigrants, and for other purposes; 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 7946) to provide benefits 
for noncitizen members of the Armed Forces, and for other purposes; and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
