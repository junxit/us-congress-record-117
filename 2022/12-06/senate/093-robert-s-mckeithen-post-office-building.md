# ROBERT S. McKEITHEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3175

```
                ROBERT S. McKEITHEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 3175) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 135 Main Street in Biloxi, Mississippi, as 
the ``Robert S. McKeithen Post Office Building'' was ordered to a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
