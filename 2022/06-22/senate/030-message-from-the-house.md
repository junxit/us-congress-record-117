# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3285, H.R. 7072, H.R. 7777

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:02 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3285. An act to amend gendered terms in Federal law 
     relating to the President and the President's spouse.
       H.R. 7072. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, to 
     modify delayed notice requirements, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 7777. An act to amend the Homeland Security Act of 
     2022 to authorize the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure 
     Security Agency to establish an industrial control systems 
     cybersecurity training initiative, and for other purposes.
```
