# Text of Senate Amendment 5109

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
  SA 5109. Mr. PAUL submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 5099 proposed by Mr. Schumer (for Mr. Murphy (for himself, 
Mr. Cornyn, Ms. Sinema, and Mr. Tillis)) to the bill S. 2938, to 
designate the United States Courthouse and Federal Building located at 
111 North Adams Street in Tallahassee, Florida, as the ``Joseph Woodrow 
Hatchett United States Courthouse and Federal Building'', and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        On page 28, line 11, insert after ``subsection (d)'' the 
     following: ``relating to a violent crime, such as assault, 
     murder, rape, domestic abuse, or animal cruelty, and 
     specified the violent crime''.
        On page 31, line 5, insert after ``922'' the following: 
     ``relating to a violent crime, such as assault, murder, rape, 
     domestic abuse, or animal cruelty, and specify the violent 
     crime''.
                                 ______
```
