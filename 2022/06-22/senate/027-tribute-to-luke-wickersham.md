# TRIBUTE TO LUKE WICKERSHAM

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO LUKE WICKERSHAM

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Luke Wickersham, 
an intern in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Luke is a graduate of Brandon High School in Brandon, SD. Currently, 
he is

[[Page S3069]]

attending the South Dakota School of Mines and Technology in Rapid 
City, SD, where he is pursuing a degree in electrical engineering. He 
is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of his 
internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Luke for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●
```
