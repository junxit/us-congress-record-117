# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 3285, H.R. 7072, H.R. 7777

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 3285. An act to amend gendered terms in Federal law 
     relating to the President and the President's spouse; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
       H.R. 7072. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, to 
     modify delayed notice requirements, and for other purposes; 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       H.R. 7777. An act to amend the Homeland Security Act of 
     2002 to authorize the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure 
     Security Agency to establish an industrial control systems 
     cybersecurity training initiative, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
```
