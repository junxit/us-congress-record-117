# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE BLAKE MOORE, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
    COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE BLAKE MOORE, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Blake Moore, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, December 22, 2022.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madame Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I, the Honorable Blake Moore, U.S. 
     Representative for the 1st congressional district of Utah, 
     have been served with a subpoena for documents issued by the 
     Third Judicial District Court, Salt Lake County, State of 
     Utah.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have not yet determined if compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                      Blake Moore,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
