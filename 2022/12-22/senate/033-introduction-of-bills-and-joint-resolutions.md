# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 5354, S. 5355, S. 5356, S. 5357

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mrs. GILLIBRAND:
       S. 5354. A bill to assess, prevent, prepare for, respond 
     to, recover, and mitigate biological threats by establishing 
     the One Health Security Council; to the Committee on Health, 
     Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. SCOTT of Florida (for himself and Mr. Rubio):
       S. 5355. A bill making emergency supplemental 
     appropriations for disaster relief for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2023, and for other purposes; read twice.
           By Mr. CASEY (for himself, Mr. Brown, and Mr. Manchin):
       S. 5356. A bill to improve compliance with mine safety and 
     health laws, empower miners to raise safety concerns, and 
     prevent future mine tragedies, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Ms. COLLINS (for herself, Mrs. Feinstein, Ms. Rosen, 
             Mr. Kelly, Ms. Hassan, and Mr. Warnock):
       S. 5357. A bill to provide compensation for United States 
     victims of Libyan state-sponsored terrorism, and for other 
     purposes; considered and passed.

                          ____________________
```
