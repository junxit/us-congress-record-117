     safety and save lives.

     Foundation, to amend the Marine Debris Act to improve the 
     administration of the Marine Debris Program of the National 
     Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, and for other 
     purposes.
       SA 6627. Mr. SCHUMER (for Mr. Manchin (for himself, Mr.
