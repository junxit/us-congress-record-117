# TRIBUTE TO KATE LUNDBERG

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO KATE LUNDBERG

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today, I recognize Kate Lundberg, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Kate is a recent graduate of George Washington University in 
Washington, DC, having earned degrees in international affairs and 
political science. This summer, Kate plans to continue serving the 
American people by working on Capitol Hill. She is a hard worker who 
has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Kate for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
