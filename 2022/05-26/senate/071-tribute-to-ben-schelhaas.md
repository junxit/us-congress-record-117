# TRIBUTE TO BEN SCHELHAAS

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO BEN SCHELHAAS

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today, I recognize Ben Schelhaas, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Ben is a recent graduate of the University of South Dakota in 
Vermillion, SD, having earned degrees in history and political science. 
This fall, Ben plans to pursue a master's degree in history at Arizona 
State University in Tempe, AZ. He is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Ben for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
