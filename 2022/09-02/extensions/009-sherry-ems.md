# SHERRY EMS

**Speaking:** Wenstrup, Brad R. (W000815) [R-OH]

```
                               SHERRY EMS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRAD R. WENSTRUP

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 2, 2022

  Mr. WENSTRUP. Madam Speaker, I rise to congratulate Sherry Ems for 
her dedication and service to our State, our Nation, and our 
servicemembers working as the Executive Director at the United Services 
Organizations Ohio these last seven years.
  For many service men and women, the USO offers the comforts of home: 
a place to rest or a meal to eat, as they ship off to another duty 
station, return from a long journey from a faraway base, or prepare to 
deploy. Sherry's service was a bright beacon of hope, integrity, and 
diligence.
  She is exemplary of the patriotism and commitment we associate with 
the USO. Over the many years she served, Sherry's contributions went a 
long way towards supporting our service men and women. She embodies the 
civic pride we have in our troops and our nation.
  I thank her for her tremendous work and service over the years at the 
USO and for our men and women in uniform, and we all wish her the best 
in her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
