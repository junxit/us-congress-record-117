# RECOGNIZING FIRE ENGINEER CURTIS TREVOR GOLD

**Speaking:** Torres, Norma J. (T000474) [D-CA]

```
              RECOGNIZING FIRE ENGINEER CURTIS TREVOR GOLD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NORMA J. TORRES

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 2, 2022

  Mrs. TORRES of California. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor 
Curtis Trevor Gold and his many years of dedication to the City of 
Ontario as an Ontario Fire Engineer.
  Curtis joined the Ontario Fire Department in 2016 as a firefighter. 
In 2019, he was promoted to fire engineer. During his time at the 
Department, he served as a member of the Urban Search & Rescue Team. 
This team involves technical rescue operations that requires 
extrication and medical stabilization of victims of various 
emergencies. As an experienced firefighter, Curtis also became a 
training instructor, mentoring younger firefighters. He was also a 
member of Ontario Professional Firefighters Local 1430.
  Sadly, our community is now in mourning. On Saturday, August 27th, 
Curtis tragically passed away in an off-duty accident. He is survived 
by his wife and family. I offer my deepest condolences on their loss.
  For his outstanding accomplishments, it is my honor to recognize 
Firefighter Curtis Trevor Gold. His years of civic service and 
commitment to the Department and the people of Ontario are worthy of 
the highest commendation. He will be missed.
```
