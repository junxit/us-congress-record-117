# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, offered the following 
prayer:
  Good and gracious God, today we experience so much division and 
distrust in our Nation. Unite us in Your one spirit. Heal our 
divisions. Give us a spirit of holy obedience and trust that, under the 
leaders of our Nation, that we may journey together to build up Your 
kingdom on this Earth and one day inherit the kingdom of eternity. We 
pray this in Your most holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
