# EXCHANGE OF LETTERS REGARDING H.R. 2415 AND H.R. 2641

**Speaking:** Grijalva, Raul M. (G000551) [D-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2415, H.R. 2641

```
         EXCHANGE OF LETTERS REGARDING H.R. 2415 AND H.R. 2641

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. RAUL M. GRIJALVA

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 15, 2022

  Mr. GRIJALVA. Madam Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
exchange of letters regarding H.R. 2415 and H.R 2641.

                                         House of Representatives,


                             Committee on energy and commerce,

                                Washington, DC, December 13, 2022.
     Hon. Raul M. Grijalva,
     Chairman, Committee on Natural Resources,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Chairman Grijalva: I write concerning H.R. 2415, the 
     ``Orphaned Well Cleanup and Jobs Act of 2021.'' This 
     legislation would amend section 349 of the Energy Policy Act 
     of 2005 to eliminate existing subsection (g) which authorized 
     a technical and financial assistant program at the Department 
     of Energy to help States remedy environmental problems caused 
     by orphaned or abandoned oil and gas wells on State or 
     private land which is within the jurisdiction of the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       In an effort to expedite the filing of the Natural 
     Resources bill report for legislative history purposes, I 
     agree to not seek a sequential referral at this time. The 
     Committee takes this action with our mutual understanding 
     that by forgoing a sequential referral request of H.R. 2415 
     at this time, we do not waive any jurisdiction over the 
     subject matter contained in this or similar legislation.
       Finally, I would appreciate your response to this letter 
     confirming this understanding and ask that a copy of our 
     exchange of letters on this matter be included in the 
     Congressional Record to memorialize our understanding.
           Sincerely,
                                               Frank Pallone, Jr.,
                                                         Chairman.
                                  ____
                                  
                                         House of Representatives,


                               Committee on Natural Resources,

                                Washington, DC, December 14, 2022.
     Hon. Frank Pallone, Jr.,
     Chairman, Committee on Energy and Commerce,
     House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Chairman Pallone: I write to you concerning H.R. 2641, 
     the ``Pacific Northwest Pumped Storage Hydropower Development 
     Act of 2021.''
       I acknowledge and appreciate that despite considering 
     seeking a sequential referral on this legislation, the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce decided not to do so when 
     the Committee on Natural Resources filed our committee report 
     on the bill.
       I thank you for your cooperation regarding this legislation 
     and look forward to continuing to work with you as this 
     measure moves through the legislative process. I will ensure 
     that our exchange of letters is included in the Congressional 
     Record.
           Sincerely,
                                                 Raul M. Grijalva,
                         Chair, House Natural Resources Committee.
```
