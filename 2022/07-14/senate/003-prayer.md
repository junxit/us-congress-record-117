# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we know that You exist. Every time we hear a baby cry or 
touch a leaf, we are reminded of Your presence in our world. Lord, 
continue to look with favor upon our Senators. Enable them to go from 
strength to strength as they strive to live in day-tight compartments. 
Guide them around the obstacles that hinder them from living for Your 
glory. As they strive to please You, empower them to stand for right 
and leave the consequences to You.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
