# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 1035, Nina Nin-Yuen Wang, of 
     Colorado, to be United States District Judge for the District 
     of Colorado.
          Richard J. Durbin, Robert P. Casey, Jr., Sherrod Brown, 
           Tammy Baldwin, Tina Smith, Jeanne Shaheen, Chris Van 
           Hollen, Elizabeth Warren, Catherine Cortez Masto, Tim 
           Kaine, Benjamin L. Cardin, Christopher Murphy, Maria 
           Cantwell, Christopher A. Coons, Jack Reed, Gary C. 
           Peters, Tammy Duckworth.

                          ____________________
```
