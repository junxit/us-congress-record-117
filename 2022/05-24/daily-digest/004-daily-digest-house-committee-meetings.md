# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2794

```
Committee Meetings
FISCAL YEAR 2023 MEMBER DAY HEARING
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, Education and Related Agencies held a hearing entitled 
``Fiscal Year 2023 Member Day Hearing''. Testimony was heard from 
Representatives Sherrill, Escobar, Garcia, and Schrier.
NATIONAL GUARD AND RESERVE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Defense held a hearing 
entitled ``National Guard and Reserve''. Testimony was heard from 
General Daniel R. Hokanson, Chief of the National Guard Bureau; 
Lieutenant General Jody J. Daniels, Chief of the U.S. Army Reserve; 
Vice Admiral John B. Mustin, Chief of the U.S. Navy Reserve; Lieutenant 
General David G. Bellon, Commander, U.S. Marine Forces Reserve; and 
Lieutenant General Richard W. Scobeee, Chief of the U.S. Air Force 
Reserve.
EXAMINING THE POLICIES AND PRIORITIES OF THE BUREAU OF INDIAN EDUCATION
Committee on Education and Labor: Subcommittee on Early Childhood, 
Elementary, and Secondary Education; and Subcommittee for Indigenous 
Peoples of the United States of the House Committee on Natural 
Resources began a joint hearing entitled ``Examining the Policies and 
Priorities of the Bureau of Indian Education''. Testimony was heard 
from Tony Dearman, Director, Bureau of Indian Education, Department of 
the Interior.
STRENGTHENING OUR COMMUNICATIONS NETWORKS: LEGISLATION TO CONNECT AND 
PROTECT
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Communications and 
Technology held a hearing entitled ``Strengthening our Communications 
Networks: Legislation to Connect and Protect''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
DIVERSITY INCLUDES DISABILITY: EXPLORING INEQUITIES IN FINANCIAL 
SERVICES FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES, INCLUDING THOSE NEWLY DISABLED 
DUE TO LONG-TERM COVID
Committee on Financial Services: Subcommittee on Diversity and 
Inclusion held a hearing entitled ``Diversity Includes Disability: 
Exploring Inequities in Financial Services for Persons with 
Disabilities, Including those Newly Disabled Due to Long-Term COVID''. 
Testimony was heard from Caroline Sullivan, Executive Director, North 
Carolina Business Committee for Education, Office of the Governor; and 
public witnesses.
BOUNDARY WATERS WILDERNESS PROTECTION AND POLLUTION PREVENTION ACT
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Energy and Mineral 
Resources held a hearing on H.R. 2794, the ``Boundary Waters Wilderness 
Protection and Pollution Prevention Act''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
