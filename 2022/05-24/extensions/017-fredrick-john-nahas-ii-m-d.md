# FREDRICK JOHN NAHAS II, M.D.

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                      FREDRICK JOHN NAHAS II, M.D.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 24, 2022

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, I would like to recognize the lifelong 
achievements of Fredrick John Nahas II. Dr. Nahas moved to Margate, 
South Jersey as a young boy and graduated from Atlantic City High 
School in 1964. In high school, he was a South Jersey swimming champion 
and was recruited to swim at the University of Pennsylvania, where he 
earned a bachelor's degree in electrical engineering. After graduating 
from University of Pennsylvania, Dr. Nahas followed in his father's 
footsteps and pursued his career as an electrical engineer working on 
the Apollo Moon Project in Florida. He then continued his education and 
graduated from Temple University School of Medicine in 1974. After 
years of hard work, Dr. Nahas opened his own practice in 1978 and 
became a board-certified surgeon in 1980. Since then, he has maintained 
certifications in both general and vascular surgery. Currently, he 
works at his solo practice in South Jersey and enjoys spending time 
with his six grandchildren. Dr. Nahas should be proud of all of his 
achievements, and it is my pleasure to recognize him. God Bless 
Fredrick, and God Bless our America.

                          ____________________
```
