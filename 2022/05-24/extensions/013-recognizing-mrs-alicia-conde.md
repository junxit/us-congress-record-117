# RECOGNIZING MRS. ALICIA CONDE

**Speaking:** Valadao, David G. (V000129) [R-CA]

```
                     RECOGNIZING MRS. ALICIA CONDE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 24, 2022

  Mr. VALADAO. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Mrs. Alicia Conde 
for her 26 years of service to the Shafter-Wasco Irrigation District.
  Since 1992, Mrs. Conde has served as a dedicated public servant, 
working tirelessly on behalf of the Shafter-Wasco Irrigation District. 
She has done critical work, such as overseeing the annual district crop 
survey program, managing the water billing and District Exempt Land 
Assessment tax installments, and maintaining the district's 
correspondence.
  For decades, Mrs. Conde has been the first point of contact for 
anybody with business in the district. She has long been an excellent 
representative of the district, and a welcoming presence for those 
coming into the office. She has built longstanding relationships with 
landowners in the area and guided many of them through difficult 
regulatory changes over her years of service.
  The loyalty and commitment to service by Mrs. Conde has been an 
inspiration to us all. I ask my colleagues in the House of 
Representatives to join me in recognizing Mrs. Alicia Conde for her 
decades of public service and contributions to the Central Valley 
community.

                          ____________________
```
