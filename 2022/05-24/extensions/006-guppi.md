# GUPPI

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                                 GUPPI

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 24, 2022

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, last week, I had the pleasure of 
attending the official storefront opening of Guppi in Wildwood, New 
Jersey. Driven by a young entrepreneur, Abby, Guppi aspires to reduce 
the plastics that pollute our oceans by creating eco-friendly apparel, 
bags, knick-knacks. Since launching her brand in 2018, Abby has created 
all her products by hand via screen-printing with ecofriendly inks and 
embroidering with threads made from recycled plastics. I am so 
impressed by Abby's work ethic, as well as her continued commitment to 
make our South Jersey community a safer and more eco-friendly 
environment. Abby truly exemplifies ambition, and I am excited to watch 
Guppi continue to grow. God bless Abby and all the other hardworking 
employees at Guppi, and God Bless our America.

                          ____________________
```
