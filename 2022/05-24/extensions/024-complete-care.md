# COMPLETE CARE

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                             COMPLETE CARE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 24, 2022

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, last week, I had the pleasure of 
attending the grand opening of the Complete Care Adult and Specialty 
Medical Professionals health center and corporate office in Bridgeton, 
South Jersey. The Complete Care Health Network began in 1973 as the 
Community Health Improvement Plan. Since then, Complete Care has grown 
to become the leading community health center in the state of New 
Jersey with a multitude of locations throughout Gloucester, Cumberland, 
and Cape May counties. The mission of the health center is to improve 
lives by providing convenient, comprehensive, high-quality healthcare 
to every community they serve. Complete Care Health Center truly does 
provide affordable and accessible care to the people of South Jersey. 
God Bless the employees and leaders of the Complete Care Health 
Network, and God Bless America.

                          ____________________
```
