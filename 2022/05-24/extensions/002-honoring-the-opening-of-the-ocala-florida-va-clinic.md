# HONORING THE OPENING OF THE OCALA, FLORIDA VA CLINIC

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
          HONORING THE OPENING OF THE OCALA, FLORIDA VA CLINIC

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 24, 2022

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Madam Speaker, we are proud to recognize the ribbon 
cutting and grand opening of the Ocala VA Clinic on June 22, 2022. This 
clinic is a tremendous triumph for our veteran community in North 
Central Florida and will serve thousands of veterans with excellent 
care. This project has been a long-time coming and I am pleased to see 
its completion. Congratulations to the North Florida/South Georgia 
Veterans Health System on another job well done on behalf of our 
Nation's heroes.

                          ____________________
```
