# RECOGNIZING CHIEF WES HENSLEY

**Speaking:** Valadao, David G. (V000129) [R-CA]

```
                     RECOGNIZING CHIEF WES HENSLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 24, 2022

  Mr. VALADAO. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Chief Wes 
Hensley on his retirement from the Tulare Police Department after 31 
years of service.
  Chief Hensley began his service to the Central Valley in 1984, 
starting as a police officer in the Visalia Police Department. 
Throughout his career, he has held multiple positions in different 
departments and sheriff's offices. Over his three decades of service to 
his community, Chief Hensley climbed the ranks, achieving the positions 
of Corporal, Sergeant, Captain, and finally Chief of Police.
  Under his leadership, Chief Hensley has led the Tulare Police 
Department through unprecedented challenges, maintaining the respect 
and credibility of the Tulare Police throughout the community. His 
presence at the helm of the Department has been critical in advancing 
the goals of his officers and ensuring the safety of the community for 
all.
  Chief Hensley's dedication, leadership, and commitment to excellence 
has been an incredible model for all those who will follow him. His 
presence will be truly missed.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the United States House of 
Representatives to join me in recognizing Chief Wes Hensley for his 
lifetime of service to the Central Valley and congratulating him on his 
recent retirement.
```
