# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:23 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that pursuant to 
section 1 of the Library of Congress Trust Fund Board Act (2 U.S.C. 
154), and the order of the House of January 4, 2021, the Speaker 
appoints the following individual on the part of the House of 
Representatives to the Library of Congress Trust Fund Board for a five-
year term, to fill the existing vacancy theron: Ms. Iris Weinshall of 
Brooklyn, New York.
```
