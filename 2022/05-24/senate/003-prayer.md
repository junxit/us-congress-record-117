# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O Lord, who governs the minds of all who are called to serve You and 
country, guide our lawmakers to fulfill Your purposes. When it is 
difficult to see the path ahead, give them the wisdom to trust the 
unfolding of Your powerful providence. Empower them to resist the sins 
that bring dishonor as they seek to live for Your glory.
  Lord, unite them to bear the burdens which are laid upon them during 
these challenging days. As they remember their accountability to You, 
inspire them to be good stewards of Your bounty. And, Lord, bless 
Ukraine.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
