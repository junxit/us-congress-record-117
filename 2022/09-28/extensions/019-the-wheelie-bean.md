# THE WHEELIE BEAN

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                            THE WHEELIE BEAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 28, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Wheelie 
Bean for receiving the Wheat Ridge Business Association's 2022 
Sustainability Green Business Award.
  The Sustainability Green Business Award is given to a business in 
Wheat Ridge with a strong commitment to protecting the environment to 
help end the climate crisis facing our planet. I thank the Wheelie Bean 
for their continued work to help create a greener, more sustainable 
Wheat Ridge.
```
