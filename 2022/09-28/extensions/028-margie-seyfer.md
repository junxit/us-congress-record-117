# MARGIE SEYFER

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                             MARGIE SEYFER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 28, 2022

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Margie 
Seyfer for receiving the Wheat Ridge Business Association's 2022 Member 
of the Year Award.
  The Member of the Year Award is given to an individual who 
exemplifies exceptional leadership and above all else, commitment to 
our community. Margie Seyfer has been an outstanding member of the 
Wheat Ridge Business Association, bringing her expertise and leadership 
to all that she does. In her tenure, she has worked in the capacity of 
membership, greeter's coordinator, speaker coordinator, and member 
welcome committee. Even after retirement, Ms. Seyfer has remained an 
active participant in our rich business community and has continued to 
remain an instrumental part of the Wheat Ridge Business Association.
  I thank Margie Seyfer for her service to the great City of Wheat 
Ridge.

                          ____________________
```
