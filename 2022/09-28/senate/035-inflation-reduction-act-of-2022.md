# INFLATION REDUCTION ACT OF 2022

**Speaking:** Cortez Masto, Catherine (C001113) [D-NV]

```
                    INFLATION REDUCTION ACT OF 2022

  Ms. CORTEZ MASTO. Mr. President, I rise today to clarify a colloquy 
between myself and Chairman Wyden placed in the Congressional Record on 
August 6, 2022. The statement in the Record references geothermal 
energy as applicable to the new ``section 48D'' of the Tax Code added 
by section 13702 of the Inflation Reduction Act. The statement should 
have referenced that section as ``section 48E.'' As explained in that 
colloquy, geothermal will qualify for the production and investment tax 
credits included in sections 13701 and 13702 of the Inflation Reduction 
Act.
```
