# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 29, 2022

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 6833

```
                ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 29, 2022

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Thursday, September 29, and that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the morning hour be

[[Page S5507]]

deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and morning business be closed; that upon the conclusion of morning 
business, the Senate resume consideration of Calendar No. 389, H.R. 
6833.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Now, Mr. President, before we adjourn, I just want to 
notify the Members that we expect to vote on the Freeman nomination 
before lunch.
```
