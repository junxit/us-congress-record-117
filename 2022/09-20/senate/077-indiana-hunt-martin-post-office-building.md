# INDIANA HUNT-MARTIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2142

```
                INDIANA HUNT-MARTIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 2142) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 170 Manhattan Avenue in Buffalo, New York, as 
the ``Indiana Hunt-Martin Post Office Building'', was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
