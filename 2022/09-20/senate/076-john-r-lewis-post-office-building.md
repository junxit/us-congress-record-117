# JOHN R. LEWIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                   JOHN R. LEWIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 5577) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 3900 Crown Road Southwest in Atlanta, 
Georgia, as the ``John R. Lewis Post Office Building'', was ordered to
```
