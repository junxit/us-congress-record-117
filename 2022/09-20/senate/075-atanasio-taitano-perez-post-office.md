# ATANASIO TAITANO PEREZ POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 3539

```
                   ATANASIO TAITANO PEREZ POST OFFICE

  A bill (H.R. 3539) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 223 West Chalan Santo Papa in Hagatna, Guam, 
as the ``Atanasio Taitano Perez Post Office'', was ordered to a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
