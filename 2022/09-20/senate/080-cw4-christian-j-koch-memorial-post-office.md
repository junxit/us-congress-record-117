# CW4 CHRISTIAN J. KOCH MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 3508

```
               CW4 CHRISTIAN J. KOCH MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

  A bill (H.R. 3508) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 39 West Main Street, in Honeoye Falls, New
```
