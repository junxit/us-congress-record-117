# SPECIALIST FOUR CHARLES JOHNSON POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 92

```
              SPECIALIST FOUR CHARLES JOHNSON POST OFFICE

  A bill (H.R. 92) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 110 Johnson Street in Pickens, South 
Carolina, as the ``Specialist Four Charles Johnson Post Office'', was 
ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
