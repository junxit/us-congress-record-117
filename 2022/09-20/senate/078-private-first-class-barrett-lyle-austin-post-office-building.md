# PRIVATE FIRST CLASS BARRETT LYLE AUSTIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 91

```
      PRIVATE FIRST CLASS BARRETT LYLE AUSTIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 91) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 810 South Pendleton Street in Easley, South 
Carolina, as the ``Private First Class Barrett Lyle Austin Post Office 
Building'', was ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, 
and passed.
```
