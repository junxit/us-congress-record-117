# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 7353

**Speaking:** Walberg, Tim (W000798) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 7353

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 7353

  Mr. WALBERG. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 7353, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Walorski of Indiana, for the 
purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Indiana?
  There was no objection.
```
