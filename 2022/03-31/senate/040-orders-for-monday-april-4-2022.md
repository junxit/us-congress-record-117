# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 4, 2022

**Speaking:** Markey, Edward J. (M000133) [D-MA]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 4, 2022

  Mr. MARKEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until 3 p.m., Monday, 
April 4; and that following the prayer and the pledge, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
