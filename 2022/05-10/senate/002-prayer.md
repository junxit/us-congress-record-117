# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Savior, lead us today as a shepherd guides sheep. Direct our lives. 
Inspire our hearts. May the talents here on Capitol Hill help bring 
unity and healing to our Nation and world.
  Lord, strengthen our lawmakers as they deal with unsolved problems 
and urgent global needs. Prepare them for the challenges yet to come. 
Make our Senators eager to lift burdens and ready to respond in service 
to humanity.
  Help us all to feel a bit of the responsibility for the challenges 
that hang heavy over our Nation and world.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
