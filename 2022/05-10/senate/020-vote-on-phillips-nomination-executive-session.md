# Vote on Phillips Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Vote on Phillips Nomination

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to start the 
vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Phillips 
nomination?
  Mr. BARRASSO. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN I announce that the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Sanders) is 
necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Wyoming (Ms. Lummis) and the Senator from Florida (Mr. Scott).
  Further, if present and voting, the Senator from Florida (Mr. Scott) 
would have voted ``nay.''
  The result was announced--yeas 75, nays 22, as follows:
```
