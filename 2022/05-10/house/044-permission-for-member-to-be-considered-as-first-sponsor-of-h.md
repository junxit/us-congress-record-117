# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 5996

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 5996

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 5996

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I 
may hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 5996, a 
bill originally introduced by Representative Fortenberry of Nebraska, 
for the purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings 
pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Georgia?
  There was no objection.
```
