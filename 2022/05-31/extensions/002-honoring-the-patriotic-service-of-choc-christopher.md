# HONORING THE PATRIOTIC SERVICE OF CHOC CHRISTOPHER

**Speaking:** Fallon, Pat (F000246) [R-TX]

```
           HONORING THE PATRIOTIC SERVICE OF CHOC CHRISTOPHER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PAT FALLON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 31, 2022

  Mr. FALLON. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor and commemorate the 
lengthy public service career of Choc Christopher.
  For the last eighteen years, Choc has faithfully served the students, 
teachers, and administrators of the Celina Independent School District 
as a Trustee. He has dedicated his career to expanding and improving 
education access for students of all ages in Celina, Texas. Moreover, 
he served as President of the Celina School Board for five years and 
worked on the Alla Hubbard Educational Trust Board for eight years. 
Choc leaves behind a powerful legacy that encompasses an efficient 
school administration, updated facilities, and expanded access to 
education. Generations of students, teachers, and administrators will 
benefit from Choc's extraordinary contributions to the Celina ISD 
community.
  Mr. Christopher was instrumental to Celina ISD's rapid growth, and 
through his leadership, the students of Celina experienced brand new, 
state-of-the-art educational facilities and sports complexes. Choc is 
also a twenty-one-year member of the Celina Quarterback Club and had 
the privilege of operating the clock for Celina High School and Celina 
Middle School football games--his favorite pastime.
  I have requested a United States flag be flown over our Nation's 
Capitol to recognize Choc Christopher's lifelong contributions to the 
good people of Celina, Texas. Furthermore, Madam Speaker, I would like 
to personally commend Mr. Christopher for his years of dedication to 
Celina ISD. May God bless him in all his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
