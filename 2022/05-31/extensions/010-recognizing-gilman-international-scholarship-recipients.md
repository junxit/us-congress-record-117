# RECOGNIZING GILMAN INTERNATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP RECIPIENTS

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
        RECOGNIZING GILMAN INTERNATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP RECIPIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 31, 2022

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize six young 
men and women in my district who were awarded the Benjamin A. Gilman 
International Scholarship to study abroad during the 2019-2020 and 
2020-2021 academic years. This program's mission to ``expand the number 
of young American citizens gaining knowledge, linguistic, and cultural 
skills that are critical to our national security and prosperity'' is 
admirable, and our community is incredibly proud of the following 
students' achievement:
  Humna Chaudhry of Bensalem, PA, studying in Singapore;
  Sabrina De Nicola of Churchville, PA, studying in Spain;
  Humna Rub of Doylestown, PA, studying in the United Kingdom;
  Ryan Smith of Warrington, PA, studying in Indonesia;
  Ian Yannuzzi of Southampton, PA, studying in New Zealand;
  Mikaila Harper of Chalfont, PA, studying in Uganda.
  At a time where our Nation's policy approach to security and 
diplomacy is actively challenged and adapting, the conviction of these 
young men and women is exceptional. We commend their participation in 
the Gilman Scholarship and are grateful for their commitment to 
strengthening the relationships and standing of the United States 
around the world.

                          ____________________
```
