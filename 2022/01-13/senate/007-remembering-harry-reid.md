# REMEMBERING HARRY REID

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                         REMEMBERING HARRY REID

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, before I begin my remarks, I see my 
dear friend, the President, sitting there--the Senator from Nevada--and 
I am just thinking, last night, as we watched Harry Reid leave the 
Senate for the final time: Harry, we miss you, but we know you are 
still here to guide us. Thank you.

                          ____________________
```
