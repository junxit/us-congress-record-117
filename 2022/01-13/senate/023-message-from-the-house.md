# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1836, H.R. 5746

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:35 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1836. A bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     ensure that the time during which members of the Armed Forces 
     serve on active duty for training qualifies for educational 
     assistance under the Post-9/11 Educational Assistance Program 
     of the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other 
     purposes.

  The message further announced that the House has agreed to the 
amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 5746) an act to amend title 
51, United States Code, to extend the authority of the National 
Aeronautics and Space Administration to enter into leases of nonexcess 
property of the Administration, with an amendment, in which it requests 
the concurrence of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
