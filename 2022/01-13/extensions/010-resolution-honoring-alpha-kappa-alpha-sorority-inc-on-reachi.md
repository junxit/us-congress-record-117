# RESOLUTION HONORING ALPHA KAPPA ALPHA SORORITY, INC. ON REACHING THE HISTORIC MILESTONE OF 114 YEARS OF SERVING COMMUNITIES

**Speaking:** Adams, Alma S. (A000370) [D-NC]

```
 RESOLUTION HONORING ALPHA KAPPA ALPHA SORORITY, INC. ON REACHING THE 
         HISTORIC MILESTONE OF 114 YEARS OF SERVING COMMUNITIES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ALMA S. ADAMS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 13, 2022

  Ms. ADAMS. Madam Speaker, along with my Congressional sorors, I rise 
to re-introduce this Resolution honoring Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, 
Inc. for her 114th anniversary. Throughout these 114 years, AKA sorors 
have carried out the mission of AKA with pride and strength. As my 
sorors near and far continue to carry out the meaningful mission of 
AKA, I extend my respect to all.
```
