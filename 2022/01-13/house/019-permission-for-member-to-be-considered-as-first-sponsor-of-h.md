# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 4394

**Speaking:** Calvert, Ken (C000059) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4394

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 4394

  Mr. CALVERT. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 4394, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Nunes of California, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
California?
  There was no objection.
```
