# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Give us the wisdom, the strength, the faith to trust in You in all 
things and above all things.
  And let all of us pray for the parents, the children, the innocent 
civilians in Ukraine who are suffering more than anybody should. Let us 
all hold them in our prayers. Amen.
```
