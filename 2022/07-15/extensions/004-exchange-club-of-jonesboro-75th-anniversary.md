# EXCHANGE CLUB OF JONESBORO 75TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Crawford, Eric A. "Rick" (C001087) [R-AR]

```
              EXCHANGE CLUB OF JONESBORO 75TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ERIC A. ``RICK'' CRAWFORD

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 15, 2022

  Mr. CRAWFORD. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor The Exchange Club 
of Jonesboro, Arkansas. The Exchange Club of Jonesboro was founded on 
August 28, 1947. This year they will be celebrating their 75th 
anniversary.
  The 70-member club is active with programs such as: honoring a Law 
Enforcement Office of the Year, handing out flags on Veterans

[[Page E740]]

Day, presenting Citizenship Awards to local sixth graders, hosting an 
activity day and cookout for children with special needs, providing a 
scholarship to Arkansas State University to assist a local student, 
sponsoring a local boy and a girl so they can attend Boys State and 
Girls State each year, sponsoring Little League baseball teams, 
dressing as Santa to provide children in local hospitals with gifts at 
Christmas, and many more community events.
  I am proud to have an organization like this in my District that 
makes an impact with a mission to inspire our communities to become 
better places to live.

                          ____________________
```
