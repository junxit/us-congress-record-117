# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
SUPPORTING CAREERS IN CONSERVATION: WORKFORCE TRAINING, EDUCATION, AND 
JOB OPPORTUNITIES
Committee on Agriculture: Subcommittee on Conservation and Forestry 
held a hearing entitled ``Supporting Careers in Conservation: Workforce 
Training, Education, and Job Opportunities''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
FISCAL YEAR 2023 MEMBER DAY HEARING
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Transportation, and 
Housing and Urban Development, and Related Agencies held a hearing 
entitled ``Fiscal Year 2023 Member Day Hearing''. Testimony was heard 
from Representative Luria.
TACKLING TEACHER SHORTAGES
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, Education and Related Agencies held a hearing entitled 
``Tackling Teacher Shortages''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.
APPROPRIATIONS--FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Homeland Security held a 
budget hearing on the Federal Emergency Management Agency. Testimony 
was heard from Deanne Criswell, Administrator, Federal Emergency 
Management Agency, Department of Homeland Security.
FISCAL YEAR 2023 MEMBER DAY HEARING
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Energy and Water 
Development, and Related Agencies held a hearing entitled ``Fiscal Year 
2023 Member Day Hearing''. Testimony was heard from Representatives 
Allen, Cloud, Mrvan, Porter, Stansbury, Stanton, and Van Drew.
DEFENSE HEALTH AND MEDICAL READINESS
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Defense held a hearing 
entitled ``Defense Health and Medical Readiness''. Testimony was heard 
from Lieutenant General R. Scott Dingle, Surgeon General of the U.S. 
Army; Rear Admiral Upper Half Bruce L. Gillingham, Surgeon General of 
the U.S. Navy; Lieutenant General Robert I. Miller, Surgeon General of 
the U.S. Air Force and U.S. Space Force; Seileen Mullen, Acting 
Assistant Secretary of Defense for Health Affairs, Department of 
Defense; and Lieutenant General Ronald J. Place, Director, Defense 
Health Agency.
APPROPRIATIONS--U.S. SECRET SERVICE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Homeland Security held a 
budget hearing on the U.S. Secret Service. Testimony was heard from 
James Murray, Director, U.S. Secret Service, Department of Homeland 
Security.

[[Page D590]]


THE INFANT FORMULA CRISIS
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Agriculture, Rural 
Development, Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies held a 
hearing entitled ``The Infant Formula Crisis''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
FISCAL YEAR 2023 MEMBER DAY HEARING
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Agriculture, Rural 
Development, Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies held a 
hearing entitled ``Fiscal Year 2023 Member Day Hearing''. Testimony was 
heard from Representatives Baird, Brown of Ohio, and Schrier.
EXAMINING THE POLICIES AND PRIORITIES OF THE OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY AND 
HEALTH ADMINISTRATION
Committee on Education and Labor: Subcommittee on Workforce Protections 
held a hearing entitled ``Examining the Policies and Priorities of the 
Occupational Safety and Health Administration''. Testimony was heard 
from Douglas Parker, Assistant Secretary of Labor for Occupational 
Safety and Health, Occupational Safety and Health Administration, 
Department of Labor; and Thomas Costa, Director, Education, Workforce, 
and Income Security, Government Accountability Office.
FORMULA SAFETY AND SUPPLY: PROTECTING THE HEALTH OF AMERICA'S BABIES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Subcommittee on Oversight and 
Investigations held a hearing entitled ``Formula Safety and Supply: 
Protecting the Health of America's Babies''. Testimony was heard from 
the following Department of Health and Human Services officials: Robert 
M. Califf, M.D., Commissioner, Food and Drug Administration; Frank 
Yiannas, Deputy Commissioner, Food Policy and Response, Food and Drug 
Administration; and Susan Mayne, Director, Center for Food Safety and 
Applied Nutrition, Food and Drug Administration; and public witnesses.
REAUTHORIZATION AND REFORM OF THE NATIONAL FLOOD INSURANCE PROGRAM
Committee on Financial Services: Subcommittee on Housing, Community 
Development and Insurance held a hearing entitled ``Reauthorization and 
Reform of the National Flood Insurance Program''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
TECHNOLOGY MODERNIZATION FUND: REWRITING OUR IT LEGACY
Committee on Oversight and Reform: Subcommittee on Government 
Operations held a hearing entitled ``Technology Modernization Fund: 
Rewriting our IT Legacy''. Testimony was heard from Raylene Yung, 
Executive Director, Technology Modernization Fund, General Services 
Administration; Gary Washington, Chief Information Officer, Department 
of Agriculture; and David Hinchman, Acting Director, Information 
Technology and Cybersecurity, Government Accountability Office.

Joint Meetings
UKRAINIAN REFUGEES
Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe: Commission concluded 
a hearing to examine supporting Ukrainian refugees, focusing on United 
States policy and visa issuance, after receiving testimony from Dana 
Francis, Acting Deputy Assistant Secretary of State, Bureau of 
Population, Refugees and Migration; Marek Magierowski, Ambassador of 
Poland to the United States; and Irina Manelis, Manelis Law, PLLC.
```
