# PATSY CLINE POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 1444

```
                        PATSY CLINE POST OFFICE

  A bill (H.R. 1444) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 132 North Loudoun Street, Suite 1 in 
Winchester, Virginia, as the ``Patsy Cline Post Office'', was ordered 
to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
