# Vote on Sweeney Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       Vote on Sweeney Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Sweeney nomination?
  Mrs. MURRAY. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.

  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Texas (Mr. Cornyn), the Senator from Texas (Mr. Cruz), and the 
Senator from Alaska (Ms. Murkowski).
```
