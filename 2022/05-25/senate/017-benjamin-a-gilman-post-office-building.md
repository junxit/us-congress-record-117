# BENJAMIN A. GILMAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 767

```
                BENJAMIN A. GILMAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 767) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 40 Fulton Street in Middletown, New York, as 
the ``Benjamin A. Gilman Post Office Building'', was ordered to a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
