# ARTURO L. IBLETO POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 735

```
                 ARTURO L. IBLETO POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 735) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 502 East Cotati Avenue in Cotati, California, 
as the ``Arturo L. Ibleto Post Office Building'', was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
