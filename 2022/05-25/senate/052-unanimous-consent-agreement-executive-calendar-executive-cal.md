# Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
            Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar

  Mr. PAUL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the cloture 
vote on the Wainstein nomination be withdrawn and that the confirmation 
vote on the nomination occur at a time to be determined by the majority 
leader or his designee in consultation with the Republican leader.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. SCHUMER. It is a unique moment. I fully support Senator Paul in 
his motion.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

[[Page S2697]]

  The Senator from Kentucky.
  Mr. PAUL. Mr. President, I also ask unanimous consent that the rest 
of the votes for this evening be 10-minute votes.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Once again, I support my colleague, the junior Senator 
from the great Commonwealth of Kentucky.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
