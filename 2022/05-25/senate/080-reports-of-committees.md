                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

     Entrepreneurship, with an amendment in the nature of a 
     substitute:
       S. 1687. A bill to amend section 21 of the Small Business

     the Boots to Business Program, and for other purposes.

     Entrepreneurship, with amendments:
       S. 3906. A bill to improve certain programs of the Small 
     Business Administration to better assist small business 
     customers in accessing broadband technology, and for other 
     purposes.
