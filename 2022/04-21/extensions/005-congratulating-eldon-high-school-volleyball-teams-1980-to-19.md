# CONGRATULATING ELDON HIGH SCHOOL VOLLEYBALL TEAMS 1980 TO 1996 INDUCTION INTO MSHOF

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
    CONGRATULATING ELDON HIGH SCHOOL VOLLEYBALL TEAMS 1980 TO 1996 
                          INDUCTION INTO MSHOF

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 21, 2022

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Madam Speaker, today I rise to recognize Missouri's 
Third District Eldon High School Volleyball teams from 1980 to 1996 in 
my home district for being inducted into the Missouri Sports Hall of 
Fame. This accomplishment is not one to be taken lightly and reflects 
the Eldon community dedication to youth athletics and the sport of 
volleyball.
  Their induction into the Missouri Sports Hall of Fame is a testament 
to the impact Eldon High School Volleyball has made on high school 
sports in Missouri. One of the best eras in Missouri high school sports 
history running from 1980 to 1996 with an astounding 14 district titles 
in this span of time and eight straight final four berths also winning 
the Class 3 state title in 1991. Eleven different players received 
AllState recognition and Coach Ann Gulshen has been inducted into the 
Missouri Sports Hall of Famer.
  You likely will not find a team more deserving of this tremendous 
achievement. The impact it has made on high school sports in Missouri 
cannot go unnoticed.
  Madam Speaker please join me in congratulating Eldon High School's 
Volleyball teams from 1980 to 1996 for being inducted into the Missouri 
Sports Hall of Fame.

                          ____________________
```
