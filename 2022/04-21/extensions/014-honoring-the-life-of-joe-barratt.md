# HONORING THE LIFE OF JOE BARRATT

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
                    HONORING THE LIFE OF JOE BARRATT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 21, 2022


  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life of 
Joe Barratt.
  Throughout his life, Joe's compassion, kindness, and creativity were 
evident to all who knew him. He had a passion for music and was a 
talented musician. Joe played cello for Concord High School's 
orchestra, taught himself how to play guitar, and was a talented 
songwriter and producer. His other interests included astrology and 
spending time with his friends in Todos Santos Plaza and Civic Park. An 
animal lover, Joe also enjoyed helping his mother, Leslie, walk dogs on 
the weekends. One of their favorite traditions was to stop at IHOP 
afterwards and Joe would indulge in the red velvet pancakes.
  Joe had an extraordinary ability to help others feel accepted and 
cared for. He was welcoming and warm to kids that needed extra love and 
kindness. He has been described as a ``shepherd amongst the misfit kids 
at school''.
  Tragically, Joe was killed in a hit-and-run accident and passed away 
on February 11, 2020. He is survived by his mother and father, Leslie 
and Joe Barratt II. He will be remembered for his gentle spirit and 
outstanding character. Please join me in honoring the life of Joe 
Barratt.

                          ____________________
```
