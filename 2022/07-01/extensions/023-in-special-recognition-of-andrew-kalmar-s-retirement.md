# IN SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF ANDREW KALMAR'S RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
          IN SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF ANDREW KALMAR'S RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, July 1, 2022

  Mr. LATTA. Madam Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Andrew Kalmar's 
retirement as Wood County Administrator and recognize his outstanding 
career. Andrew has been administrator for twenty-two years and has 
proven himself to be a leader for both his department and county.
  Andrew, originally from Troy, Ohio, graduated from Bowling Green 
State University with a Bachelor of Science Degree in Education. He 
also received a Master of Public Administration degree. Andrew began 
his career with the Wood County Commissioners in January 2000 after 
serving as the director of the Wood County Park District.
  Over the span of Andrew's tenure as administrator, Wood County has 
built a strong financial position, experienced economic growth and 
development, made improvements to county facilities, and increased 
efficiency of departmental operations. Also, Andrew played a key role 
in the construction of the Alvin L. Perkins Atrium at the Wood County 
Courthouse and office complex. During the last two years, Andrew has 
been instrumental in guiding Wood County through the COVID-19 pandemic 
while still maintaining productivity throughout all aspects of county 
work. Andrew's character and leadership have contributed to the success 
of Wood County, making it a remarkable place to live and work.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in paying special 
tribute to Andrew Kalmar in recognition of his service to Wood County. 
On behalf of the people of the Fifth District of Ohio, I wish Andrew 
all the best in his future endeavors and thank him for his many years 
of service.

                          ____________________
```
