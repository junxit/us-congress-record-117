# CONGRATULATIONS TO AUSTIN RHODES

**Speaking:** Allen, Rick W. (A000372) [R-GA]

```
                    CONGRATULATIONS TO AUSTIN RHODES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RICK W. ALLEN

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, July 1, 2022

  Mr. ALLEN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Austin Rhodes 
for thirty years in broadcasting at WGAC-AM 580 in Augusta.
  I have been fortunate to have been a guest on Austin's radio program 
during my time in Congress and it is a pleasure to recognize him and 
his hard work in this chamber.
  For three decades, Austin's program has brought news and commentary 
to countless listeners across Georgia and his hard work and dedication 
have enabled him to cement a lasting legacy in talk radio.
  Congratulations to Austin and his entire team over at WGAC on thirty 
years of the Austin Rhodes Show.

                          ____________________
```
