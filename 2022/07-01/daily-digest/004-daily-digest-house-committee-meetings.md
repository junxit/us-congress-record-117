# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Appropriations: On June 29, 2022, Full Committee held a 
markup on the FY 2023 State, Foreign Operations, and Related Programs 
Appropriations Bill; and the FY 2023 Interior, Environment, and Related 
Agencies Appropriations Bill.

[[Page D719]]

The FY 2023 State, Foreign Operations, and Related Programs 
Appropriations Bill and the FY 2023 Interior, Environment, and Related 
Agencies Appropriations Bill were ordered reported, as amended.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Appropriations: On June 30, 2022, Full Committee held a 
markup on the FY 2023 Transportation, and Housing and Urban 
Development, and Related Agencies Appropriations Bill; the FY 2023 
Labor, Health and Human Services, Education, and Related Agencies 
Appropriations Bill; and the revised report on the Suballocation of 
Budget Allocations for FY 2023. The revised report on the Suballocation 
of Budget Allocations for FY 2023 passed. The FY 2023 Transportation, 
and Housing and Urban Development, and Related Agencies Appropriations 
Bill and the FY 2023 Labor, Health and Human Services, Education, and 
Related Agencies Appropriations Bill were ordered reported, as amended.
INVESTING IN PUBLIC HEALTH: LEGISLATION TO SUPPORT PATIENTS, WORKERS, 
AND RESEARCH
Committee on Energy and Commerce: On June 29, 2022, Subcommittee on 
Health held a hearing entitled ``Investing in Public Health: 
Legislation to Support Patients, Workers, and Research''. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.
NO TIME TO WASTE: SOLUTIONS FOR AMERICA'S BROKEN RECYCLING SYSTEM
Committee on Energy and Commerce: On June 30, 2022, Subcommittee on 
Environment and Climate Change held a hearing entitled ``No Time to 
Waste: Solutions for America's Broken Recycling System''. Testimony was 
heard from David Allaway, Senior Policy Analyst, Department of 
Environmental Quality, Oregon; and public witnesses.
BOOM AND BUST: INEQUALITY, HOMEOWNERSHIP, AND THE LONG-TERM IMPACTS OF 
THE HOT HOUSING MARKET
Committee on Financial Services: On June 29, 2022, Full Committee held 
a hearing entitled ``Boom and Bust: Inequality, Homeownership, and the 
Long-Term Impacts of the Hot Housing Market''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
COMBATTING TECH BRO CULTURE: UNDERSTANDING OBSTACLES TO INVESTMENTS IN 
DIVERSE-OWNED FINTECHS
Committee on Financial Services: On June 30, 2022, Task Force on 
Financial Technology held a hearing entitled ``Combatting Tech Bro 
Culture: Understanding Obstacles to Investments in Diverse-Owned 
Fintechs''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
OVERSIGHT OF IMMIGRANT MILITARY MEMBERS AND VETERANS
Committee on the Judiciary: On June 29, 2022, Subcommittee on 
Immigration and Citizenship held a hearing entitled ``Oversight of 
Immigrant Military Members and Veterans''. Testimony was heard from 
Jennifer MacDonald, Senior Advisor to the Secretary for Health, 
Department of Veterans Affairs; Stephanie Miller, Director, Officer and 
Enlisted Personnel Management, Department of Defense; and Debra Rogers, 
Director, Immigrant Military Members and Veterans Initiative, 
Department of Homeland Security.
PRIVACY IN THE AGE OF BIOMETRICS
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: On June 29, 2022, 
Subcommittee on Investigations and Oversight held a hearing entitled 
``Privacy in the Age of Biometrics''. Testimony was heard from Candice 
Wright, Director, Science, Technology Assessment, and Analytics, 
Government Accountability Office; Charles H. Romine, Director, 
Information Technology Laboratory, National Institute of Standards and 
Technology, Department of Commerce; and Arun Ross, Professor, Site 
Director, Center for Identification Technology Research, National 
Science Foundation.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
