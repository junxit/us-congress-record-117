# NORTHWEST COMMUNITY ACTION CENTER 40 YEAR CELEBRATION

**Speaking:** Newhouse, Dan (N000189) [R-WA]

```
         NORTHWEST COMMUNITY ACTION CENTER 40 YEAR CELEBRATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DAN NEWHOUSE

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 30, 2022

  Mr. NEWHOUSE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Northwest 
Community

[[Page E1014]]

Action Center as they celebrate 40 years of service to the communities 
of Central Washington.
  The Northwest Community Action Center first opened in 1982 to help 
expand on the mission of the Yakima Valley Farm Workers Clinic, helping 
families weatherize their homes, pay their bills, navigate the 
healthcare system, and provide them with after-care school services.
  Since its opening, the Northwest Community Action Center has expanded 
its service programs to meet the needs of families across Washington 
and Oregon.
  Today, community members can take advantage of the variety of 
services they now offer, which include adult education classes, English 
language classes, homelessness services, citizenship classes, and so 
much more.
  I extend my congratulations and gratitude to the Northwest Community 
Action Center for its leadership and many years of service to our 
Central Washington communities.

                          ____________________
```
