# NATIONAL HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Newhouse, Dan (N000189) [R-WA]

```
                    NATIONAL HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DAN NEWHOUSE

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 30, 2022

  Mr. NEWHOUSE. Madam Speaker, I rise today in honor of National 
Hispanic Heritage Month to celebrate the accomplishments and 
contributions of Latino and Hispanic Americans in Central Washington, 
and around the United States.
  Hispanic Heritage Month is about recognizing the history of our 
Hispanic neighbors, and honoring the hardships endured to create a 
better life for future generations. In Central Washington, our Hispanic 
neighbors strengthen our communities through their strong family 
values, cultural diversity, and incredibly hard work ethics. Our region 
could not prosper without the Hispanic farmers, police officers, 
teachers, and soldiers who support our society.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring our Hispanic 
American friends, families, and neighbors and in recognizing National 
Hispanic Heritage Month.

                          ____________________
```
