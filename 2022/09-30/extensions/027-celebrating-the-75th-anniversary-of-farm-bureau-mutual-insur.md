# CELEBRATING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF FARM BUREAU MUTUAL INSURANCE COMPANY OF IDAHO

**Speaking:** Simpson, Michael K. (S001148) [R-ID]

```
   CELEBRATING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF FARM BUREAU MUTUAL INSURANCE 
                            COMPANY OF IDAHO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MICHAEL K. SIMPSON

                                of idaho

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 30, 2022

  Mr. SIMPSON. Madam Speaker, I rise today to call attention to an 
Idaho icon and institution, Farm Bureau Mutual Insurance Company of 
Idaho, for exceptional service to its customers and the community. Farm 
Bureau Insurance is celebrating 75 years of providing insurance to the 
residents and farmers of Idaho.
  Farm Bureau started offering insurance to Idahoans in 1947. They are 
now the second largest insurance writer in the state and the number one 
farms and ranch insurer in Idaho. They are working to provide peace of 
mind to Idaho families, farms, and businesses of Idaho by protecting 
what matters most.
  Over the last 75 years, Farm Bureau has been actively contributing to 
the health and prosperity of Idaho's communities. As an active 
corporate citizen, Farm Bureau has been a positive force in Idaho by 
sponsoring Christmas in the Night Skies, Relay for Life, Salute to 
Idaho Veterans, Elevate Leadership Summit, and the Gold Star Families 
Memorial Monument among other causes and events over the last 75 years.
  Farm Bureau has been awarded an AM Best financial strength rating of 
excellent or higher since 1959. In 1996, they earned the Better 
Business Bureau's Torch award for ethics. In 2021, they were awarded 
the best insurance company in Idaho by Forbes. In both 2020 and 2021, 
Farm Bureau earned Demotech's Star Award for superior business 
practices and customer satisfaction.
  Madam Speaker, I would like to join their customers, clients, and our 
community, in honoring Farm Bureau Mutual Insurance Company of Idaho 
for 75 years of exceptional service to our great state.
```
