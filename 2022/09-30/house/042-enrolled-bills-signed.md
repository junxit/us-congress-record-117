# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 4877, H.R. 6833, H.R. 7698

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly an 
enrolled bill of the House of the following title, which was thereupon 
signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 6833. An act making continuing appropriations for 
     fiscal year 2023, and for other purposes.

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, further reported and found 
truly enrolled bills of the House of the following titles, which were 
thereupon signed by the Speaker pro tempore, Mr. Beyer:

       H.R. 4877. An act to amend the Small Business Act to 
     require the Small Business and Agriculture Regulatory 
     Enforcement Ombudsman to create a centralized website for 
     compliance guides, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 7698. An act to designate the outpatient clinic of the 
     Department of Veterans Affairs in Ventura, California, as the 
     ``Captain Rosemary Bryant Mariner Outpatient Clinic''.

  Examined and found truly enrolled September 29, 2022.

                          ____________________
```
