# PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 6833

**Speaking:** DeLauro, Rosa L. (D000216) [D-CT]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 45, H.R. 6833

```
       PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 6833

  Ms. DeLAURO. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to take from the 
Speaker's table the concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 45) providing 
for a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 6833, and ask for its 
immediate consideration in the House.
  The Clerk read the title of the concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Connecticut?
  There was no objection.
  The text of the concurrent resolution is as follows:

                            S. Con. Res. 45

       Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives 
     concurring), That, in the enrollment of H.R. 6833, the Clerk 
     of the House of Representatives shall amend the title so as 
     to read: ``Making continuing appropriations for fiscal year 
     2023, and for other purposes.''.
  The concurrent resolution was concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
