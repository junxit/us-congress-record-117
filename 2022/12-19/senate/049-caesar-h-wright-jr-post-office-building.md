# CAESAR H. WRIGHT JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 6042

```
               CAESAR H. WRIGHT JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

       A bill (H.R. 6042) to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 213 William Hilton Parkway 
     in Hilton Head Island, South Carolina, as the ``Caesar H. 
     Wright Jr. Post Office Building'' was ordered to a third
```
