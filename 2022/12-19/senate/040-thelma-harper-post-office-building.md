# THELMA HARPER POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5271

```
                   THELMA HARPER POST OFFICE BUILDING

       A bill (H.R. 5271) to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 2245 Rosa L Parks Boulevard 
     in Nashville, Tennessee, as the ``Thelma Harper Post Office 
     Building'', which had been reported from the Committee on 
     Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs, was ordered to a 
     third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
