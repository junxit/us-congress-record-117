# PFC JAMES ANDERSON, JR., POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1095

```
             PFC JAMES ANDERSON, JR., POST OFFICE BUILDING

       A bill (H.R. 1095) to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 101 South Willowbrook Avenue 
     in Compton, California, as the ``PFC James Anderson, Jr., 
     Post Office Building'', which had been reported from the 
     Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs, was 
     ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and 
     passed.
```
