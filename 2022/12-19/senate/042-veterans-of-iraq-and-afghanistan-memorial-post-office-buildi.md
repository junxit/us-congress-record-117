# VETERANS OF IRAQ AND AFGHANISTAN MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 6386

```
     VETERANS OF IRAQ AND AFGHANISTAN MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

       A bill (H.R. 6386) to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 450 West Schaumburg Road in 
     Schaumburg, Illinois, as the ``Veterans of Iraq and 
     Afghanistan Memorial Post Office Building'', which had been 
     reported from the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs, was ordered to a third reading, was 
     read the third time, and passed.
```
