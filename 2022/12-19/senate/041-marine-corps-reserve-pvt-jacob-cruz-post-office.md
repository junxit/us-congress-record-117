# MARINE CORPS RESERVE PVT JACOB CRUZ POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 5900

```
            MARINE CORPS RESERVE PVT JACOB CRUZ POST OFFICE

       A bill (H.R. 5900) to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 2016 East 1st Street in Los 
     Angeles, California, as the ``Marine Corps Reserve PVT Jacob 
     Cruz Post Office'', which had been reported from the 
     Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs, was 
     ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and 
     passed.
```
