# JOHN R. HATCHER III POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5659

```
                JOHN R. HATCHER III POST OFFICE BUILDING

       A bill (H.R. 5659) to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 1961 North C Street in 
     Oxnard, California, as the ``John R. Hatcher III Post Office 
     Building'', which had been reported from the Committee on 
     Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs, was ordered to a 
     third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
