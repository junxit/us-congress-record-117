# DIRECTING THE SECRETARY OF VETERANS AFFAIRS TO SEEK TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH THE NATIONAL ACADEMIES OF SCIENCES, ENGINEERING, AND MEDICINE FOR A REVIEW OF EXAMINATIONS, FURNISHED BY THE...

**Measures:** H.R. 6064

```
 DIRECTING THE SECRETARY OF VETERANS AFFAIRS TO SEEK TO ENTER INTO AN 
  AGREEMENT WITH THE NATIONAL ACADEMIES OF SCIENCES, ENGINEERING, AND 
 MEDICINE FOR A REVIEW OF EXAMINATIONS, FURNISHED BY THE SECRETARY, TO 
 INDIVIDUALS WHO SUBMIT CLAIMS TO THE SECRETARY FOR COMPENSATION UNDER 
  CHAPTER 11 OF TITLE 38, UNITED STATES CODE, FOR MENTAL AND PHYSICAL 
              CONDITIONS LINKED TO MILITARY SEXUAL TRAUMA

  A bill (H.R. 6064) to direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to 
seek to enter into an agreement with the National Academies of 
Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine for a review of examinations, 
furnished by the Secretary, to individuals who submit claims to the 
Secretary for compensation under chapter 11 of title 38, United States 
Code, for mental and physical conditions linked to military sexual 
trauma was ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and 
passed.
```
