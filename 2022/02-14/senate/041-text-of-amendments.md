# TEXT OF AMENDMENTS

**Measures:** H.R. 2497

```
                           TEXT OF AMENDMENTS

  SA 4926. Mr. SCHUMER (for Mr. Bennet) proposed an amendment to the 
bill H.R. 2497, to establish the Amache National Historic Site in the 
State of Colorado as a Unit of the National Park System, and for other 
purposes; as follows:

       In section 3, strike subsection (f) and insert the 
     following:
       (f) Land Acquisition Authority.--The Secretary may acquire 
     land or interests in land located within the boundary of the 
     Camp Amache National Historic Landmark, as generally depicted 
     on the Map, only by donation.
```
