# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 36, S. 895

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested.

       S. 895. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide an exemption from gross income for mandatory 
     restitution or civil damages as recompense for trafficking in 
     persons.
  The message also announced that the Senate has agreed to a concurrent 
resolution of the following title in which the concurrence of the House 
is requested:

       S. Con. Res. 36. Concurrent Resolution honoring the life 
     and legacy of Ulysses S. Grant in commemoration of his 200th 
     Birthday.

                          ____________________
```
