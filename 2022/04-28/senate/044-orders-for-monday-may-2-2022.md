# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 2, 2022

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                     ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 2, 2022

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m. Monday, 
May 2, and that following the prayer and pledge,

[[Page S2225]]

the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day and morning business be closed; that upon the 
conclusion of morning business, the Senate proceed to executive session 
to resume consideration of the Frost nomination; further, that the 
cloture motions filed during today's session ripen at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
