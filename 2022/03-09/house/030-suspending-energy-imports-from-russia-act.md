the Chamber that we are here to do the job of the American people. We

judgment, and the judgment of many of my colleagues, that we should

question and to remind the Chamber that it is our job to vote. It is 
our job to be here and do the work of the American people.
  Madam Speaker, we have not had an amendment on the floor of the House 
in open debate since May of 2016, to the best of my understanding. We 
need to restore this Chamber. We need to debate. We need to vote.
  Madam Speaker, with that, I will withdraw my reservation of the right 
to object.
