# CONGRATULATING THE RECIPIENTS OF THE 2022 H.E.B. ISD AWARDS OF EXCELLENCE

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
    CONGRATULATING THE RECIPIENTS OF THE 2022 H.E.B. ISD AWARDS OF 
                               EXCELLENCE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 9, 2022

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate thirty-
three students from the Hurst Euless Bedford Independent School 
District. These incredible students have been selected to receive the 
District's Awards of Excellence for the 2021-2022 school year.
  Through their talent, hard work, and unmatched dedication, this 
year's group of recipients earned awards in a variety of fields that 
include visual and performing arts, business and industry, science, 
mathematics, and communications, among others. It is an honor to 
highlight the following young men and women for their unique talents: 
JaNya Chandler, Elise Pham, Christian Amayat, Nicholas McDaniel, 
Patrick Hannigan, Nathaniel Taylor, Peytonn Stevens, Christopher 
Coleman, Emma Brambila, Payton McAdams, Rachel Chen, Matthew Nguyen, 
Leticia Aguilar, Maurecia Bessman, Analicia Alonzo, Kaylee Flippin,

Norwood, Maverick Ganzo, Nicholas King, Amirah Wooden, Isabella Cruz, 
Ethan Anderson, Sophie Hawkins, Ethan Rangel, Taylor King, Edith Lara, 
Colton Neuville, Tanvi Rajpurohit, and Sean Powell.
```
