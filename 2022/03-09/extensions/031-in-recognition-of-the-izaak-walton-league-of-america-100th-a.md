# IN RECOGNITION OF THE IZAAK WALTON LEAGUE OF AMERICA 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
 IN RECOGNITION OF THE IZAAK WALTON LEAGUE OF AMERICA 100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 9, 2022

  Mr. WITTMAN. Madam Speaker, I rise today in recognition of the 100th 
Anniversary of the Izaak Walton League of America (IWLA), an 
association of dedicated fishermen, outdoorsmen and women, hunters and 
conservationist that have fought relentlessly to maintain and improve 
our environment. Over the past 100 years, they have advocated for 
strong state and national policies to protect our air, water, and 
wildlife.
  Madam Speaker, I ask you to join me in recognizing the 
accomplishments of the Izaak Walton League of America. May God bless 
the next 100 years of their conservation work, and I look forward to 
seeing their excellence for many years into the future.

                          ____________________
```
