# CELEBRATING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MOUNT ORAB MAYOR BRUCE LUNSFORD

**Speaking:** Wenstrup, Brad R. (W000815) [R-OH]

```
   CELEBRATING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MOUNT ORAB MAYOR BRUCE LUNSFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRAD R. WENSTRUP

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 9, 2022

  Mr. WENSTRUP. Madam Speaker, I rise today in memory of Mt. Orab Mayor 
Bruce Lunsford, who passed away on February 21, 2022, at the age of 68.
  Mayor Lunsford was a dedicated husband, father, and grandfather as 
well as a diligent public servant who was an unwavering advocate for 
Mount Orab. His love of country and commitment to bettering his 
community were as indisputable as they were indispensable.
  Bruce Lunsford began public service with the Mt. Orab City Council at 
the age of 18 and served as Mayor of Mount Orab for over 41 years.
  He made a lasting impression on this Earth and his community. Bruce's 
impact on Mt. Orab, Brown County, and all that knew him will be felt 
for generations as his legacy lives on.
  With the greatest respect, we are all forever grateful for the 
service of Mayor Bruce Lunsford and I am personally proud to have known 
him.
  I ask for everyone to join me in praying for Bruce's wife, Becky, his 
family and friends, for the community he served, and the lives he 
touched.
  Rest in peace, Mayor Lunsford.

                          ____________________
```
