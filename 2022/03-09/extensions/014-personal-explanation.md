# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Price, David E. (P000523) [D-NC]

**Measures:** H.Res. 956, H.R. 3967

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DAVID E. PRICE

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 9, 2022

  Mr. PRICE of North Carolina. Madam Speaker, on March 2nd, 2022, due 
to a visit by Vice President Kamala Harris to my congressional 
district, I was unable to be present to cast my votes for amendments to 
H.R. 3967, the Honoring our PACT Act of 2021, and H.Res. 956, a 
resolution supporting the people of Ukraine. Had I been present for 
roll call No. 51, 52, 53 and 54, I would have voted ``YEA'' on 
H.Res.956, voted ``YEA'' on the Bipartisan En Bloc No. 1 offered by Mr. 
Takano, voted ``NAY'' on Amendment No. 5 offered by Mrs. Miller-Meeks 
and Mr. Bost, and voted ``YEA'' on Amendment No. 6 offered by Mrs. 
Brownley and Mrs. Miller-Meeks.

                          ____________________
```
