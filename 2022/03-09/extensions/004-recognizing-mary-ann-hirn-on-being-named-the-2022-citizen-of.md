# RECOGNIZING MARY ANN HIRN ON BEING NAMED THE 2022 CITIZEN OF THE YEAR BY THE CASS CITY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
 RECOGNIZING MARY ANN HIRN ON BEING NAMED THE 2022 CITIZEN OF THE YEAR 
                  BY THE CASS CITY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 9, 2022

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mary Ann Hirn 
on being named the 2022 Citizen of the Year by the Cass City Chamber of 
Commerce.
  Mary Ann began her career as a nurse, and later owned a business with 
her late husband Bob, who was named Citizen of the Year in 2003. She 
has also been involved with many groups in the Cass City community, 
including the local Catholic church, the Cass City Historical Society, 
the Friends of the Thumb Octagon Barn, the Friends of Rawson Memorial 
Library, and the Hills and Dales Hospital Auxiliary.
  One recommendation letter rightly proclaimed, ``Look about and you 
will see her footprints throughout the community.'' I thank Mary Ann 
for her years of humble service to the Cass City community and 
congratulate her on earning 2022's Citizen of the Year award. As 
someone who does not do this for the attention or acclamation, I am 
confident she will continue to make an imprint on the community she 
loves for years to come.
```
