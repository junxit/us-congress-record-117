# SENATE RESOLUTION 739--AUTHORIZING THE PRINTING WITH ILLUSTRATIONS OF A DOCUMENT ENTITLED "COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS, UNITED STATES SENATE, 1867-2022"

**Measures:** S.Res. 739

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 739--AUTHORIZING THE PRINTING WITH ILLUSTRATIONS OF A 
DOCUMENT ENTITLED ``COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS, UNITED STATES SENATE, 
                              1867-2022''

  Mrs. MURRAY (for Mr. Leahy (for himself and Mr. Shelby)) submitted 
the following resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 739

       Resolved, That there be printed with illustrations as a 
     Senate document a compilation of materials entitled 
     ``Committee on Appropriations, United States Senate, 1867-
     2022'', and that there be printed six hundred additional 
     copies of such document for the use of the Committee on 
     Appropriations.
```
