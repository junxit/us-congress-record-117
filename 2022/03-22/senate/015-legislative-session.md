```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate will now resume legislative 
session.


 =========================== NOTE ===========================

following appears: MORNING BUSINESS Ms. HASSAN. Mr. President, I 
ask unanimous consent that the Senate be in a period of morning

minutes each. The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so
```
