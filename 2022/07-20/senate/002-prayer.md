# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Redeemer, answer us in seasons of trouble. Send us Your help 
from Your sacred hills. Our souls long for You, for we find strength 
and joy in Your presence. Lord, increase our faith, and teach us to 
trust You even during life's storms.
  When our Senators endure dark nights of the soul, enable them to find 
strength in Your presence. May they claim Your promise that You will 
never leave or forsake them and that nothing can separate them from 
Your love. Help them seek in every undertaking to know and do Your 
will.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
