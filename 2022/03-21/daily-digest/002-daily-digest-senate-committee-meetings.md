NOMINATION
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee held a hearing to examine the 
nomination of Ketanji Brown Jackson, of the District of Columbia, to be 
an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the United States, the 
nominee, who was introduced by Thomas B. Griffith, Judge (Ret.), U.S. 
Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit, Washington, 
D.C., and Lisa M. Fairfax, University of Pennsylvania Carey Law School, 
Philadelphia, testified in her own behalf. Hearing recessed subject to 
the call and will meet again on Tuesday, March 22, 2022.
