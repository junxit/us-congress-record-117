# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

**Measures:** H.R. 3076, H.R. 3755

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 4932. Ms. COLLINS (for herself and Ms. Murkowski) 
     submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by her to the 
     bill H.R. 3755, to protect a person's ability to determine 
     whether to continue or end a pregnancy, and to protect a 
     health care provider's ability to provide abortion services; 
     which was ordered to lie on the table.
       SA 4933. Mr. SCOTT of Florida submitted an amendment 
     intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 3076, to 
     provide stability to and enhance the services of the United 
     States Postal Service, and for other purposes; which was 
     ordered to lie on the table.
```
