# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 1230

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 1 of House Resolution 
1230, the House stands adjourned until noon on Tuesday, August 30, 
2022.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  August 26, 2022, on page H7723, in the third column, the 
following appeared: noon on Tuesday, August 30.
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: noon on Tuesday, 
August 30, 2022.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


  Thereupon (at 10 o'clock and 2 minutes a.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until Tuesday, August 30, 2022, at noon.
```
