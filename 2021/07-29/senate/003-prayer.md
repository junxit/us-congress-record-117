# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Precious Lord, You have been our dwelling place in all generations, 
sustaining us with Your goodness and mercy. Today, surround our 
Senators with the protection of Your divine favor, enabling them to 
obey Your command to be productive.
  Lord, continue to strengthen them to follow Your precepts, fulfilling 
Your purposes as they find joy in Your presence. Keep them from the 
things that bring regret. Increase their faith, providing them with 
courage to live for Your glory.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
