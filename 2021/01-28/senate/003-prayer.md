# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord, we look to You for refuge. You are master of ocean, Earth, and 
sky. Every good thing we have comes from You. Your wisdom is worth much 
more than silver or gold. Though troubles multiply in our Nation and 
world, You simply need to speak to bring order from chaos and harmony 
from discord.
  Lord, use our lawmakers and instruct them in Your precepts. Inspire 
them to live for Your glory. Use them to hasten the day when Your will 
will be done on Earth, even as it is done in Heaven.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
