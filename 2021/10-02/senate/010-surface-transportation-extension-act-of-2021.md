# SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2021

**Speaking:** Wyden, Ron (W000779) [D-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 5434

```
              SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2021

  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 5434, which is at the 
desk.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5434) to provide an extension of Federal-aid 
     highway, highway safety, and transit programs, and for other 
     purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
considered read a third time and passed, and that the motion to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 5434) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
