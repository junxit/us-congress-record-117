# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3120

**Measures:** H.R. 3120

```
           By Mr. SMITH of Nebraska:
       H.R. 3120.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 4 of the Constitution provides 
     that Congress shall have power ``To establish a uniform Rule 
     of Naturalization.''
```
