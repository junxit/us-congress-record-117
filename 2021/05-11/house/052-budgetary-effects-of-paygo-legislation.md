# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 768, H.R. 1629

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote

[[Page H2192]]

on passage, for printing in the Congressional Record, that H.R. 768, 
the Block, Report, And Suspend Suspicious Shipments Act of 2021, as 
amended, would have no significant effect on the deficit, and 
therefore, the budgetary effects of such bill are estimated as zero.
                                  ____

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 1629, the Fairness in Orphan Drug 
Exclusivity Act, as amended, would have no significant effect on the 
deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such bill are 
estimated as zero.

                          ____________________
```
