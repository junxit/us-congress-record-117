# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, thank You for the joy You give us when we follow Your 
guidance. Lord, You have provided us with Your Holy Word as a light to 
illuminate life's journey, and Your precepts inspire us with confidence 
and delight.
  As our lawmakers daily receive Your wisdom from devotional time with 
You, permit Your peace that exceeds anything we can understand to guard 
their hearts, even during turbulent seasons. Lord, use our Senators for 
Your glory, empowering them to stay productive throughout the days of 
their lives.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
