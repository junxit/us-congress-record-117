# ORDER OF BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                           ORDER OF BUSINESS

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that upon the 
conclusion of morning business on Monday, February 8, the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nomination: 
Calendar No. 6, Denis Richard McDonough, to be Secretary of Veterans 
Affairs; further, that at 5:30 p.m., the Senate vote without 
intervening action or debate on the nomination; that if confirmed, the 
motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table and the 
President be immediately notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
