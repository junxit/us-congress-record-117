# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty Redeemer, today, show our lawmakers Your unfailing love. 
Surround them with the shield of Your divine protection. Keep their 
steps on the right paths, providing them with wisdom to serve You and 
country faithfully. May they not hesitate to fulfill Your purposes.
  Lord, guard them with Your omnipotence and fill them with the power 
of Your Spirit.
  Arise, O Lord. Stand with those who seek to protect freedom. Let the 
smile of Your face shine on this land we love.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
