# CELEBRATING THE CAREER AND LEGACY OF HAMILTON COUNTY JUDGE ETHNA COOPER

**Speaking:** Wenstrup, Brad R. (W000815) [R-OH]

```
CELEBRATING THE CAREER AND LEGACY OF HAMILTON COUNTY JUDGE ETHNA COOPER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRAD R. WENSTRUP

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, February 4, 2021

  Mr. WENSTRUP. Madam Speaker, I rise today to thank Judge Ethna Cooper 
for her many years of service to Hamilton County and its residents.
  Judge Cooper was appointed to the Hamilton County Court of Common 
Pleas by Governor Taft in 2003.
  In addition to her service on the Court, Judge Cooper founded and has 
served as the Presiding Judge of the Hamilton County Felony Veterans' 
Treatment Court since 2011.
  This court was the first Felony Veterans' Treatment Court in the 
United States, addressing the issues that led to a veteran's contact 
with the criminal justice system.
  Judge Cooper has served Hamilton County with diligence and honor.
  I want to thank Judge Cooper again and wish her the best in the 
future.

                          ____________________
```
