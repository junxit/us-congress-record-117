# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 2021-12-07

```
                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR TUESDAY,

                            DECEMBER 7, 2021

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: to hold hearings 
to examine the nominations of Parisa Salehi, of the District of

SD-215.

Russia policy, 2:30 p.m., SD-G50/VTC.

Guantanamo, 10 a.m., SH-216.

hearing to examine the U.S. Capitol Police following the January 6th 
attack on the Capitol, 10 a.m., SR-301.
```
