# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, most Holy, we are grateful that You are more ready to hear us 
than we are to pray. We praise You that You know our needs even before 
we call. Provide us with listening ears, responsive hearts, and willing 
spirits.
  Lord, bless our Senators. Fill their lives with meaning and shower 
them with faith. Reveal to them the issues that matter most so that 
their work will glorify You. Keep them from becoming weary in doing 
what is right, as they embrace Your promise that their harvest will 
come.
  We pray in Your righteous Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
