# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```

                     COMMITTEE ON FOREIGN RELATIONS

session of the Senate on Monday, December 6, 2021, at 6 p.m., to
```
