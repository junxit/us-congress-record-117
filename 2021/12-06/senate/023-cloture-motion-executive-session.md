# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 585, Rachael S. Rollins, of 
     Massachusetts, to be United States Attorney for the District 
     of Massachusetts for the term of four years.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Patrick J. Leahy, 
           Mazie K. Hirono, Tammy Duckworth, Jon Tester, Patty 
           Murray, Debbie Stabenow, Amy Klobuchar, Mark R. Warner, 
           Catherine Cortez Masto, Cory A. Booker, Tammy Baldwin, 
           Bernard Sanders, Elizabeth Warren, Gary C. Peters.

                          ____________________
```
