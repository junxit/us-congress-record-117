# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 482, Michael D. Smith, of Virginia, 
     to be Chief Executive Officer of the Corporation for National 
     and Community Service.
         Charles E. Schumer, Tammy Baldwin, Angus S. King, Jr., 
           Patrick J. Leahy, John W. Hickenlooper, Jon Ossoff, 
           Richard J. Durbin, Jeff Merkley, Benjamin L. Cardin, 
           Sheldon Whitehouse, Kirsten E. Gillibrand, Mark Kelly, 
           Kyrsten Sinema, Joe Manchin III, Edward J. Markey, 
           Richard Blumenthal, Jack Reed, Raphael G. Warnock, 
           Jeanne Shaheen.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
mandatory quorum calls for the cloture motions filed today, December 6, 
be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
