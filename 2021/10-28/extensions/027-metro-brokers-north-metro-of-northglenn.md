# METRO BROKERS NORTH METRO OF NORTHGLENN

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                METRO BROKERS NORTH METRO OF NORTHGLENN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 28, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Metro 
Brokers North Metro of Northglenn, Colorado for receiving the City of 
Northglenn Business of the Year Award in Longevity.
  Metro Brokers North Metro is a full-service real estate house 
dedicated to helping buyers, sellers, commercial tenants, landlords, 
and investors manage their profits. Their team of knowledgeable and 
dedicated agents gives their clients a host of valuable information, 
leading to better decisions and an accurate understanding of their 
assets. This commitment to providing quality service has distinguished 
this business for many years, over which they have built a thriving 
organization that seeks to provide the best possible service.
  It is my honor to extend my thanks to Metro Brokers North Metro for 
their superb business that has truly stood the test of time. Real 
estate is an industry that can be easily abused if led by the wrong 
people, but this team has consistently demonstrated their honesty, 
celerity, and transparency when it comes to assisting their clients. 
The community at Northglenn applauds their service and anticipates 
their business for many years to come.

                          ____________________
```
