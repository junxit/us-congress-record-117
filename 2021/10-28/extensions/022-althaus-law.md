# ALTHAUS LAW

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                              ALTHAUS LAW

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 28, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Althaus Law 
of Northglenn, Colorado for earning the City of Northglenn's Small 
Business of the Year Award.
  The Small Business of the Year Award recognizes a local business with 
up to twenty employees that had an outstanding year of business and 
leadership in the community. Althaus Law, led by founder Jeff Althaus, 
is dedicated to estate law in Colorado, helping countless families plan 
for their future through real estate investing. With an emphasis on 
giving back to the community, Althaus Law is an engaged and valued 
small business in Northglenn.
  Congratulations to Althaus Law for receiving this award and I wish 
them all the best in the future. I thank them for their contribution to 
our community.

                          ____________________
```
