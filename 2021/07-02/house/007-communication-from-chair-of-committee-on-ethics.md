# COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON ETHICS

**Measures:** H.Res. 73

```
            COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON ETHICS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the chair of the Committee on Ethics:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                Committee on Ethics, July 1, 2021.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Pelosi: On June 1, 2021, the Committee on 
     Ethics (Committee) received an appeal from Representative 
     Virginia Foxx of a fine imposed pursuant to House Resolution 
     73. The appeal was received after the Committee adopted its 
     written rules.
       On June 29, 2021, a majority of the Committee agreed to the 
     appeal.
           Sincerely,
     Theodore E. Deutch,
       Chairman.
     Jackie Walorski,
       Ranking Member.

       

                          ____________________
```
