# LAURYN KARIS JENKINS

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                          LAURYN KARIS JENKINS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 4, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Lauryn Karis Jenkins for earning the Top Photography Award for her 
creative and unique photograph as part of the 2021 Congressional Art 
Competition.
  As a first-time participant in the art show and a student at Wheat 
Ridge High School, Lauryn showcased a photograph entitled, ``In a 
Dream''. This artwork used traditional photography and is a black and 
white print of shadow puppets.
  Lauryn spent countless hours and used numerous tools to learn new 
techniques and refine her photography skills and education in the arts. 
I appreciate her participation in the competition and the support 
provided by her teacher at Wheat Ridge High School.
  Congratulations to Lauryn Karis Jenkins for her hard work and 
participation in the 2021 Congressional Art Competition. I am certain 
she will exhibit the same dedication and character in her future 
accomplishments, and I wish her the best of luck going forward.

                          ____________________
```
