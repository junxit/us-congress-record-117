# SHEEA SAU

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                               SHEEA SAU

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 4, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Sheea Sau for earning the ``Best in Show'' Award and second place 
finish for her creative and unique drawing as part of the 2021 
Congressional Art Competition.
  As a first-time participant in this art show, this student from 
Golden Senior High School created a work of art ``Lost in the Madness 
of Uncertainty''. This artwork is a portrait using acrylic paint, 
prisma color pencil, and gel pen, marker.
  Sheea spent countless hours and used numerous tools to learn new 
techniques and refine her drawing skills and education in the arts. I 
appreciate her participation in the competition and the support 
provided by her teacher at Golden Senior High School.
  Congratulations to Sheea Sau for her hard work and participation in 
the 2021 Congressional Art Competition. I am certain she will exhibit 
the same dedication and character in her future accomplishments, and I 
wish her the best of luck going forward.

                          ____________________
```
