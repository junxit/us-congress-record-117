# HONORING NATASHA LEWIS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                         HONORING NATASHA LEWIS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 4, 2021

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Ms. 
Natasha Lewis.
  Ms. Lewis graduated on April 29, 2021, obtaining her Ph.D. in 
Pharmaceutical Sciences from the University of Mississippi. Ms. Lewis 
is originally from Port Gibson, Mississippi and is a graduate of Port 
Gibson High School, Class of 2013.
  While at the University of Mississippi, Ms. Lewis was a Lucky Day 
Scholar and received numerous professional certifications--IPE Naloxone 
Training, delivering MTM services, Pharmacy-Centered Diabetes Care, 
Pharmacy-Based Immunization Delivery and Basic Life Support (BLS) 
Certification. Ms. Lewis' professional leadership, honors and awards 
include serving as Community Services Chair SNPhA, Pharmacy Ambassador, 
the nominating Committee Chair of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, 
Incorporated, 2nd Vice President of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority and the 
recipient of the Walgreen Diversity Scholarship.
  Ms. Lewis served as a Pharmacy Intern at the University of 
Mississippi Medical Center, as well as the CVS Pharmacy in Oxford. She 
is the daughter of Mrs. Norma Thompson-Lewis of Port Gibson, MS, and 
Mr. Joseph Lewis of Boston, MA. Ms. Lewis is the twin sister to Natalie 
Lewis who received her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Baptist 
College of Health Science and is currently a Traveling Nurse.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Ms. 
Natasha Lewis for her passion and dedication for success and desire to 
make a difference in the community.

                          ____________________
```
