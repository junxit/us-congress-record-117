# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 51

```
Committee Meetings
ENDING THE CYCLE: EXAMINING WAYS TO PREVENT DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND 
PROMOTE HEALTHY COMMUNITIES
Committee on Education and Labor: Subcommittee on Civil Rights and 
Human Services held a hearing entitled ``Ending the Cycle: Examining 
Ways to Prevent Domestic Violence and Promote Healthy Communities''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
LIFT AMERICA: REVITALIZING OUR NATION'S INFRASTRUCTURE AND ECONOMY
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Full Committee held a hearing 
entitled ``LIFT America: Revitalizing our Nation's Infrastructure and 
Economy''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
H.R. 51: MAKING D.C. THE 51ST STATE
Committee on Oversight and Reform: Full Committee held a hearing 
entitled ``H.R. 51: Making D.C. the 51st State''. Testimony was heard 
from Muriel Bowser, Mayor, District of Columbia, Washington D.C.; 
Fitzroy Lee, Interim Chief Financial Officer, Washington D.C.; Phil 
Mendelson, Chairman, Council of the District of Columbia, Washington 
D.C.; Mainon Schwartz, Legislative Attorney, Congressional Research 
Service, Library of Congress; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
No joint committee meetings were held.
```
