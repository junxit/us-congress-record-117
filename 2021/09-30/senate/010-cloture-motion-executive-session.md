# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Carper, Thomas R. (C000174) [D-DE]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before 
the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close the debate on the 
     nomination of Executive Calendar No. 354, Rohit Chopra, of 
     the District of Columbia, to be Director, Bureau of Consumer 
     Financial Protection for a term of five years.
         Charles E. Schumer, Christopher Murphy, Martin Heinrich, 
           Edward J. Markey, Patty Murray, Tina Smith, Tammy

           Alex Padilla, Tammy Duckworth, Richard J. Durbin, 
           Richard Blumenthal, Jacky Rosen, Robert P. Casey, Jr., 
           Gary C. Peters.

call has been waived.

nomination of Rohit Chopra, of the District of Columbia, to be 
Director, Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection for a term of five 
years, shall be brought to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
  The clerk will call the roll.
```
