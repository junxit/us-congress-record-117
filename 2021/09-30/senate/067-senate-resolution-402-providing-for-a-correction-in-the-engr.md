# SENATE RESOLUTION 402--PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF S. RES. 357

**Measures:** S.Res. 357, S.Res. 402

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 402--PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF 
                              S. RES. 357

  Mr. HAWLEY submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 402

       Resolved, That in the engrossment of the resolution S. Res. 
     357, the Secretary of the Senate shall make the following 
     correction:
       (1) In the sixth whereas clause of the preamble, strike
```
