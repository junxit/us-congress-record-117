# EXPLANATION OF "NO" VOTE ON H.R. 1629

**Speaking:** McKinley, David B. (M001180) [R-WV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1629

```
                EXPLANATION OF ``NO'' VOTE ON H.R. 1629

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID B. McKINLEY

                            of west virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 12, 2021

  Mr. McKINLEY. Madam Speaker, I voted No on H.R. 1629. Although I 
support the bill, I do not support the tactics by which it was brought 
to the floor. H.R. 1629 was once a bipartisan bill but now, like so 
many other good bills, it will fail due to the pettiness of 
partisanship.

                          ____________________
```
