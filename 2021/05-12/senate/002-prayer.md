# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, continue to guard the path of the godly. Lord, You have 
filled us with reverential awe for You. So please keep our feet from 
stumbling or slipping. We acknowledge that without the shield of Your 
favor, our lawmakers labor in vain.
  Bless them as they embrace Your promise in Psalm 84:11, which states, 
``The Lord will withhold no good thing from those who do what is 
right.'' May this promise motivate our Senators to refuse to deviate 
from integrity.
  Mighty God, be a refuge and strength for us all.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
