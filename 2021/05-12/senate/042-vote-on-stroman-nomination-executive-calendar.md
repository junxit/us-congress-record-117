**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                       Vote on Stroman nomination

  Mr. SANDERS. I know of no further debate on the Stroman nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there further debate?
  Hearing none, the question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to 
the Stroman nomination?
```
