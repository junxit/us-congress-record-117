# NOTICE OF A TIE VOTE UNDER S. RES. 27

**Speaking:** Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA]

**Measures:** S.Res. 27

```
                 NOTICE OF A TIE VOTE UNDER S. RES. 27

  Mrs. MURRAY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to print the 
following letter in the Congressional Record.
  There being no objection, the material was ordered to be printed in 
the Record, as follows:

     To the Secretary of the Senate:
       PN1261, the nomination of Jennifer Ann Abruzzo, of New 
     York, to be General Counsel of the National Labor Relations 
     Board, having been referred to the Committee on Health, 
     Education, Labor, and Pensions, the Committee, with a quorum 
     present, has voted on the nomination as follows--
       On the question of reporting the nomination without 
     recommendation, 11 ayes to 11 noes.
       In accordance with section 3, paragraph (1)(A) of S. Res. 
     27 of the 117th Congress, I hereby give notice that the 
     Committee has not reported the nomination because of a tie 
     vote, and ask that this notice be printed in the Record 
     pursuant to the resolution.

                          ____________________
```
