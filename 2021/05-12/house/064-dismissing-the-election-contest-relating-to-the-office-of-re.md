# DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FOURTEENTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF ILLINOIS

**Measures:** H.Res. 379, H.Res. 380

```
       DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF 
 REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FOURTEENTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF ILLINOIS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 7 of House Resolution 
380, House Resolution 379 is hereby adopted.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 379

       Resolved, That the election contest relating to the office 
     of Representative from the Fourteenth Congressional District 
     of Illinois is dismissed.
```
