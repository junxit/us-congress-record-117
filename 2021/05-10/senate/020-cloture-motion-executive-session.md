# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The cloture motion having been 
presented under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the 
motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 112, Donet Dominic Graves, Jr., of 
     Ohio, to be Deputy Secretary of Commerce.
         Charles E. Schumer, Jon Ossoff, Margaret Wood Hassan, 
           Richard J. Durbin, Sheldon Whitehouse, Ron Wyden, 
           Benjamin L. Cardin, Jacky Rosen, Cory A. Booker, Tammy 
           Duckworth, Tammy Baldwin, Jon Tester, Edward J. Markey, 
           John Hickenlooper, Brian Schatz, Gary C. Peters, Tina 
           Smith, Christopher Murphy.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Finally, I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory 
quorum calls for the cloture motions filed today, May 10, be waived.

                          ____________________
```
