# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, vindicate those who cling to the truth. They seek to 
walk on Your path, to honor You with their work, and to glorify You 
with their lives. May these lawmakers remember that You are their light 
and salvation, bestowing mercy and honor on them.
  Lord, You refuse to withhold any good thing from those who walk 
blameless before You. Continue to strengthen our Senators, who strive 
to please You with their thoughts, words, and actions. Empower them as 
they seek and pursue peace for our Nation and world.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
