# COMMUNICATION FROM THE SERGEANT AT ARMS

**Measures:** H.Res. 8

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE SERGEANT AT ARMS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Sergeant at Arms of the House of 
Representatives:

                                   Office of the Sergeant at Arms,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, March 29, 2021.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to section 3(s) of House 
     Resolution 8, following consultation with the Office of 
     Attending Physician, I write to provide you further 
     notification that the public health emergency due to the 
     novel coronavirus SARS-CoV-2 remains in effect.
           Sincerely,
                                              Timothy P. Blodgett,
     Acting Sergeant at Arms.

                          ____________________
```
