# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 374

**Measures:** H.R. 374

```
            By Mrs. BOEBERT:
        H.R. 374.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (Commerce Clause) of the 
     Constitution of the United States which grants Congress the 
     power ``To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes''; and
       Article I, Section 9, Clause 7 (Appropriations Clause) of 
     the Constitution of the United States which grants Congress 
     the power of the purse and ensures ``No money shall be drawn 
     from the Treasury, but in consequence of appropriations made 
     by law.''
```
