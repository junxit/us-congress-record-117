# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 22, 2021

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 22, 2021

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., Friday, 
January 22; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; finally, that upon conclusion of 
morning business, the Senate proceed to executive session as provided 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, now, for the information of Senators, 
there will be a rollcall vote on confirmation of the Austin nomination 
to be Secretary of Defense at approximately 10:30 tomorrow.
  We also hope to vote on confirmation of the Yellen and Blinken 
nominations this week.

                          ____________________
```
