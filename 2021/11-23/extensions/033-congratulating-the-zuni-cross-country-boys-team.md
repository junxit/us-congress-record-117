# CONGRATULATING THE ZUNI CROSS-COUNTRY BOYS TEAM

**Speaking:** Leger Fernandez, Teresa (L000273) [D-NM]

```
            CONGRATULATING THE ZUNI CROSS-COUNTRY BOYS TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. TERESA LEGER FERNANDEZ

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 23, 2021

  Ms. LEGER FERNANDEZ. Madam Speaker, I am pleased to send my 
congratulations to the Zuni Cross-Country Boys Team who won the New 
Mexico State Cross Country Championships for 2021. Their win is even 
more impressive during a pandemic that made it impossible to train last 
year. They make us all proud.
  Zuni's cross-country program is in a class of its own. I am so 
impressed with its leadership in the running world especially in 
hosting one of the largest meets with more than 30 schools in 
attendance. It is an honor to represent a community where the running 
culture is so strong.
  I extend my warmest wishes for the future. I have no doubt Zuni will 
continue to impress us all through the ages.

                          ____________________
```
