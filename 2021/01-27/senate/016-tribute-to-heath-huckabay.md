# TRIBUTE TO HEATH HUCKABAY

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN], Sullivan, Dan (S001198) [R-AK]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO HEATH HUCKABAY

  Mrs. BLACKBURN. Mr. President, this week Team Blackburn is saying 
goodbye to Heath Huckabay, who came to us last January as part of a 
fellowship program between my office and the Oak Ridge National 
Laboratory, which is located in Oak Ridge, just outside of Knoxville, 
TN.
  It has been an interesting year to say the least, but Heath has risen 
to the occasion and impressed each and every one of us with his breadth 
of knowledge and his ability to adapt to the hectic pace of life in the 
Senate. We did our best to persuade him to stay with us a little 
longer, but I am confident that his colleagues at Oak Ridge will be 
happy to see him return, as well as his wife Emily and little Elliot. 
They are looking forward to having him home.
  It was an honor working with him this past year. We will miss him, 
and we wish him all the best as he heads back to Tennessee.
  I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SULLIVAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Ms. Baldwin). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
