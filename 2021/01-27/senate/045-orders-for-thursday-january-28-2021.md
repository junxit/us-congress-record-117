# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 28, 2021

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 28, 2021

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, January 28; further, that following the prayer and the Pledge 
of Allegiance, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate be in a period 
of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
