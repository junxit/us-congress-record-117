# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Environment and Public Works: Committee ordered favorably 
reported the nomination of Michael Stanley Regan, of North Carolina, to 
be Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency; and an 
original resolution authorizing expenditures by the committee and 
adopted its rules of procedure for the 117th Congress.
NOMINATION
Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: Committee 
concluded a hearing to examine the nomination of Neera Tanden, of 
Massachusetts, to be Director of the Office of Management and Budget, 
after the nominee, who was introduced by Senators Klobuchar and Booker, 
testified and answered questions in her own behalf.
```
