# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                       ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the trial 
adjourn until 12 noon tomorrow, Wednesday, February 10; and that this 
order also constitute the adjournment of the Senate.
  There being no objection, at 5:10 p.m., the Senate, sitting as a 
Court of Impeachment, adjourned until Wednesday, February 10, 2021, at 
12 noon.
```
