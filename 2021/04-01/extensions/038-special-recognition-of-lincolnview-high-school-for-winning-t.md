# SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF LINCOLNVIEW HIGH SCHOOL FOR WINNING THE DIVISION V STATE CHEERLEADING CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF LINCOLNVIEW HIGH SCHOOL FOR WINNING THE DIVISION 
                   V STATE CHEERLEADING CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. LATTA. Madam Speaker, it is with a great deal of pride that I 
rise to pay a very special tribute to an outstanding high school 
cheerleading squad in Ohio's Fifth Congressional District. The young 
athletes of the Lincolnview High School cheerleading squad have 
represented their school ably on their way to becoming the Division V, 
Building Category, State Cheerleading champs. In their effort to 
surpass all other squads in the Division V State Cheerleading 
Championship, the Lincolnview cheerleaders employed outstanding 
teamwork, overcoming the challenges posed by intense competition.
  Due to COVID-19 restrictions, the Lincolnview cheerleaders had to 
radically adjust their practice routine to a virtual mode as they 
prepared for their normal season and STATE competition. The squad 
submitted a well-rehearsed competition video in February and after 
judging, STATE athletic officials delivered the championship trophy to 
the squad in March. The Lincolview cheerleading squad has won the 
championship 2 years in a row and three times in the last 4 years. The 
members of this very special squad have carried on that tradition by 
showing both the individual efforts and teamwork that their sport 
requires to achieve championship performance. Their hard work and 
dedication which culminated in this accomplishment is truly 
outstanding.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in paying special 
tribute to the Lincolnview High School cheerleaders. On behalf of the 
people of the Fifth District of Ohio, I am proud to recognize this 
great achievement.

                          ____________________
```
