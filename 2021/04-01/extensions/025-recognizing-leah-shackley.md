# RECOGNIZING LEAH SHACKLEY

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING LEAH SHACKLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Leah Shackley, a freshman at Bedford High School, on 
earning two medals at the 2021 PIAA Class 2A Swimming Championship.
  Leah won a silver medal in the girls 100-yard Backstroke with a time 
of 55.65 seconds. She also collected a sixth-place finish in the girls 
100-yard Freestyle with a time of 52.79 seconds. Leading up to the 
state championship, Leah collected first place finishes in both events 
at the District 6 Championship. She is a member of the Blair Regional 
YMCA Tigersharks Swim Team. In addition to these two state medals, she 
has won four medals in the Western Pennsylvania District Championships 
and a USA Eastern Zones championship award.
  On behalf of the 13th District of Pennsylvania, I congratulate Leah 
Shackley on a successful season and wish her every continued success.

                          ____________________
```
