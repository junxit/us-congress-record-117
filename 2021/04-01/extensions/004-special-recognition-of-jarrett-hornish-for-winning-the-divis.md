# SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF JARRETT HORNISH FOR WINNING THE DIVISION III WRESTLING STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
  SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF JARRETT HORNISH FOR WINNING THE DIVISION III 
                      WRESTLING STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. LATTA. Madam Speaker, it is with a great deal of pride that I 
rise to pay a very special tribute to an outstanding high school 
wrestler in Ohio's Fifth Congressional District. Jarrett Hornish of 
Wayne Trace High School has represented his school ably on his way to 
achieving the Division III State wrestling title. Jarrett overcame the 
challenges posed by intense competition in his effort to win the 
Division III State Wrestling Championship.
  In pursuing the State championship, Jarrett Hornish defeated his 
opponent in the finals of the 126-pound classification. His hard work 
and dedication, both on and off the mat, in achieving this 
accomplishment is truly outstanding. He has achieved an impressive 
season record bringing pride to his school and community.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in paying special 
tribute to Jarrett Hornish of Wayne Trace High School. On behalf of the 
people of the Fifth District of Ohio, I am proud to recognize this 
great achievement.
```
