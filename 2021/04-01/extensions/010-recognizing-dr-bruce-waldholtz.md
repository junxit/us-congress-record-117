# RECOGNIZING DR. BRUCE WALDHOLTZ

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                    RECOGNIZING DR. BRUCE WALDHOLTZ

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize Dr. 
Bruce Waldholtz for receiving the American Cancer Society's National 
Volunteer Leadership Award.
  For 20 years, Dr. Waldholtz has generously devoted his time and 
energy as a volunteer with the American Cancer Society. Together with 
the American Cancer Society, Dr. Waldholtz is working to raise 
awareness of issues surrounding colorectal cancer, including screening 
methods and the importance of early detection. He has worked in his 
local community and has been an advocate for important legislation that 
would help to prevent and treat colorectal cancer. Recently, Dr. 
Waldholtz partnered with police chiefs in his

[[Page E325]]

area to launch a social media awareness campaign encouraging members of 
their community to follow current guidance on cancer screenings and 
prevention.
  Congratulations to my friend Dr. Bruce Waldholtz on receiving this 
well-deserved award. I am grateful for his work to protect American 
patients, and I wish him continued success in this lifesaving advocacy.

                          ____________________
```
