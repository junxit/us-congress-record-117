# RECOGNIZING MIKE MASTOVICH

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MIKE MASTOVICH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise to congratulate 
Johnstown Tribune-Democrat reporter Mike Mastovich on being named CNHI 
LLC's 2020 top sportswriter in CNHI's Division I. Mike has been with 
the Johnstown Tribune-Democrat since 1986 and has covered the Johnstown 
sports community for over 35 years.
  Throughout his career, Mike has told important stories for the 
Johnstown community--from record-setting coaching achievements at UPJ 
to Johnstown's professional hockey journey and countless high school 
sports milestones. Mike is at the center of our region's sports history 
and is a treasured member of this community.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's 13th Congressional District, it is my 
privilege to congratulate Mike Mastovich on this outstanding 
achievement and wish him continued success.

                          ____________________
```
