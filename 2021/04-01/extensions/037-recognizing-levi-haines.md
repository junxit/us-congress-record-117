# RECOGNIZING LEVI HAINES

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING LEVI HAINES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Levi Haines of Biglerville High School for winning the 
PIAA Class 2A Wrestling Championship.
  As a standout wrestler, Haines earned a gold medal in the 145-pound 
championship match against rival Nathan Higley of Sullivan County. In 
the past two years, he captured back-to-back silver medals in the PIAA 
Championship match. Haines finished this season undefeated winning 
thirty matches. As a junior, he has collected a career record of 100-5. 
He is coached by his father, Ken Haines, who also won a state 
championship in 1995 for Gettysburg. He has become the fourth student 
at Biglerville to earn this prestigious title.
  On behalf of the 13th District of Pennsylvania, I congratulate Levi 
Haines on a successful season and wish him every continued success as 
he pursues his education and athletic goals.

                          ____________________
```
