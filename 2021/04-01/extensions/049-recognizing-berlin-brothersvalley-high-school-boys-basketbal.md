# RECOGNIZING BERLIN BROTHERSVALLEY HIGH SCHOOL BOYS BASKETBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
   RECOGNIZING BERLIN BROTHERSVALLEY HIGH SCHOOL BOYS BASKETBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate the Berlin Brothersvalley High School boys basketball team 
on finishing second in the PIAA Class IA Championship.
  This was the first time in school history that the Berlin 
Brothersvalley team advanced to the PIAA state title round. The 51-47 
point game against Nativity BVM was decided in overtime. The team was 
led by Elijah Sechler, who scored 20 points and collected 11 rebounds. 
Pace Prosser scored 15 points while Abe Countryman grabbed 13 rebounds.
  The 2020-2021 players for the Berlin Brothersvalley Mountaineers 
were: Ryan Blubaugh, Isaac Etris, Will Spochart, Preston Foor, Tyler 
Miller, Elijah Sechler, Caden Montgomery, Lukas Coughenour, Tuck 
Hillegass, Craig Jarvis, Pace Prosser, Abe Countryman, Caleb Rohrs, 
Brayden Deeter, and Jalen Melton. The students were led by head coach 
Tanner Prosser.
  On behalf of the 13th District of Pennsylvania, I congratulate these 
students and their coaches on a successful season and wish them every 
continued success.

                          ____________________
```
