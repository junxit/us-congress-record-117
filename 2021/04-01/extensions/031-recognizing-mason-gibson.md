# RECOGNIZING MASON GIBSON

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING MASON GIBSON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Mason Gibson of Bishop McCort High School for achieving 
second place in the PIAA Class 2A Wresting Championship.
  Gibson had a remarkable freshman season competing in the 120-pound 
championship match against state champion Brett Ungar. Mason, the top-
seeded freshman, had previously won the Super 32 title last year as an 
Eighth grader.
  On behalf of the 13th District of Pennsylvania, I congratulate Mason 
on a successful season and wish him every continued success as he 
pursues his education and athletic goals.

                          ____________________
```
