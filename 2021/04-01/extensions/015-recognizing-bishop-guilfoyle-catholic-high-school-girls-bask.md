# RECOGNIZING BISHOP GUILFOYLE CATHOLIC HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS BASKETBALL

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
   RECOGNIZING BISHOP GUILFOYLE CATHOLIC HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS BASKETBALL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate the Bishop Guilfoyle Catholic High School girls basketball 
team on winning the PIAA Class 1A Championship.
  The 2020-2021 Bishop Guilfoyle Catholic's team consists of 16 
players, including: Aurielle Brunner, Sarah Geishauser, Sophia Warner, 
Claire Cammarata, Bella Adams, Jasen Quinn, Lainey Farabaugh, Ally 
Bagley, Lah Homan, Alyssa Franks, Reagan McClurkin, Finley Steinbugl, 
Daniela Scipioni, Teresa Haigh, Aley Beauchamp, and Tessa Klock.
  This victory marks Bishop Guilfoyle Catholic's seventh PIAA 
championship in girls basketball and first state championship since 
2010. The team won a decisive 62-47 victory over Jenkins High School. 
The team was led by Teresa Haigh who scored 16 points and added 10 
rebounds. Lah Homan collected her 1000th career point during the game 
by scoring 14 points and grabbing 13 rebounds.
  The students were coached by Kristi Kaack and Adam Kaack.
  On behalf of the 13th District of Pennsylvania, I congratulate these 
students and their coaches on their tremendous season and wish them 
every continued success.

                          ____________________
```
