# RECOGNIZING THE STUDENTS OF JOHNSTOWN CHRISTIAN SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
         RECOGNIZING THE STUDENTS OF JOHNSTOWN CHRISTIAN SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize the 
students of Johnstown Christian School in Hollsopple, Pennsylvania, for 
their compassion, work ethic, and community service.
  During the school's second annual ``Feed the Need'' packing party, 
students labeled, filled, and sealed more than 10,000 packages of 
nutritious meals to feed children both in Haiti and in the Greater 
Johnstown region. In total, 8,500 of the meals will reach children in 
Haiti who are struggling with food insecurity, and the remaining 1,500 
will be sent to local organizations that provide services in the 
Johnstown community. Acts of service like this are invaluable to our 
Johnstown community both in teaching compassion to our students and in 
providing help to those in need. I am grateful for the Johnstown 
Christian School, and it is my hope they continue to be a model for 
success.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's 13th Congressional District, I thank the 
students of Johnstown Christian School for their work to help those in 
need both in their community and in Haiti.

                          ____________________
```
