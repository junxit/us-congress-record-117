# RECOGNIZING JULIA STRINE

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JULIA STRINE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 1, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Julia Strine on winning a state championship in the 2021 
PIAA Class 3A swimming contest.
  Julia is a senior at Shippensburg High School. She won the girls 100-
yard Breaststroke with a time of 1:02.33 winning by just .29 seconds. 
Julia showed true determination and grit to win the gold medal. Prior 
to winning this prestigious award, she placed seventh at the PIAA 
swimming championships her freshman year. Her desire to win a gold 
medal was spurred by this experience. Continued determination has led 
her to be named Female Athlete of the Winter in the Waynesboro Record 
Herald.
  On behalf of the 13th District of Pennsylvania, I congratulate Julia 
Strine on a successful season and wish her every continued success as 
she pursues her education and athletic goals.

                          ____________________
```
