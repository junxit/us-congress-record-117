# Order of Procedure (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Wyden, Ron (W000779) [D-OR]

```
                           Order of Procedure

  Mr. WYDEN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding rule XXII, all postcloture time on executive Calendar 
Nos. 264 and 159 be considered expired at 4:45 p.m. today; that the 
vote on confirmation of the Anderson nomination occur at 4:45 p.m. 
today; and that the vote on confirmation of the Meyer nomination occur 
at a time to be determined by the majority leader, following 
consultation with the Republican leader on Thursday, September 30.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The senior Senator from Oregon.
```
