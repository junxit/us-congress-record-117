# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], Sasse, Ben (S001197) [R-NE]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to executive session and resume consideration of the following 
nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of Jessica 
Lewis, of Ohio, to be an Assistant Secretary of State (Political-
Military Affairs).
  Mr. McCONNELL. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SASSE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
