# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who rules over the waters, hear our cry for help. Lord, 
protect and defend this land we love. Guide our Nation through the 
night with Your light from above, using our lawmakers to do Your work 
on Earth. Lord, stand close to our Senators. Be their shepherd in these 
challenging times. Give them strength for life's marathon, as You bless 
them with Your peace.
  Mighty God, we will not be defeated, because we place our hope in 
You.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
