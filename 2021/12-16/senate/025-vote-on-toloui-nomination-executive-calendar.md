# Vote on Toloui Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Warner, Mark R. (W000805) [D-VA], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       Vote on Toloui Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Warnock). The question is, Will the Senate 
advise and consent to the Toloui nomination?
  Mr. WARNER. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from California (Mrs. 
Feinstein) and the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Leahy) are necessarily 
absent.

from Wyoming (Ms. Lummis), the Senator from Idaho (Mr. Risch), the 
Senator from South Dakota (Mr. Rounds), and the Senator from Alabama 
(Mr. Shelby).
```
