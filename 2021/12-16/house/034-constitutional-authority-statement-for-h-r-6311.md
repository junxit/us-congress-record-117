# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6311

**Measures:** H.R. 6311

```
            By Mrs. CAROLYN B. MALONEY of New York:
       H.R. 6311.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the Constitution of the 
     United States (the Necessary and Proper Clause) grants 
     Congress the power to enact this legislation.
```
