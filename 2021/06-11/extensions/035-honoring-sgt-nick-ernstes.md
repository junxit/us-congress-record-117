# HONORING SGT. NICK ERNSTES

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                       HONORING SGT. NICK ERNSTES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 11, 2021

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the heroic 
actions of Sgt. Nick Ernstes of the Hancock County Sheriff's 
Department.
  While off-duty, Sgt. Ernstes saved the life of a woman who had been 
stabbed repeatedly in the parking lot of a local convenience store.
  After arriving on the scene, Sgt. Ernstes acted quickly and applied 
the proper medical attention that likely prevented the women from 
losing her life.
  Thank God for Sgt. Nick Ernstes for his heroic actions and for 
serving our community. And I thank every member of law enforcement for 
bravely fulfilling their mission to protect and serve.

                          ____________________
```
