  Ms. CORTEZ MASTO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nominations: Calendar Nos. 18 through 25 and all nominations placed on 
the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Marine Corps, Navy, and 
Space Force Service; that the nominations be confirmed en bloc; that 
the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table en 
bloc with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions be 
in order to any of the nominations; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action and the Senate then resume legislative 
session.
