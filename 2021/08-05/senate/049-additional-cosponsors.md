                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 350

  At the request of Ms. Hassan, the name of the Senator from Wisconsin 
(Ms. Baldwin) was added as a cosponsor of S. 350, a bill to amend the 
Public Health Service Act to reauthorize certain programs under part A 
of title XI of such Act relating to genetic diseases, and for other 
purposes.

(Mr. Cotton) was added as a cosponsor of S. 401, a bill to amend the 
Public Health Service Act to prohibit governmental discrimination
