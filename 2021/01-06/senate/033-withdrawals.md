```
                              WITHDRAWALS

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
January 6, 2021 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nominations:

Kirstjen Nielsen, resigned, which was sent to the Senate on January 3, 
2021.

January 6, 2021 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the
```
