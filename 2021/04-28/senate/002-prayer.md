# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, give our lawmakers the wisdom to remember to be grateful 
for all the things You have already done.
  Lord, You have sustained our Nation during seasons of war and peace. 
You have helped us find creative ways to strive for a more perfect 
Union. You have provided us with solutions to difficult problems just 
when we needed You most.
  Eternal Spirit, let this day be a time when Senators feel gratitude 
for Your bountiful blessings and faithfulness. May they express this 
gratitude by striving to live one day at a time, focusing on Your 
mercy, love, and grace.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
