# EXECUTIVE MESSAGES REFERRED

```
                      EXECUTIVE MESSAGES REFERRED

  In executive session the Presiding Officer laid before the Senate 
messages from the President of the United States submitting sundry 
nominations which were referred to the appropriate committees.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S2287, April 28, 2021, in the middle of the second 
column, the following appears: EXECUTIVE MESSAGES REFERRED In 
executive session the Presiding Offiicer laid before the Senate 
messages from the President of the United States submitting sundry 
nominations which were referred to the appropriate committees
  
  The online Record has been corrected to read: EXECUTIVE MESSAGES 
REFERRED In executive session the Presiding Officer laid before 
the Senate messages from the President of the United States 
submitting sundry nominations which were referred to the 
appropriate committees


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


  (The messages received today are printed at the end of the Senate 
proceedings.)

                          ____________________
```
