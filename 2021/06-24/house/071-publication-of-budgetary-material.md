      ALLOCATIONS AND OTHER BUDGETARY LEVELS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2022

                                         House of Representatives,


                                      Committee on the Budget,

                                    Washington, DC, June 24, 2021.
       Madam Speaker; Pursuant to sections 1 and 3 of House 
     Resolution 467 (117th Congress) and the Congressional Budget 
     Act of 1974, I hereby submit for printing in the 
     Congressional Record: (1) allocations for fiscal year 2022 
     for the House Committee on Appropriations, and (2) a list of 
     discretionary accounts identified for advance appropriations 
     in fiscal year 2022 appropriations bills.
       This filing is made for technical purposes as required by 
     House Resolution 467, and the budgetary material published 
     herein is for the purposes of enforcing titles III and IV of 
     the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 and other budgetary 
     enforcement provisions. If there are any questions, please 
     contact Jennifer Wheelock or Sam Wice of the Budget Committee 
     staff.
