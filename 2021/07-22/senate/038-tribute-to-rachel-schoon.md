# TRIBUTE TO RACHEL SCHOON

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO RACHEL SCHOON

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Rachel Schoon, an intern 
in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has done for 
me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several 
weeks.
  Rachel is from Brandon, SD, where she graduated a semester early from 
homeschool to participate in a 6-month-long mission trip overseas. 
Currently, she is attending South Dakota State University in Brookings, 
SD, where she is pursing degrees in communications and political 
science. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the 
most out of her internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Rachel for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
