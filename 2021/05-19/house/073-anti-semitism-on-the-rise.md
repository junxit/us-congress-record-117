# ANTI-SEMITISM ON THE RISE

**Speaking:** Gohmert, Louie (G000552) [R-TX]

```
                       ANTI-SEMITISM ON THE RISE

  (Mr. GOHMERT asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. GOHMERT. Mr. Speaker, this is a very sad time in America. As was 
discussed earlier today in debate, we used to have bipartisan 
agreements on things. One of those was when anti-Semitism was raising 
its ugly head. One party here on the floor still speaks up against, 
will vote against anti-Semitic statements and call them out for what 
they are, and another will not.
  I heard my friend across the aisle, Brad Sherman, just appropriately 
decrying the violence, these anti-Jewish folks in California, this 
incident. Well, that surely could be something we could once again, 
come back together in a bipartisan way and condemn the 40 percent rise 
in violence against Semitic people. It is time to come together on 
that. It will show the world.

                          ____________________
```
