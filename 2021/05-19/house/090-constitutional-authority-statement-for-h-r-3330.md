# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3330

**Measures:** H.R. 3330

```
            By Mr. GOSAR:
        H.R. 3330.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       the Property Clause of the U.S. Constitution, Article IV, 
     Section 3, Clause 2, which gives Congress authority over the 
     lands, territories, or other property of the United States.
```
