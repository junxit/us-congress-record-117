# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who soars on the wind and stabilizes us through the 
challenges of life, we worship You. You remain the source for all our 
blessings. Your daily mercies prompt us to praise Your Name. Lord, You 
forgive our disobedience and clear our record of guilt.
  Today, use our lawmakers for Your glory. Guide them along the best 
path for their lives as You fill them with Your peace. Lord, as they 
depend on You for each decision, surround them with Your unfailing 
love. Give us all pure hearts so that we may serve You with honor.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
