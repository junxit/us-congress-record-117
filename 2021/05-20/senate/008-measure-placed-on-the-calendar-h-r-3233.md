**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 3233

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3233

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The leader is correct.
  The clerk will read the bill by title for a second time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3233) to establish the National Commission to 
     Investigate the January 6 Attack on the United States Capitol 
     Complex, and for other purposes.

calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further 
proceedings.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection having been heard, the 
bill will be placed on the calendar.
```
