# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, You store up goodness for those who have reverence for 
You. Give our lawmakers such reverential awe for Your purposes that 
they will receive the maximum You desire to give.
  Lord, lavish them with Your protection and grace, blessing them as a 
watching nation and world look at the unfolding of Your prevailing 
providence. May our Senators strive to walk with integrity, staying on 
the path that You have chosen. Show them daily the wonders of Your 
unfailing love. Continue to be their help and shield as they worship 
and trust You.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
