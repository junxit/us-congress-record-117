# DIRECTING THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN THE ENROLLMENT OF THE BILL S. 1605

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 24, S. 1605

```
   DIRECTING THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN THE 
                     ENROLLMENT OF THE BILL S. 1605

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Chair lays before the 
House the following concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 24) Directing 
the Secretary of the Senate to make corrections in the enrollment of 
the bill S. 1605.
  The Clerk read the title of the concurrent resolution.
  There was no objection.
  The text of the concurrent resolution is as follows:

                            S. Con. Res. 24

       Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives 
     concurring), That in the enrollment of the bill S. 1605, the 
     Secretary of the Senate shall make the following corrections:
       (1) In the table in section 2301(a), in the item relating 
     to Eielson Air Force Base, strike ``$44,850,00'' in the 
     amount column and insert ``$44,850,000''.
       (2) In section 4601, in the table relating to Military 
     Construction, Navy, in the item relating to AEGIS Ashore 
     Barracks Planning and Design in Redzikowo, Poland, insert 
     ``0'' in the Conference Authorized column.
  The concurrent resolution was concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
