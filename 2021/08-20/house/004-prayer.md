# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Gary Studniewski, St. Peter's Catholic Church, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Almighty God, we beseech Your blessings upon the proceedings of this 
House in order to govern wisely and justly the United States of America 
for which it stands. Bless all Members and agents of this House that 
they may know the privilege of their service and receive the due reward 
that comes from that service.
  Keep America united under Your care and help our citizens realize the 
blessings of liberty and justice that come about through unity and 
patriotism, You who reign supreme and forever.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
