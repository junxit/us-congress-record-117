# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5068

**Measures:** H.R. 5068

```
            By Mr. JACOBS of New York:
        H.R. 5068.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress ``to provide for the common Defence'', 
     and ``to make Rules for the Government and Regulation of the 
     land and naval Forces'' as enumerated in Article I, section 8 
     of the United States Constitution.
```
