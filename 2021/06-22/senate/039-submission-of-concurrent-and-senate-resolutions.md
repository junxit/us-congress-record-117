read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

       S. Res. 282. A resolution recognizing July 1, 2021, as the 
     100th anniversary of the Government Accountability Office and 
     commending the service of the Government Accountability 
     Office to Congress and the United States; considered and 
     agreed to
