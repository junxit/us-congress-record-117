# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 23, 2021

**Speaking:** Van Hollen, Chris (V000128) [D-MD]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 23, 2021

  Mr. VAN HOLLEN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m., 
Wednesday, June 23; that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; further, upon the conclusion of 
morning business, the Senate proceed to executive session to resume 
consideration of the Boardman nomination; further, that if cloture is 
invoked on the Boardman nomination, all postcloture time expire at 5:45 
p.m.; finally, that if the nomination is confirmed, the motions to 
reconsider are considered made and laid upon the table with no 
intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
