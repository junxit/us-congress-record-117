# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** S. 8

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-80. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Board of 
     Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco, CA, 
     relative to Resolution No. 428-21, recognizing Abortion as 
     Healthcare, objecting to Texas Senate Bill 8 and urging 
     approaches that uplift autonomy for women and other birthing 
     people; urging the Biden Administration and Congress to pass 
     legislation protecting Reproductive Rights; and declaring the 
     City and County of San Francisco a Champion of Reproductive 
     Freedom and Justice; which was referred to the Committee on 
     Energy and Commerce.
```
