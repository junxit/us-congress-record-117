                   COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR WEDNESDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: business 
meeting to consider the nominations of Jessica Rosenworcel, of

Communications Commission, and Alan Davidson, of Maryland, to be

Secretary (Management), Mark Brzezinski, of Virginia, to be

Florida, to be Ambassador to the Kingdom of Belgium, all of the
