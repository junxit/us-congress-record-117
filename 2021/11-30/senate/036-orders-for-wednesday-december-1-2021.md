# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 1, 2021

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4350

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 1, 2021

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, finally, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 12 noon, 
Wednesday, December 1; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and morning business be closed; and that upon the 
conclusion of morning business, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 
4350, the National Defense Authorization Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
