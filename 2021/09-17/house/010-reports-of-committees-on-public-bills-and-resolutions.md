         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

follows:

       Mr. SMITH of Washington: Committee on Armed Services. 
     Supplemental report on H.R. 4350. A bill to authorize

     year, and for other purposes (Rept. 117-118, Pt. 2).
       Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi: Committee on Homeland

     flights or flight segments originating in the United States 
     without additional security re-screening, and for other 
     purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 117-123). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
