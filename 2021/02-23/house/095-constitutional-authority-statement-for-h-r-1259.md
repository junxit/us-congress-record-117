# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1259

**Measures:** H.R. 1259

```
            By Mr. ROSENDALE:
        H.R. 1259.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to establish an uniform Rule of 
     Naturalization as enumerated in Article I, Section 8, Clause 
     4 of the United States Constitution.
```
