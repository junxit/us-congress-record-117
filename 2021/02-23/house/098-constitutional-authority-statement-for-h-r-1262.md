# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1262

**Measures:** H.R. 1262

```
            By Mr. STAUBER:
        H.R. 1262.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution, 
     specifically Clause 3 (related to the regulation of Commerce 
     with foreign Nation, and among the several States, and with 
     Indian tribes).
```
