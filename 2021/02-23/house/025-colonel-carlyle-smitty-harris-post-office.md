unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 208) to designate the facility of the United States

Mississippi, as the ``Colonel Carlyle `Smitty' Harris Post Office'', on 
which the yeas and nays were ordered.

gentleman from Maryland (Mr. Mfume) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
