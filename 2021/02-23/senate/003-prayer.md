# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, in times of trouble You hear us. We cry to You and 
receive Your answers. May we embrace Your precepts as You guide us 
through the fog of our earthly pilgrimage.
  Lord, may our lawmakers trust You for strength. Provide them with the 
powers of wisdom, discipline, and discernment. You have promised that 
those who trust patiently in You will soar high on wings like eagles, 
running without weariness and walking without fainting.
  Mighty God, we praise Your matchless Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
