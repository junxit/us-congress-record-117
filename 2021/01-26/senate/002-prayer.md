# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, the Lord of all, prepare our lawmakers today to serve 
You and country. Give them grateful hearts for Your daily blessings as 
they comprehend that their times are in Your hands. Lord, inspire them 
to make a right relationship with You their top priority. Like a potter 
with clay, mold and make them as You desire, so that they may be 
vessels of honor for Your Kingdom. May they daily seek You and find joy 
in Your presence.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
