# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of Antony 
John Blinken, of New York, to be Secretary of State.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the time 
until 12 noon will be equally divided between the two leaders or their 
designees
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Senator from Illinois.
```
