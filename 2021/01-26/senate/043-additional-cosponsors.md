# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 4, S. 13

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 13

  At the request of Mr. Scott of South Carolina, the names of the 
Senator from Montana (Mr. Daines) and the Senator from Tennessee (Mrs. 
Blackburn) were added as cosponsors of S. 13, a bill to establish an 
advisory committee to make recommendations on improvements to the 
security, integrity, and administration of Federal elections.


                              S.J. RES. 4

  At the request of Mr. Rubio, the names of the Senator from Idaho (Mr. 
Risch), the Senator from Utah (Mr. Lee) and the Senator from Montana 
(Mr. Daines) were added as cosponsors of S.J. Res. 4, a joint 
resolution proposing an amendment to the Constitution of the United 
States to require that the Supreme Court of the United States be 
composed of not more than 9 justices

                          ____________________
```
