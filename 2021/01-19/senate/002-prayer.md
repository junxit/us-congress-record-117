# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, ruler of all nations. As the inauguration of President-
Elect Joseph Biden approaches, bless and heal America. Bless this land 
of amber waves of grain with the righteousness that exults nations. 
Heal this land of curvaceous mountains by removing from it sins that 
bring reproach to any people.
  Lord, use our lawmakers as your servants of reconciliation and hope, 
empowering them to fulfill your purposes on Earth.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
