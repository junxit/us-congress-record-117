# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2021, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on January 14, 2021, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, received a message from the House of 
Representatives, announcing that the House of Representatives has 
impeached for high crimes and misdemeanors Donald John Trump, President 
of the United States; the House of Representatives adopted an article 
of impeachment against Donald John Trump, which the managers on the 
part of the House of Representatives have been directed to carry to the 
Senate; and Mr. Raskin, Ms. DeGette, Mr. Cicilline, Mr. Castro of 
Texas, Mr. Swalwell, Mr. Lieu, Ms. Plaskett, Mr. Neguse, and Ms. Dean, 
have been appointed such managers.

                          ____________________
```
