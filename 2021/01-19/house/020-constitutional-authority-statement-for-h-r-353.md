# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 353

**Measures:** H.R. 353

```
           By Mrs. MURPHY of Florida:
       H.R. 353.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, which gives Congress the power to 
     provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States; the power to regulate commerce with foreign 
     nations and among the several states; and the power to make 
     all laws which shall be necessary and proper for carrying 
     into execution the foregoing powers.
```
