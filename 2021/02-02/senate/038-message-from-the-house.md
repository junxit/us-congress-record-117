# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:31 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that pursuant to 
sections 5580 and 5581 of the revised statues (20 U.S.C. 42-43), and 
the order of the House of January 4, 2021, the Speaker appoints the 
following Members on the part of the House of Representatives to the 
Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution: Ms. Matsui of 
California and Ms. Roybal-Allard of California.
```
