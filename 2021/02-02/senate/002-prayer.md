# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  All-powerful God, who lives above the highest Stars, we borrow our 
heartbeats from You. Each day Your power enables us to live and breathe 
and have our being. When our hopes are uprooted, You comfort us with 
Your presence.
  Lord, guide our lawmakers on a road that leads to a clear conscience. 
Inspire them to refuse to think, say, or do anything that would 
disappoint You. Help them to hear, even in the beauty of falling snow, 
whispers of Your power.
  We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
