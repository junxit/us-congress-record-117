# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 716

**Measures:** H.R. 716

```
           By Mr. MANN:
       H.R. 716.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article II, Section 3 of the United States Consitution, 
     which states that the President ``. . . shall take Care that 
     the Laws be faithfully executed.''
```
