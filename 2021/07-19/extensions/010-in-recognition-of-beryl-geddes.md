# IN RECOGNITION OF BERYL GEDDES

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                     IN RECOGNITION OF BERYL GEDDES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 19, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise to congratulate 
Beryl Geddes of Blair County, Pennsylvania as she celebrates her 100th 
birthday. Mrs. Geddes is a loving mother, grandmother, great-
grandmother, and great-great-grandmother.
  Born on July 9, 1921 in Altoona, Pennsylvania. Beryl graduated from 
Tyrone High School in 1939 and married Chester Geddes the following 
year. Beryl traveled across the country with her husband during his 
time in the military and later returned home to Tyrone, Pennsylvania, 
where she worked.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's 13th Congressional District, I 
congratulate Beryl Geddes on this very special birthday and wish her 
many more happy, healthy years to come.

                          ____________________
```
