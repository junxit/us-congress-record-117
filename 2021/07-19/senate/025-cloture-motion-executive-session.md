# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 142, Bonnie D. Jenkins, of New 
     York, to be Under Secretary of State for Arms Control and 
     International Security.
         Charles E. Schumer, Ben Ray Lujan, Jeff Merkley, Raphael 
           Warnock, Alex Padilla, Sheldon Whitehouse, Christopher 
           A. Coons, Benjamin L. Cardin, Jack Reed, Patrick J. 
           Leahy, Tammy Baldwin, Robert P. Casey, Jr., Christopher 
           Murphy, Tim Kaine, John Hickenlooper, Angus S. King, 
           Jr. Tammy Duckworth.

                          ____________________
```
