# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I have 2 requests for committees to meet 
during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of the 
Majority and Minority leaders.
  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet dring today's 
session of the Senate:


                     Subcommittee on Cybersecurity

  The Subcommittee on Cybersecurity of the Committee on Armed Services 
is authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on Monday, July 
19, 2021, at 6 p.m., to conduct a hearing.


                    Subcommittee on Strategic Forces

  The Subcommittee on Strategic Forces of the Committee on Armed 
Services is authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on 
Monday, July 19, 2021, at 5:30 p.m., to conduct a hearing.

                          ____________________
```
