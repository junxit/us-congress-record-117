# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, our gracious King, You are the one clear power of love 
in the midst of lesser powers. Lord, thank You for giving us the 
confidence to know that You hear and answer prayer.
  We pray for those who seek to recover and rebuild after the tornadoes 
in many States. Have mercy upon them as they deal with the ravages of 
nature.
  Lord, teach our Senators how to discover Your love in each other and 
to see Your magnificent image in all creation.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
