# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Schneider, Bradley Scott (S001190) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4350

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. BRADLEY SCOTT SCHNEIDER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 24, 2021

  Mr. SCHNEIDER. Madam Speaker, I rise today regarding a vote I missed 
on September 23, 2021. Had I been present for Roll Call Vote 284 to 
H.R. 4350, the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2022, 
I would have voted no. I do not believe that a 10 percent across the 
board reduction in funding, as this amendment proposed, is a 
responsible way to reduce spending.
```
