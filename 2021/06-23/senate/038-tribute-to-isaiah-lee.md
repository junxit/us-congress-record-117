# TRIBUTE TO ISAIAH LEE

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ISAIAH LEE

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Isaiah Lee, an 
intern in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Isaiah is a graduate of Roosevelt High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, he is attending Northwestern College in Orange City, IA, 
where he is majoring in political science. He is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Isaiah for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
