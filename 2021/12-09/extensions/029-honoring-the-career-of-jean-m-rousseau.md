# HONORING THE CAREER OF JEAN M. ROUSSEAU

**Speaking:** Costa, Jim (C001059) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING THE CAREER OF JEAN M. ROUSSEAU

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM COSTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 9, 2021

  Mr. COSTA. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor the career of Jean M. 
Rousseau. Since 2015, Jean served as the County Administrative Office 
for the Fresno County Board of Supervisors.
  Jean has been a citizen of the San Joaquin Valley his entire life. He 
has spent his marriage and his time raising his three children in the 
Central Valley. Jean attended California State University, Fresno where 
he received his bachelor's degree in Business Administration with an 
emphasis in accounting in 1986. Prior to attending college, he 
graduated from San Joaquin Memorial High School, my own alma mater. 
Jean has been with high school sweetheart, Andria for 38 years.
  Before working for Fresno County, Jean was County Administrative 
Officer for Tulare County, and he held various other accounting-related 
positions throughout the Valley. His experience as a Certified Public 
Accountant has been an essential asset to the Central Valley for more 
than 30 years.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the career 
of Jean M. Rousseau. His service and contributions to the Central 
Valley will be missed. I join his family, friends, and the community in 
celebrating Jean's successful career and wish him the best of luck in 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
