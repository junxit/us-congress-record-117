# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Ms. CANTWELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations: 
Calendar Nos. 135, 136, 137, 138, 139, 140, 141, and all nominations on 
the Secretary's Desk in the Air Force, Army, Marine Corps, Navy, and 
Space Force; that the nominations be confirmed en bloc; that the 
motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table en

[[Page S3492]]

bloc with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions be 
in order to any of the nominations; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's actions; and that the Senate then resume 
legislative session.
```
