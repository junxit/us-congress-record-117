# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 59, Deanne Bennett Criswell, of New 
     York, to be Administrator of the Federal Emergency Management 
     Agency, Department of Homeland Security.
         Charles E. Schumer, Gary C. Peters, Ron Wyden, Jack Reed, 
           Benjamin L. Cardin, Patrick J. Leahy, Michael F. 
           Bennet, Tim Kaine, Christopher Murphy, Richard J. 
           Durbin, Christopher A. Coons, Cory A. Booker, Martin 
           Heinrich, Chris Van Hollen, Edward J. Markey, Sherrod 
           Brown, Bernard Sanders, Robert P. Casey, Jr.

                          ____________________
```
