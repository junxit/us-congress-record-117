# HONORING ANTHONY TATE

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                         HONORING ANTHONY TATE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 4, 2021

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor a 
tenacious and ambitious gentleman, Mr. Anthony Tate. Anthony has shown 
what can be done through hard work, dedication, and a desire to 
achieve.
  Anthony Tate is a Chicago native, Yazoo City resident and graduate of 
Yazoo City High School. Tate has been employed at Mississippi State 
Hospital (MSH) for over 11 years and is a Mental Health Technician 
working in Forensic Services.
  Anthony has been named MSH August Employee of the Month for Direct 
Care. The MSH Employee of the Month award recognizes employees who have 
made outstanding contributions to the hospital through their work.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. 
Anthony Tate for his passion and desire to serve others.

                          ____________________
```
