# CONGRATULATING MARLA CLAY ON HER RETIREMENT FROM COACHING VOLLEYBALL AT GALESBURG HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Bustos, Cheri (B001286) [D-IL]

```
CONGRATULATING MARLA CLAY ON HER RETIREMENT FROM COACHING VOLLEYBALL AT 
                         GALESBURG HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHERI BUSTOS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 4, 2021

  Mrs. BUSTOS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Marla Clay 
on her retirement following 27 seasons as coach of the Galesburg High 
School Volleyball Team.
  Marla started coaching the Silver Streaks volleyball team in 1994 and 
currently holds a 407-429 career mark with the team. Throughout her 
tenure, she has helped countless young athletes develop their skills 
and a lifetime a love of sport. I want to thank Marla for her many 
years of service at Galesburg High School as a coach and teacher.
  It is because of leaders such as Marla Clay that I am especially 
proud to serve Illinois' 17th Congressional District. Madam Speaker, I 
would like to again formally congratulate Marla Clay on her retirement 
after 27 years as the Silver Streaks' volleyball coach.

                          ____________________
```
