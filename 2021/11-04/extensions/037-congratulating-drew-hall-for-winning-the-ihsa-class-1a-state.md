# CONGRATULATING DREW HALL FOR WINNING THE IHSA CLASS 1A STATE CHAMPIONSHIPS FOR GOLF

**Speaking:** Bustos, Cheri (B001286) [D-IL]

```
     CONGRATULATING DREW HALL FOR WINNING THE IHSA CLASS 1A STATE 
                         CHAMPIONSHIPS FOR GOLF

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHERI BUSTOS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 4, 2021

  Mrs. BUSTOS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Drew Hall 
for winning the IHSA Class 1A State Championships for Golf.
  Drew Hall first picked up a golf club 15 years ago, and with his 
skills on the green and the support of his family, he won first place 
at the IHSA State Championships. Drew recorded a 36-hole score of 144 
at state and now joins Rockridge boys' golf history as one of the only 
state champions in golf in Rockridge High School history. At the end of 
the season, Hall's 3-under par 68 at Riverdale's Wayne Brinkmeier 
Invitational tied the 18-hole record and was the third of his three 
tournament titles. He also won gold at the Monmouth-Roseville Invite 
and Kewanee's Boiler Invite. As a former athlete, I commend the 
determination and hard work Drew has shown this season. I am proud to 
see him represent his community so well throughout the state with his 
dedication and passion for golf.
  It is because of student leaders such as Drew Hall that I am 
especially proud to serve Illinois' 17th Congressional District. Madam 
Speaker, I would like to again formally congratulate Drew Hall for 
winning the IHSA Class 1A State Championship in golf.

                          ____________________
```
