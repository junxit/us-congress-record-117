# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 2441

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported that on June 30, 
2021, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill:

       H.R. 2441. To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to 
     expand the Rural Access Network for Growth Enhancement 
     Program of the Department of Veterans Affairs, and to direct 
     the Comptroller General of the United States to conduct a 
     study to assess certain mental health care resources of the 
     Department of Veterans Affairs available to veterans who live 
     in rural areas.
```
