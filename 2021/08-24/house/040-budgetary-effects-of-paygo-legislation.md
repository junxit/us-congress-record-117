# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 978, H.R. 2617, H.R. 3599

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 978, the Chai Suthammanont 
Remembrance Act of 2021, as amended, would have no significant effect 
on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such bill are 
estimated as zero.
                                  ____

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 2617, the Performance Enhancement 
Reform Act, as amended, would have no significant effect on the 
deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such bill are 
estimated as zero.
                                  ____

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 3599, the Federal Rotational Cyber 
Workforce Program Act of 2021, as amended, would have no significant 
effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such 
bill are estimated as zero.

                          ____________________
```
