proceed to the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 302, 303, 304, 305, 
306, 307, 308, 309, and 310, and all nominations on the Secretary's 
desk in the Air Force, Army, and Foreign Service; that the nominations 
be confirmed en bloc; that the motions to reconsider be considered made

further motions be in order to any of the nominations; that any related 
statements be printed in the Record; and that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action and the Senate then resume 
legislative session.
