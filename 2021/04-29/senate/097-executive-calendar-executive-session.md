                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. KAINE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations: 
Calendar Nos. 42 and 43; Calendar No. 71, with the exception of Col. 
Jonathan C. Rice, IV; Calendar Nos. 72 through 106; and all nominations 
placed on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Marine Corps, 
and Navy; that the nominations be confirmed en bloc; that the motions 
to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with no 
intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order to 
any of the nominations; that the President be immediately notified of 
the Senate's action; and that the Senate then resume legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
