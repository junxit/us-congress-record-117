# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Kaine, Tim (K000384) [D-VA]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. KAINE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nomination: 
Calendar No. 70; that the nomination be confirmed; that the motion to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with no 
intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order to 
the nomination; that the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action; and that the Senate then return to legislative 
session.
```
