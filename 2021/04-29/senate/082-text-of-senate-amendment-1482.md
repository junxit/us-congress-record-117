# Text of Senate Amendment 1482

**Speaking:** Lee, Mike (L000577) [R-UT]

```
  SA 1482. Mr. LEE submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 1460 proposed by Mr. Carper (for himself and Mrs. Capito) 
to the bill S. 914, to amend the Safe Drinking Water Act and the 
Federal Water Pollution Control Act to reauthorize programs under those 
Acts, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

        At the end, add the following:

                         TITLE III--LIMITATION

     SEC. 301. LIMITATION ON SOURCES OF ASSISTANCE.

       Notwithstanding any other provision of law, an entity may 
     not receive a grant, loan, or other assistance from more than 
     1 program under this Act or an amendment made by this Act.
                                 ______
```
