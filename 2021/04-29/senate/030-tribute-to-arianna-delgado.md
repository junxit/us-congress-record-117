# TRIBUTE TO ARIANNA DELGADO

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO ARIANNA DELGADO

● Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Arianna for her hard work as 
an intern in the Senate Republican conference. I recognize her efforts 
and contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Arianna is a native of Maryland. She is a senior at the University of 
Maryland, College Park, where she studies government and politics. She 
has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made her an invaluable 
asset to our office. The quality of her work is reflected in her great 
efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Arianna for the dedication she has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of 
our team. I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
