# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, teach us the mystery of life. Help us not to be victims 
but victorious in this season of challenge and change.
  Lord, lead us to a place of understanding in spite of trials and 
tribulations. Empower us to triumph because You love us. Today, 
instruct our lawmakers as they seek to do Your will. Inspire them to 
focus on the priorities of Your providence.
  Lord, show them Your truth so that they will be instruments of Your 
purposes. Transform their lives from a hurried succession of days into 
a walk with You that brings enduring peace.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
