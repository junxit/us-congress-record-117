# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal and ever-blessed God, the Alpha and Omega, thank You for the 
gift of this day. Give us the wisdom to use it to enrich lives by 
service to others. Use our lawmakers to continue Your momentum in our 
world, enabling them to become salt and light to this generation.
  Lord, as our Senators strive to be productive members of the family 
of humanity, empower them to do Your will. Supply their needs out of 
the bounty of Your celestial riches. If they fall, may they fall 
forward as they trust You to remain the author and finisher of their 
faith.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
