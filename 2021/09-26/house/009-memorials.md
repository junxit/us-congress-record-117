# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.Res. 78

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       ML-102. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to Senate Resolution No. 78, 
     Declaring support for the people of Cuba in their fight 
     against a destructive communist regime and to urge the United 
     States Government and the Biden Administration to take action 
     to protect the basic human rights of the Cuban people; which 
     was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
```
