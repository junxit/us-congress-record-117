# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-63. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Mayor and 
     City Council of Hialeah, FL, relative to Resolution No. 2021-
     098, urging the President of the United States of America to 
     support the people of Cuba by condemning the Cuban socialist/
     communist, tyrannical one-party regime, by taking the actions 
     and expressing support for the Cuban people; which was 
     referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
```
