       H.R. 4089 would require the Department of Homeland Security 
     (DHS) to develop and disseminate best practices for vehicle 
     rental companies and dealers to report suspicious behavior 
     when a rental vehicle is sold. DHS would be required to 
     consult with state, local, and tribal governments and 
     industry stakeholders and report to the Congress on 
     implementation of the best practices after one year. Based on 
     the cost of similar activities, CBO estimates that 
     implementing the bill would cost less than $500,000 over the 
     2021-2026 period; such spending would be subject to the 
     availability of appropriated funds.
