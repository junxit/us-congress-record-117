   EXPRESSING SOLIDARITY WITH CUBAN CITIZENS FOR FUNDAMENTAL FREEDOMS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Tonko). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX,

condemning the Cuban regime's acts of repression, and calling for the 
immediate release of arbitrarily detained Cuban citizens, on which the 
yeas and nays were ordered.
