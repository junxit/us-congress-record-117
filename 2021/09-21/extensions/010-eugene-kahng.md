# EUGENE KAHNG

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                              EUGENE KAHNG

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 21, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Eugene Kahng 
of Wheat Ridge, Colorado for receiving the City of Wheat Ridge's Annual 
Council Partnership Award.
  The award is given to a member of the Wheat Ridge community that has 
demonstrated dedication, service and leadership to the local community. 
Eugene owns and manages the Colorado + brew pub in Wheat Ridge, which 
has become a favorite among Wheat Ridge residents since its opening. 
Eugene's contribution to the community will be recognized by members of 
the Wheat Ridge City Council during the awards reception.
  Congratulations to Eugene Kahng for his contribution to the Wheat 
Ridge community and for receiving this award.

                          ____________________
```
