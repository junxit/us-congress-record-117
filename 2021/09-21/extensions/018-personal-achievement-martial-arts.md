# PERSONAL ACHIEVEMENT MARTIAL ARTS

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                   PERSONAL ACHIEVEMENT MARTIAL ARTS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 21, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Personal 
Achievement Martial Arts of Wheat Ridge, Colorado for receiving the 
City of Wheat Ridge's 17th Annual Business of the Year Award.
  Led by a persevering team, Personal Achievement Martial Arts was able 
to have an impactful year of education and training for local youth in 
the field of martial arts, despite the COVID-19 pandemic. They offer a 
wide array of programs spanning from preschool-aged children all the 
way up to adulthood. Personal Achievement Martial Arts strives to make 
an inclusive environment for every one of their students, and pass on 
their skills of self-defense on to eager learners.
  Congratulations to Personal Achievement Martial Arts for their 
contribution to the Wheat Ridge community and for receiving this award.

                          ____________________
```
