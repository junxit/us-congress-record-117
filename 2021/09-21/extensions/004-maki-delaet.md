# MAKI DELAET

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                              MAKI DELAET

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 21, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Maki DeLaet 
of Wheat Ridge, Colorado for receiving the Wheat Ridge Business 
Association's Member of the Year Award and the City of Wheat Ridge's 
Special Recognition City Award.
  The Member of the Year Award is given to a member of the Wheat Ridge 
Business Association for their continued work both with the 
organization and the community as a whole. The Special Recognition City 
Award is given to a person or organization in Wheat Ridge who 
exemplifies outstanding service, leadership, and participation in the 
community and aims to recognize them for their achievement. As 
President of the Wheat Ridge Business Association since 2018, Maki has 
contributed a significant amount of time to the organization and the 
larger Wheat Ridge community. Along with her work with the association, 
Maki is a successful leader in business with her company, EduCyber.
  Congratulations to Maki for her well-deserved recognition, and I 
thank her for her contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
