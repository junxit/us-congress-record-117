# Unanimous Consent Agreement (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Unanimous Consent Agreement

  Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Senators Thune, Heinrich, 
and Lujan be able to complete their remarks prior to the scheduled 
vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there any objection?
  Seeing none, without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DURBIN. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
