# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
OPERATIONS IN CYBERSPACE AND BUILDING CYBER CAPABILITIES ACROSS THE 
DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Cyber, Innovative 
Technologies, and Information Systems held a hearing entitled 
``Operations in Cyberspace and Building Cyber Capabilities Across the 
Department of Defense''. Testimony was heard from Mieke Eoyangy, Deputy 
Assistant Secretary of Defense for Cyber Policy, Office of the Under 
Secretary of Defense for Policy, Department of Defense; and General 
Paul M. Nakasone, U.S. Army, Commander, U.S. Cyber Command and 
Director, National Security Agency, Department of Defense.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
