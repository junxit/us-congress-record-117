# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 3268, S. 3269

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mrs. BLACKBURN (for herself and Mr. Wicker):
       S. 3268. A bill to authorize the Secretary of 
     Transportation to issue a request for proposals for a 
     private-sector entity to develop an operating model for an 
     interoperable gray chassis pool at the rail ramps around 
     Memphis; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
           By Ms. MURKOWSKI (for herself and Mr. Sullivan):
       S. 3269. A bill to provide for the recognition of certain 
     Alaska Native communities and the settlement of certain 
     claims under the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources.

                          ____________________
```
