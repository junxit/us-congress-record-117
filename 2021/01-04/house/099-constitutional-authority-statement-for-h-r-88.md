# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 88

**Measures:** H.R. 88

```
           By Mr. DUNCAN:
       H.R. 88.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 1, to provide for the Common Defence 
     (and general welfare) of the United States; Article I, 
     Section 3, to regulate Commerce with foreign nations; Article 
     I, Section 10, to punish felonies and offences against the 
     Law of Nations; Article I, Section 11, to grant letters of 
     reprisal and rules regarding captures.
```
