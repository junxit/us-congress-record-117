# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 323

**Measures:** H.R. 323

```
            By Ms. BASS:
        H.R. 323.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section l of the United States Constitution, 
     providing--``All legislative Powers herein granted shall be 
     vested in a Congress of the United States, which shall 
     consist of a Senate and House of Representatives.''
```
