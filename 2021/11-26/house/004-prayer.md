# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Great are Your tender mercies, O Lord; Your compassion is beyond our 
comprehension. Give us all that we need according to Your promises.
  Many are the concerns which seem to overwhelm us. Give us hope in the 
face of all we endure, according to Your loving kindness.
  Sometimes Your righteousness seems beyond our ability to live into. 
Give us new life according to Your mercy.
  How awesome are Your thoughts, O Lord; how vast are the sum of them. 
Give us Your wisdom according to Your steadfast love.
  Your word is true from the beginning. All that is, is derived from 
Your supreme truth. Give us Your grace to yield to Your will, according 
to Your everlasting love.
  In the strength of Your name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
