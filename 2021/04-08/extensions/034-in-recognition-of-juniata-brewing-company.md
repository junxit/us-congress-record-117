# IN RECOGNITION OF JUNIATA BREWING COMPANY

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
               IN RECOGNITION OF JUNIATA BREWING COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 8, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Juniata Brewing Company on winning the New Construction 
Award, which showcases excellence in development, from the Huntingdon 
County Chamber of Commerce.
  The Juniata Brewing Company opened its doors in March 2020 and faced 
numerous challenges amid the COVID-19 pandemic. Overcoming these 
obstacles, the Juniata Brewing Company team adapted their business to 
provide curbside growler pick up and offered delivery services within 
the community
  The Juniata Brewing Company seeks to enrich the community and expand 
our region's craft brewing industry. Small businesses such as the 
Juniata Brewing Company are a staple of our community. Congratulations 
to the team for persevering through unprecedented challenges, and I 
wish them continued success.

                          ____________________
```
