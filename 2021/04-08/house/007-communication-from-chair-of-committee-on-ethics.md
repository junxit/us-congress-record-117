# COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON ETHICS

**Measures:** H.Res. 73

```
            COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON ETHICS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the chair of the Committee on Ethics:

                                         House of Representatives,


                                          Committee on Ethics,

                                   Washington, DC, March 30, 2021.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Pelosi: On February 26, 2021, the Committee on 
     Ethics (Committee) received an appeal from Representative 
     Louie Gohmert of a fine imposed pursuant to House Resolution 
     73. The appeal was received after the Committee adopted its 
     written rules.
       A majority of the Committee did not agree to the appeal.
           Sincerely,
                                               Theodore E. Deutch,
                                                         Chairman.
                                                  Jackie Walorski,
     Ranking Member.

                          ____________________
```
