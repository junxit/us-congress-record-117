# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 320, S. 415

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, March 11, 2021.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate on March 11, 2021, at 3:27 
     p.m.:
       That the Senate passed S. 320.
       That the Senate passed S. 415.
       Appointments:
       Commission on the Naming of Items of the Department of 
     Defense that Commemorate the Confederate States of America or 
     Any Person Who Served Voluntarily with the Confederate States 
     of America
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
                                                   Gloria J. Lett,
     Deputy Clerk.

                          ____________________
```
