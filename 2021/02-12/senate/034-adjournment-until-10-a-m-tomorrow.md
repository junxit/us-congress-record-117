# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the trial 
adjourn until 10 a.m. tomorrow, Saturday, February 13, and that this 
also constitute the adjournment of the Senate.
  There being no objection, at 6:29 p.m., the Senate, sitting as a 
Court of Impeachment, adjourned until Saturday, February 13, 2021, at 
10 a.m.
```
