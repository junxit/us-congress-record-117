# SENATE RESOLUTION 29--ELECTING SONCERIA ANN BERRY AS SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 29--ELECTING SONCERIA ANN BERRY AS SECRETARY OF THE 
                                 SENATE

and agreed to:
```
