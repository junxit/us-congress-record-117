# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 781

**Measures:** H.R. 781

```
            By Mr. VAN DREW:
        H.R. 781.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Id. art. I, Sec. 8, cl. 3.
       Congress is enumerated the power to regulate commerce. This 
     bill regulates how a telecommunications company may set 
     commercial service billing rates for a Veteran's Service 
     Organizations.
```
