# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 335

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported that on January 21, 
2021, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill:

       H.R. 335. To provide for an exception to a limitation 
     against appointment of persons as Secretary of Defense within 
     seven years of relief from active duty as a regular 
     commissioned officer of the Armed Forces.
```
