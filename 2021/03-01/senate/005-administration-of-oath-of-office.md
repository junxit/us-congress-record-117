# ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

**Measures:** S.Res. 29

```
                    ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Pursuant to S. Res. 29, the Secretary of 
the Senate-elect will now present herself to the desk to take the oath 
of office.
  Sonceria Ann Berry, escorted by Mr. Schumer, advanced to the desk of 
the President pro tempore, and the oath prescribed by law was 
administered to her by the President pro tempore.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Congratulations, Madam Secretary.
  (Applause.)

                          ____________________
```
