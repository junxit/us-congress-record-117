# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who commands the morning to appear, we place our trust 
in You. Lord, You keep us from dishonor. Continue to show our lawmakers 
the path where they should fall. Point them to the right road. When 
they have doubts, remove their uncertainty with Your wisdom. When they 
have fears, remind them that You are their refuge and strength. When 
they experience failure, strengthen them to rise again. Lord, lead them 
by Your truth and teach them how to honor You.
  We pray in Your matchless Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
