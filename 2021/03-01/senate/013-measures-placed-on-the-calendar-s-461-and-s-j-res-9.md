# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 461 AND S.J. RES. 9

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 9, S. 461

```
        MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 461 AND S.J. RES. 9

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I understand there are two measures at 
the desk due for a second reading en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The leader is correct.
  The clerk will read the bills by title en bloc for the second time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 461) to create a point of order against 
     legislation modifying the number of Justices of the Supreme 
     Court of the United States.
       A joint resolution (S.J. Res. 9) proposing an amendment to 
     the Constitution of the United States to require that the 
     Supreme Court of the United States be composed of nine 
     justices.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, in order to place the bills on the 
calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further 
proceedings en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection being heard, the bills will be 
placed on the calendar.
  Mr. SCHUMER. I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
