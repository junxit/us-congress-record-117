# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 2441

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled a bill of the House of the following title, which was 
thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 2441. An act to direct the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs to expand the Rural Access Network for Growth 
     Enhancement Program of the Department of Veterans Affairs, 
     and to direct the Comptroller General of the United States to 
     conduct a study to assess certain mental health care 
     resources of the Department of Veterans Affairs available to 
     veterans who live in rural areas.

                          ____________________
```
