# HONORING THOMAS HARPER

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
                         HONORING THOMAS HARPER

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY KIM

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 6, 2021

  Mr. KIM of New Jersey. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Thomas A. 
Harper.
  Mr. Harper proudly served his country during the Korea War and 
embodies the resilience and fortitude of the great men and women who 
serve in our Nation's military. During his time in the U.S. Army, Mr. 
Harper gained the role of Sergeant First Class, served in the Battle of 
Pork Chop Hill and was awarded two Purple Hearts. For Mr. Harper's 
valor and service in the Battle of Pork Chop Hill, Gregory Peck, an 
actor in the movie, Pork Chip Hill, met with Mr. Harper to conduct 
research for his role.
  I would like to formally thank Mr. Harper for his service to the 
United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
