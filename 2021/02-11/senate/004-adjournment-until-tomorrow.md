# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                       ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, finally, I ask unanimous consent that the 
trial adjourn until 12 noon tomorrow, Friday, February 12, and this 
also constitute the adjournment of the Senate.
  There being no objection, at 4:24 p.m., the Senate, sitting as a 
Court of Impeachment, adjourned until Friday, February 12, 2021, at 12 
noon.
```
