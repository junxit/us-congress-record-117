# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 188, H.Res. 839

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 11(b) of House 
Resolution 188, the House stands adjourned until 10 a.m. tomorrow as a 
further mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable Robert 
Joseph Dole.
  Thereupon (at 9 o'clock and 57 minutes p.m.), under its previous 
order and pursuant to H. Res. 839, the House adjourned until tomorrow, 
Wednesday, December 8, 2021, at 10 a.m., as a further mark of respect 
to the memory of the late Honorable Robert Joseph Dole.
```
