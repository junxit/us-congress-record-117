# Debt Ceiling (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                              Debt Ceiling

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I want to begin today with a short update 
on the debt ceiling. Over the past few days, we have made good progress 
on this issue, and I am optimistic that we will be able to prevent the 
awful prospect of the U.S. defaulting on its sovereign debt for the 
first time ever. Nobody wants to see the United States default on its 
debts.
  As Secretary Yellen has warned, a default could eviscerate everything 
we have done to recover from the COVID crisis. We don't want to see 
that and I don't believe we will see that and I continue to thank all 
of my colleagues for cooperating in good faith to preserve the full 
faith and credit of the United States.
```
