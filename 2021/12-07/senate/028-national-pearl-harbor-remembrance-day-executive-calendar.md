# National Pearl Harbor Remembrance Day (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Carper, Thomas R. (C000174) [D-DE]

```
                 National Pearl Harbor Remembrance Day

  Madam President, the last thing I would say is this is also Pearl 
Harbor Day. It is a day for us to remember those who lost their lives, 
sacrificed their lives standing up for us all those years ago, on 
December 7, 1941.
  With that, I yield the floor.
```
