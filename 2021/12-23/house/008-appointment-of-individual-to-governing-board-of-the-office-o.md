# APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO GOVERNING BOARD OF THE OFFICE OF CONGRESSIONAL ETHICS

**Measures:** H.Res. 8

```
     APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO GOVERNING BOARD OF THE OFFICE OF 
                          CONGRESSIONAL ETHICS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment, pursuant to section 4(c) of House Resolution 8, 117th 
Congress, and the order of the House of January 4, 2021, of the 
following individual to serve on the Governing Board on the Office of 
Congressional Ethics:
  Nominated by the Speaker after consultation with the minority leader:
  Mr. William Paul Luther, Minnesota, alternate, for the remainder of 
the term as alternate of Mr. Mike Barnes.
```
