# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, the fountain of every blessing, provide our Senators 
this day reverence to realize Your presence, humility to know their own 
needs, and trust to ask You for help. Give them also the courage to 
obey Your precepts and to live for Your glory.
  Lord, walk with them as they work, giving them the wisdom to believe 
that there is no purity without vigilance, no learning without study, 
and no crown without a cross. Strengthen their resolve to choose the 
right and shun the wrong.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
