# IN RECOGNITION OF DEBBIE ECKENRODE

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                   IN RECOGNITION OF DEBBIE ECKENRODE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 11, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise to congratulate 
Debbie Eckenrode on receiving the Blair County Chamber of Commerce's 
Joseph P. Rosenhamer Chamber Spirit Award, which recognizes a longtime 
Chamber member who embodies the traits we hope to see in business 
leaders.
  Debbie has been an outstanding member of the Blair County Chamber of 
Commerce and frequently leads efforts to support the chamber's 
community events. Additionally, she mentors new chamber members and 
entrepreneurs in the region. Debbie is a dedicated member and ensures 
that the organization maintains its robust presence in the community.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's 13th Congressional District, I 
congratulate Debbie on this accomplishment and thank her for her 
service to the Blair County community.

                          ____________________
```
