# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You have always been our helper. Continue to teach our 
lawmakers to live for Your glory.
  Lord, lead them along the paths that will keep our Nation strong. 
Give them confidence in Your guidance and a passion for Your truth. 
Strengthen them so that they will be courageous in defense of 
righteousness. Inspire them to wait patiently for the ultimate triumph 
of Your prevailing providence. May they find spiritual nourishment 
simply by being kind to each other.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
