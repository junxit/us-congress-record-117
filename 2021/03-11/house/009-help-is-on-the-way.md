# HELP IS ON THE WAY

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                           HELP IS ON THE WAY

  (Mr. GARAMENDI asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)

[[Page H1330]]

  

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Madam Speaker, I rise to echo the words of our 
President: Americans, help is on the way.
  The House of Representatives and Senate have passed the rescue bill, 
and there is money for vaccinations, money to open the schools, and 
money for small businesses. The opportunity to revise and rebuild our 
economy is at hand. The President will soon sign this legislation, and 
help is on the way.
```
