# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 39, Vivek Hallegere Murthy, of 
     Florida, to be Medical Director in the Regular Corps of the 
     Public Health Service, subject to qualifications therefor as 
     provided by law and regulations, and to be Surgeon General of 
     the Public Health Service for a term of four years.
         Charles E. Schumer, Patrick J. Leahy, Richard J. Durbin, 
           Christopher A. Coons, Benjamin L. Cardin, Jon Tester, 
           Richard Blumenthal, Michael F. Bennet, Sheldon 
           Whitehouse, Sherrod Brown, Jeanne Shaheen, Debbie 
           Stabenow, Thomas R. Carper, Margaret Wood Hassan, 
           Elizabeth Warren, Patty Murray, Alex Padilla, Tina 
           Smith, Tim Kaine.

                          ____________________
```
