# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, during this sometimes chaotic season, remind our 
lawmakers anew that You will never leave or forsake us. Give them the 
wisdom to pray as if work had nothing to do with results and then to 
work as if prayer did not matter. As our Senators surrender life's 
crushing blows to Your mercy, transform their setbacks into stepping 
stones to abundant faith, hope, and joy. Use our lawmakers this day for 
Your glory.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
