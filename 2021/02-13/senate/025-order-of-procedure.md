# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that following

the Senate resume consideration of Calendar No. 10; that the cloture 
motions ripen at 5:30 p.m., Monday, February 22; finally, that the 
mandatory quorum calls for the cloture motions filed today, February 
13, be waived.
```
