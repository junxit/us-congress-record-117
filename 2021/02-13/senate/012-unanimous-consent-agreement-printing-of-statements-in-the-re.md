# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--PRINTING OF STATEMENTS IN THE RECORD AND PRINTING OF SENATE DOCUMENT OF IMPEACHMENT PROCEEDINGS

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--PRINTING OF STATEMENTS IN THE RECORD AND 
         PRINTING OF SENATE DOCUMENT OF IMPEACHMENT PROCEEDINGS

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Secretary be authorized to include statements of Senators explaining 
their votes, either given or submitted during the legislative sessions 
of the Senate through March 1, along with the full record of the 
Senate's proceedings and the filings by the parties in a Senate 
document printed under the supervision of the Secretary of the Senate 
that will complete the documentation of the Senate's handling of these 
impeachment proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
