# BONNIE ZEE DAY

**Speaking:** Pocan, Mark (P000607) [D-WI]

```
                             BONNIE ZEE DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MARK POCAN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 16, 2021

  Mr. POCAN. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize Bonnie Zee with the 
following Proclamation:
  Whereas, Bonnie Zee began her career as the elected Town of Albany 
Clerk in April of 1975 and has continued to serve as a valuable, and 
trusted asset to her community to this day; and
  Whereas, Bonnie Zee's vast knowledge, dedication, and outstanding 
performance of her duties throughout her tenure has earned her the 
respect, and admiration of her community; and
  Whereas, Bonnie Zee's service as Town of Albany Clerk resulted in 
efficient and effective Town government for nearly five decades;
  Now, therefore, I hereby proclaim April 20, 2021 as Bonnie Zee Day in 
honor of her 46 years of public service to her community, the Town of 
Albany, and the State of Wisconsin. I join her friends, family, and 
colleagues in offering her best wishes on her much-deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
