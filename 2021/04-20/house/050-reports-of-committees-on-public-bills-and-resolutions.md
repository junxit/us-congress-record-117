# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 51, H.Res. 330, H.R. 1333, H.R. 1573

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. RASKIN: Committee on Rules. H. Res. 330. A resolution 
     providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 51) to provide 
     for the admission of the State of Washington, D.C. into the 
     Union; providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1573) to 
     clarify the rights of all persons who are held or detained at 
     a port of entry or at any detention facility overseen by U.S. 
     Customs and Border Protection or U.S. Immigration and Customs 
     Enforcement; providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 
     1333) to transfer and limit Executive Branch authority to 
     suspend or restrict the entry of a class of aliens; and for 
     other purposes (Rept. 117-22). Referred to the House 
     Calendar.

                          ____________________
```
