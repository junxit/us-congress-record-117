# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 188, H.Res. 333

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to section 11(b) of House Resolution 188, and 
pursuant to House Resolution 333, the House stands adjourned until noon 
tomorrow as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late 
Honorable Walter F. Mondale, former Vice President of the United 
States.
  Thereupon (at 6 o'clock and 16 minutes p.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until tomorrow, Wednesday, April 21, 2021, 
at noon, as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late 
Honorable Walter F. Mondale.

                          ____________________
```
