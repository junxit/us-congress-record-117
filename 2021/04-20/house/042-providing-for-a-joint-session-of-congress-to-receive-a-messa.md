# PROVIDING FOR A JOINT SESSION OF CONGRESS TO RECEIVE A MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 30, H.Res. 330

```
PROVIDING FOR A JOINT SESSION OF CONGRESS TO RECEIVE A MESSAGE FROM THE 
                               PRESIDENT

  Pursuant to section 7(a) of House Resolution 330, H. Con. Res. 30 is 
adopted.
  The text of the concurrent resolution is as follows:

                            H. Con. Res. 30

       Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate 
     concurring), That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the 
     Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, April 28, 
     2021, at 9:00 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such 
     communication as the President of the United States shall be 
     pleased to make to them.

                          ____________________
```
