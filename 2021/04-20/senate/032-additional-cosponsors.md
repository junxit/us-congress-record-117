                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 40

  At the request of Ms. Smith, her name was added as a cosponsor of S. 
40, a bill to address the fundamental injustice, cruelty, brutality, 
and inhumanity of slavery in the United States and the 13 American 
colonies between 1619 and 1865 and to establish a commission to study 
and consider a national apology and proposal for reparations for the 
institution of slavery, its subsequent de jure and de facto racial and 
economic discrimination against African Americans, and the impact of 
these forces on living African Americans, to make recommendations to 
the Congress on appropriate remedies, and for other purposes.

  At the request of Mr. Rubio, the names of the Senator from Wisconsin 
(Ms. Baldwin) and the Senator from Nebraska (Mrs. Fischer) were added 
as cosponsors of S. 65, a bill to ensure that goods made with forced 
labor in the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region of the People's Republic 
of China do not enter the United States market, and for other purposes.
