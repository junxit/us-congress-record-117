# IN RECOGNITION OF MARY FUTCH

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                      IN RECOGNITION OF MARY FUTCH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 31, 2021

  Ms. TLAIB. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate Ms. 
Mary Futch, a long-time resident of Detroit in Michigan's 13th 
Congressional District as she celebrates her 100th birthday.
  Ms. Futch was born and raised in Kosciusko, Mississippi, but she is 
proud to call Detroit her home. In fact, she has lived at the same 
address on Maxwell Street for more than sixty years. Married to the 
late Ollie Futch, Ms. Futch was a devoted wife and mother of ten 
children. Ms. Futch, or Momma Futch, is well-known in her neighborhood 
and church community at Mt. Zion Missionary Baptist Church.
  Please join me in wishing Momma Futch the happiest of birthdays, 
surrounded by love and warm wishes of her family and friends.

                          ____________________
```
