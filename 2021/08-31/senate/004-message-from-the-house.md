# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 5085

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  Pursuant to the order of today, at 10:30 a.m., a message from the 
House of Representatives, delivered by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading 
clerks, announced that the House has passed the following bill, in 
which it requests the concurrence of the Senate: H.R. 5085, an act to 
amend section 1113 of the Social Security Act to provide authority for 
increased payments for temporary assistance to United States citizens 
returned from foreign countries, and for other purposes.
```
