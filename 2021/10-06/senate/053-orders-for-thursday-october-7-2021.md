# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2021

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2021

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, October 7; that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; and that upon the conclusion of 
morning business, the Senate proceed to executive session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
