# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, at this time, I ask unanimous consent that 
the Senate proceed to the following nominations en bloc: Calendar No. 
188, 255, and 256; that the Senate vote on the nominations en bloc 
without intervening action or debate; that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or 
debate; that any statements related to the nominations be printed in 
the Record and that the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the 
nominations of Gwen Graham, of Florida, to be Assistant Secretary for 
Legislation and Congressional Affairs, Department of Education; Roberto 
Josue Rodriguez, of the District of Columbia, to be Assistant Secretary 
for Planning, Evaluation, and Policy Development, Department of 
Education; and Elizabeth Merrill Brown, of Maryland, to be General 
Counsel, Department of Education?
  The nominations were confirmed en bloc.
  Mr. CASEY. Thank you, Mr. President.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Republican whip.
```
