# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, create in us a hunger and thirst for righteousness. May 
Your presence and power direct our actions. Strengthen our Senators to 
do the work of freedom. Rescue them from trouble and give them triumph 
over life's trials. Lord, remind them that all things are possible to 
those who believe in You.
  O God, who rules forever, hear our prayers. Incline Your ears to us 
and grant us Your peace. Keep us from slipping as we trust You to save 
us.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
