# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Lisa Wink Schultz, Washington, D.C., offered the following 
prayer:
  Almighty God, creator of heaven and Earth, of all that is seen and 
unseen, today we are thankful that You have made us relational beings, 
made in Your image.
  May our relationships on Capitol Hill be fruitful and marked by joy, 
peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, and self-control.
  Where we have discord and faction, help us to remember that the 
entire law is fulfilled by loving our neighbors as ourselves.
  Lord, we long to be fruitful image bearers, help us in Your mighty 
name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
