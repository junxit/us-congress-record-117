# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 2265, H.R. 5142

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 2265, the Financial Exploitation 
Prevention Act of 2021, as amended, would have no significant effect on 
the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such bill are 
estimated as zero.
                                  ____

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 5142, to award posthumously a 
Congressional Gold Medal, in commemoration to the servicemembers who 
perished in Afghanistan on August 26, 2021, during the evacuation of 
citizens of the United States and Afgahn allies in Hamid Karzai 
International Airport, and for other purposes, as amended, would have 
no significant effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary 
effects of such bill are estimated as zero.

                          ____________________
```
