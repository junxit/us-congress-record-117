# HONORING CAROL COON

**Speaking:** Meuser, Daniel (M001204) [R-PA]

```
                          HONORING CAROL COON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DANIEL MEUSER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 25, 2021

  Mr. MEUSER. Madam Speaker, it is my honor to formally acknowledge the 
ordination of Carol Coon, who was ordained a minister in the River of 
Life Fellowship Church.
  Carol has been a devout Christian all her life and has lived her life 
in a manner focused always on glorifying God. Her ordination is the 
fulfillment of a long-held dream and consummates her lifelong journey 
of faith in God. As the Member of Congress for Pennsylvania's Ninth 
District as well as Carol's nephew

[[Page E1142]]

and Godson, it is my honor to congratulate her on her ordination and 
let her know how proud her family, church, and community are of her 
wonderful accomplishment.
  In addition to wishing her well and praying for her in her ministry, 
I also know that many people will benefit spiritually from Carol's 
ministry.
  May God continue to bless Carol, her family, and her church.

                          ____________________
```
