CORONAVIRUS RECOVERY
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: On Thursday, February 
18, 2021, Committee concluded a hearing to examine the Coronavirus 
crisis, focusing on paving the way to an equitable recovery, after 
receiving testimony from Mayor Jamael Tito Brown, Youngstown, Ohio; 
John A. Costa, Amalgamated Transit Union, Silver Spring, Maryland; 
Jyoshu Tsushima, Legal Aid Society of Columbus, Columbus, Ohio; Douglas 
Holtz-Eakin, American Action Forum, Washington, D.C.; and John 
Cracchiolo, Sal and Jerry's Bakery, West Babylon, New York.
