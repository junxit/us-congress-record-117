# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
COVID-19 AND THE CHILD CARE CRISIS
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on the Departments of Labor, 
Health and Human Services, Education, and Related Agencies held a 
hearing entitled ``COVID-19 and the Child Care Crisis''. Testimony was 
heard from Theresa Hawley, First Assistant Deputy Governor, Education, 
Office of Governor, Illinois; and public witnesses.
DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS COVID-19 RESPONSE
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Military Construction, 
Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies held a hearing entitled 
``Department of Veterans Affairs COVID-19 Response''. Testimony was 
heard from Richard Stone, Acting Under Secretary for Health, Veterans 
Health Administration, Department of Veterans Affairs.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on House Administration: Full Committee held a business 
meeting on Committee Resolutions to establish procedures in contested 
election cases properly filed under the Federal Contested Election Act, 
and related matters. Committee Resolutions to establish procedures in 
contested election cases properly filed under the Federal Contested 
Election Act, and related matters were adopted.
A PATHWAY FOR PEACE IN AFGHANISTAN: EXAMINING THE FINDINGS AND 
RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE AFGHANISTAN STUDY GROUP
Committee on Oversight and Reform: Subcommittee on National Security 
held a hearing entitled ``A Pathway for Peace in Afghanistan: Examining 
the Findings and Recommendations of the Afghanistan Study Group''. 
Testimony was heard from the following Afghanistan Study Group 
officials: Kelly A. Ayotte, Co-Chair; General Joseph F. Dunford, Jr. 
(Retired), Co-Chair; and Nancy Lindborg, Co-Chair.

[[Page D137]]


THE SCIENCE OF COVID-19 VACCINES AND ENCOURAGING VACCINE UPTAKE
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Full Committee held a 
hearing entitled ``The Science of COVID-19 Vaccines and Encouraging 
Vaccine Uptake''. Testimony was heard from Dr. Philip Huang, M.D., 
Director and Health Authority, Dallas County Department of Health and 
Human Services, Texas; Keith Reed, Deputy Commissioner, Oklahoma State 
Department of Health; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held
```
