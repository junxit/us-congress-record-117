# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1084

**Measures:** H.R. 1084

```
            By Mr. WALBERG:
        H.R. 1084.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The authority to enact this bill is derived from but not 
     limited to, Clause 3 of Section 8 of Article I of the United 
     States Constitution. ``To regulate Commerce with foreign 
     Nations, and among the several States, and the Indian 
     Tribes.''
```
