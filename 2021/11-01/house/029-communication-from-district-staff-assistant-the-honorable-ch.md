# COMMUNICATION FROM DISTRICT STAFF ASSISTANT, THE HONORABLE CHARLIE CRIST, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
  COMMUNICATION FROM DISTRICT STAFF ASSISTANT, THE HONORABLE CHARLIE 
                       CRIST, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Abimael Jimenez, District Staff Assistant, the 
Honorable Charlie Crist, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, October 27, 2021.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I, Abimael Jimenez, have been served 
     with a subpoena for testimony issued by the Small Claims 
     Division of the County Court for Pinellas County, Florida.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Abimael Jimenez,
     District Staff Assistant.

                          ____________________
```
