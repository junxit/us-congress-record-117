# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, make us instruments of Your love. Lord, use our 
Senators today as ambassadors of reconciliation. Direct them in their 
work, surrounding them with Your gracious love. Let all their plans and 
purposes be in accordance with Your holy will. May they desire to serve 
You and country with faithfulness.
  Lord, enlighten them with Your wisdom so they will find solutions to 
the problems that challenge our Nation and world. Make them good 
stewards of their calling, guiding them to use their influence for Your 
glory.
  We pray in Your gracious Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
