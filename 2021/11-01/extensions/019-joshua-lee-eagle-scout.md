# JOSHUA LEE--EAGLE SCOUT

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                        JOSHUA LEE--EAGLE SCOUT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 1, 2021

  Mr. VAN DREW. Madam Speaker, the rank of Eagle Scout is the highest, 
and most prestigious in the Boy Scouts of America. Anyone involved in 
the organization will understand the determination, persistence, and 
hard work it takes to obtain the rank. Joshua has not only demonstrated 
his commitment to his troop, but also his community, school, and 
himself. He exemplifies what it means to be, ``physically strong, 
mentally awake and morally straight.'' Congratulations to Joshua on his 
extraordinary accomplishment. God Bless Joshua and God Bless America.
```
