# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, The Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, President Kennedy encouraged us not to ask 
what our country can do for us, but what we can do for our country.
  Jesus of Nazareth said that He had not come to be served but to 
serve.
  Lord, may we embark upon this new day, and every day, with that same 
spirit of service.
  May we humbly serve You, and our sisters and brothers, in our every 
word and deed. Then there will be true charity and true peace.
  We make this prayer in Your holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
