# APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO GOVERNING BOARD OF OFFICE OF CONGRESSIONAL ETHICS

**Measures:** H.Res. 8

```
APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO GOVERNING BOARD OF OFFICE OF CONGRESSIONAL 
                                 ETHICS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment, pursuant to section 4(c) of House Resolution 8, 117th 
Congress, and the order of the House of January 4, 2021, of the 
following individual to serve on the Governing Board of the Office of 
Congressional Ethics, effective upon the resignation of Mr. David E. 
Skaggs:
  Nominated by the Speaker after consultation with the minority leader:
  Mr. Mike Barnes, Florida, Chair, for the remainder of the term of Mr. 
David E. Skaggs.

                          ____________________
```
