# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Appropriations: Full Committee held a business meeting on 
the Revised Suballocation of Budget Allocations for FY22; and a markup 
on the Energy and Water Development, and Related Agencies 
Appropriations Bill, FY 2022; and the Transportation, and Housing and 
Urban Development,

[[Page D783]]

and Related Agencies Appropriations Bill. The Revised Suballocation of 
Budget Allocations for FY22 was approved. The Energy and Water 
Development, and Related Agencies Appropriations Bill, FY 2022; and the 
Transportation, and Housing and Urban Development, and Related Agencies 
Appropriations Bill were ordered reported, as amended.
I AM WHO I SAY I AM: VERIFYING IDENTITY WHILE PRESERVING PRIVACY IN THE 
DIGITAL AGE
Committee on Financial Services: Task Force on Artificial Intelligence 
held a hearing entitled ``I Am Who I Say I Am: Verifying Identity while 
Preserving Privacy in the Digital Age''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
EXAMINING THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY COUNTERING WEAPONS 
OF MASS DESTRUCTION OFFICE
Committee on Homeland Security: Subcommittee on Emergency Preparedness, 
Response, and Recovery held a hearing entitled ``Examining the U.S. 
Department of Homeland Security Countering Weapons of Mass Destruction 
Office''. Testimony was heard from Gary Rasicot, Acting Assistant 
Secretary, Countering Weapons of Mass Destruction, Department of 
Homeland Security; and Christopher P. Currie, Director, Homeland 
Security and Justice, Government Accountability Office.
THE IMPLICATIONS OF BRNOVICH V. DEMOCRATIC NATIONAL COMMITTEE AND 
POTENTIAL LEGISLATIVE RESPONSES
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on the Constitution, Civil 
Rights, and Civil Liberties held a hearing entitled ``The Implications 
of Brnovich v. Democratic National Committee and Potential Legislative 
Responses''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
FOSTERING EQUITY IN ENERGY INNOVATION
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: Subcommittee on Energy 
held a hearing entitled ``Fostering Equity in Energy Innovation''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
