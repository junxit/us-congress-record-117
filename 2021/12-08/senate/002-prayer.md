# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Come to us now, gracious God, as we seek Your presence. Shine Your 
light into the hearts of our lawmakers, empowering them to work for 
Your honor and glory. Lord, give them the wisdom to strive for 
integrity in every area of their lives, staying true to You regardless 
of the consequences. Remind them that because of You they are never 
alone. Let their lives be examples of people who are passionate about 
loving You.
  We pray in Your faithful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
