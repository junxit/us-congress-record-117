             ORAL HEALTH LITERACY AND AWARENESS ACT OF 2021

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 4555) to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
authorize a public education campaign across all relevant programs of 
the Health Resources and Services Administration to increase oral 
health literacy and awareness, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.

rules and pass the bill.
  This will be a 5-minute vote.
