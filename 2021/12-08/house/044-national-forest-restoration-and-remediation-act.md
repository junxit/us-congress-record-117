            NATIONAL FOREST RESTORATION AND REMEDIATION ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 4489) to amend the Act of June 20, 1958, to require 
that certain amounts collected by the United States with respect to 
lands under the administration of the Forest Service be invested into 
interest bearing obligations, and for other purposes, as amended, on 
which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Georgia (Mr.   David Scott) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, as amended.
  This will be a 5-minute vote.
