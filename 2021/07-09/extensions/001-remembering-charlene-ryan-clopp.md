# REMEMBERING CHARLENE RYAN CLOPP

**Speaking:** Ryan, Tim (R000577) [D-OH]

```
                    REMEMBERING CHARLENE RYAN CLOPP

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. TIM RYAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, July 9, 2021

  Mr. RYAN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Charlene 
Ryan Clopp, formerly of Warren, who passed away in her home on Tuesday, 
June 8, 2021, at the age of 65.
  Charlene leaves behind her husband, Daniel Clopp.
  Charlene was a true light in this world. She was kind, loving, 
generous, and always there with a helping hand. Her spirit was 
infectious, and she enjoyed spending time with her family, friends, and 
dog named Prince.
  She will be deeply missed by her three brothers, Allen (Marcy) Ryan 
of Ashtabula, Dennis (Janice) Ryan of Cortland, James Ryan of Warren; 
one sister, Kathleen (Larry) Nash of Alpharetta, Georgia; along with 
her nieces and nephews.
  Charlene was my aunt on my father's side. I know my father and all 
his siblings will deeply miss her. My condolences go out to all whose 
lives she so deeply touched.

                          ____________________
```
