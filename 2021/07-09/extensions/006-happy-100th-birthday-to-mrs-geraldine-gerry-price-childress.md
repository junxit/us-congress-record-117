# HAPPY 100TH BIRTHDAY TO MRS. GERALDINE (GERRY) PRICE CHILDRESS BAGLEY

**Speaking:** Lamborn, Doug (L000564) [R-CO]

```
 HAPPY 100TH BIRTHDAY TO MRS. GERALDINE (GERRY) PRICE CHILDRESS BAGLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DOUG LAMBORN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, July 9, 2021

  Mr. LAMBORN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to extend my sincerest 
congratulations and Happy Birthday wishes to a true woman of God and 
public servant, Mrs. Geraldine (Gerry) Price Childress Bagley, who 
celebrated her 100th birthday on June 15, 2021.
  Over the last century, Gerry has lived through the Roaring '20s, the 
Great Depression, countless wars, the Cold War, the moon landings, and 
amazing innovations such as the discovery of penicillin, color 
television and the internet. She has also witnessed the tenure of 18 
United States Presidents beginning with Warren G. Harding.

[[Page E748]]

  Mrs. Geraldine Price Childress Bagley has lived with conviction, 
genuine faith, and dignity for the past century. Through her 100 years, 
she says, ``God is faithful.''
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the U.S. House of 
Representatives to join my wife, Jeanie, and I, along with the people 
of Colorado's Fifth Congressional District, in honoring an outstanding 
patriot, friend, and prayer warrior, Mrs. Geraldine Price Childress 
Bagley, for a lifetime of dedicated service to her family, her 
community, and her Nation. God has blessed her for over 100 years, and 
for that, and the lives she has touched, we can only say, To God be the 
glory.

                          ____________________
```
