# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 10

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
AUTHORIZATIONS FOR USE OF MILITARY FORCE
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee received a closed briefing on 
S.J. Res. 10, to repeal the authorizations for use of military force 
against Iraq, focusing on recent United States military strikes in Iraq 
and Syria, from Joey Hood, Acting Assistant Secretary, Bureau of Near 
Eastern Affairs, and Joshua Dorosin, Office of the Legal Advisor, both 
of the Department of State; and Catherine Visser, Special Counsel to 
the General Counsel, and Dana Stroul, Deputy Assistant Secretary for 
the Middle East, both of the Department of Defense.
```
