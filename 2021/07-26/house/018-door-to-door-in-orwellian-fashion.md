# DOOR-TO-DOOR IN ORWELLIAN FASHION

**Speaking:** Biggs, Andy (B001302) [R-AZ]

```
                   DOOR-TO-DOOR IN ORWELLIAN FASHION

  (Mr. BIGGS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BIGGS. Mr. Speaker, Biden's team of neighborhood, basically, 
disturbers, showed up at a constituent's house this past weekend 
demanding that they reveal their vaccine status, after asking for them 
by name.
  Our agents on the border cannot test the tens of thousands that are 
being released into the country on a monthly basis, and tens of 
thousands are going in without COVID testing and without vaccines. But 
Biden-Harris, the first President with a hyphenated named, is going 
door-to-door in an Orwellian fashion to pester Americans about 
vaccines.
  Welcome to the authoritarian turn of the new Democrat party.

                          ____________________
```
