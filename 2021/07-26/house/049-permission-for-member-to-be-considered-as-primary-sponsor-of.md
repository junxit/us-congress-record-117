# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 1744

**Speaking:** Pappas, Chris (P000614) [D-NH]

**Measures:** H.R. 1744

```
 PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 1744

  Mr. PAPPAS. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the primary sponsor of H.R. 1744, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Hastings of Florida, for the 
purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Ms. Bourdeaux). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from New Hampshire?
  There was no objection.
```
