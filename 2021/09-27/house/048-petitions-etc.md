# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:
       PT-64. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the City Council 
     of Honolulu, Hawaii, relative to Resolution 21-155, urging 
     the President, Congress and the United States Secretary of 
     Education to cancel Federal student loan debt; to the 
     Committee on Education and Labor.
       PT-65. Also, a petition of the St. Charles Parish Council, 
     relative to Resolution No. 6577, in support of resuming 
     Federal Offshore Leasing in the Gulf of Mexico; to the 
     Committee on Natural Resources.
```
