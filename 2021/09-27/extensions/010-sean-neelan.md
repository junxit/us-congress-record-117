# SEAN NEELAN

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                              SEAN NEELAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 27, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sean Neelan 
of Wheat Ridge, Colorado for receiving the 2021 Wheat Ridge Chamber of 
Commerce Volunteer of the Year Award.
  The Wheat Ridge Chamber of Commerce Volunteer of the Year Award 
recognizes a member of the Wheat Ridge community for their outstanding 
volunteer services and contribution to their local community. Sean 
Neelan is the recipient of this year's award due to his unmatched 
willingness and dedication to answer the call of his community. Sean 
went above and beyond and dedicated countless hours of service to the 
Wheat Ridge community.
  Congratulations to Sean Neelan for his public service and I extend my 
deep appreciation for his contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
