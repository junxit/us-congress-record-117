# KIWANIS CLUB OF WHEAT RIDGE

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                      KIWANIS CLUB OF WHEAT RIDGE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 27, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Kiwanis 
Club of Wheat Ridge, Colorado for receiving the 2021 Wheat Ridge 
Chamber of Commerce Nonprofit of the Year Award.
  The Wheat Ridge Chamber of Commerce Nonprofit of the Year Award is 
given to an organization in Wheat Ridge which has gone above and beyond 
in the name of service to the Wheat Ridge community. Kiwanis 
International is a nonprofit organization that partakes in volunteer 
opportunities all around the world. Their chapter in Wheat Ridge has 
shown amazing commitment and leadership to our community at a time when 
they were needed the most.
  Congratulations to the Kiwanis Club of Wheat Ridge for receiving this 
award and I thank them for their contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
