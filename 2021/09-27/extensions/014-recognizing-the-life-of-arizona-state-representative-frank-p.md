# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF ARIZONA STATE REPRESENTATIVE FRANK PRATT

**Speaking:** Gosar, Paul A. (G000565) [R-AZ]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF ARIZONA STATE REPRESENTATIVE FRANK PRATT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PAUL A. GOSAR

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 27, 2021

  Mr. GOSAR. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
life of Arizona State Representative Franklin M. Pratt. Frank 
represented the 8th Legislative District of Arizona well and served his 
community as a member of both the House and the Senate. A principled 
conservative who stood firmly for the values he believed in, Frank 
tirelessly advocated for the people of rural Arizona. He loved the 
state he called home and led a long and admirable life of civil 
service.
  In addition to Frank's public service as a state legislator, he also 
served his community as a farmer, rancher, businessman, and was a 
dedicated family man. Madam Speaker, I am here to express my 
appreciation for the impact of Frank's service in our Arizona 
communities and to offer my deepest condolences to his family and 
beloved wife, Janice. Frank's achievements and community impact will 
never be forgotten by the people of Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
