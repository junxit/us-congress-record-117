# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Lord God, our refuge and strength, we praise You that we have 
nothing to fear. Because we remember how You have sustained our Nation 
in the past, we trust Your providence to help us reach our desired 
destination.
  Lord, continue to lead our lawmakers as they seek Your wisdom, 
justice, and grace. May they remember that those who would leave a 
legacy of faithfulness must stride to be faithful to You.
  Inspire our Senators to perform their appointed duties with such 
reverence that their service will be like the sun shining forth on a 
cloudless morning.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
