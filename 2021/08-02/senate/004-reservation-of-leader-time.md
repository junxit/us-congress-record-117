# RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI], Carper, Thomas R. (C000174) [D-DE]

```
                       RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the leadership 
time is reserved.
  The Senator from Michigan.
  Mr. PETERS. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. CARPER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Peters). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
