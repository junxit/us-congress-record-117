# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 34, H.R. 1915, H.R. 2668, H.R. 2928, H.R. 2931, H.R. 3078, H.R. 3119, H.R. 3684

```
Committee Meetings
APPROPRIATIONS--DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on State, Foreign Operations, 
and Related Programs held a budget hearing on the Department of the 
Treasury. Testimony was heard from Janet Yellen, Secretary, Department 
of the Treasury.
FY22 BUDGET REQUEST FOR NUCLEAR FORCES AND ATOMIC ENERGY DEFENSE 
ACTIVITIES
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Strategic Forces held a 
hearing entitled ``FY22 Budget Request for Nuclear Forces and Atomic 
Energy Defense Activities''. Testimony was heard from Melissa Dalton, 
Acting Assistant Secretary of Defense for Strategy, Plans, and 
Capabilities, Department of Defense; Lieutenant General James Dawkins, 
Deputy Chief of Staff for Strategic Deterrence and Nuclear Integration, 
U.S. Air Force; Charlie Verdon, Acting Administrator, National Nuclear 
Security Administration, Department of Energy; and Vice Admiral Johnny 
Wolfe, Director, Strategic Systems Programs, U.S. Navy.
ENDING CHILD HUNGER: PRIORITIES FOR CHILD NUTRITION REAUTHORIZATION
Committee on Education and Labor: Subcommittee on Civil Rights and 
Human Services held a hearing entitled ``Ending Child Hunger: 
Priorities for Child Nutrition Reauthorization''. Testimony was heard 
from public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Full Committee held a markup on H.J. 
Res. 34, a joint resolution providing for congressional disapproval 
under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of a rule submitted by 
the Environmental Protection Agency relating to ``Oil and Natural Gas 
Sector: Emission Standards for New, Reconstructed, and Modified Sources 
Review''; H.R. 2928, the ``Cyber Sense Act

[[Page D627]]

of 2021''; H.R. 2931, the ``Enhancing Grid Security through Public-
Private Partnerships Act''; H.R. 3078, the ``Pipeline and LNG Facility 
Cybersecurity Preparedness Act''; H.R. 3119, the ``Energy Emergency 
Leadership Act''; and H.R. 2668, the ``Consumer Protection and Recovery 
Act''. H.J. Res. 34, H.R. 2928, H.R. 2931, H.R. 3078, and H.R. 3119 
were ordered reported, without amendment. H.R. 2668 was ordered 
reported, as amended.
UNACCOMPANIED CHILDREN AT THE BORDER: FEDERAL RESPONSE AND THE WAY 
FORWARD
Committee on Homeland Security: Subcommittee on Border Security, 
Facilitation, and Operations held a hearing entitled ``Unaccompanied 
Children at the Border: Federal Response and the Way Forward''. 
Testimony was heard from David Shahoulian, Assistant Secretary, Border 
Security and Immigration, Department of Homeland Security; Benjamine 
Huffman, Executive Assistant Commissioner, Enterprise Services, U.S. 
Customs and Border Protection, Department of Homeland Security; 
Katherine D. Dueholm, Acting Deputy Assistant Secretary, Bureau of 
Western Hemisphere Affairs, Department of State; and Patrick J. 
Lechleitnery, Acting Executive Associate Director, Homeland Security 
Investigations, Immigration and Customs Enforcement, Department of 
Homeland Security.
OVERSIGHT OF THE FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION
Committee on the Judiciary: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Oversight of the Federal Bureau of Investigation''. Testimony was 
heard from Christopher Wray, Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation, 
Department of Justice.
UTILIZATION OF SMALL CONTRACTORS IN THE INFRASTRUCTURE PLAN
Committee on Small Business: Subcommittee on Contracting and 
Infrastructure held a hearing entitled ``Utilization of Small 
Contractors in the Infrastructure Plan''. Testimony was heard from 
public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure: Full Committee 
concluded a markup on updated subcommittee roster; H.R. 1915, the 
``Water Quality Protection and Job Creation Act of 2021''; and H.R. 
3684, the ``INVEST in America Act''. The updated subcommittee roster 
was adopted. H.R. 1915 and H.R. 3684 were ordered reported, as amended.
MINDING THE TAX GAP: IMPROVING TAX ADMINISTRATION FOR THE 21ST CENTURY
Committee on Ways and Means: Subcommittee on Select Revenue Measures; 
and Subcommittee on Oversight held a joint hearing entitled ``Minding 
the Tax Gap: Improving Tax Administration for the 21st Century''. 
Testimony was heard from Mark Mazur, Deputy Assistant Secretary for Tax 
Policy, Department of the Treasury; Doug O'Donnell, Deputy Commissioner 
for Services and Enforcement, Internal Revenue Service, Department of 
the Treasury; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
