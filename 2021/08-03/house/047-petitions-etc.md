# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-45. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the New York 
     City Council, relative to Resolution No. 920-A, urging the 
     United States Congress and the New York State Legislature to 
     support a woman's right to abortion, and to oppose a ban on 
     sex-selective abortions, which perpetuate racial stereotypes 
     and undermine access to care; to the Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce.
       PT-46. Also, a petition of the Legislature of Rockland 
     County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 274, reaffirming 
     Israel's right to exist and take such actions as may be 
     necessary to defend itself against outside attacks; to the 
     Committee on Foreign Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
