# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S.Res. 333, S. 1046, S. 1301, S. 2045

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, August 2, 2021.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate on August 2, 2021, at 2:00 
     p.m.:
       That the Senate passed S. 1046.
       That the Senate passed S. 1301.
       That the Senate passed S. 2045.
       That the Senate agreed to Relative to the death of the 
     Honorable Carl Levin former United States Senator from the 
     State of Michigan S. Res. 333.
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
                                                Cheryl L. Johnson,
     Clerk.

                          ____________________
```
