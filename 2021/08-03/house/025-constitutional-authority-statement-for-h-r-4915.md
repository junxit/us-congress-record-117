# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4915

**Measures:** H.R. 4915

```
            By Mr. McCLINTOCK:
        H.R. 4915.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article 4, Section 3, Clause 2 of the U.S. Constitution, 
     which confers on Congress the power to make all needful Rules 
     and Regulations respecting the property belonging to the 
     United States.
```
