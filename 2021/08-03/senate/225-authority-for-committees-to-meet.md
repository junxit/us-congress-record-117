**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I have 12 requests for committees to meet 
during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of the
