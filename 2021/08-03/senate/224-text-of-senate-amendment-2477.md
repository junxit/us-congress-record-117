# Text of Senate Amendment 2477

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK]

  SA 2477. Mr. LANKFORD submitted an amendment intended to be proposed

herself, Mr. Portman, Mr. Manchin, Mr. Cassidy, Mrs. Shaheen, Ms.
