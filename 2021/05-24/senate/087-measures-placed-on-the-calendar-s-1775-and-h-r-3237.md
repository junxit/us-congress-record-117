# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1775 and H.R. 3237

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 1775, H.R. 3237

```
         MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1775 and H.R. 3237

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I understand there are two bills at the 
desk due for a second reading en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The leader is correct.
  The clerk will read the bills by title for the second time en bloc.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1775) to address gun violence, improve the 
     availability of records to the National Instant Criminal 
     Background Check System, address mental illness in the 
     criminal justice system, and end straw purchases and 
     trafficking of illegal firearms, and for other purposes.
       A bill (H.R. 3237) making emergency supplemental 
     appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2021, 
     and for other purposes.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, in order to place the bills on the 
calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further 
proceeding en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bills will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
