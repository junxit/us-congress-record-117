# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 22, H.R. 26, H.R. 27

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 22, the Congressional Budget 
Justification Transparency Act of 2021, as amended, would have no 
significant effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects 
of such bill are estimated as zero.
                                  ____

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 26, the Construction Consensus 
Procurement Improvement Act of 2021, as amended, would have no 
significant effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects 
of such bill are estimated as zero.
                                  ____

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 27, the Settlement Agreement 
Information Database Act of 2021, as amended, would have no significant 
effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such 
bill are estimated as zero.

                          ____________________
```
