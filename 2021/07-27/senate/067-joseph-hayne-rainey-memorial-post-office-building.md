# JOSEPH HAYNE RAINEY MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 264

```
           JOSEPH HAYNE RAINEY MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 264) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1101 Charlotte Street in Georgetown, South 
Carolina, as the ``Joseph Hayne Rainey Memorial Post Office Building'',
```
