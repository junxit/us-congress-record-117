# CONGRATULATIONS TO MEYER SHANK RACING

**Speaking:** Balderson, Troy (B001306) [R-OH]

```
                 CONGRATULATIONS TO MEYER SHANK RACING

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TROY BALDERSON

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 16, 2021

  Mr. BALDERSON. Madam Speaker, congratulations to Meyer Shank Racing 
on winning this year's Indianapolis 500! Based in Licking County, Meyer 
Shank Racing launched in 1994 and is currently expanding operations in 
Pataskala. It's taken a lot of hard work and determination to get to 
this point, and this team deserves every bit of success. Helio 
Castroneves excelled behind the wheel during a riveting race this year. 
Mike Shank is remarkable and has led this team to many accomplishments. 
It's such a joy to watch this team thrive, and I wish them all the best 
in their future competitions.

                          ____________________
```
