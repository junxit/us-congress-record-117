# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4572

**Measures:** H.R. 4572

```
           By Mrs. MURPHY of Florida:
       H.R. 4572.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article, Section 8, Clause 1: which gives Congress the 
     power to provide for the general welfare of the United 
     States.
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: which gives Congress the 
     power to regulate commerce among the several states.
```
