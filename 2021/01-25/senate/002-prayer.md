# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, the rock of our salvation, teach us to live as 
contemplative activists, reflecting Your sacred guidance and obeying 
Your commands.
  Remind our lawmakers that You direct the steps of good people, 
leading the faithful to the destination You have chosen. Lord, stir the 
hearts of our Senators until they possess a holiness that strives to 
carve tunnels of hope through mountains of despair.
  We offer You our hearts, mighty God, for we trust Your prevailing and 
loving providence.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
