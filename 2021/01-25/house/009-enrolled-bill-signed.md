# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 335

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Gloria J. Lett, Deputy Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled a bill of the House of the following title, which was 
thereupon signed by the Speaker pro tempore, Mr. Hayer, on Thursday, 
January 21, 2021:

       H.R. 335. An act to provide for an exception to a 
     limitation against appointment of persons as Secretary of 
     Defense with seven years of relief from active duty as a
```
