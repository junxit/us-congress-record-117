# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 447, Denise Campbell Bauer, of 
     California, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and 
     Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the French 
     Republic, and to serve concurrently and without additional 
     compensation as Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary 
     of the United States of America to the Principality of 
     Monaco.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Brian Schatz, 
           Martin Heinrich, Alex Padilla, Jacky Rosen, Margaret 
           Wood Hassan, Benjamin L. Cardin, Richard Blumenthal, 
           Michael F. Bennet, Angus S. King, Jr., Jon Ossoff, 
           Bernard Sanders, Christopher Murphy, Sheldon 
           Whitehouse, Sherrod Brown, Christopher A. Coons.

                          ____________________
```
