# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 2930, H.R. 3531

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 2930, the Safeguard Tribal Objects 
of Patrimony Act of 2021, as amended, would have no significant effect 
on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such bill are 
estimated as zero.
                                  ____

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 3531, the Women Who Worked on the 
Home Front World War II Memorial Act, as amended, would have no 
significant effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects 
of such bill are estimated as zero.

                          ____________________
```
