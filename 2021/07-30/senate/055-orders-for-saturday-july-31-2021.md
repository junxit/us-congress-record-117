# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, JULY 31, 2021

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                   ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, JULY 31, 2021

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 11 a.m., 
Saturday, July 31; that following the prayer and pledge, the Executive 
Journal be approved to date, and the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
