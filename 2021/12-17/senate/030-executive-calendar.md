# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the nomination.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of David 
Herrera Urias, of New Mexico, to be United States District Judge for 
the District of New Mexico.
  Mr. DURBIN. I yield back all time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Urias 
nomination?
  Mr. DURBIN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
```
