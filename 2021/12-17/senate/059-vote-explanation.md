# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, had there been a rollcall vote on 
confirmation of Executive Calendar No. 323, Marcela Escobari, of 
Massachusetts, to be an Assistant Administrator of the United States 
Agency for International Development, I would have voted no.
  Had there been a rollcall vote on confirmation of Executive Calendar 
No. 519, Julissa Reynoso Pantaleon, of New York, to be Ambassador 
Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to 
the Kingdom of Spain, and to serve concurrently and without additional 
compensation as Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the 
United States of America to the Principality of Andorra, I would have 
voted no.
```
