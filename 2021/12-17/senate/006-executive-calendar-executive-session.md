# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the following 
nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Atul Atmaram Gawande, of 
Massachusetts, to be an Assistant Administrator of the United States 
Agency for International Development.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from California.
  Mr. PADILLA. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Padilla). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
