# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 3377

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, December 17, 
2021, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bill:

       S. 3377. An act to empower the Chief of the United States 
     Capitol Police to unilaterally request the assistance of the 
     DC National Guard or Federal law enforcement agencies in 
     emergencies without prior approval of the Capitol Police 
     Board.
```
