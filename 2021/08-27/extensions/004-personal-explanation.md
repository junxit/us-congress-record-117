# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Costa, Jim (C001059) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM COSTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 27, 2021

  Mr. COSTA. Madam Speaker, due to unforeseen circumstances, I missed 
the vote on final passage of H.R. 4, the John R. Lewis Voting Rights 
Advancement Act of 2021. I am an original cosponsor of the bill and 
support the legislation, having previously voted in support of this 
bill when it passed the House on December 6, 2019. Had I been present, 
I would have voted NAY on Roll Call No. 259 and YEA on Roll Call No. 
260.
```
