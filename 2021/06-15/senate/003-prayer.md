# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Holy God, known to us in countless ways, we seek to magnify Your 
glorious Name.
  Lord, You are worthy of our praise, for You have been our help in 
ages past. You are our hope for all of our tomorrows.
  Remind us all that we are temporary creatures who long for a sense of 
permanence. Inspire our Senators with the knowledge that You are 
changeless and that they can trust that Your mercies will endure.
  May our lawmakers continue to depend on You for the vindication of 
every just cause and for the forgiveness of every sin.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
