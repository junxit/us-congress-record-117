# WE MUST PROTECT THE SANCTITY OF LIFE

**Speaking:** Rose, John W. (R000612) [R-TN]

```
                  WE MUST PROTECT THE SANCTITY OF LIFE

  (Mr. ROSE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. ROSE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to be a voice for the most 
innocent and vulnerable among us. This Sunday many families, including 
mine, will celebrate Father's Day.
  My faith and my family are the most important parts of my life, and I 
couldn't be more grateful for my wife, Chelsea; and our two sons, Guy 
and Sam. Being the father of Guy and Sam has only intensified my belief 
that we must protect the sanctity of life. Our society cannot leave our 
most vulnerable unprotected.
  Safeguarding the right to life has always been one of my top 
priorities, and I will continue working to protect life at every stage 
of development.
  As a Christian and a father, I will always fight for the right to 
life and for traditional family values. We must protect those who 
cannot protect themselves.

                          ____________________
```
