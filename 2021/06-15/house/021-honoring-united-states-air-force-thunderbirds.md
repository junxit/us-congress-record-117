# HONORING UNITED STATES AIR FORCE THUNDERBIRDS

**Speaking:** Horsford, Steven (H001066) [D-NV]

```
             HONORING UNITED STATES AIR FORCE THUNDERBIRDS

  (Mr. HORSFORD asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HORSFORD. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize the glorious 
Thunderbirds of Nellis Air Force Base, who are celebrating 65 years in 
Las Vegas this month.
  Since their establishment, the Thunderbirds, America's Ambassadors in 
Blue, have performed in front of millions worldwide, defying gravity 
with stunning aerobatics that showcase our military power and might.
  Their mission is to recruit, retain, and inspire. With each dazzling 
performance, they rouse America's future heroes to serve our Nation.
  Congratulations on 65 years, Thunderbirds. You are the pride of 
Nevada's Fourth District and the pride of our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
