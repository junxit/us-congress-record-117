# Text of Senate Amendment 2133

**Speaking:** Padilla, Alex (P000145) [D-CA]

```
  SA 2133. Mr. PADILLA (for himself, Mr. Moran, Mr. Rounds, Mr. Schatz, 
Mrs. Feinstein, and Ms. Smith) submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill H.R. 3684, to authorize funds for Federal-
aid highways, highway safety programs, and transit programs, and for 
other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

[[Page S5248]]

  


     SEC. __. EXPANDING THE FUNDING AUTHORITY FOR RENOVATING, 
                   CONSTRUCTING, AND EXPANDING CERTAIN FACILITIES.

       Section 509 of the Indian Health Care Improvement Act (25 
     U.S.C. 1659) is amended--
       (1) by striking ``minor'' before ``renovations''; and
       (2) by striking ``, to assist'' and all that follows 
     through ``standards''.
                                 ______
```
