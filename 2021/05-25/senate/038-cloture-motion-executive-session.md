# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 134, Eric S. Lander, of 
     Massachusetts, to be Director of the Office of Science and 
     Technology Policy.
         Charles E. Schumer, Jacky Rosen, Patrick J. Leahy, 
           Richard J. Durbin, Benjamin L. Cardin, Robert P. Casey, 
           Jr., Elizabeth Warren, Christopher A. Coons, Gary C. 
           Peters, Angus S. King, Jr., Sheldon Whitehouse, 
           Christopher Murphy, Chris Van Hollen, Mazie K. Hirono, 
           Tammy Duckworth, Tina Smith, Ben Ray Lujan
```
