# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 111, Anton George Hajjar, of 
     Maryland, to be a Governor of the United States Postal 
     Service for a term expiring December 8, 2023.
         Charles E. Schumer, Patty Murray, Alex Padilla, Sheldon 
           Whitehouse, Jeff Merkley, Jack Reed, Debbie Stabenow, 
           Benjamin L. Cardin, Patrick J. Leahy, Elizabeth Warren, 
           Jacky Rosen, Richard Blumenthal, Tina Smith, John 
           Hickenlooper, Michael F. Bennet, Tim Kaine, Brian 
           Schatz
```
