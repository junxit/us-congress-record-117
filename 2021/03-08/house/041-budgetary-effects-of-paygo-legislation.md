# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 1085, H.R. 1491

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 1085, a bill to award three 
congressional gold medals to the United States Capitol Police and those 
who protected the U.S. Capitol on January 6, 2021, as amended, would 
have no significant effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary 
effects of such bill are estimated as zero.
                                  ____

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 1491, the Fair Debt Collection 
Practices for Servicemembers Act, as amended, would have no significant 
effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such 
bill are estimated as zero.

                          ____________________
```
