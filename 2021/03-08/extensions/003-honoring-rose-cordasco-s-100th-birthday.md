# HONORING ROSE CORDASCO'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Kim, Andy (K000394) [D-NJ]

```
                HONORING ROSE CORDASCO'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY KIM

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 8, 2021

  Mr. KIM of New Jersey. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Rose 
Cordasco's 100th birthday. Rose is a loving, kind mother, sister, 
grandmother and great-grandmother. She is beloved by her family and 
from how her family has described her, it is clear that she has made 
the most of each of her one-hundred years.
  I wish her the best on achieving this milestone.

                          ____________________
```
