# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 2, 2021

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 2, 2021

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate be in a period of morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
