# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1499

**Measures:** H.R. 1499

```
           By Mr. GOSAR:
       H.R. 1499.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, clause 2 which provides Congress 
     with the power to ``dispose of and make all needful Rules and 
     Regulations respecting the Territory and other Property 
     belonging to the United States.'' in this case the sale of 
     federal land for economic development.
```
