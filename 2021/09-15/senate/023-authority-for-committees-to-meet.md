# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Klobuchar, Amy (K000367) [D-MN]

                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mrs. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I have 6 requests for committees to 
meet during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of 
the Majority and Minority leaders.
  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:

     hearing.

     September 15, 2021, at 9:30 a.m., to conduct a hearing on 
     nominations.


                     Committee on Foreign Relations

       The Committee on Foreign Relations is authorized to meet 
     during the session of the Senate on Wednesday, September 15, 
     2021, at 10:30 a.m., to conduct a hearing on nominations.


                     Committee on Foreign Relations

       The Committee on Foreign Relations is authorized to meet 
     during the session of the Senate on Wednesday, September 15, 
     2021, at 2:30 p.m., to conduct a hearing on nominations.


                       Committee on the Judiciary

       The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during 
     the session of the Senate on Wednesday, September 15, 2021, 
     at 9:45 a.m., to conduct a hearing on nominations.


                       Committee on the Judiciary

       The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during 
     the session of the Senate on Wednesday, September 15, 2021,
