```

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to the 
consideration of S. Res. 365, submitted earlier today.

  The resolution (S. Res. 365) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.

under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')
```
