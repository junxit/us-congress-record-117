# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

nomination, which the clerk will report.

District of Columbia, to be United States Trade Representative, with 
the rank of Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary.
```
