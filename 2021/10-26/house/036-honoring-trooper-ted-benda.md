# HONORING TROOPER TED BENDA

**Speaking:** Hinson, Ashley (H001091) [R-IA]

```
                              {time}  1215
                       HONORING TROOPER TED BENDA

  (Mrs. HINSON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. HINSON. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and the 
passing of a hero with deep roots in the tight-knit community of 
Waukon, Iowa, Iowa State Patrol Trooper Ted Benda.
  On October 14, Trooper Benda was on duty, answering the calls of 
citizens in northeast Iowa, when he experienced a tragic accident. He 
later passed from his injuries on October 20.
  This is the devastating loss of a father, a husband, a son, a friend, 
a mentor, a dedicated public servant who made it his life's mission to 
keep his community safe in Iowa.
  This tragedy is a reminder that brave law enforcement officers often 
sacrifice for a purpose greater than themselves. So I join all Iowans 
in mourning the loss of Trooper Benda, and my heart is with his family, 
his friends, and fellow troopers during this very difficult time.

                          ____________________
```
