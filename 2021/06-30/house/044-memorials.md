# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 24, S. 27, H.Con.Res. 30, H.Con.Res. 38, S. 46

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-45. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Louisiana, relative to House 
     Concurrent Resolution No. 24, memorializing the United States 
     Congress and the Louisiana

[[Page H3580]]

     congressional delegation to take such actions as are 
     necessary to fully fund the Livestock Indemnity Program in 
     response to the negative impact created by losses to the 
     Louisiana livestock industry as a result of Hurricane Laura 
     and Hurricane Delta; to the Committee on Agriculture.
       ML-46. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 30, memorializing the Congress to take such 
     actions as are necessary to pass a stimulus plan that 
     includes funds for unemployment, housing, local government, 
     struggling businesses, education and health care; to the 
     Committee on Appropriations.
       ML-47. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 27, 
     memorializing the Congress of the United States to take such 
     actions as are necessary to reauthorize the Atchafalaya 
     National Heritage Area; to the Committee on Natural 
     Resources.
       ML-48. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 46, 
     memorializing the Louisiana congressional delegation and the 
     United States Congress to take such actions as are necessary 
     to rename the United States Department of Veterans Affairs' 
     Lafayette Community Based Outpatient Clinic in honor of 
     Rodney C. Hamilton Sr.; to the Committee on Veterans' 
     Affairs.
       ML-49. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 38, memorializing the Louisiana Congressional 
     Delegation and the United States Congress to take such 
     actions as are necessary to defeat passage of the ``Ocean-
     Based Climate Solutions Act of 2020'' and to protect future 
     opportunities for oil and gas exploration on the Outer 
     Continental Shelf; jointly to the Committees on House 
     Administration, Foreign Affairs, Natural Resources, Science, 
     Space, and Technology, Transportation and Infrastructure, and 
     Ways and Means.

                          ____________________
```
