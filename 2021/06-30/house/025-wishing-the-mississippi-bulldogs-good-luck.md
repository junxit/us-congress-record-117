# WISHING THE MISSISSIPPI BULLDOGS GOOD LUCK

**Speaking:** Guest, Michael (G000591) [R-MS]

```
               WISHING THE MISSISSIPPI BULLDOGS GOOD LUCK

  (Mr. GUEST asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. GUEST. Madam Speaker, tonight the eyes of the Nation will be on 
TD Ameritrade Park in Omaha, Nebraska, as the Mississippi State 
Bulldogs face the Vanderbilt Commodores to determine who will be 
crowned the national champion in NCAA Baseball.
  Mississippi State will not only be playing for their fellow students, 
the faculty, and alumni, but also for our great State as they seek to 
bring home the first Division 1 National Baseball Championship in 
Mississippi history.
  Led by the offense of Tanner Allen, Rowdey Jordan, and Kamren James 
and the pitching of Will Bednar and Landon Sims, this group of athletes 
has brought life and excitement back to the people of Mississippi in 
the wake of a deadly pandemic.
  We could not be prouder of these young men and all that they have 
accomplished. Tonight, I and the entire State of Mississippi will be 
hanging on every pitch of this winner-take-all game at the College 
World Series.
  Good luck and Hail State.

                          ____________________
```
