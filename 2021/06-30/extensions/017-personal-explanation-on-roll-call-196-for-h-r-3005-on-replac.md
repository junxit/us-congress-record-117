**Speaking:** Turner, Michael R. (T000463) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 3005, H.R. 7573

```
 PERSONAL EXPLANATION ON ROLL CALL 196 FOR H.R. 3005 ON REPLACING THE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL R. TURNER

  Mr. TURNER. Madam Speaker, on Roll Call 196 for H.R. 3005, I 
inadvertently voted No when I intended to vote Yes.
  Madam Speaker, my record shows my support for H.R. 3005 in the past. 
In the 116th Congress, this legislation, as H.R. 7573, passed the House 
of Representatives, and I voted Yes at that time. I intended to vote 
Yes on H.R. 3005 in the 117th Congress, and I am pleased to see that it 
passed the House.
```
