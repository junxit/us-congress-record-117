# ORDER OF BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                           ORDER OF BUSINESS

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that upon the 
conclusion of morning business on January 25, the Senate proceed to 
executive session to consider the following nomination: Executive 
Calendar No. 2, the nomination of Janet Yellen to be the Secretary of 
the Treasury; further, that the time until 5:30 be equally divided 
between the two leaders or their designees, and that at 5:30 the Senate 
vote without intervening action or debate on the nomination; that if 
confirmed, the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table and the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
