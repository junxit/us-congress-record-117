# HONORING WILLIAM ALLEN PULLUM

**Speaking:** Gaetz, Matt (G000578) [R-FL]

```
                     HONORING WILLIAM ALLEN PULLUM

  (Mr. GAETZ asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GAETZ. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor the life of William 
Allen Pullum.
  Navarre is a community in my district in Florida that is one of the 
fastest growing. It is vibrant, full of warfighters, workers, and 
lovely people. Bill Pullum is the father of Navarre.
  He was born in 1947 and lived most of his life in this community. He 
joined the Army in 1967 as an Army helicopter

[[Page H2915]]

pilot and earned the Silver and Bronze Stars, as well as two 
Distinguished Flying Crosses in Vietnam before being honorably 
discharged in 1970.
  He was involved in every aspect of life from real estate to business 
development, chambers of commerce, Kiwanis Clubs. There was hardly a 
cause in Navarre that Bill Pullum and his family did not support.
  He was dedicated as a family member, a husband, a father, and was 
instrumental in mission work in Honduras throughout his life.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in honoring the late Bill Pullum for his 
life of service to our country and to the community in my district.

                          ____________________
```
