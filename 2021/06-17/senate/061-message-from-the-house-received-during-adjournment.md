# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S. 475

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2021, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on June 16, 2021, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the House has passed the following bill, without 
amendment:

       S. 475. An act to amend title 5, United States Code, to 
     designate Juneteenth National Independence Day as a legal 
     public holiday.

                          ____________________
```
