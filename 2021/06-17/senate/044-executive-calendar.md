**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

proceed to consider the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 178, 179,

motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with 
no intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order to 
any of the nominations; and that the President be immediately notified 
of the Senate's actions and the Senate then resume legislative session.
