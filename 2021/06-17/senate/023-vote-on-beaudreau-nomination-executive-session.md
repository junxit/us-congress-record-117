# Vote on Beaudreau Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Toomey, Patrick (T000461) [R-PA]

```
                      Vote on Beaudreau Nomination

  Under the previous order, the question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Beaudreau nomination?
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.

  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from New Jersey (Mr. Booker) 
and the Senator from Michigan (Mr. Peters) are necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the Senator 
from North Dakota (Mr. Cramer).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Schatz). Are there any other Senators in 
the Chamber desiring to vote?
```
