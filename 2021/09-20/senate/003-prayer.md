# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who comes to us with light and life, turn the 
illumination of Your love upon our lawmakers, providing them with 
strength for today and bright hopes for tomorrow. May they feel Your 
presence moment by moment and comprehend that You can still make the 
crooked places straight.
  Lord, remind them that they are stewards of Your blessings and 
accountable to You for their actions. Help them also to remember that 
You require faithfulness from all who serve Your Kingdom.
  We pray in Your wonderful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
