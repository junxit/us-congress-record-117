# MISSED VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Braun, Mike (B001310) [R-IN]

```
                        MISSED VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. BRAUN. Mr. President, to honor the heroism of fallen Corporal 
Humberto Sanchez, I went to pay my solemn respects on September 13 and 
14, 2021, and was not present for votes. Corporal Sanchez was killed in 
the attack on Kabul airport helping Americans and allies evacuate the 
country.

[[Page S6549]]

Corporal Sanchez is an American hero, and his sacrifice will never be 
forgotten.
```
