# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF DWIGHT EMANUELSON

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
           HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF DWIGHT EMANUELSON

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Madam Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart 
to remember and honor Dwight Emanuelson of Hilton Head, South Carolina, 
who sadly passed recently at the age of 88.
  Dwight had an impactful career serving his community. After 
graduating from Cornell University, he began a lifelong career as a 
successful broker that brought him to Savannah, Georgia. Although 
Dwight worked in the financial services industry for over 60 years, his 
passion for life and love for his family is what defined him.
  Dwight also had a lifelong passion for art, which he shared 
generously with the Telfair Museum of Art in Savannah.
  When Dwight wasn't admiring artwork, he was enjoying the outdoors 
with his children and grandchildren. Dwight's dedication to improving 
the lives of others will never be forgotten.
  I am proud to rise today to honor Dwight and his lifetime of service 
to Georgia's First Congressional District. My thoughts and prayers are 
with his family, friends, and all who knew him during this most 
difficult time.

                          ____________________
```
