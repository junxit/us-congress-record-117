                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                     Week of September 21 through 
                           September 24, 2021

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will continue consideration of the nomination of 
Margaret Irene Strickland, to be United States District Judge for the 
District of New Mexico, and vote on the motion to invoke cloture 
thereon at 11:30 a.m. If cloture is invoked on the nomination, Senate 
will vote on confirmation thereon at 2:30 p.m.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
