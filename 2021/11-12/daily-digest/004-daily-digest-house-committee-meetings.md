# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 5891

```
Committee Meetings
HUNGER AMONG VETERANS AND SERVICEMEMBERS: UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM AND 
EVALUATING SOLUTIONS
Committee on Agriculture: On November 10, 2021, Subcommittee on 
Nutrition, Oversight, and Department Operations held a hearing entitled 
``Hunger Among Veterans and Servicemembers: Understanding the Problem 
and Evaluating Solutions''. Testimony was heard from Tim Keefe, 
Veteran, U.S. Navy; and public witnesses.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on Education and Labor: On November 10, 2021, Full Committee 
held a markup on H.R. 5891, the ``Retirement Improvement and Savings 
Enhancement Act''. H.R. 5891 was ordered reported, as amended.
WEATHERING THE STORM: REAUTHORIZING THE NATIONAL WINDSTORM IMPACT 
REDUCTION PROGRAM
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology: On November 10, 2021, 
Subcommittee on Research and Technology held a hearing entitled 
``Weathering the Storm: Reauthorizing the National Windstorm Impact 
Reduction Program''. Testimony was heard from Linda G. Blevins, Deputy 
Assistant Director, Directorate for Engineering, National Science 
Foundation; Michael Grimm, Assistant Administrator for Risk Management, 
Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department of Homeland Security; 
and

[[Page D1253]]

Scott Weaver, Director, National Windstorm Impact Reduction Program, 
National Institute of Standards and Technology, Department of Commerce.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
