# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT, 116TH CONGRESS

**Measures:** H.R. 2444, H.R. 4508

```
   MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT, 116TH CONGRESS


                         Enrolled Bills Signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2019, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on January 3, 2021, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker pro tempore (Mr. Beyer) had signed the 
following enrolled bills:

       H.R. 2444. An act to authorize the Secretary of State to 
     make direct loans under section 23 of the Arms Export Control 
     Act, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4508. An act to expand the number of scholarships 
     available to Pakistani women under the Merit and Needs-Based 
     Scholarship Program.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2019, 
the enrolled bills were signed on January 3, 2021, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, by the Acting President pro tempore (Mr. 
McConnell).

                          ____________________
```
