# SWEARING IN OF MEMBERS

```
                         SWEARING IN OF MEMBERS

  The SPEAKER. The Members-elect will rise and raise their right hand.
  The Members-elect rose, and the Speaker administered the oath of 
office to them as follows:

       Do you solemnly swear that you will support and defend the 
     Constitution of the United States against all enemies, 
     foreign and domestic; that you will bear true faith and 
     allegiance to the same; that you take this obligation freely, 
     without any mental reservation or purpose of evasion; and 
     that you will well and faithfully discharge the duties of the 
     office on which you are about to enter, so help you God.

  The SPEAKER. Congratulations, you are now Members of the 117th 
Congress.
  Will Members who have been sworn in please clear the Chamber so that 
we can swear in the next group. Thank you.
  The SPEAKER. The Chair is prepared to swear in the group of Members-
elect currently in the Chamber. The Members-elect will rise.
  The Chair will now administer the oath of office. All Members-elect 
will raise their right hand.
  The Members-elect rose, and the Speaker administered the oath of 
office to them as follows:

       Do you solemnly swear that you will support and defend the 
     Constitution of the United States against all enemies, 
     foreign and domestic; that you will bear true faith and 
     allegiance to the same; that you take this obligation freely, 
     without any mental reservation or purpose of evasion; and 
     that you will well and faithfully discharge the duties of the 
     office on which you are about to enter, so help you God.

  The SPEAKER. Congratulations. You are now Members of the 117th 
Congress.
  The Chair is prepared to swear in the group of Members-elect 
currently in the Chamber. The Members-elect will rise.
  The Chair will now administer the oath of office. All Members-elect 
will raise their right hand.
  The Members-elect rose, and the Speaker administered the oath of 
office to them as follows:

       Do you solemnly swear that you will support and defend the 
     Constitution of the United States against all enemies, 
     foreign and domestic; that you will bear true faith and 
     allegiance to the same; that you take this obligation freely, 
     without any mental reservation or purpose of evasion; and 
     that you will well and faithfully discharge the duties of the 
     office on which you are about to enter, so help you God.

                      Announcement by the Speaker

  The SPEAKER. The swearing in ceremony will be at 7:05 of any 
remaining Members-elect who have not been sworn in. So if you are in 
your office, or whatever, come on over now because we want to proceed 
with other business.
  This is the schedule as it was scheduled--last call. So thank you 
all, and I see we have some already here.
  We have about 6 more minutes.
  The Chair is prepared to swear in the group of Members-elect 
currently present in the Chamber. The Members-elect will rise.
  The Chair will now administer the oath of office. All Members-elect 
will raise their right hand.
  The Members-elect rose, and the Speaker administered the oath of 
office to them as follows:

       Do you solemnly swear that you will support and defend the 
     Constitution of the United States against all enemies, 
     foreign and domestic; that you will bear true faith and 
     allegiance to the same; that you take this obligation freely, 
     without any mental reservation or purpose of evasion; and 
     that you will well and faithfully discharge the duties of the 
     office on which you are about to enter, so help you God.

  The SPEAKER. Congratulations. You are now Members of the 117th 
Congress.
```
