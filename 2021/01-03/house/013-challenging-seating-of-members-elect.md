# CHALLENGING SEATING OF MEMBERS-ELECT

**Speaking:** Roy, Chip (R000614) [R-TX]

```
                  CHALLENGING SEATING OF MEMBERS-ELECT

  Mr. ROY. Madam Speaker, on my responsibility as a Member-elect, I 
challenge the seating of Members-elect from the States of Arizona, 
Georgia, Michigan, Nevada, Pennsylvania, and Wisconsin.
  The SPEAKER. Under the precedents, the Chair will ask the Members-
elect of those States' delegations to refrain from taking the oath with 
the other Members-elect.

                          ____________________
```
