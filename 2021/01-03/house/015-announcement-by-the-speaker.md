# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

```
                      ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

  The SPEAKER. Will the new Members-elect come forward to be sworn in.
  As was said, due to the ongoing pandemic, the Chair will swear in 
remaining Members-elect in groups. The Chair will now swear in the 
group of first-term Members-elect present in the Chamber.
  And as I announced, all Members-elect who are in the Chamber who wish 
to raise their hand and take the oath are welcome to do so.
```
