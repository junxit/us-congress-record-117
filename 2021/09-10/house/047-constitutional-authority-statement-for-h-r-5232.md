# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5232

**Measures:** H.R. 5232

```
           By Mr. McHENRY:
       H.R. 5232.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to to coin money, regulate the Value 
     thereof, and of foreign coin, and to fix the Standard of 
     Weights and Measures as enumerated in Article I, Section 8, 
     Clause 5 of the United States Constitution.
```
