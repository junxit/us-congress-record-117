ZONED OUT: EXAMINING THE IMPACT OF EXCLUSIONARY ZONING ON PEOPLE, 
RESOURCES, AND OPPORTUNITY
Committee on Financial Services: Subcommittee on Housing, Community 
Development and Insurance held a hearing entitled ``Zoned Out: 
Examining the Impact of Exclusionary Zoning on People, Resources, and 
Opportunity''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
COLORADO RIVER DROUGHT CONDITIONS AND RESPONSE MEASURES--DAY ONE
Committee On Natural Resources: Subcommittee on Water, Oceans, and 
Wildlife held a hearing entitled ``Colorado River Drought Conditions 
and Response Measures--Day One''. Testimony was heard from Tanya 
Trujillo, Assistant Secretary for Water and Science, Department of the 
Interior; Tom Buschatzke, Director, Department of Water Resources, 
Arizona; Peter Nelson, Chairman, Colorado River Board of California, 
Glendale, California; John Entsminger, General Manager, Southern Nevada 
Water Authority, Las Vegas, Nevada; Rebecca Mitchell, Director, 
Colorado Water Conservation Board, Denver, Colorado; John D'Antonio, 
State Engineer, Albequerque, New Mexico; Gene Shawcroft, General 
Manager, Central Utah Water Conservancy District, Orem, Utah; Pat 
Tyrrell, Wyoming Commissioner to the Upper Colorado River Commission; 
and public witnesses.
