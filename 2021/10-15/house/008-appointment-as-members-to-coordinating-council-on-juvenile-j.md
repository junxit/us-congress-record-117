# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COORDINATING COUNCIL ON JUVENILE JUSTICE AND DELINQUENCY PREVENTION

```
APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COORDINATING COUNCIL ON JUVENILE JUSTICE AND 
                         DELINQUENCY PREVENTION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 206 of the Juvenile 
Justice and Delinquency Prevention Act of 1974 (34 U.S.C. 11116), the 
Chair appoints the following members on the part of the House to the 
Coordinating Council on Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention:
  Ms. Sheryl Davis of San Francisco, California, to a 3-year term; and
  Ms. Renee Rodriguez-Betancourt of Edinburg, Texas, to a 2-year term.
```
