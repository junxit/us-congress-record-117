# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 876

**Measures:** H.R. 876

```
            By Mr. GONZALEZ of Ohio:
        H.R. 876.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the Constitution stating 
     that Congress has the authority to ``make all laws which 
     shall be necessary and proper for carrying into execution the 
     foregoing powers, and all other powers vested by 
     Constitution.''
```
