# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 27, 2021, AT 3 P.M.

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 386

```
        ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 27, 2021, AT 3 P.M.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the provisions 
of S. Res. 386.
  There being no objection, as a further mark of respect to the late 
Robert Britton Dove, Parliamentarian Emeritus of the U.S. Senate, the 
Senate, at 9:47 p.m., adjourned until Monday, September 27, 2021, at 3 
p.m.
```
