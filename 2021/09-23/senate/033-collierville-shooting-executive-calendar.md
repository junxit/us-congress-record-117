# Collierville Shooting (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN], Menendez, Robert (M000639) [D-NJ]

```
                         Collierville Shooting

  Mrs. BLACKBURN. Mr. President, as I come to the floor today, I want 
to acknowledge that we have an active shooter situation in 
Collierville, TN, right outside of Memphis. We have spoken with 
authorities. We have worked with State and Federal authorities. We are 
aware that there are 13 individuals who have been shot. The shooter is 
dead. But we are very grateful for law enforcement that have stepped up 
in this situation and prayerful for those who have been adversely 
impacted and have been victims of this shooting situation.
  Mr. President, I ask that I have permission to complete my remarks 
before the scheduled vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
