# Vote on Pan Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         Vote on Pan Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. All time on the nomination has expired.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Pan 
nomination?
  Mr. PETERS. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from California (Mrs. 
Feinstein) is necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the Senator 
from South Dakota (Mr. Rounds).
  The result was announced--yeas 68, nays 30, as follows:
```
