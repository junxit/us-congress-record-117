# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Mighty God, who observes all who live on Earth, we give our lives to 
You this day and trust You with our future. Our times are in Your 
hands, for without Your power we would not see tomorrow.
  As our Senators travel on this journey called life, show them the 
right path, O Lord. Point out to them the road to follow. Continue to 
lead them by Your truth, as they place their hope in You.
  Lord, thank You for Your compassion and unfailing love.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
