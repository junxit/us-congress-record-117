# COVID-19 BANKRUPTCY RELIEF EXTENSION ACT OF 2021

**Measures:** H.R. 1651

```
            COVID-19 BANKRUPTCY RELIEF EXTENSION ACT OF 2021

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Chair lays before the 
House the following bill (H.R. 1651) to amend the CARES Act to extend 
the sunset for the definition of a small business debtor, and for other 
purposes, along with the Senate amendment thereto.
  There was no objection.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the Senate amendment.
  The Clerk read as follows:
  Senate amendment:

       Strike section 2(c).
  The amendment was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
