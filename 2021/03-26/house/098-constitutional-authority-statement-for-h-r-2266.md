# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2266

**Measures:** H.R. 2266

```
            By Mr. WALBERG:
        H.R. 2266.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section I. All legislative Powers herein granted 
     shall be vested in a Congress of the United States, which 
     shall consist of a Senate and House of Representatives.
       Section 8, Clause 1: The Congress shall have Power To lay 
     and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and Excises, to pay the 
     Debts and provide for the common Defence and general

[[Page H1695]]

     Welfare of the United States; but all Duties, Imposts and 
     Excises shall be uniform throughout the United States.
```
