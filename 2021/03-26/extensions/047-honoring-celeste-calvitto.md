# HONORING CELESTE CALVITTO

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                       HONORING CELESTE CALVITTO

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 26, 2021

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Celeste Calvitto of 
Dearborn County, Indiana. Celeste served as the Republican County Vice 
Chairwoman of Dearborn County. She committed her time, talents and 
energy to the Sixth Congressional District and the rest of the Hoosier 
state. I am grateful for her service.
  Not only is Celeste a successful businesswoman, she is also a fervent 
leader and volunteer in her community. She founded the Dearborn County 
Anti-Litter Initiative, the One Dearborn Economic Development 
organization, is a member of the Highpoint Health Foundation Board, and 
sponsors the annual Mardi Gras Ball to benefit the Children's Advocacy 
Center of Southeastern Indiana.
  Finally, I have gotten to know Celeste over the last four years, and 
I am grateful for her friendship. I thank Celeste.

                          ____________________
```
