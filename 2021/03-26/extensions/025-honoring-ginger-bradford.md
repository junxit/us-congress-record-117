# HONORING GINGER BRADFORD

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                        HONORING GINGER BRADFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 26, 2021

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Ginger Bradford of 
Ripley County, Indiana.
  Ginger served as the Republican County Chairwoman of Ripley County. 
She committed her time, talents and energy to the Sixth Congressional 
District and the rest of the Hoosier state. I am grateful for her 
service.
  A dedicated public servant, Ginger currently serves Ripley County as 
county clerk. She is also a fervent leader and volunteer in her 
community, often the first to volunteer her time and always willing to 
help at a moment's notice.
  Finally, I have gotten to know Ginger over the last four years, and I 
am grateful for her friendship.
  I thank Ginger Bradford.
```
