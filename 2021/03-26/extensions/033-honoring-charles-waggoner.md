# HONORING CHARLES WAGGONER

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                       HONORING CHARLES WAGGONER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 26, 2021

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Charles Waggoner of 
Jennings County, Indiana. Charles served as the Republican County Vice 
Chairman of Jennings County. He committed his time, talents and energy 
to the Sixth Congressional District and the rest of the Hoosier state. 
I am grateful for his service.
  Not only is Charles a successful businessman, he is also a fervent 
leader and volunteer in his community. Charles is actively engaged in 
civic and community endeavors, often the first to volunteer his time 
and always willing to help at a moment's notice.
  Finally, I have gotten to know Charles over the last four years, and 
I am grateful for his friendship. I thank Charles Waggoner.
```
