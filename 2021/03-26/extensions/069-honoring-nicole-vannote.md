# HONORING NICOLE VANNOTE

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                        HONORING NICOLE VANNOTE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 26, 2021

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Nicole VanNote of 
Randolph County, Indiana. Nicole has dedicated her service to improving 
her community and helping get conservative officials elected.
  I want to thank Nicole for serving in the role of Randolph County 
Republican Vice-Chair. Nicole has helped move the conservative agenda 
forward, and I appreciate all the work she has done for her community 
and her support.

                          ____________________
```
