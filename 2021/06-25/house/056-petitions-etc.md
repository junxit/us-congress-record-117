# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** S. 937, H.R. 1843

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-39. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Council of 
     the City of New York, New York, relative to Resolution No. 
     1619, calling upon the United States Congress to pass, and 
     the President to sign, the COVID-19 Hate Crimes Act (H.R. 
     1843/S. 937), which would facilitate the expedited review of 
     COVID-19 hate crimes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       PT-40. Also, a petition of the Legislature of Rockland 
     County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 178 of 2021, 
     urging the Congress to repeal the cap on the State and Local 
     (SALT) deduction; to the Committee on Ways and Means.
       PT-41. Also, a petition of the City Council of Boston, 
     Massachusetts, relative to Resolution, urging the United 
     States Congress to enact the Energy Innovation and Carbon 
     Dividend Act; jointly to the Committees on Ways and Means, 
     Energy and Commerce, and Foreign Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
