# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. DURBIN. I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory quorum call 
with respect to the Gupta nomination be waived.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before the Senate the pending 
cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 62, Vanita Gupta, of Virginia, to 
     be Associate Attorney General.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Mazie K. Hirono, 
           Tammy Baldwin, Tammy Duckworth, Alex Padilla, Maria 
           Cantwell, Sheldon Whitehouse, Cory A. Booker, Debbie

nomination of Vanita Gupta, of Virginia, to be Associate Attorney 
General, shall be brought to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
```
