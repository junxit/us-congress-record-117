# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of the universe and all who dwell therein, every nation, every 
tribe, every people and language stands before Your throne.
  In gratitude, we stand alongside our brothers and sisters to 
celebrate Asian American Pacific Islander Month, acknowledging the 
first known Japanese immigrants in 1843, the Chinese laborers who 
sacrificed their lives and dignity to build the transcontinental 
railroad, the Vietnamese for whose freedom many spilled blood.
  We pause to stop and consider how You have brought them and countless 
others, Koreans, Filipinos, Indians, and Pacific Islanders, to these 
shores as immigrants, refugees, or even war brides, blessing their 
journeys with hope for the freedom and opportunity our country's vast 
resources provide.
  Lord, we acknowledge and honor so many who have made significant 
contributions to law and medicine, science and technology, arts and 
entertainment. How humbled we are by the wideness of Your embrace and 
how indebted we are to the evidence of Your hand in all of creation.
  Free us, then, from our hatred or indifference toward those whom we 
perceive as the ``other.'' Instead, reconcile us to Yourself and to our 
compatriots, that we may consider how, by Your divine providence and 
grace, O Lord, we are a richer Nation because of the blessing of our 
diversity.
  It is in the unity of Your name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
