# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, do not permit negativity to triumph over us. Use our 
Senators to remind us with their faith and works that the best is yet 
to be. As they remember that Your mighty power is at work in our Nation 
and world, may their optimism create a contagion of hope.
  Lord, continue to accomplish in our lives infinitely more than we 
request or imagine. We exalt You, for You continue to rescue us. We 
trust You to save us though the storms may rage.
  We pray in Your glorious Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
