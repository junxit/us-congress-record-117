# Text of Senate Amendment 1519

**Speaking:** Johnson, Ron (J000293) [R-WI]

```
  SA 1519. Mr. JOHNSON submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill S. 1260, to establish a new Directorate for 
Technology and Innovation in the National Science Foundation, to 
establish a regional technology hub program, to require a strategy and 
report on economic security, science, research, innovation, 
manufacturing, and job creation, to establish a critical supply chain 
resiliency program, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       In section 6203, strike ``$252,000,000'' and insert 
     ``$184,524,000''.
                                 ______
```
