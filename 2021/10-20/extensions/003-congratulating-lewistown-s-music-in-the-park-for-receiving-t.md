# CONGRATULATING LEWISTOWN'S "MUSIC IN THE PARK" FOR RECEIVING THE 2020 GOVERNOR'S HOMETOWN PROJECT AWARD

**Speaking:** Bustos, Cheri (B001286) [D-IL]

```
CONGRATULATING LEWISTOWN'S ``MUSIC IN THE PARK'' FOR RECEIVING THE 2020 
                   GOVERNOR'S HOMETOWN PROJECT AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHERI BUSTOS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 20, 2021

  Mrs. BUSTOS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the residents 
of Lewistown, Illinois for their weekly community event, ``Music in the 
Park.''
  Each Thursday night during the summer, Lewistown residents bring lawn 
chairs and blankets to the town square to listen to local musicians 
perform as a part of ``Music in the Park.'' This event has been a vital 
place of gathering and community and a safe and positive outlet for 
human connection during the COVID-19 pandemic.
  It is because of gatherings such as ``Music in the Park'' and their 
organizers that I am especially proud to serve Illinois' 17th 
Congressional District. Madam Speaker, I would like to again formally 
recognize Lewistown, IL for their community event, ``Music in the 
Park.''

                          ____________________
```
