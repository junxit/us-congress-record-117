# Order Of Procedure (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Smith, Tina (S001203) [D-MN], Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

```
                           Order Of Procedure

  Ms. SMITH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding rule XXII, at 10:30 a.m., on Thursday, October 21, the 
Senate vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the Lin, Parker, and 
Perez nominations, in that order; and that if cloture is invoked on any 
of the nominations, all postcloture time will be considered expired at 
1:45 p.m.; further, that if cloture is invoked on the Lin nomination, 
the confirmation vote occur at 1:45 p.m. on Thursday; finally, that if 
cloture is invoked on either the Parker or Perez nominations, the 
confirmation votes begin at 5:30 p.m. on Monday, October 25.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
