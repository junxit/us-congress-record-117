# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  All-powerful God, Sovereign of this beloved land, You are our 
fortress, and You desire justice to be done. As our Senate jurors 
remember their accountability to You, use them to cause justice to roll 
down like waters and righteousness like a mighty stream.
  Lord, during this impeachment trial, give our lawmakers the gift of 
discernment so that they will know truth from falsehood. Inspire them 
to commit their thoughts and decisions to You.
  Let Your Kingdom come and Your will be done on Earth as it is done in 
Heaven.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
