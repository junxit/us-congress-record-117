# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                       ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the trial adjourn 
until 12 noon tomorrow, Thursday, February 11, and that this order also 
constitute the adjournment of the Senate.
  There being no objection, at 7:40 p.m., the Senate, sitting as a 
Court of Impeachment, adjourned until Thursday, February 11, 2021, at 
12 noon.
```
