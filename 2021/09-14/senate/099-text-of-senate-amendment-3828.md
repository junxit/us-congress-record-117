# Text of Senate Amendment 3828

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
  SA 3828. Mr. SCHUMER (for Mr. Murphy) proposed an amendment to the 
resolution S. Res. 339, expressing support for the designation of 
September 25, 2021, as ``National Ataxia Awareness Day'', and raising 
awareness of ataxia, ataxia research, and the search for a cure; as 
follows:

       In the fifth whereas clause of the preamble, insert ``in 
     the United States'' after ``individuals''.

                          ____________________
```
