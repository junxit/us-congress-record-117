# RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF MRS. FANNIE "CINDY" McCLAIN

**Speaking:** Kelly, Trent (K000388) [R-MS]

```
         RECOGNIZING THE LIFE OF MRS. FANNIE ``CINDY'' McCLAIN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TRENT KELLY

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 14, 2021

  Mr. KELLY of Mississippi. Madam Speaker, I rise today to celebrate 
the life of Mrs. McClain who turns 100 years old on September 26, 2021. 
I congratulate her on a long and healthy life and decades of service to 
her community.
  Mrs. McClain is a gifted seamstress. She is known for her ability to 
adapt any pattern and create something beautiful overnight. She was 
also an athlete with the Hernando Community Ladies Softball Team. She 
was a domestic worker and a seamstress at Minor France Dress Shop in 
Memphis before retiring.
  Mrs. McClain was married to Mr. Cornelius Lloyd McClain and the 
mother of two sons, Alfred and Calvin. She is a lifelong member of 
Rising Sun Missionary Baptist Church in Hernando.
  I am thrilled to wish Mrs. McClain a happy 100th birthday and offer 
my most sincere congratulations.
```
