# CELEBRATING WASHINGTON FIRE COMPANY NO. 2

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
               CELEBRATING WASHINGTON FIRE COMPANY NO. 2

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 14, 2021

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Washington 
Fire Company No. 2 of Madison, Indiana on its 175th anniversary.
  Established on January 22, 1846, the firehouse is one of the last 
remaining Greek Revival firehouses in the United States. Washington 
Fire Company No. 2 is the oldest active firehouse in the Hoosier state.
  I congratulate Washington Fire Company No. 2 and thank them for 175 
years of service.

                          ____________________
```
