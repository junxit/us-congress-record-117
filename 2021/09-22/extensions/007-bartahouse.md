# BARTAHOUSE

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                               BARTAHOUSE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 22, 2021

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize BartaHouse 
of Wheat Ridge, Colorado for receiving the City of Wheat Ridge's 17th 
Annual Reinvestment Award.
  The award is given to a Wheat Ridge business that has reinvested 
significant resources back into our community. BartaHouse is an eye-
catching, four-room boutique hotel that offers an amazing rooftop with 
views of the beautiful Rocky Mountains. In 2019, BartaHouse engaged in 
a full-scale renovation, rebuilding from the ground up. The renovations 
and modern decor continue to be a beautiful part of our community.
  Congratulations to BartaHouse for being a leader in the Wheat Ridge 
community and for receiving this award.

                          ____________________
```
