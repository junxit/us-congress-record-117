# MOMENT OF SILENCE IN REMEMBRANCE OF AMERICANS WHO HAVE PASSED AWAY FROM THE COVID-19 VIRUS

```
MOMENT OF SILENCE IN REMEMBRANCE OF AMERICANS WHO HAVE PASSED AWAY FROM 
                           THE COVID-19 VIRUS

  The SPEAKER. The Chair asks all Members in the Chamber, as well as 
Members and staff throughout the Capitol, to rise for a moment of 
silence in remembrance of the 800,000 Americans who have passed away 
from the COVID-19 virus. Let us pray that they rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
