                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Ms. CANTWELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate consider the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 600, 601, 602, 
603, 604, and all nominations on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, 
Army, Marine Corps, Navy, and Space Force; that the nominations be 
confirmed en bloc; the motions to reconsider be considered made and 
laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate; that no 
further motions be in order to any of the nominations; that the 
President be immediately notified of the Senate's action and the Senate 
then resume legislative session.
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
