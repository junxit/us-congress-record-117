                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 41

  At the request of Mrs. Capito, the name of the Senator from Colorado 
(Mr. Hickenlooper) was added as a cosponsor of S. 41, a bill to amend 
the Public Health Service Act, the Employee Retirement Income Security 
Act of 1974, the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, and the Patient 
Protection and Affordable Care Act to require coverage of hearing 
devices and systems in certain private health insurance plans, and for 
other purposes.
