# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 188, H.Res. 312

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Beatty). Pursuant to section 11(b) of 
House Resolution 188, and pursuant to House Resolution 312, the House 
stands adjourned until noon tomorrow, as a further mark of respect to 
the memory of the late Honorable Alcee L. Hastings.
  Thereupon (at 6 o'clock and 54 minutes p.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until tomorrow, Thursday, April 15, 2021, at 
noon, as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable 
Alcee L. Hastings.
```
