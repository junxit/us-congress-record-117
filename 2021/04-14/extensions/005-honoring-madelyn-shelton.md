# HONORING MADELYN SHELTON

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                        HONORING MADELYN SHELTON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 14, 2021

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Madelyn Shelton of 
Scott County, IN. Maddy is currently a student at Hanover College 
studying history and political science. Maddy has dedicated her time 
and youth to improving her community and helping get conservative 
officials elected.
  I want to thank Maddy for serving in the role of Scott County 
Republican Vice-Chair. Maddy has helped move the conservative agenda 
forward and I appreciate all the work she has done for her community 
and her support.

                          ____________________
```
