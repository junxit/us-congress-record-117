# Vote on Mallory Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Hirono, Mazie K. (H001042) [D-HI], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```

has expired.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Mallory 
nomination?

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?

  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from South Dakota (Mr. Rounds) and the Senator from North Carolina (Mr. 
Tillis).
```
