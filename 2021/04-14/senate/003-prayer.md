# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  God, our God, we honor Your Name. You have been our help in ages 
past; you are our hope for the years to come.
  Continue to guide our Senators with Your love. Answer them when they 
cry to You for assistance. Be for them a shade by day and a defense by 
night. May they exercise sound judgment as they listen closely to the 
whisper of Your wisdom that will keep them on the path that leads to 
life. May they trust in Your unfailing love to make the crooked places 
straight. Lord, give them the wisdom to permit godliness to fill their 
lives with light and joy.
  We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
