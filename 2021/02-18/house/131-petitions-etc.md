# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-1. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson, a private citizen, relative to requesting the 
     enactment of Federal legislation that would disallow a mere 
     credit inquiry from becoming, in and of itself, a blemish 
     upon a person's credit-worthiness; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Financial Services.
```
