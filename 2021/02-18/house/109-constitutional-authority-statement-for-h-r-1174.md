# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1174

**Measures:** H.R. 1174

```
           By Mr. ROSENDALE:
       H.R. 1174.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to dispose of and make all needful 
     Rules and Regulations respecting the Territory and other 
     Property belonging to the United States as enumerated in 
     Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2.
```
