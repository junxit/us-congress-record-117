# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The bill clerk read the nomination of Linda Thomas-Greenfield, of 
Louisiana, to be the Representative of the United States of America to 
the United Nations, with the rank and status of the Ambassador 
Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary, and the Representative of the United 
States of America in the Security Council of the United Nations.
```
