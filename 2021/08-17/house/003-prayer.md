# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Pastor Zack Randles, Waterfront Church, Washington, D.C., offered the 
following prayer:
  Lord, Your Word tells us that You give strength to the weary, and You 
increase the power of the weak. You are our place to go when we are 
empty and our second wind when we are worn down.
  How do You restore the weary warrior? If we wait on You, You will 
refill us. If we look to You, You will guide us. If we listen to You, 
You will lead us. You make broken wings soar and weak legs sprint. King 
David often calls You a fortress. You are a place of rest and peace no 
matter what comes to our gates.
  Strengthen us today, God, as we acknowledge You. And be our fortress 
of peace when we run to You. I proclaim these things in the mighty name 
of Jesus.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
