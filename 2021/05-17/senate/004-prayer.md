# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Let us pray.
  Loving God, keep us far from the paths of evil. Remind us that You 
have no desire to rain on our parade. Instead, You want for us all to 
experience a rich and satisfying life. Give our lawmakers wisdom to 
avoid the traps that cause them to deviate from Your plan for their 
lives.
  Lord, keep them from setting an ambush for themselves. May they 
instead listen to Your counsel and walk securely, protected by Your 
loving and prevailing providence. Provide them with a passion to choose 
reverence for You and obedience to Your commands so that You can use 
them as peacemakers for our Nation and world.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
