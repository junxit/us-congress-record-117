# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Ms. CANTWELL. Madam President, on November 15, 2021, I was unable to 
be present for the rollcall vote No. 466 on the Motion to invoke 
cloture on Executive Calendar No. 401, the nomination of Graham Steele 
to be an Assistant Secretary of the Treasury.
  However, had I been present, I would have voted in favor of the 
motion to invoke cloture. I supported Mr. Steele's nomination based on 
his strong track record as a respected expert on financial policy and 
consumer protection and his years of service in senior level positions 
here in the Senate.
```
