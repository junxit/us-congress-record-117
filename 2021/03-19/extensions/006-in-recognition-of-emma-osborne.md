# IN RECOGNITION OF EMMA OSBORNE

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                     IN RECOGNITION OF EMMA OSBORNE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 19, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Emma Osborne on winning the Gold Key award at the 2021 
Central PA Scholastic Art and Writing Competition.
  Emma currently attends Bermudian Springs High School near York 
Springs, Pennsylvania and is passionate about art. Her painting, Rage 
Quilt, garnered her the top award at the

[[Page E278]]

2021 Central PA Scholastic Art and Writing Competition, which was first 
established in 1923 to support teenage artists and writers.
  Congratulations to Emma on this remarkable achievement, and I wish 
her continued success as she pursues her love of art.

                          ____________________
```
