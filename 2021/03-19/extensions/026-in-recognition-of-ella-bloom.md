# IN RECOGNITION OF ELLA BLOOM

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                      IN RECOGNITION OF ELLA BLOOM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 19, 2021

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Ella Bloom on winning the Gold Key Award at the 2021 
Central PA Scholastic Art and Writing Competition.
  Ella attends Bermudian Springs High School near York Springs, 
Pennsylvania and is passionate about art. Her drawing and illustration 
of a French Horn garnered her the top award at the 2021 Central PA 
Scholastic Art and Writing Competition, which was first established in 
1923 to support teenage artists and writers.
  Congratulations to Ella on this remarkable achievement, and I wish 
her continued success as she pursues her love of art.

                          ____________________
```
