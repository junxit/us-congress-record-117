# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2087

**Measures:** H.R. 2087

```
           By Mr. DUNCAN:
       H.R. 2087.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This legislation seeks to pull previously spent money back 
     into the Treasury. To the extent that this raises revenues, 
     it is authorized by Article I, Section 7. To the extent that 
     this is an extension of the power of the purse, it is 
     authorized by Article I, Section 9.
```
