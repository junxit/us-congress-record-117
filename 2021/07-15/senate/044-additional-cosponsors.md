                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 79

  At the request of Mr. Booker, the name of the Senator from Vermont 
(Mr. Leahy) was added as a cosponsor of S. 79, a bill to eliminate the

qualified access technology for the blind.
