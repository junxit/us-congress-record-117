# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 19, 2021

**Speaking:** Cortez Masto, Catherine (C001113) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 19, 2021

  Ms. CORTEZ MASTO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., 
Monday, July 19; that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour 
be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
