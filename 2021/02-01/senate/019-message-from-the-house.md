# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 10

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:32 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following concurrent resolution, in which it requests the 
concurrence of the Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 10. Concurrent resolution permitting the 
     remains of the late United States Capitol Police Officer 
     Brian D. Sicknick to lie in honor in the rotunda of the 
     Capitol.
```
