# Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
            Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, first, I ask unanimous consent that the 
5:30 p.m. confirmation vote on the nomination of Alejandro Mayorkas to 
be Secretary of the Department of Homeland Security occur on Tuesday, 
February 2, at 2:30, with all other provisions of the agreement 
remaining in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
