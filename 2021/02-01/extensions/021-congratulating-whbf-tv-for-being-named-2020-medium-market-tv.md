# CONGRATULATING WHBF-TV FOR BEING NAMED 2020 MEDIUM MARKET TV STATION OF THE YEAR BY THE ILLINOIS BROADCASTERS ASSOCIATION

**Speaking:** Bustos, Cheri (B001286) [D-IL]

```
CONGRATULATING WHBF-TV FOR BEING NAMED 2020 MEDIUM MARKET TV STATION OF 
           THE YEAR BY THE ILLINOIS BROADCASTERS ASSOCIATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHERI BUSTOS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, February 1, 2021

  Mrs. BUSTOS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize WHBF-TV for 
being named 2020 Medium Market TV Station of the Year by the Illinois 
Broadcasters Association.
  The Illinois Broadcasters Association is the leading advocate for the 
broadcast industry in Illinois and is engaged in shaping public policy 
to create a positive legislative and regulatory environment for its 
members. They award TV stations who are the best at what they do, and 
they have named WHBF-TV the best of the medium market TV stations in 
Illinois. As a former journalist, I understand the importance of local 
media and the prestige carried by this award. I commend WHBF-TV for the 
work that they do to inform the public and create a positive work 
environment.
  It is because of community leaders such as WHBF-TV that I am 
especially proud to serve Illinois' 17th Congressional District. Madam 
Speaker, I would like to again formally congratulate WHBF-TV for 
receiving the 2020 Medium Market TV Station of the Year Award.

                          ____________________
```
