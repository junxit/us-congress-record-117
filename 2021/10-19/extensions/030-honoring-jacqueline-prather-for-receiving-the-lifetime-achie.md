# HONORING JACQUELINE PRATHER FOR RECEIVING THE LIFETIME ACHIEVEMENT AWARD FROM THE GIRL SCOUTS OF NORTHEASTERN NEW YORK

**Speaking:** Stefanik, Elise M. (S001196) [R-NY]

```
  HONORING JACQUELINE PRATHER FOR RECEIVING THE LIFETIME ACHIEVEMENT 
          AWARD FROM THE GIRL SCOUTS OF NORTHEASTERN NEW YORK

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 19, 2021

  Ms. STEFANIK. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Jacqueline Prather 
for receiving the Lifetime Achievement Award from the Girl Scouts of 
Northeastern New York.
  Jacqueline Prather is the dedicated Child Care Director of the 
Plattsburgh YMCA Bright Beginnings. She embodies everything the YMCA 
stands for and always does her job with respect and care. In addition 
to her work at the YMCA, she is a military wife who has supported 
numerous community projects at every location her husband has been 
stationed. Her impact over the past 25 years has been exceptionally 
far-reaching. She has worked to ensure over 2,000 babies, toddlers, and 
preschoolers have a loving place to prepare for kindergarten.
  Ms. Prather truly embodies the spirit of care present throughout the 
North Country. She is a wonderful role model for girls throughout the 
region. On behalf of New York's 21st Congressional District, I would 
like to thank Ms. Prather for her commitment to young people and her 
community, and congratulate her for this well-deserved recognition.

                          ____________________
```
