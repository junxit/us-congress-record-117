# SENATE RESOLUTION 422--AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE ATRIUM IN THE PHILIP A. HART SENATE OFFICE BUILDING FOR A BIPARTISAN HALLOWEEN DOG PARADE ON OCTOBER 27, 2021

**Measures:** S.Res. 422

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

SENATE RESOLUTION 422--AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE ATRIUM IN THE PHILIP 
A. HART SENATE OFFICE BUILDING FOR A BIPARTISAN HALLOWEEN DOG PARADE ON 
                            OCTOBER 27, 2021

  Mr. TILLIS submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to.:

                              S. Res. 422

       Resolved,

     SECTION 1. USE OF THE ATRIUM IN THE HART SENATE OFFICE 
                   BUILDING FOR A BIPARTISAN HALLOWEEN DOG PARADE.

       The atrium in the Philip A. Hart Senate Office Building is 
     authorized to be used on October 27, 2021, for a bipartisan 
     Halloween dog parade.
```
