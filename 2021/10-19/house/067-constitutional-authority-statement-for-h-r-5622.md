# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5622

**Measures:** H.R. 5622

```
            By Mr. GALLEGO:
        H.R. 5622.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8, clause 18 allows Congress to make 
     all laws ``which shall be necessary and proper for carrying 
     into execution'' any of Congress's enumerated powers.
```
