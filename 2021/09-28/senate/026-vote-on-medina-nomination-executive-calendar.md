# Vote on Medina Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       Vote on Medina Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Medina nomination?
  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  (Mr. BOOKER assumed the Chair.)

Feinstein) and the Senator from Arizona (Ms. Sinema) are necessarily 
absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Kansas (Mr. Moran).
```
