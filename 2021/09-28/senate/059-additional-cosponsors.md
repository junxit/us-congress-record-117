                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 480

  At the request of Mr. Daines, the name of the Senator from Wyoming 
(Mr. Barrasso) was added as a cosponsor of S. 480, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the deduction for 
qualified business income.
