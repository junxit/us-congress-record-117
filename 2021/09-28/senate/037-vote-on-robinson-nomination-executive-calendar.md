# Vote on Robinson Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Vote on Robinson Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. All time has expired.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Robinson 
nomination?
  Mr. CARDIN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from California (Mrs. 
Feinstein), the Senator from Connecticut (Mr. Murphy), the Senator from 
Arizona (Ms. Sinema), and the Senator from Michigan (Ms. Stabenow) are 
necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Kansas (Mr. Moran) and the Senator from Kentucky (Mr. Paul).
```
