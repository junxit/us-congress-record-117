             GREAT DISMAL SWAMP NATIONAL HERITAGE AREA ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Cuellar). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule 
XX, the unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the 
rules and pass the bill (H.R. 1154) to authorize the Secretary of the

Area, and for other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
