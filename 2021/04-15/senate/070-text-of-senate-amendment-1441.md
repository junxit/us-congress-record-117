# Text of Senate Amendment 1441

**Speaking:** Lee, Mike (L000577) [R-UT]

```
  SA 1441. Mr. LEE submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 937, to facilitate the expedited review of COVID-19 
hate crimes, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       On page 2, line 8, strike ``sole''.
       On page 2, line 9, strike ``expedited''.
       On page 2, beginning on line 20, strike ``, except that the 
     Attorney General may extend such period as appropriate''.
       Beginning on page 2, strike line 25 and all that follows 
     through page 3, line 8 and insert the following: ``States 
     Code) that is motivated by the actual or perceived race, 
     ethnicity, age, color, religion, national origin, sexual 
     orientation, gender, gender identity, or disability of any 
     person.''.
       Beginning on page 3, strike line 9 and all that follows 
     through page 4, line 2.
```
