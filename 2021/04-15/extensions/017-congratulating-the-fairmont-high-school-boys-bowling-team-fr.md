# CONGRATULATING THE FAIRMONT HIGH SCHOOL BOYS BOWLING TEAM FROM KETTERING, OHIO ON WINNING THE STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Turner, Michael R. (T000463) [R-OH]

```
    CONGRATULATING THE FAIRMONT HIGH SCHOOL BOYS BOWLING TEAM FROM 
           KETTERING, OHIO ON WINNING THE STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL R. TURNER

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 15, 2021

  Mr. TURNER. Madam Speaker, I want to congratulate the Fairmont High 
School Boys Bowling Team in Kettering, Ohio, which is in my 
congressional district, on winning the Division I state championship.
  After nine previous appearances in the state tournament, this year's 
win was a fitting finish to an historic season that saw the Fairmont 
Firebirds bowling team bring home its first-ever state title. Months of 
preparation, practice, and experience enabled these talented athletes 
to out-bowl the competition and roll to victory in the tournament. 
These young men have set an example for all of us that success in life 
comes to those who are willing to set goals, and work hard to achieve 
them.
  I congratulate Head Coach Jeremy Fleck and Assistant Coach Matt 
Mahaffey on their victory in the state championship, and especially the 
student athletes: Dayton Foster; Dylan Potts; Tyler Milton; Tyler 
Stegemoller; Colton Mahaffey; and Isaiah Shannon.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the young men on the 
Fairmont High School Bowling Team on their hard-fought victory. They 
have made all of us from the Miami Valley proud.

                          ____________________
```
