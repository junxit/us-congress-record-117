# TRIUMPH INCORPORATED PROVIDES QUALITY CHILDCARE

**Speaking:** Auchincloss, Jake (A000148) [D-MA]

```
            TRIUMPH INCORPORATED PROVIDES QUALITY CHILDCARE

  (Mr. AUCHINCLOSS asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. AUCHINCLOSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in support of investing in 
our care economy.
  Recently, I visited Triumph Incorporated, a Head Start program in the 
city of Taunton in my district. Triumph provides low-cost and high-
quality early education and childcare for children in our community.
  Senator Warren and I heard from parents about the impact Triumph made 
on their lives. In addition to caring for their children, the facility 
provided a space for community and friendship for young families. From 
kindergarten readiness to nurturing children with developmental 
differences, the educators and administrators at Triumph show up for 
those in need.
  Every family in America deserves access to high-quality, affordable, 
and accessible early childhood education and childcare. By investing in 
caregiving, we will help Americans get back to work, create good-paying 
jobs, and build an economy that works for everyone.
```
