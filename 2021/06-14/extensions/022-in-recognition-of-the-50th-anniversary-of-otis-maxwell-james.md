# IN RECOGNITION OF THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF OTIS MAXWELL JAMES, JR. AND SUE ANN WILKINS JAMES

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
 IN RECOGNITION OF THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF OTIS MAXWELL JAMES, JR. AND 
                         SUE ANN WILKINS JAMES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 14, 2021

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the
```
