         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

       Mr. McGOVERN: Committee on Rules. House Resolution 716. 
     Resolution providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 
     2119) to amend the Family Violence Prevention and Services 
     Act to make improvements; providing for consideration of the

     1938 to expand access to breastfeeding accommodations in the

     consideration of the bill (H.R. 3992) to amend the Age 
     Discrimination in Employment Act of 1967 to prohibit 
     employers from limiting, segregating, or classifying 
     applicants for employment; relating to consideration of the

     Human Services of physical activity recommendations for

     to the House Calendar.
